\section*{Data availability}
The source data supporting the figures, tables, benchmark evaluation, expert assessment, knowledge libraries, AI-generated protocols and closed-loop optimisation results are available at https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/8671. The deposited dataset includes the benchmark topic set, structured post-cutoff paper labels, strategy and protocol evaluation outputs, anonymized expert scoring tables, structured strategy, mechanism and template knowledge libraries, AI-generated experimental protocols, interpretability backtracking records, the mechanism-informed Bayesian optimisation report, machine-readable source data for the reported experimental tables and optimisation trajectory, and source data underlying the experimental figure panels, including R-T, M-T, M-H, Jc-B and XRD numerical data together with SEM/AFM image source files. Third-party papers used to construct benchmark labels are identified by DOI in the Supplementary Information and in the deposited metadata. Additional information required to reanalyse the data is available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
