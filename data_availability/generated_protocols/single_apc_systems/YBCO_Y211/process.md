  - (005) peak FWHM <0.15° (Philips X'Pert MRD, Cu Kα, 0.02° step, 10 s/step).  
  - Rocking curve Δω <0.2° for (103) reflection (10 arcsec resolution).  
- **RHEED**: Streak persistence >30 min (K-Space KSA 400, 20 keV, 1° incident angle).  

**Step 6: Performance Benchmarking**  
- **Transport Measurements**:  
  - *J<sub>c</sub>* at 77 K, 3 T: Four-probe method (1 µV/cm criterion, 100 µm bridge width). Target ≥1.5 MA/cm².  
  - Angular uniformity: ±10% *J<sub>c</sub>* variation across 0°–90° (8 T PPMS, 0.5° resolution).  
- **Flux Creep**: Relaxation rate *S* < 0.015 (4.2 K, 5 T, 1 h measurement).  

##### Factors to Consider  
1. **Carbon Control**:  
   - Repeat grinding/calcination (2 cycles: 600°C/2 h in Ar + 880°C/5 h in air) if Raman D/G ratio >0.1 (Renishaw inVia, 514 nm laser).  
2. **Lattice Matching**:  
   - Maintain Y211/YBCO mismatch at -0.15% via PLD stoichiometry (Y:Ba:Cu = 1:1.98:3.02±0.02).  
3. **Defect Correlation**:  
   - Ensure Y211 spacing (80±20 nm) matches ξ(2 nm) < *u* < λ(150 nm) via TEM histogram analysis.  

##### Expected Outcomes  
- *J<sub>c</sub>* enhancement: 30% (1.56 MA/cm² at 77 K, 3 T) vs. undoped YBCO.  
- Thermal stability: *L<sub>c</sub>(T)* scaling validated up to 70 K (0.9*T<sub>c</sub>*).
