##### FINAL EXPERIMENT: ##### Experimental Plan: Optimizing BYNO Doping in EuBCO for Enhanced Flux Pinning via Artificial Pinning Centers (APCs)

##### 1. Experimental Objective
The primary goal of this experiment is to optimize the doping concentration of BYNO (Ba2YNbO6) in EuBCO (EuBa2Cu3O7-δ) to form strong and uniformly distributed nanorods as artificial pinning centers (APCs). This optimization aims to enhance the flux-pinning properties, thereby improving the critical current density (Jc) under high magnetic fields and elevated temperatures. The experiment will also validate the solubility limits of BYNO in EuBCO through phase diagram studies and TEM-EDS mappings.

##### 2. Experimental Materials and Instruments
- **Materials**:
  - EuBCO (EuBa2Cu3O7-δ) target material
  - BYNO (Ba2YNbO6) doping material
  - High-purity oxygen (O2) for controlled atmosphere
  - Substrates (e.g., IBAD-MgO templates) for film deposition
  - Zirconia media for high-energy ball milling
- **Instruments**:
  - Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) for nanorod structure observation
  - Pulsed Laser Deposition (PLD) system for film growth
  - X-ray Diffraction (XRD) for phase and orientation analysis
  - Energy-Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS) for compositional analysis
  - Four-probe measurement setup for Jc determination
  - Thermogravimetric Analysis-Mass Spectrometry (TGA-MS) for carbon content monitoring
  - In-situ Raman spectroscopy for strain evolution tracking

##### 3. Experimental Steps

###### 3.1 Preparation Phase

####### 3.1.1 Target Preparation
- **Step 1: Precursor Synthesis**:
  - Prepare EuBCO and BYNO powders via solid-state reaction.
  - Mix EuBCO with varying concentrations of BYNO (e.g., 2%, 4%, 6% molar ratios).
  - Perform high-energy ball milling using zirconia media at 300 rpm for 4 hours to achieve submicron particle size distribution (D50 < 1μm).
  - Grind the mixture thoroughly to ensure homogeneity.
  - Calcinate the mixture at 900°C for 12 hours in flowing O2 to reduce carbon content.
  - Monitor carbon content using TGA-MS to optimize decarbonization temperature-time profile.
  - Repeat grinding and calcination steps to minimize carbon content further.
  - Perform XRD phase purity verification after calcination to prevent secondary phase formation.

####### 3.1.2 Substrate Preparation
- **Step 2: Substrate Cleaning**:
  - Clean IBAD-MgO substrates using ultrasonic cleaning in acetone and ethanol for 10 minutes each.
  - Rinse substrates with deionized water and dry with nitrogen gas.
- **Step 3: Substrate Annealing**:
  - Anneal substrates at 800°C in a flowing O2 atmosphere for 1 hour to remove surface contaminants and improve crystallinity.

###### 3.2 Implementation Phase

####### 3.2.1 PLD Deposition
- **Step 4: PLD System Setup**:
  - Set PLD parameters: substrate temperature = 800°C, O2 pressure = 0.3 mbar, laser fluence = 2 J/cm² (KrF excimer, 248 nm), repetition rate = 10 Hz.
  - Ensure the PLD chamber is evacuated to a base pressure of 1×10⁻⁶ mbar before introducing O2.
- **Step 5: Film Deposition**:
  - Deposit EuBCO-BYNO composite films with a thickness of ~1.2 µm.
  - Monitor deposition in real-time using Reflection High-Energy Electron Diffraction (RHEED) for strain control.
  - Use in-situ Raman spectroscopy to track interfacial strain evolution during deposition.

####### 3.2.2 Microstructural Characterization
- **Step 6: TEM Analysis**:
  - Prepare cross-sectional TEM samples via Focused Ion Beam (FIB) milling.
  - Analyze nanorod formation, diameter (5-8 nm), and spacing (20-50 nm).
  - Confirm nanorod alignment with the c-axis of EuBCO.
- **Step 7: XRD Analysis**:
  - Perform ω-scan XRD to confirm c-axis alignment (FWHM < 3°).
  - Analyze phase purity and lattice parameters.

####### 3.2.3 Transport Measurements
- **Step 8: Jc Measurement**:
  - Perform four-probe transport measurements at 77 K in magnetic fields up to 5 T.
  - Measure angular dependence of Jc (θ = 0° to 90°) to evaluate pinning anisotropy.
  - Calculate pinning force density (Fp) using the scaling law Fp ~ B²/(B + B0).

###### 3.3 Analysis Phase

####### 3.3.1 Data Analysis
- **Step 9: Critical Current Density (Jc) Analysis**:
  - Determine the critical current (Ic) from the V-I curves where the voltage exceeds the 1 μV/cm criterion.
  - Calculate the critical current density (Jc) using the formula: Jc = Ic / (t × w), where t is the total film thickness and w is the bridge width.
- **Step 10: Flux Pinning Analysis**:
  - Analyze the magnetic field dependence of Jc to evaluate the flux-pinning properties.
  - Compare the Jc performance with undoped EuBCO films to assess the impact of BYNO doping.
  - Calculate pinning force (Fp) using Fp = Jc × μ₀H and identify peak Fp values.

##### 4. Factors to be Considered
- **Solubility of BYNO in EuBCO**: Ensure doping concentration does not exceed solubility limits to avoid secondary phase formation.
- **Uniformity of Doping**: Verify homogeneous distribution of BYNO via EDS mapping.
- **Nanorod Formation**: Optimize deposition parameters to achieve uniform nanorod distribution.
- **Reproducibility**: Confirm nanorod structure and Jc enhancement across multiple samples.
- **Carbon Contamination**: Minimize carbon content through repeated grinding, calcination, and TGA-MS monitoring.

##### 6. Conclusion
This experimental plan aims to optimize BYNO doping in EuBCO to enhance flux pinning through the formation of nanorod APCs.
