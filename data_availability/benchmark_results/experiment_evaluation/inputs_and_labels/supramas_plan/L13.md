# Experimental Plan: MOCVD REBCO–BaZrO3 nanocomposite protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Reconstructed experimental overview

Fabricate and compare a single nominal 1.5 µm layer of (Y0.5Gd0.5)Ba2Cu3O7−δ containing approximately 10 mol% embedded BaZrO3 (BZO) columns with an undoped, otherwise matched mixed-RE film. Determine structural morphology, magnetic current density Js, dynamic relaxation Q, transport behavior and evidence consistent with δl/δTc pinning hypotheses. Column density, diameter and full-thickness continuity are structural targets; supplied performance values are reference comparisons, not measured outcomes or release criteria.

The proposed comparison comprises three independent growths per composition and matched blank templates. Each parent supplies mapped structural, magnetic and transport coupons plus a reserve; sibling coupons are not independent replicates. Use one qualified IBAD-MgO/YSZ/CeO2 architecture and hold matrix composition, thickness, thermal history and measurement geometry constant.

Resources include qualified metal-organic precursors, high-purity O2/Ar, an interlocked and calibrated MOCVD reactor, oxygen furnace, XRD, AFM, thickness metrology, FIB/HAADF-STEM with compositional and diffraction capability, VSM-PPMS and qualified high-current cryogenic transport equipment. Powder composite targets are excluded. In-house buffer preparation and pressure-compatible RHEED are conditional, not core requirements.

First qualify templates, precursor delivery, incorporation, instruments and analysis procedures. Then perform continuous nanocomposite growth and matched oxygenation, followed by nondestructive characterization, mapped coupon allocation and destructive structural sampling. Apply structural disposition gates before matched magnetic and transport measurements. Finally consolidate uncertainty-aware GIS analysis, supported phase mapping, reference comparisons and failure records.

All P settings require pilot calibration; unresolved gas flows, total pressures, loading definitions and acceptance tolerances remain prerequisites. Anticipated outputs are traceable process records, structural distributions, background-corrected and geometry-normalized measurements, supported pinning interpretations and explicitly bounded or unresolved findings.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Qualify the comparison matrix, materials, equipment and operating envelope

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and sample matrix
Fabricate (Y0.5Gd0.5)Ba2Cu3O7−δ containing approximately 10 mol% embedded BZO columns in one 1.5 µm layer. Determine morphology, magnetic current density Js, dynamic relaxation Q and evidence for hybrid δl/δTc pinning. Supplied performance values are comparison targets, not measurements or release criteria.

| IDs | Composition and purpose | Allocation |
|---|---|---|
| B10-01…03 | Approximately 10 mol% BZO | Three independent MOCVD growths |
| R0-01…03 | Undoped, identical mixed-RE matrix | Three matched independent growths |
| BL-01…03 | Identical template without superconducting film | Magnetic/background controls |

Replication and pairing are proposed. Alternate composition order; record reactor cleaning and carryover. Each parent supplies C: structural coupon, M: unpatterned rectangular magnetic coupon, and T: transport coupon. Preserve a reserve. Map all coupon positions; siblings are not independent replicates. Hold template lot/stack, substrate orientation, matrix stoichiometry, thickness, growth temperature, oxygen history and measurement geometry constant. Adjust precursor delivery only as needed to maintain matrix composition when introducing BZO.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and prerequisites
Use certified Y, Gd, Ba, Cu and Zr metal-organic precursors; the draft's tmhd compounds and Cu(acac)2 are candidates requiring delivery/decomposition qualification. Use high-purity O2/Ar, compatible precursor solvent if needed, qualified IBAD-MgO templates with YSZ beneath CeO2, approved cleaning solvents, resist/etchant and qualified low-resistance metal contacts. Powder composite targets are not MOCVD feedstocks and are excluded.

Required: interlocked MOCVD reactor with calibrated heater, mass-flow controllers, pressure gauges and deposition-rate monitor; oxygen furnace; XRD with rocking/pole scans; AFM; thickness metrology; FIB and HAADF-STEM with EDS/diffraction; VSM-PPMS; four-probe cryogenic transport apparatus with high-current supply and nanovoltmeter. PLD is needed only if qualified buffers are unavailable.

Before release, verify precursor identities, vapor-pressure stability, BZO molar denominator, incorporated stoichiometry, template compatibility, and pressure/temperature calibration. Approve the GIS implementation, equations, assumptions and analysis windows before examining composition-dependent outcomes. Record instrument serial numbers and calibration dates. RHEED is optional only if compatible with reactor pressure; it is not a required growth monitor.

##### 3. Consolidated operating conditions
All settings below marked P are proposals drawn from the draft or selected here; they require pilot calibration, not attribution to the task. Unspecified tolerances must be approved before production.

| Operation | Setting and control |
|---|---|
| Template | One qualified IBAD-MgO/YSZ/CeO2 stack; draft buffer thicknesses 100/50 nm are P |
| Buffer screening | P: cap rocking FWHM ≤0.5°, in-plane FWHM ≤5°, AFM RMS <2 nm |
| MOCVD precondition | P: 700°C, 30 min, O2 partial pressure 50 Torr; total pressure/gas flows unresolved |
| Growth | P: 750±10°C; O2 partial pressure 20 Torr; total pressure and carrier flows require qualification |
| Rate/thickness | P: 20 nm min−1; nominal 75 min gives 1500 nm. Endpoint by calibrated metrology; P acceptance 1.5±0.1 µm and spatial variation ≤5% |
| Loading | Task: approximately 10 mol% BZO; define denominator and analytical acceptance before use |
| Oxygenation | P: 450±10°C, 2 h, flowing O2 at approximately 1 atm; flow must be qualified; cool at 5°C min−1 |
| Magnetic temperatures | P: 3, 4.2, 10, 20, 30, 40, 49, 50, 60, 70, 77, 80, 85 K; add measured 0.76Tc and points near Tc |
| Field evaluation | P: μ0H=0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.5, 8 T; primary H∥c |
| Dynamic sweep rates | Task range: 50–200 Oe s−1; P selection 50, 100, 200 Oe s−1 = 0.005, 0.010, 0.020 T s−1 |
| Measurement stability | P: temperature ±0.1 K, angle ≤0.5°; verify actual field/rate uncertainty |
| Transport | P: width 50 µm, tap spacing 1.0 mm, Ec=1 µV cm−1 ±5%; measure actual dimensions |

Instrument prerequisites: temperature coverage must include 3 K and the normal state above measured Tc. To evaluate complete hysteresis branches through 8 T, propose ±9 T endpoints, subject to verifying adequate reversal margin and magnet ramp capability. An 8 T-limited magnet cannot automatically provide valid loop width/Q at its endpoint.

- The proposed independent-growth comparison separates composition effects from template, thermal-history and geometry differences; sibling coupons retain their parent identity rather than becoming independent replicates. **Source:** B000, B001, B002.
- MOCVD requires qualified precursor delivery and template compatibility. Candidate precursor identities remain subject to qualification, and powder composite targets are explicitly excluded. **Source:** B003, B004, B005.
- P settings are proposals rather than established recipes. Oxygen partial pressure does not resolve total pressure or carrier flow, and unspecified tolerances remain approval prerequisites. **Source:** B006, B007.
- Complete-loop measurements through 8 T require verified reversal margin and ramp capability; an instrument limited to 8 T does not automatically support valid endpoint loop widths or Q. **Source:** B008.

### Experimental Task 2: Prepare qualified templates, grow matched films and complete oxygenation hand-offs

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Template intake → growth operator:** Assign IDs; photograph and inspect for damage. Obtain supplier stack/texture records. Clean only using a template-compatible, qualified sequence; the draft's acetone/IPA/water ultrasonication is not assumed harmless. Dry with N2. Measure texture and three AFM fields. Quarantine damaged or unqualified templates.
2. If preparing buffers in-house, qualify the full stack separately before core growth. The draft's PLD temperatures, pressures and pulse energies are starting proposals, not a transferable recipe: calibrate fluence, rate, thickness and interface compatibility. Do not introduce different buffer stacks between arms.
3. Leak-test reactor, verify exhaust/interlocks and run delivery witnesses. Establish Y:Gd=1:1 and matrix RE:Ba:Cu=1:2:3 after accounting for Ba consumed by BZO. Zr/RE feed ratio alone does not establish BZO mol%. Record the convention and incorporation calibration.
4. Apply the qualified precondition, then co-deliver RE/Ba/Cu/Zr for B10; omit Zr for R0. Maintain continuous nanocomposite growth, not sequential BZO layers. Log temperature, total/O2 pressures, flow histories and endpoint. Reject interrupted/untraceable runs from the matched primary analysis.
5. Oxygenate both arms identically and cool using the qualified schedule. Inspect cracks and measure thickness/composition. Preserve raw deposition traces.
6. **Growth operator → characterization lead:** Transfer dry, labeled parents with process records. Perform nondestructive characterization before cutting; map C/M/T/reserve positions. **Characterization lead → magnetic/transport operators:** Supply actual dimensions, structural status and coupon maps. Destructive STEM uses C, never the completed M or T specimen.

#### Related Knowledge

- Template-compatible cleaning and separately qualified in-house buffers protect the fixed comparison architecture; draft cleaning and PLD conditions are not transferable recipes. **Source:** B009.
- Delivery qualification must account for Ba consumed by BZO while maintaining the mixed-RE matrix. Zr/RE feed ratio alone does not establish incorporated BZO molar loading. **Source:** B009.
- Continuous co-delivery creates the specified nanocomposite workflow rather than sequential BZO layers. Matched oxygenation, traceable process records and nondestructive characterization precede mapped coupon hand-offs. **Source:** B009.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize film texture and column morphology and apply structural disposition gates

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structure and quality gates
XRD: proposed Cu Kα wavelength approximately 1.5406 Å, 2θ=5–90°, step 0.02°. Identify REBCO (00l); use allowed REBCO (005) for rocking scans and off-axis (103) for in-plane texture. CeO2 (002)/(111) and YSZ (111) are appropriate buffer checks. For cubic BZO, (200) and (002) are symmetry-equivalent families; weak/missing BZO intensity does not establish absence. Record instrument broadening. P film rocking-screen threshold: <1°; retain full distributions rather than only pass/fail.

AFM: P three 5×5 µm² fields per parent, 512² pixels; save raw and consistently flattened images. Inspect cracks optically/SEM.

Prepare protected cross-sectional and plan-view STEM specimens; P final FIB polish at 2 kV. Calibrate STEM dose rather than imposing an unsupported universal dose limit. Use HAADF, EDS including Y/Gd/Ba/Cu/Zr, and nanodiffraction to identify BZO and unwanted phases. Acquire full-thickness cross-section montages and ≥50 column diameters across ≥3 locations; continuity cannot be inferred from 100–300 nm segments. Count ≥10 distributed plan-view fields with area, edge-count rules and uncertainty recorded.

Calculate density N/A. At 1.1×10^11 cm−2=1.1×10^15 m−2, Bφ=Φ0n≈2.28 T and characteristic spacing n−1/2≈30 nm. A 100×100 nm² field contains approximately 11 columns. These are derived checks, not measured results.

**Disposition:** Unwanted phases, cracks, discontinuous/agglomerated columns, poor texture or thickness/composition failures trigger quarantine and diagnosis. Characterize failures if useful, label them off-target, and replace them for the matched primary set. Report “not detected within sampled area/detection limit,” never absolute phase absence. Column-density acceptance tolerance remains a prerequisite.

#### Related Knowledge

- XRD texture checks and consistently processed AFM images provide complementary structural records. Weak or missing BZO diffraction intensity does not establish its absence. **Source:** B010, B011.
- Full-thickness STEM montages, elemental analysis and diffraction support column and phase identification; short cross-sectional segments cannot establish continuity through the film. **Source:** B012.
- Density, matching-field and spacing calculations are consistency checks, not measured results. Distributed sampling, counting rules and uncertainty are necessary to interpret the structural observations. **Source:** B012, B013.
- Off-target structure or composition triggers quarantine and diagnosis. Phase statements remain limited to the sampled area and detection limit, and column-density acceptance tolerance is still pending. **Source:** B014.

### Experimental Task 4: Measure magnetic hysteresis, dynamic relaxation and sibling transport behavior

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Magnetometry, transport and analysis
###### Magnetic chain
Measure blank templates and holder backgrounds using identical orientation, temperatures and rates; center each M coupon. Measure Tc independently from low-field magnetization and transport transition. Zero-field cool from above Tc for each standardized loop sequence; record residual field and sweep history. Acquire symmetric full loops at each rate after thermal stabilization. Verify actual rates from timestamps and exclude reversal transients or unstable-temperature intervals.

For rectangular coupons with in-plane dimensions a≤b in cm and film volume V=abd, use Js=20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)] A cm−2, where ΔM is full loop width in emu cm−3 after background correction. Normalize moment to superconducting film volume, not substrate volume. Validate Bean applicability, demagnetization/self-field effects and low-field exclusions; do not apply this expression to transport data.

At matched T/H, regress ln Js against ln|dB/dt| to obtain Q and uncertainty. Reject points below loop-width quantification limits. Compare Q<0.06 specifically below 50 K through the measured valid field range. Zero-applied-field magnetic Js is a critical-state estimate, not automatically a transport self-field measurement.

###### Transport chain
Pattern sibling T bridges using a separately qualified etch process; the draft's 0.1% HNO3/30 s is only a pilot candidate. Inspect width, edge damage and isolation. Qualify contact metallurgy/annealing without oxygen loss; do not adopt the draft's N2 contact anneal untested. Measure tap spacing, film thickness and contact I–V behavior.

Nominal area=50 µm×1.5 µm=7.5×10−7 cm². Thus 15 MA cm−2 requires 11.25 A; 4 MA cm−2 requires 3 A. The draft's 100 mA source reaches only 0.133 MA cm−2. Use a qualified high-current system with headroom, leads and thermal management; proposed 20 A capacity requires verification. At 1 mm spacing, Vc=EcL=0.1 µV=100 nV: demonstrate offset-corrected noise below this criterion.

Acquire low-current R(T), defining Tc midpoint and 10–90% width. Measure I–V at 3/4.2 K self-field and at 0.76Tc, 77 K and above, through 8 T where superconducting. Ramp/pulse duration requires heating calibration; reverse current to remove offsets. Set validated current/voltage/temperature trips. Calculate Jc=Ic/(wd); report current-limited points as lower bounds. Cross-check magnetic trends without equating differing electric-field criteria.

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched blank and holder measurements, standardized cooling and sweep histories, and timestamp-verified rates support background-corrected magnetic comparisons. **Source:** B015.
- The rectangular-coupon Bean expression uses superconducting film volume and actual geometry. Q comes from matched-temperature and matched-field rate dependence, with quantification limits and applicability checks retained. **Source:** B016, B017.
- Bridge etching and contact processing require separate qualification. The nominal bridge dimensions imply high-current and low-noise demands that the draft's low-current source cannot meet. **Source:** B018, B019.
- Transport uses measured dimensions, qualified heating control and offset removal. Current-limited results are lower bounds, and differing electric-field criteria prevent automatic equivalence with magnetic Js. **Source:** B020.

### Experimental Task 5: Consolidate pinning inference, supported phase maps, diagnosis and controlled reporting

#### Specific Content

###### Pinning inference and phase map
Apply the preapproved GIS procedure to background-corrected Js(T,H) and Q(T,H), propagating errors and documenting thermal/quantum-creep applicability. Compare reconstructed normalized temperature dependence with δl and δTc hypotheses; morphology or a normalized force fit alone cannot establish their fractions. Report Uc/kB only from a validated inversion with stated creep model. Define irreversibility by an explicit signal threshold; report bounds if it exceeds 8 T. Map detectable hysteresis, Q and supported elastic/creep regimes; do not invent phase boundaries. Define the lift-factor denominator before calculation.

##### 7. Diagnosis, records and controlled feedback
Low current: check heating/contact offsets, oxygenation, thickness and texture before changing growth. Poor columns: examine incorporation, delivery drift and temperature calibration. Cracks: check template interfaces and cooling. Asymmetric loops/noisy Q: recenter, repeat blanks, inspect field history and actual ramp rates.

Record all raw files, calibrations, dimensions, image selection rules, excluded points, backgrounds, fits/scripts and uncertainties under parent/coupon IDs. Change one calibrated factor at a time in pilot samples; repeat both arms after any process change. Keep failures and all deviations visible.

Controls: ventilated precursor handling, SDS-compatible PPE, oxygen-clean gas hardware, solvent/acid segregation, exhausted furnace/reactor, laser/FIB interlocks, cryogen oxygen-deficiency monitoring, magnet exclusion zones and high-current trips. Stop on leaks, loss of cooling/exhaust or uncontrolled heating.

Optional extensions only after the matched core: H⊥c/angular scans and fixed-field temporal relaxation. Report normalized S=−dln|Mirr|/dln(time) separately from Q; neither replaces dynamic measurements or controls.

#### Related Knowledge

- Preapproved GIS analysis must propagate errors and state creep-model applicability. Morphology or normalized force fitting alone cannot establish δl/δTc fractions, and unsupported phase boundaries must not be introduced. **Source:** B021.
- Diagnosis checks measurement artifacts and existing process records before growth changes. Controlled pilot feedback changes one calibrated factor at a time and repeats both comparison arms after a process change. **Source:** B022, B023.
- The listed precursor, oxygen, thermal, cryogenic, magnetic and high-current controls are execution prerequisites, with explicit stop conditions for unsafe operation. **Source:** B024.
- Angular measurements and fixed-field temporal relaxation are optional extensions after the matched core. Temporal S is reported separately and does not replace dynamic Q measurements or controls. **Source:** B025.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Consolidated execution plan

#### 1. Release prerequisites and matched design

Use one qualified IBAD-MgO/YSZ/CeO2 template architecture for approximately 10 mol% BZO and undoped (Y0.5Gd0.5)Ba2Cu3O7−δ films, each a single nominal 1.5 µm layer. Retain the proposed three independent growths per arm and matched blanks. Alternate composition order; record cleaning and carryover. Hold matrix stoichiometry, template, thickness, thermal history and geometry constant. Map structural, magnetic, transport and reserve coupons; siblings are not independent replicates.

Before production, qualify precursor identity, delivery/decomposition, incorporation, the BZO molar denominator, template compatibility and instrument calibrations. Approve unspecified tolerances, column-density acceptance and GIS equations, assumptions and windows before composition-dependent analysis. Candidate precursors, cleaning, etching and contact processes remain unqualified until approved. Powder targets are excluded; in-house buffers require separate qualification, and RHEED is optional only when pressure-compatible.

#### 2. Fabrication and structural release

Inspect, document and characterize templates; quarantine damage or incompatibility. Verify reactor leak integrity, exhaust, interlocks and delivery witnesses. Establish Y:Gd=1:1 and matrix RE:Ba:Cu=1:2:3 while accounting for Ba incorporated into BZO; feed ratio alone cannot establish loading.

Retain the following as P settings requiring calibration: preconditioning at 700°C for 30 min with O2 partial pressure 50 Torr; growth at 750±10°C with O2 partial pressure 20 Torr; rate 20 nm min−1, with nominal 75 min corresponding to 1500 nm. Total pressures and carrier flows remain unresolved. Use calibrated thickness endpoints; P acceptance is 1.5±0.1 µm with spatial variation ≤5%. Co-deliver RE/Ba/Cu/Zr continuously for BZO films and omit Zr for controls. Record complete process traces; exclude interrupted or untraceable runs from the matched primary analysis.

Oxygenate both arms identically using a qualified schedule; the P proposal is 450±10°C for 2 h in flowing O2 near atmospheric pressure, followed by cooling at 5°C min−1. Oxygen flow requires qualification.

Perform nondestructive XRD, AFM, crack inspection and thickness/composition checks before cutting. Use structural coupons for protected cross-sectional and plan-view STEM, including full-thickness montages, compositional/diffraction identification and distributed column sampling. Compare with target density approximately 1.1×10^11 cm−2, diameter 5–9 nm and c-axis continuity. Retain distributions and uncertainty; derived matching-field checks are not observations. Quarantine structural failures, label them off-target and replace them for the matched set. Report non-detection only within sampling and detection limits.

#### 3. Magnetic and transport measurements

Measure blanks and holders under matched conditions; center magnetic coupons and determine Tc independently by magnetization and transport. Standardize zero-field cooling, residual-field records and sweep history. Acquire stabilized full loops with primary H∥c using the source temperature/field grid and measured 0.76Tc. Dynamic rates span 50–200 Oe s−1; P selections are 50, 100 and 200 Oe s−1. Verify actual rates and uncertainties. Proposed ±9 T endpoints require adequate reversal margin; an 8 T limit does not establish valid endpoint widths.

Background-correct moment, normalize to superconducting film volume and apply the rectangular Bean expression only after geometry and applicability checks. Exclude unstable intervals, reversal transients and unquantifiable widths. Obtain Q and uncertainty from matched-T/H logarithmic rate regression.

Qualify bridge processing and contacts; measure actual width, thickness and tap spacing. P dimensions are 50 µm width and 1.0 mm spacing, with Ec=1 µV cm−1 ±5%. Verify high-current headroom, criterion-level noise, heating calibration and trips. Acquire R(T) and I–V at the specified low-temperature, reduced-temperature and high-temperature conditions through valid fields. Reverse current for offsets, report current-limited lower bounds and do not equate magnetic and transport criteria.

#### 4. Interpretation, records and safety

Apply preapproved GIS with propagated uncertainty and stated creep applicability. Infer δl/δTc support cautiously; report Uc/kB only from validated inversion. Define irreversibility thresholds and lift-factor denominators; report bounds rather than invented phase boundaries. Compare supplied Js, lift-factor, Uc/kB and Q targets without claiming achievement; distinguish reduced-temperature comparisons from 77 K objectives.

Preserve raw data, exclusions, failures and deviations. Diagnose artifacts before changing growth; pilot changes require both arms to be repeated. Maintain the specified precursor, oxygen, exhaust, interlock, cryogenic, magnet and high-current safeguards; stop for leaks, cooling/exhaust loss or uncontrolled heating. Angular scans and temporal relaxation remain optional after the matched core.
