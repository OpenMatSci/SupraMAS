# Experimental Plan: Y123/Y211 layered-precursor bulk protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Experimental overview

Compare layered C-MPP precursors with CPP-only and MPP-only controls for seeded Y123/Y211 bulk fabrication, assessing porosity, cracking, levitation force density, relaxation and supporting evidence of single-domain texture. C-MPP places CPP above MPP; the precursor layers become a bulk composite rather than separate superconducting films. Supplied performance values are reference targets, not measured outcomes.

The proposed design uses three independently pressed bulks per arm, common external green geometry, pressing history, support, buffer preparation and seed orientation. All arms share one furnace run, oxygenation history and force-testing procedure. Randomized furnace positions, blinded analysis codes and retained fabrication failures support the comparison.

Materials include the specified precursor powders, CeO2, Yb2O3 support, CPP buffers and SmBCO seeds. Equipment covers contained powder preparation, qualified pressing, TSMG and oxygen furnaces, dimensional metrology, SCML-05 force testing, a Hall probe and Halbach guideway, optical microscopy/ImageJ, XRD and COMSOL. Qualified electron microscopy is conditional on investigating nanoscale redistribution.

Before batching, approve the unresolved composition denominators, MPP ratio and oxygen state, layer fraction, buffer state/thickness and support thickness. Proposed mixing, pressing, geometry, growth and force-trajectory settings remain subject to qualification. Fabricate and inspect the assemblies, perform common TSMG and oxygenation at 450 °C for 220 h, then measure intact bulks at 77 K before destructive sectioning.

Anticipated outputs are traceable fabrication yield, dimensional maps, force loops and relaxation traces, section porosity and crack maps, supporting texture measurements, and conditional geometry normalization. Report the supplied force-density, relaxation and normalized-force targets separately. Levitation and microscopy do not establish fatigue life or transport critical current.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Approve recipes, establish comparison arms and qualify preparation resources

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and release prerequisites
Compare C-MPP with CPP and MPP under identical seeded growth, oxygenation and 77 K levitation conditions. Test whether the layered precursor improves porosity, cracking, force density and relaxation while retaining single-domain texture. All outcomes below are targets or supplied observations, not measurements made under this proposal. Levitation and microscopy alone do not establish fatigue life or transport critical current.

Before releasing powder preparation, approve a signed recipe defining: the denominator of CPP's 35 mol % Y211; the denominator and distribution of 1 wt % CeO2; MPP Y2O3:Ba2Cu3Ox ratio and oxygen state; C-MPP layer fraction; buffer thickness/state; and Yb2O3 support thickness. These are not fully specified. Proposed conventions are 35 mol % of total Y123+Y211 formula units and CeO2 mass equal to 1% of undoped precursor mass, applied consistently to both precursor types and buffers. Confirm these conventions rather than silently adopting them. Do not substitute 35 wt % or a 70:30 recipe.

Qualify press capacity, die dimensions, furnace uniformity, seed survival, oxygen delivery, XRD geometry, force capacity and cryogenic fixture clearance before the core batch.

##### 2. Samples and controls
| IDs | Main precursor | Shared assembly |
|---|---|---|
| B01-CPP-01 to -03 | CPP throughout | Yb2O3 support → precursor → 6 mm CPP buffer → SmBCO seed |
| B01-MPP-01 to -03 | MPP throughout | Same |
| B01-CMP-01 to -03 | Lower MPP, upper CPP | Same |

Three independently pressed bulks per arm are proposed; fields of view and repeated loops are not independent samples. An equal green-volume CPP:MPP split is a proposed initial C-MPP choice requiring qualification. Use identical external green geometry, seed orientation, buffer preparation, support and pressing history. Record green density rather than forcing all compositions to the same density.

Randomize furnace positions within the calibrated uniform zone. Preserve blind analysis codes. Photograph and retain failed specimens; include fabrication yield and failure counts separately from performance of intact bulks.

##### 3. Materials, equipment and consolidated settings
Materials: Y123, Y211, Y2O3, Ba2Cu3Ox, CeO2 and Yb2O3 powders; SmBCO crystals; CPP buffers; compatible refractory setters; oxygen; liquid nitrogen; diamond cutting/polishing consumables. Record supplier, lot, purity, moisture and particle-size certificates; purchase quantities follow approved recipes, not arbitrary draft masses.

Equipment: calibrated balance, contained mixer/mill and sieve, rated die/press, TSMG furnace, oxygen furnace, dimension gauges, camera, optical microscope and ImageJ, diamond saw/polisher, XRD, SCML-05, Hall probe, Halbach guideway and COMSOL. Nanoscale confirmation requires qualified electron microscopy, discussed below.

| Parameter | Setting and status | Control/verification |
|---|---|---|
| Final bulk geometry | Proposed area near 550 mm²; thickness near 9.5 mm | Map actual geometry; retain 441–676 mm² and 9–10 mm for validated normalization |
| Circular reference diameter | Derived: √(4×550/π) = 26.46 mm | Determine green diameter from measured shrinkage |
| Compaction | Proposed 200 MPa, 5 min, common to all arms | Pressure ±5%; qualify die, load and ejection |
| TSMG | Proposed schedule in §4 | Calibrated specimen-zone temperature within ±2 °C; log actual profile |
| Oxygenation | 450 °C, 220 h, task-specified | Proposed ±2 °C; count hold after temperature stabilization |
| Oxygen flow/cooling | Proposed 1 L/min; 1 °C/min to ambient | Verify flowmeter and safe exhaust; flow ±10% |
| Force environment | 77 K; 0.67 T at 5 mm | Record bath pressure/temperature and field map |
| Force acquisition | Proposed 10 Hz | Force uncertainty ≤0.1 N and gap uncertainty ≤0.1 mm are qualification goals |
| Relaxation | 180 s | Proposed duration tolerance ±1 s |

Press load is pressure × die area. At 26.46 mm diameter, 200 MPa requires 110 kN, approximately 11.2 tonne-force; the larger green die needs more. A 2–3 tonne load is not equivalent. Do not use an undersized press. The former 16 mm bulk has only 201 mm² area and is outside the stated normalization range.

#### Related Knowledge

- Recipe approval is necessary because composition denominators, MPP composition, layer fraction and assembly thicknesses remain unresolved. The stated denominator conventions are proposals, not released recipes. **Source:** B001.
- Independent bulks, shared assembly conditions, randomized furnace positions and blinded analysis distinguish arm-level comparisons from repeated observations on one specimen; fabrication failures remain part of yield reporting. **Source:** B003, B004, B005.
- Equipment qualification must match the proposed geometry and pressure. Press capacity follows pressure multiplied by die area, so a smaller nominal load cannot substitute for the required compaction load. **Source:** B002, B008, B009.

### Experimental Task 2: Prepare, press, assemble, grow and oxygenate the bulk specimens

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Powder preparation.** Weigh approved recipes; record actual masses and balance uncertainty. Prepare CPP and MPP separately with equal mixing exposure. Qualify mixing duration/media on sacrificial powder; use zirconia media only after checking contamination and particle coarsening. The draft's 300 rpm/4 h wet milling is a candidate, not a validated requirement. If used, record solvent loading in explicit units and dry to constant mass in solvent-rated equipment. Sieve agglomerates using a proposed 100 µm mesh. Do not assume milling guarantees submicron particles. Start binder-free; binder use requires an approved burnout qualification.
2. **Pressing.** Load MPP first, level without disturbing the bed, then load CPP for C-MPP. Use the same loading pauses for controls. Compact with the qualified common schedule; release slowly and eject with support. Record layer masses, dimensions, mass, geometric density and visible defects. No unsupported 92–95% theoretical-density gate applies to this multiphase green body. Reject/repress cracked or delaminated green bodies before furnace loading, retaining the failure record.
3. **Assembly hand-off.** Transfer accepted labeled pellets to growth staff with recipe and inspection sheets. Place on a common Yb2O3 support layer over a qualified refractory setter; Yb2O3 is a growth support, not a third active precursor layer. Center the 6 mm CPP buffer and SmBCO seed, c-axis approximately normal to the top. Record orientation and photographs.
4. **TSMG.** Qualify this draft-supported candidate schedule on sacrificial assemblies: heat at 5 °C/min to 1050 °C, hold 1 h; cool at 1 °C/min to 1012 °C, hold 30 min; cool at 1 °C/h to 980 °C; cool at 100 °C/h to ambient. Confirm complete precursor decomposition, seed survival and the actual growth window before using it. Do not combine it with the conflicting 1100 °C or 1 °C/h approach-to-growth schedules. Run all core arms together without composition-specific adjustments. Archive controller and independent temperature records.
5. **Growth inspection.** Photograph facets, seed condition, parasitic grains, top bulging and cracks; map shrinkage. Four facet lines support but do not prove single-domain growth. Quarantine loose or fractured material from force testing. Do not discard growth failures from yield comparisons.
6. **Oxygenation hand-off.** Transfer intact bulks with growth records to oxygenation staff. Anneal all arms at 450 °C for 220 h in qualified flowing oxygen; cool using the common approved rate. Reinspect cracks and dimensions. Surface blackness does not verify oxygenation. Qualification on sacrificial material should confirm a stable superconducting transition or oxygen-content endpoint for this thickness.
7. **Testing hand-off.** Transfer intact, unsectioned bulks and dimensional maps to force testing. Complete force measurements before destructive sectioning. Return thermally recovered samples to characterization with their measurement history.

#### Related Knowledge

- Separate CPP and MPP preparation with equal mixing exposure preserves the comparison. Milling media, candidate wet-milling conditions and any binder use require qualification rather than assumptions about particle size or cleanliness. **Source:** B010.
- C-MPP loading places MPP below CPP, while matched control loading pauses and common pressing preserve procedural comparability. Green cracks or delamination are addressed before furnace loading, with failures retained. **Source:** B010.
- The growth schedule remains a sacrificial-assembly qualification candidate. Common growth and oxygenation histories, documented hand-offs and force testing before sectioning maintain specimen traceability. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 3: Measure intact-bulk levitation loops and force relaxation

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Force loops and relaxation
Calibrate force zero/span, fixture background, gap origin and stage travel. Map guideway field at the actual measurement location; 0.67 T is a field specification at 5 mm, not the required trapped field. Verify load-cell capacity exceeds anticipated forces with safety margin and the fixture cannot collide with the guideway.

Proposed common trajectory: warm above the superconducting transition between independent tests; cool in liquid nitrogen away from the guideway at a verified negligible-field location; stabilize for 10 min. Transfer using a reproducible nonmagnetic fixture while maintaining immersion/thermal contact. Define gap from the bulk's lower face to the guideway surface, documenting intervening cryostat walls.

Start at 30 mm, approach 5 mm and return at 1 mm/s. Qualify that 30 mm is sufficiently low-field and extend it if necessary. Record force, gap, time and temperature throughout. Perform a separate freshly ZFC approach to 5 mm, stop, then acquire 180 s relaxation. Define t=0 as completed stage settling, report settling delay, and use R = 100[F(0)−F(180 s)]/F(0). This proposed definition must be confirmed for comparison with the supplied 13% target; it is not logarithmic SQUID relaxation. Repeat twice per bulk with independent ZFC histories; report both traces and their average.

Retain raw maximum force and volume V=A×t. Reference V0=550×9.5=5225 mm³. Only after validation use Fnorm=Fmax(V0/V). Fv=Fmax/V. At V0, 0.0127 N/mm³ gives 66.36 N, whereas 76 N implies 0.01455 N/mm³. Report both supplied targets separately pending clarification.

#### Related Knowledge

- Force calibration, gap definition, fixture clearance and local field mapping establish the measurement basis. The specified 0.67 T at 5 mm describes the guideway field, not a trapped-field requirement. **Source:** B011, B012.
- Independent zero-field-cooled histories distinguish repeated tests. The proposed relaxation measure uses force loss over 180 s after stage settling and must be confirmed for comparison with the supplied target. **Source:** B012, B013.
- Raw force and actual volume remain primary records. Reference-volume normalization is conditional, and the supplied force-density and normalized-force targets must remain separate because they are inconsistent at the reference volume. **Source:** B014.

### Experimental Task 4: Characterize geometry, microstructure and texture and qualify force normalization

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Characterization and normalization
**Geometry/optics:** Before sectioning, measure area outline and thickness at center and multiple peripheral locations. Report taper, bulging and uncertainty. Cut an a–c section through the seed axis using a guarded diamond saw and manufacturer-approved coolant; do not use flammable ethanol coolant without specifically rated equipment. Grind 500→1200→2400 grit and polish 3→1→0.25 µm diamond, adjusting loads on sacrificial material to prevent pullout.

Acquire calibrated overview mosaics and at least five systematically located fields per upper, middle and lower region. Use matched illumination, pixel scale and preparation across arms. Lock an ImageJ pore segmentation method after manually checking a proposed 10% of images; separate cracks, pores, inclusions and polishing pullout. Report area-weighted section porosity, spatial variation and detection limit. The supplied 18.1% versus 25.1% values are comparison observations, not mandatory batch rejection thresholds. Record all detectable macro-cracks, including length and opening; do not permit cracks merely because they are smaller than 0.6 mm.

Optical images at 1 µm/pixel cannot measure 20–260 nm Y211. If testing nanoparticle redistribution, use calibrated high-resolution SEM and phase-sensitive analysis; employ TEM/diffraction if the 20 nm end or Y211 identity remains unresolved. Require sufficient pixels across the smallest reported particle and report sampling bias. Otherwise state nanoscale redistribution is unverified.

**XRD support:** Calibrate wavelength/angles and measure top-face θ–2θ scans, proposed 20–80° with 0.02° steps, plus regional (005) rocking curves and off-axis (103) pole figures where equipment permits. For Cu Kα≈1.5406 Å and supplied lattice parameters, d103≈2.727 Å, 2θ103≈32.8°; d005=2.336 Å, 2θ005≈38.5°. Locate actual peaks before scanning. Compare sector orientations; (00l) dominance alone proves neither in-plane alignment nor one domain. Do not impose film-derived FWHM thresholds or assume micro-XRD capability.

**COMSOL:** Use electromagnetic H-formulation with measured guideway fields, actual shapes, identical temperature/history and documented superconducting E–J assumptions. Obtain or independently justify Jc(B) and exponent inputs; do not fit each arm solely to force and call that validation. Demonstrate mesh/time-step convergence and near-linear force scaling over the stated geometry windows. Proposed allowable normalization deviation is 5%, declared before analysis. If inputs or linearity are inadequate, report raw force and direct geometry-matched comparisons without extrapolated normalization.

#### Related Knowledge

- Dimensional mapping and matched a–c section preparation support area-weighted porosity analysis. Segmentation separates pores, cracks, inclusions and polishing pullout rather than treating all dark features as pores. **Source:** B015, B016.
- Optical resolution cannot establish the specified nanoscale Y211 distribution. Conditional electron microscopy requires adequate resolution and phase identification; otherwise redistribution remains unverified. **Source:** B017.
- Regional orientation measurements support texture assessment, but facet appearance or dominant (00l) reflections alone do not prove a single domain or in-plane alignment. **Source:** B010, B018.
- COMSOL normalization depends on documented electromagnetic inputs, convergence and demonstrated near-linear scaling within the specified geometry windows. Inadequate inputs or linearity require raw-force and geometry-matched reporting instead. **Source:** B019, B014.

### Experimental Task 5: Apply safety controls, disposition failures and archive controlled comparisons

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Disposition, safety and controlled feedback
Use powder containment, eye protection and appropriate respiratory controls; guard presses and saws. Control solvent ignition, hot-furnace exposure and oxygen-enrichment hazards. Provide cryogenic PPE, ventilation/oxygen-deficiency monitoring, magnet exclusion zones and nonmagnetic tools. Stop for fixture motion, collisions, oxygen-system faults or specimen shedding.

Seed corrosion/parasitic grains: inspect buffer placement, seed survival and temperature calibration. Delamination: inspect layer loading and pressure release. Cracks/bulging: examine thermal gradients, support contact and oxygenation history. Low force: first check gap, ZFC history, temperature, calibration, geometry and oxygenation; then examine texture/porosity. Apparent nanoscale refinement: check resolution and phase assignment before interpretation.

Archive recipes, sample custody, calibration files, furnace/oxygen logs, dimensions, photographs, raw traces/images, masks, analysis scripts and simulation inputs. Report arm-level means, scatter, uncertainty and yield without hiding exclusions. Freeze settings during the core batch; investigate changes on sacrificial material, then repeat all three arms together if needed. Optional magnetometry-based Jc or mechanical fatigue tests may extend interpretation but cannot replace controls. No transport-current test is specified: current limits, tap spacing and voltage criterion are therefore not assigned.

#### Related Knowledge

- Powder, machinery, solvent, furnace, oxygen, cryogenic and magnet hazards require the stated controls, with stop conditions for fixture motion, collision, oxygen faults or specimen shedding. **Source:** B020.
- Failure review follows the recorded fabrication and measurement chain: assembly and thermal checks for growth defects, loading and release checks for delamination, and measurement conditions before interpreting low force. **Source:** B021.
- Frozen core-batch settings, retained exclusions and complete records support transparent arm-level reporting. Optional magnetometry or fatigue testing extends interpretation without replacing controls; no transport-current procedure is specified. **Source:** B022.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Consolidated execution plan

#### 1. Release the comparison and safety prerequisites
Approve a signed recipe before powder preparation. Resolve the denominator of CPP's 35 mol % Y211, the denominator/distribution of 1 wt % CeO2, MPP Y2O3:Ba2Cu3Ox ratio and oxygen state, C-MPP layer fraction, buffer thickness/state and Yb2O3 support thickness. Proposed total-formula-unit and undoped-mass conventions require confirmation; do not substitute weight-percent Y211 or a 70:30 recipe.

Propose three independently pressed bulks per arm: CPP-only, MPP-only and C-MPP with CPP above MPP. The equal green-volume layer split remains pending qualification. Match external green geometry, pressing history, seed orientation, buffer and support; measure rather than force equal green density. Randomize calibrated furnace positions and preserve blinded codes.

Qualify press/die capacity, furnace uniformity, seed survival, oxygen delivery, XRD geometry, force capacity and cryogenic clearance. Apply powder containment, machinery guarding, solvent/fire controls, hot-furnace and oxygen safeguards, cryogenic PPE and ventilation/oxygen-deficiency monitoring, magnet exclusion zones and nonmagnetic tools. Stop for fixture motion, collisions, oxygen faults or shedding.

#### 2. Fabricate and oxygenate under common conditions
Use approved masses and traceable powder records. Prepare CPP and MPP separately with equal mixing exposure. Qualify media and mixing on sacrificial powder; 300 rpm/4 h wet milling and a 100 µm sieve are candidates, not validated requirements. Start binder-free; binder use needs approved burnout qualification.

For C-MPP, load MPP then CPP; match loading pauses in controls. Proposed compaction is 200 MPa for 5 min, subject to capacity, load and ejection qualification. Record layer masses, dimensions, density and defects; reject/repress cracked or delaminated green bodies while retaining failures. Assemble accepted pellets on common Yb2O3 support and a qualified setter, with the centered 6 mm CPP buffer and SmBCO seed.

Qualify the candidate TSMG schedule on sacrificial assemblies: 5 °C/min to 1050 °C, hold 1 h; 1 °C/min to 1012 °C, hold 30 min; 1 °C/h to 980 °C; 100 °C/h to ambient. Confirm decomposition, seed survival and growth window before running all arms together without composition-specific adjustments. Archive actual temperatures. Inspect growth, shrinkage and cracks; quarantine loose/fractured material without removing failures from yield.

Oxygenate intact bulks at 450 °C for 220 h after stabilization. Proposed flow is 1 L/min and cooling is 1 °C/min; qualify delivery and exhaust. Confirm a stable transition or oxygen-content endpoint on sacrificial material. Reinspect and transfer intact bulks to force testing before sectioning.

#### 3. Measure force before destructive characterization
Calibrate force, background, gap and travel; verify guideway field of 0.67 T at 5 mm and fixture clearance. At 77 K, use independently reset ZFC histories, a verified negligible-field cooling location and proposed 10 min stabilization. Define gap from the lower bulk face, including cryostat-wall documentation.

The proposed loop approaches from 30 to 5 mm and returns at 1 mm/s; qualify the starting field and extend distance if needed. Record force, gap, time and temperature. Acquire separate freshly ZFC 180 s relaxation after settling, report settling delay and confirm R = 100[F(0)−F(180 s)]/F(0) for target comparison. Repeat twice per bulk with independent histories; retain both traces and their average.

#### 4. Characterize and conditionally normalize
Retain raw maximum force and mapped volume. After force testing, prepare matched a–c sections with guarded, coolant-compatible cutting and qualified polishing. Acquire calibrated mosaics and systematic regional fields; lock checked ImageJ segmentation, separate defects and report area-weighted porosity, uncertainty and all detectable macro-cracks. Supplied porosity values are references, not rejection gates.

Use calibrated XRD peaks and regional orientation measurements as texture support, without unsupported acceptance thresholds. Nanoscale redistribution requires qualified high-resolution, phase-sensitive microscopy; otherwise report it unverified.

COMSOL H-formulation requires measured fields, actual shapes, justified superconducting inputs and convergence. Validate near-linear scaling over 441–676 mm² area and 9–10 mm thickness against the proposed 5% deviation criterion before normalization to 5225 mm³. Otherwise report raw and geometry-matched comparisons. Keep 0.0127 N/mm³, relaxation ≤13% and normalized force ≥76 N as separate reference targets.

#### 5. Close out transparently
Archive custody, recipes, calibrations, thermal logs, dimensions, images, traces, masks, scripts and simulation inputs. Report arm-level means, scatter, uncertainty, yield and exclusions. Freeze core settings; investigate changes sacrificially before repeating all arms together. Optional magnetometry or fatigue tests do not replace controls; no transport-current test is specified.
