# Experimental Plan: Thickness-dependent pinning in VLS-PLD YBCO

## Experimental Draft

### Stage 1 — Define, qualify and fabricate the thickness series
The objective is to test whether increasing undoped YBCO thickness preserves texture and increases field-dependent critical current density and apparent creep barrier. The architecture remains VLS-assisted PLD on qualified IBAD-MgO-buffered metal, with Ba-Cu-O liquid maintained at the growth front, no intentional pinning additives and no separate functional Ba-Cu-O layer.

Compare nominal thicknesses of 0.94, 1.50, 2.20 and 3.70 µm. Three independent, randomized deposition blocks are proposed; coupons from the same deposition are technical subsamples. Use qualified substrate and target lots, matched substrate blanks and traceable coupon positions. Required resources include liquid-precursor-capable KrF PLD, calibrated temperature and oxygen-pressure controls, XRD, SEM-EDX, ICP-OES, physical thickness metrology, SQUID and four-probe transport equipment.

Qualify incoming materials, actual equipment capability, liquid transfer and wetting, precursor stability, pulse-count thickness calibration and the longest growth before freezing common processing conditions. Starting settings and oxygenation remain proposed rather than established recipes.

### Stage 2 — Characterize and release traceable coupons
Perform nondestructive XRD before coupon allocation, then assess morphology, composition and physical thickness. Apply the supplied texture and a-axis gates, retain diagnostic failures and distinguish ICP-derived YBCO-equivalent thickness from physical thickness. Residual droplets are characterized without unsupported size or density limits.

### Stage 3 — Measure, compare and control dispositions
Measure four-probe transition behavior before high-current transport, then obtain field-dependent transport and SQUID relaxation with documented geometry, backgrounds, field history and uncertainty. Compare Jc at 77 K, 2.5 T and apparent U₀* at 77 K, 2 T; address 70 K separately. Supplied performance values are reference targets, not outcomes. Anticipated outputs are traceable process records, qualification decisions, individual measurements and uncertainty-qualified thickness comparisons, including failures and unresolved conversions.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage 1 — Define the comparison matrix and qualify resources and proposed settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Objective and sample matrix
Test whether increasing undoped YBCO thickness preserves texture and raises field-dependent Jc and apparent creep barrier U₀*. Use IBAD-MgO-buffered metal throughout; retain liquid-mediated growth, not a YBCO/Ba-Cu-O multilayer.

| Identifier | Nominal thickness | Core comparison |
|---|---:|---|
| V094-B01…03 | 0.94 µm | Thin reference |
| V150-B01…03 | 1.50 µm | Intermediate |
| V220-B01…03 | 2.20 µm | Intermediate |
| V370-B01…03 | 3.70 µm | Thick endpoint |

B01–03 are proposed independent deposition blocks, each including all thicknesses in randomized order. Use one qualified substrate lot and target lots. Append -M for magnetic coupon, -T for transport bridge, -C for destructive chemistry and -W for witness. Coupons from one deposition are technical subsamples, not independent replicates. Record their original positions. Include an uncoated substrate from the same lot for magnetic and digestion blanks.

The supplied 77 K, 2.5 T values, 10⁸ and 1.14×10⁹ A/m², imply an 11.4-fold Jc ratio; they are targets, not guaranteed outcomes. The separate 70 K statement is tested independently.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and prerequisites
Use a certified nominal Y:Ba:Cu =1:2:3 YBCO target, Ba-Cu-O precursor without pinning additives, qualified IBAD-MgO metal coupons, oxygen, compatible mounting hardware, gold contacts and validated digestion reagents. Record the complete substrate/buffer stack and metal alloy; Hastelloy is acceptable only if its supplied stack is qualified. Do not polish or reconstruct purchased buffers.

Required equipment: interlocked 248 nm KrF PLD with liquid-precursor delivery, calibrated heater/pyrometer, oxygen pressure control, XRD with Eulerian cradle, SEM-EDX, ICP-OES, profilometer or cross-section microscopy, SQUID, variable-temperature four-probe cryostat, current source and nanovoltmeter. AFM/reflectance monitoring is useful but does not substitute for thickness or roughness calibration.

Before release, verify actual PLD capability at 860–940 °C and 53–200 Pa, SQUID operation at 30–77 K and at least 3 T, and transport operation through Tc and at 60–77 K, 3 T. A 760–800 °C heater or 20–300 mTorr controller is insufficient. Verify background subtraction, magnetic sensitivity, 50 nV transport resolution, field alignment and sample-holder dimensions.

##### 3. Consolidated operating conditions
Except for task ranges and structural targets, settings below are proposed choices requiring qualification; freeze them before producing the comparison series.

| Parameter | Operating choice / requirement |
|---|---|
| Growth temperature | Start at 900 °C; proposed stability ±5 °C; remain within 860–940 °C |
| Growth O₂ | Start at 100 Pa; proposed stability ±5%; permitted 53–200 Pa |
| Laser | 248 nm; start 2.0 ±0.1 J/cm², 8 Hz |
| Spot / spacing | Start 2×4 mm² and 50 mm target–substrate distance; measure actual values |
| Liquid precursor | Start BaO:CuO =3:7 molar; delivery/loading unresolved until qualification |
| Thickness | Four matrix values; proposed ±5% target and within-coupon uniformity |
| Cooling / oxygenation | Start 5 °C/min to 450 °C; 1 atm O₂, 1 h; then 5 °C/min to ambient |
| Film gates | Δω ≤0.72°; Δϕ ≤1.49°; a-axis fraction <2.8%; Tc ≥90 K |
| Measurement stability | Proposed ±0.1 K, field accuracy ±0.5%, B parallel c within 1° |
| Transport geometry | Proposed width 50 µm; measured tap spacing 500 µm; dimension uncertainty ≤2% |
| Transport criterion | 1 µV/cm =10⁻⁴ V/m; at 500 µm, Vc =50 nV |

The proposed spot area is 0.08 cm², requiring approximately 0.16 J/pulse at 2 J/cm². Verify delivered pulse energy after optical losses. Deposit by calibrated pulse count, not assumed 30–120 minute durations.

- The supplied Jc values and their ratio are reference targets. The separate 70 K statement remains an independent measurement question rather than an established result. **Source:** B003.
- Equipment must support the actual growth and measurement envelopes. Starting growth, precursor, geometry and oxygenation settings require qualification before they are frozen for the comparison series; delivered pulse energy and pulse-count thickness calibration cannot be assumed. **Source:** B004, B005, B006, B007, B008, B009.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage 1 — Qualify liquid-mediated growth, fabricate films and complete oxygenation

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Incoming qualification → deposition operator.** Inspect buffer integrity and lot documentation; measure representative buffer texture without imposing YBCO film limits on MgO. Clean by the supplier-compatible solvent procedure and dry; avoid abrasion, aggressive water exposure and residue. Verify target composition by certificates plus XRD/ICP where needed. Contamination, buffer damage or unexplained secondary phases trigger quarantine.
2. **Pilot calibration.** With shutter closed, condition rotating targets and verify fluence, plume geometry, temperature and pressure. Establish precursor transfer that actually wets the growth front. A nearby molten crucible alone does not demonstrate VLS. Determine melt stability, wetting, replenishment and consumption using interrupted-growth witnesses and surface/composition observations. Qualify the proposed 3:7 precursor; its phase state at the chosen temperature/pressure is not assumed.
3. **Thickness calibration.** Relate pulse count to profilometric/cross-sectional thickness and ICP mass loading at each endpoint. Reflectance fringes may support this only after calibration. Qualify the longest growth for liquid depletion, cracking and texture loss. Select a common liquid-delivery schedule per unit deposited thickness; document total precursor exposure. Do not tune individual comparison samples mid-run.
4. **Core growth.** Mount coupons with a validated, noncontaminating high-temperature fixture. Stabilize qualified growth conditions, establish liquid wetting, then ablate YBCO while maintaining the liquid growth front. Log pulse count, temperature, pressure, fluence, precursor delivery and reflectance. Stop at calibrated thickness. Mark excursions immediately; do not quietly compensate them.
5. **Oxygenation.** Close shutters and cool under oxygen. Increase pressure only within chamber ratings; use a separate oxygen furnace if atmospheric treatment is unsupported. Apply the qualified common anneal and cooling schedule. Do not quench by default. ICP does not establish oxygen content y.
6. **Growth → characterization.** Photograph, map coupon positions and inspect for cracks/delamination. Complete nondestructive XRD first; then allocate magnetic, transport and chemistry coupons. Never send digested, coated or ion-milled material to SQUID as an untreated film.

#### Related Knowledge

- Incoming buffer integrity, supplier-compatible cleaning and target qualification precede deposition. Contamination, damage or unexplained secondary phases require quarantine rather than continuation. **Source:** B010.
- VLS qualification requires precursor transfer that wets the growth front, with observations of melt stability, replenishment and consumption. A nearby molten crucible does not establish liquid-mediated growth, and the proposed precursor phase state remains unresolved until qualified. **Source:** B010.
- Pulse-count calibration, longest-growth qualification and a common liquid-delivery schedule support controlled thickness comparisons. Common qualified oxygenation and documented hand-offs preserve comparability and prevent unsuitable specimens from entering magnetic measurements. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage 2 — Characterize texture, droplets, composition and physical thickness

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural, chemical and thickness characterization
**XRD:** Use calibrated Cu Kα optics, λ =1.5406 Å. Start with 2θ–ω over 10–80°. Locate YBCO (005) experimentally: c≈11.68 Å gives 2θ≈38.5° by Bragg’s law. Acquire its rocking curve, proposed ±2°, 0.005° steps, increasing range if tails are truncated. Measure in-plane FWHM using an asymmetric reflection such as (103), with calibrated tilt and full 0–360° φ coverage. Its 2θ is approximately 32.8°, dependent on lattice parameters. Record fitting method and instrumental broadening.

Quantify a-axis material using validated orientation-specific integrated reflections, structure/geometry corrections and detection limits; a (103) φ-scan alone is not an a-axis volume fraction. Survey center and edges, proposed five positions. The <0.03% statement requires separately demonstrated sensitivity; the core gate is <2.8%.

**SEM-EDX:** Image uncoated grounded coupons first; reserve any coated specimen for microscopy only. Start at 5 kV for morphology and 15 kV for EDX, adjusting to limit substrate contribution. Map proposed five 10×10 µm² fields plus visible defects. Compare droplets against adjacent matrix and standards, reporting composition, size, coverage and spatial distribution. Stoichiometric YBCO has Cu/Y=3; Cu/Y>2 is not a valid Cu-rich threshold. Do not impose unsupported droplet limits or infer nanoscale composition below EDX resolution.

**ICP-OES:** Digest a measured-area sacrificial coupon using a validated recovery procedure, blanks, standards and spikes. Record Y, Ba and Cu areal loadings. Estimate YBCO-equivalent thickness from Y loading only after validating Y partitioning and density; explicitly distinguish it from physical thickness and residual Ba-Cu-O mass. Confirm physical thickness independently. Investigate discordance rather than assuming a density or treating ICP as an oxygen assay.

**Characterization → measurement:** Release intact, traceable coupons meeting the structural gates. Preserve failures as labeled diagnostic specimens; exclude them from the qualified primary comparison without deleting their data.

#### Related Knowledge

- Rocking curves and asymmetric-reflection azimuthal scans assess different texture components. A-axis quantification requires validated orientation-specific integrated reflections and corrections; an azimuthal scan alone does not establish its volume fraction. **Source:** B011, B012.
- SEM-EDX compares droplets with adjacent matrix and standards while limiting substrate contribution. Cu/Y greater than two is not a valid Cu-rich threshold, and composition below EDX resolution must not be inferred. **Source:** B013.
- ICP-OES establishes elemental areal loadings through validated recovery and controls. YBCO-equivalent thickness requires qualified partitioning and density assumptions and remains distinct from independently measured physical thickness and residual precursor mass. **Source:** B014.
- Structural release applies to intact, traceable coupons. Failed specimens remain labeled diagnostic material with retained data rather than being silently removed. **Source:** B015.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage 3 — Measure transition temperature, transport current and magnetic relaxation

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Superconducting measurements
**Four-probe Tc:** Before high-current testing, measure R(T) from approximately 100 to 80 K at a proposed 10 µA, repeating at lower current to exclude heating. Record onset, midpoint, zero resistance and transition width. Use midpoint ≥90 K as the proposed operational gate; retain all definitions because the supplied Tc convention is unspecified.

**Transport:** Pattern -T coupons into 50 µm bridges with voltage taps 500 µm apart, measured independently of total bridge length. Use qualified low-damage milling and gold contacts; verify ohmic behavior, substrate isolation and unchanged Tc. Do not apply an unqualified Ar contact anneal. Check contact heating rather than accepting a fixed resistance limit.

At B parallel c, measure 77 K, 2.5 T first, then proposed 60 and 70 K at 2, 2.5 and 3 T. Stabilize temperature/field; use current reversal, offset subtraction and ascending/descending sweeps. Begin with 0.1 mA increments near criterion and short, heating-checked dwell times. Interpolate Ic at E=Vc/Ltap and compute Jc=Ic/(wt) using physical local thickness.

For 50 µm width, supplied targets require Ic≈4.70 mA at 0.94 µm and 211 mA at 3.7 µm. A 1 A source is sufficient for those targets, not necessarily other conditions. Use a proposed 0.5 A ceiling, lowered by thermal qualification; if no crossing occurs, report a lower bound. Abort for heating, unstable voltage or irreversible damage.

**SQUID:** Measure unpatterned rectangular -M coupons with recorded dimensions, film volume and substrate orientation. Subtract matched substrate/holder signals. At proposed 30, 60, 70 and 77 K, zero-field cool, ramp identically to 2, 2.5 and 3 T along c, then record relaxation after field settling. Define t=0 at ramp completion; record settling dead time. Start with 10–3600 s acquisition, extending until a stable logarithmic fitting interval is available. Reset above Tc between histories; acquire hysteresis loops for critical-state calibration.

For the irreversible moment mirr, fit S=−d ln|mirr|/d ln t and report U₀*=kBT/S in joules and U₀*/kB=T/S in kelvin. Report fit interval and uncertainty; this is an apparent, current-dependent barrier. Do not use a logarithm of a dimensional decay rate.

Convert moment to J using a documented finite-rectangle critical-state model. For full-width a≤b, the rectangular Bean estimate is J=2ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)] in SI, with ΔM the full loop width in A/m; verify applicability to thin perpendicular-field films. Relaxation E–J conversion additionally requires a validated thin-film Faraday/geometry inversion, not identification of dm/dt with electric field. Freeze that model and time normalization before analysis; otherwise retain raw relaxation and mark E–J conversion pending. Magnetic and transport Jc use different electric-field scales and need not coincide.

#### Related Knowledge

- Low-current transition measurements precede high-current testing. Qualified bridge preparation must preserve Tc, establish ohmic contacts and substrate isolation, and use independently measured tap spacing and local physical thickness. **Source:** B016, B017, B018.
- Transport Jc is normalized by measured width and thickness at the stated electric-field criterion. Heating qualification governs the proposed current ceiling; absence of a criterion crossing produces a lower bound rather than an invented Ic. **Source:** B018, B019.
- SQUID relaxation requires matched background subtraction, controlled field histories and an explicit time origin. The logarithmic slope of irreversible moment gives an apparent, current-dependent barrier with a reported fitting interval and uncertainty. **Source:** B020, B021.
- Magnetic-current conversion requires a documented applicable critical-state model. Relaxation E–J conversion additionally requires validated thin-film geometry inversion; without it, raw relaxation is retained and conversion remains pending. **Source:** B022.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage 3 — Consolidate comparisons, record failures and control safe feedback

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Dispositions, safety and controlled feedback
Record native instrument files, calibration IDs, geometry, uncertainties, blanks, process histories, gate decisions and analysis scripts under each sample ID. Report individual replicates and block-level variation; calculate thickness ratios with propagated uncertainty. Compare U₀* at 77 K, 2 T and Jc at 77 K, 2.5 T without presuming a positive trend.

For poor texture, recheck alignment/curvature, then temperature, buffer and liquid depletion. For cracks, inspect mounting and cooling. For low Tc, investigate oxygenation before pinning. For anomalous relaxation, check substrate subtraction, settling and field history. For low transport Jc, inspect bridge damage, contacts and local thickness. Reprocessing creates a separately labeled branch, not a silent replacement.

Use laser/UV interlocks, hot-zone shielding, oxygen-compatible pressure-rated plumbing, cryogenic ventilation and magnet exclusion controls. Handle Ba/Cu compounds and acid digestion in appropriate containment; segregate metal-containing waste. Stop on unsafe pressure, gas leakage, interlock failure or sample overheating.

Make recipe changes only after diagnosing a completed block; repeat the full thickness block under the newly qualified conditions. Optional conventional-PLD endpoint controls or field-angle tests must be separately labeled and cannot replace the core thickness series. Thickness, texture and creep correlations do not by themselves measure correlation length L or prove L≥d.

#### Related Knowledge

- Individual replicate records, block-level variation and propagated uncertainty support thickness comparisons without presuming improvement. Native files, calibration identifiers, geometry and gate decisions remain linked to sample identifiers. **Source:** B023.
- Failure diagnosis distinguishes texture, cracking, oxygenation, magnetic-history and transport-preparation issues. Reprocessing is a separately labeled branch, and recipe changes require a newly qualified full thickness block. **Source:** B024, B026.
- Laser, hot-zone, oxygen-pressure, cryogenic, magnet and chemical controls are prerequisites. Unsafe pressure, leakage, interlock failure or overheating trigger a stop. **Source:** B025.
- Conventional-PLD endpoint controls and field-angle tests are optional, separately labeled additions rather than replacements for the core series. Thickness, texture and creep correlations do not establish correlation length or prove its relation to thickness. **Source:** B026.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage 1 — Define, qualify and fabricate
Execute Tasks 1–2 as the preparation and fabrication stage. Compare undoped VLS-assisted PLD YBCO at nominal thicknesses of 0.94, 1.50, 2.20 and 3.70 µm on qualified IBAD-MgO-buffered metal. Retain liquid at the growth front; do not substitute a multilayer architecture or add pinning additives. Use the proposed three independent deposition blocks, each containing all thicknesses in randomized order. Preserve coupon positions, distinguish technical subsamples from independent depositions and include same-lot uncoated blanks.

Before release, verify substrate/buffer and target documentation, buffer integrity, supplier-compatible cleaning, equipment capability and measurement sensitivity. PLD must support 860–940 °C and 53–200 Pa oxygen; measurement systems must cover the specified temperature, field and voltage requirements. Do not polish or reconstruct purchased buffers. Quarantine contamination, damage or unexplained phases.

Qualify the proposed starting recipe: 900 °C, 100 Pa oxygen, 248 nm irradiation at 2.0 ±0.1 J/cm² and 8 Hz, with measured spot and spacing. The proposed 3:7 BaO:CuO precursor composition, transfer/loading, wetting, phase state and replenishment remain qualification prerequisites. Use interrupted-growth witnesses and surface/composition observations already specified in the source. Calibrate pulse count against physical thickness and ICP loading at each endpoint; qualify the longest growth for depletion, cracking and texture loss. Freeze common conditions and precursor delivery per deposited thickness before comparison production; do not retune individual samples mid-run.

Log growth histories and excursions. Apply the common qualified oxygenation schedule, starting from the proposed 5 °C/min cooling to 450 °C, 1 atm oxygen for 1 h, then 5 °C/min to ambient. Respect chamber ratings and use a separate oxygen furnace if needed. Photograph and inspect films, then complete nondestructive XRD before coupon allocation.

### Stage 2 — Characterize and release
Execute Task 3 using traceable specimens. Measure phase/texture by calibrated XRD, including rocking curves, in-plane scans and validated a-axis quantification. Apply film gates of Δω ≤0.72°, Δϕ ≤1.49° and a-axis fraction <2.8%. The additional <0.03% statement requires separately demonstrated sensitivity.

Image uncoated grounded coupons and characterize residual droplets by SEM-EDX relative to adjacent matrix and standards, without unsupported droplet limits or sub-resolution composition claims. Reserve coated specimens for microscopy. Use validated ICP digestion, blanks, standards and spikes for elemental areal loadings. Confirm physical thickness independently; treat Y-loading-derived YBCO-equivalent thickness as conditional on qualified partitioning and density. Investigate disagreement, and do not use ICP as an oxygen assay. Release intact coupons meeting structural gates; preserve failed specimens and their data outside the qualified primary comparison.

### Stage 3 — Measure, compare and control dispositions
Execute Tasks 4–5. Measure low-current R(T) before high-current testing, report onset, midpoint, zero resistance and transition width, and apply the proposed midpoint Tc ≥90 K gate. Qualify low-damage bridge preparation and gold contacts, verify unchanged Tc and avoid unqualified contact annealing.

Measure transport first at 77 K, 2.5 T with field parallel to c, then at the proposed additional temperatures and fields. Use measured bridge width, tap spacing and local physical thickness, stabilized conditions, current reversal and offset subtraction. Determine Ic at 1 µV/cm and calculate Jc = Ic/(wt). Apply the proposed 0.5 A ceiling only after thermal qualification, lowering it as required; report lower bounds without criterion crossings and abort for heating, instability or irreversible damage.

For SQUID, use unpatterned coupons, measured film volume, matched substrate/holder subtraction and identical zero-field-cooled histories. Record ramp completion, settling dead time and logarithmic fitting interval; reset above Tc between histories. Report S and apparent U₀* = kBT/S with uncertainty. Freeze applicable magnetic-current and time-normalization models; retain raw relaxation and mark E–J conversion pending without validated thin-film inversion. Magnetic and transport Jc need not coincide.

Compare Jc at 77 K, 2.5 T and U₀* at 77 K, 2 T with propagated uncertainty and block-level variation; test 70 K separately. Supplied Jc values remain targets. Retain failures, label reprocessing branches and change recipes only after diagnosis followed by a newly qualified full block. Optional controls remain separate. Maintain all specified laser, thermal, oxygen, cryogenic, magnet and chemical safeguards, stopping for unsafe conditions.
