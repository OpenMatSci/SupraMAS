# Experimental Plan: Protocol: dual-defect epitaxial YBCO films

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Qualify resources and fabricate matched films

**Objective:** Test whether substrate-seeded planar defects and in-film BYNTO columns cooperatively improve high-field transport while preserving epitaxy in approximately 250 nm YBCO films on (001) STO. The supplied structural and performance values are reference targets, not measured outcomes.

Use the proposed three independent fabrication blocks, each containing pristine YBCO (P), BYNTO-only (N), PAD ZrO2-decoration-only (Z), and combined (NZ) films. Randomize deposition order and match substrate lot, thickness, thermal treatment, oxygenation, and measurement history. Multiple bridges remain technical replicates.

Required resources include qualified pure and composite PLD targets, aqueous zirconium/polymer PAD materials, spin coating and controlled-atmosphere furnaces, calibrated KrF PLD, AFM, XRD, HAADF-STEM/EDX, bridge fabrication facilities, and four-probe transport equipment covering 4.2–77 K and 0–18 T. Release PAD, target-manufacture, and oxygenation recipes before core fabrication. Resolve the composite at.% denominator and the inconsistent nominal island morphology. Retain all proposed/P settings as qualification-dependent. Decorate substrates before matched PLD growth and oxygenation; do not deposit separate BYNTO layers or a continuous BaZrO3 buffer.

### Stage II — Characterize structure and apply review gates

Perform nondestructive AFM/XRD before bridge processing, followed by representative destructive microscopy. Assess film continuity, texture, column architecture, and local interface chemistry without presuming that the intended coating or defects formed. Record uncertainties, failed witnesses, and review dispositions.

### Stage III — Measure transport and consolidate evidence

Use qualified bridges for the core proposed B∥c comparison. Normalize critical current by measured superconducting cross-section, compare all four arms, and assess pinning-force scaling only where the measurement window supports it. Anticipated outputs are traceable fabrication records, structural measurements, transport curves, uncertainty-aware comparisons, and explicitly unresolved qualifications—not certified enhancement or mechanism.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Qualify materials, comparison blocks, and operating settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and sample matrix
Test whether substrate-seeded planar defects and in-film BYNTO columns cooperatively improve high-field transport without degrading epitaxy. Preserve the approximately 250 nm YBCO nanocomposite on (001) STO; do not deposit separate BYNTO layers or a blanket BaZrO3 buffer.

| Arm | STO treatment | PLD target |
|---|---|---|
| P | Undecorated, sham thermal treatment | Pure YBCO |
| N | Undecorated, sham thermal treatment | YBCO + nominal 2.5 at.% Ba2YNbO6 + 2.5 at.% Ba2YTaO6 |
| Z | PAD ZrO2 decoration | Pure YBCO |
| NZ | PAD ZrO2 decoration | Same composite as N |

**Proposed design:** three independent fabrication blocks, each containing all four arms. IDs: `B01-P`, `B01-N`, `B01-Z`, `B01-NZ`, continuing through B03. Append `-W` for witnesses, `-T1` for transport bridges and `-L1` for lamellae. Multiple bridges are technical replicates, not independent films. Randomize deposition order within blocks and record target changes. Match substrate lot, thickness, temperature, oxygenation and measurement history.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Use (001) STO, proposed 5 × 5 mm²; qualified pure-YBCO and composite targets; aqueous zirconium precursor and compatible polymer; DI water, acetone, isopropanol; Au pads/wire and lithography consumables. Zirconium acetate is a proposed precursor; polymer identity, molecular weight, precursor/polymer ratio and solution chemistry remain calibration prerequisites. No aerosol nozzle or carrier-gas process substitutes for polymer-assisted deposition.

Required equipment: aqueous PAD preparation and spin-coating facilities; controlled-atmosphere furnace; KrF 248 nm PLD with calibrated substrate thermometry, fluence and oxygen pressure; AFM; Cu-Kα XRD; cross-sectional HAADF-STEM/EDX and lamella preparation; bridge patterning, profilometry and wire bonding; four-probe current source, low-noise voltage acquisition and an 18 T cryostat covering 4.2–77 K.

Before core fabrication, release signed recipes for PAD, target manufacture and oxygenation. Resolve the at.% denominator: do not convert it silently to 5 mol.% or use only Nb. Prefer qualified targets; if manufactured locally, establish mixing/sintering conditions through phase, composition and ablation tests. Avoid ZrO2 milling media that could contaminate nominally Zr-free controls. Verify magnet, cryostat, current-lead and source capabilities at the required operating combinations; a generic cryostat designation is insufficient.

##### 3. Consolidated settings
Task values are targets, not guaranteed outcomes. All tolerances and entries marked **P** are proposed operating choices requiring calibration.

| Operation | Setting | Control/release |
|---|---|---|
| Substrate cleaning, P | Acetone 10 min → IPA 10 min → DI rinse → N2 dry | Inspect for residue; no routine ion milling |
| PAD | Aqueous polymer route, before PLD | Recipe released from witness trials |
| Islands, task | 45 nm diameter; 25 nm height; 375 µm^-2; 20% coverage | Reconcile joint morphology before core release |
| PLD, task | 840 °C; 1.8 nm s^-1; approximately 250 nm | P tolerances: ±10 °C, ±10% rate, ±10% thickness |
| PLD, P | O2 200 ±20 mTorr; fluence 2.0 ±0.2 J cm^-2; distance 50 ±1 mm | Recalibrate rate and composition at these settings |
| Laser frequency | Calibrated to 1.8 nm s^-1 | Not fixed at 5–10 Hz |
| Oxygenation, P | 450 ±10 °C, 1 h, nominal 1 atm O2; cooling 10 °C min^-1 | Validate pressure rating and superconducting transition |
| XRD | YBCO (005) rocking curve | Task target 0.15°; P review threshold >0.20° |
| Transport, task | 4.2–77 K; 0–18 T; 1 µV cm^-1 | P stability ±0.1 K, field calibration ≤0.1%, alignment ±0.5° |

At 1.8 nm s^-1, 250 nm requires approximately **139 s** of open-shutter growth, excluding transients. A draft rate of 0.5 Å/pulse would require **36 Hz**, not 5–10 Hz; establish actual yield per pulse and confirm laser capability. If the required rate cannot be achieved, stop rather than silently change the task condition.

#### Related Knowledge

- The four-arm matrix separates substrate decoration and composite-target contributions; proposed matched independent blocks distinguish film replication from multiple technical bridge measurements. **Source:** B000, B001, B002.
- PAD chemistry, target composition, and oxygenation require signed release recipes. The atomic-percent denominator remains unresolved, and nominally Zr-free controls must not acquire contamination from target preparation. **Source:** B003, B005.
- Task targets and proposed operating settings have different status. Growth frequency must follow calibrated yield per pulse and the required deposition rate rather than an inherited frequency range. **Source:** B006, B007, B008.

### Experimental Task 2: Prepare PAD-decorated substrates and grow matched YBCO films

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Targets → PLD operator:** obtain composition certificates, phase scans and target IDs. Map ablation uniformity on witnesses. Proposed conditioning is 5 min shuttered pre-ablation with target rotation; qualify duration and clean shields between target types.
2. **Clean STO → PAD operator:** clean and inspect all substrates. Record orientation and baseline AFM. Establish any surface-termination anneal on witnesses; apply identically across arms. Do not add ion damage as a new pinning factor.
3. **PAD calibration:** prepare a documented aqueous zirconium/polymer solution, recording masses, pH, ageing, filtration and viscosity. Spin-coat witnesses; thermally remove polymer and crystallize islands using a furnace profile selected from residue and morphology checks. Vary solution loading, spin program and thermal profile one factor at a time. Run undecorated sham witnesses through the same thermal cycle.
4. **Morphology gate:** AFM at a proposed minimum of five center/edge fields per decorated substrate, including 5 × 5 µm² surveys and smaller high-resolution scans. Measure height, tip-corrected lateral dimensions, object density and segmented coverage separately. For nonoverlapping 45 nm circular footprints, 375 µm^-2 implies **59.6%**, not 20%, coverage. At 375 µm^-2, 20% corresponds to approximately **26 nm equivalent footprint diameter**. Resolve whether the supplied diameter denotes a different metric, or islands overlap/cluster. Do not claim all nominal metrics were met without evidence. Retain failed witnesses; do not strip/recoat core samples without a new ID.
5. **Released substrates → PLD:** load cleanly, avoiding contact with decorated faces. Heat under the qualified oxygen program. Deposit pure YBCO for P/Z and composite YBCO-BYNTO for N/NZ using matched calibrated thickness and rate. Record actual substrate-temperature calibration, pressure, pulse count, fluence, frequency and shutter time.
6. **Local interface formation:** allow the ZrO2-derived BaZrO3 coating to develop during growth; do not add a continuous layer. Its formation and thickness are hypotheses to verify, not process facts. Oxygenate all arms using the same released cycle. The transition from growth pressure to oxygenation pressure must follow chamber ratings and the validated cooling sequence.
7. **Films → characterization:** package separately in clean dry containers. Perform nondestructive AFM/XRD before bridge processing. Reserve witnesses for destructive microscopy; prepare transport bridges only after initial review.

#### Related Knowledge

- Witness-based PAD qualification and matched sham thermal treatment preserve the substrate-treatment comparison without adding ion damage as another pinning factor. **Source:** B009.
- Diameter, density, and coverage must be measured separately because the supplied nominal island metrics do not jointly describe nonoverlapping circular footprints. Core release requires reconciliation rather than an unsupported claim of agreement. **Source:** B009.
- Pure and composite films require matched calibrated growth and oxygenation. A local BaZrO3 coating is a hypothesis for characterization, not an established process outcome or authorization to deposit a continuous layer. **Source:** B009.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize film texture, interfaces, and defect architecture

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural characterization and dispositions
**AFM:** preserve raw height data, segmentation thresholds and uncertainty. Decorated surfaces with 25 nm islands are not subject to a <1 nm global RMS requirement. Inspect final films for cracks, droplets and discontinuities.

**XRD:** acquire θ–2θ phase scans, then the isolated YBCO (005) ω scan and off-axis YBCO (103)/STO (111) φ scans. Select reflection positions from indexed scans and instrument geometry; verify accessibility and overlap rather than assume a fixed 20–60° window suffices. Derive c from measured d005 using c = 5d005; supplied a alone cannot locate (005). Report widths in degrees with step size, optics, fit and instrumental broadening. A rocking curve alone does not establish biaxial texture.

**HAADF-STEM/EDX:** survey interfaces and full film thickness in all arms, including two lateral locations per selected film. Identify BYNTO using Ba/Y/Nb/Ta maps, and distinguish Zr-rich islands from a Ba-containing coating through imaging plus chemistry/diffraction. Measure column diameter, continuity, tilt/meander, local coating thickness, stacking faults and Y248 intergrowths. Define the counting plane and area denominator before comparing fault density with 2×10^4 µm^-2; cross-sectional counts are not automatically plan-view densities.

Proposed lamella preparation: coarse FIB at 30 kV, final polish ≤5 kV. Begin STEM near 20 pA and verify dose tolerance on sacrificial regions. Record voltage, detector angles, pixel size, dwell, area and accumulated dose; reduce exposure upon oxygen-loss or structural changes.

**Gate:** target columns 5–16 nm, approximately 0.15° out-of-plane FWHM, and the specified local defect architecture. Hold discontinuous films or uncertain compositions for diagnosis. Flag >0.20° FWHM for review, not automatic selective exclusion. Archive all failures and reasons; fabricate replacement matched blocks where necessary.

#### Related Knowledge

- AFM records morphology and continuity, while indexed phase scans, rocking curves, and off-axis scans provide distinct structural information. A rocking curve alone does not establish biaxial texture. **Source:** B010, B011.
- HAADF-STEM with elemental mapping distinguishes BYNTO columns, Zr-rich islands, and a possible Ba-containing coating. Defect-density comparisons require a declared counting plane and area denominator. **Source:** B012.
- Proposed lamella and imaging settings remain dose-qualified. Structural review retains uncertain compositions, discontinuities, and failures rather than selectively excluding films by rocking-curve width. **Source:** B013, B014.

### Experimental Task 4: Qualify transport bridges and acquire four-probe measurements

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Transport preparation and measurement
Pattern proposed **40 µm-wide bridges**, approximately 2 mm uniform active length, with **1.00 mm measured voltage-tap separation**. Use qualified lithography/low-damage etching rather than unqualified laser cutting. Measure width and local film thickness; exclude contact metal from superconducting cross-section. Deposit proposed 100 nm Au contact pads and bond 25 µm Au wire using witness-qualified settings. Inspect isolation, adhesion and shorts. Record contact resistance and contact-heating tests; <1 Ω alone is not a safe high-current criterion.

For 40 µm × 250 nm, area is **1×10^-7 cm²**: 3 MA cm^-2 requires 0.30 A; a 5 A source reaches 50 MA cm^-2 geometrically, subject to lead/heating limits. At 1 mm tap spacing, the criterion is **0.10 µV = 100 nV**. Demonstrate proposed ≤10 nV offset/noise uncertainty. A proposed irreversibility threshold of 100 A cm^-2 corresponds to **10 µA**; use a calibrated low-current range with ≤1 µA steps rather than the draft's 1 mA resolution.

**Bridge → cryostat:** secure thermal anchoring, strain relief, shielded twisted pairs and separated current/voltage leads. First measure low-current R(T) through the transition, reporting onset, midpoint, zero resistance and width without imposing an unsupported 90 K acceptance value.

Core orientation is proposed B∥c, perpendicular to in-plane current. Measure at proposed temperatures 4.2, 20, 40, 50, 65 and 77 K; fields 0–18 T in 0.5 T steps. Stabilize each point before acquisition. Ramp current adaptively through the criterion, retaining points below and above it; reverse current to remove thermoelectric offsets. Refine increments near Ic, repeat selected points and log dwell/ramp history. Stop on heating, voltage runaway or contact failure; reduce current and recheck a reference point. Never extrapolate Ic beyond source or thermal limits.

#### Related Knowledge

- Transport normalization depends on measured bridge width and film thickness, excluding contact metal. Contact resistance alone does not establish safe high-current operation. **Source:** B015, B016.
- The voltage criterion and proposed irreversibility threshold require appropriately qualified voltage-noise and low-current capabilities; nominal source capacity does not override heating or lead limits. **Source:** B016.
- Low-current transition measurements precede the core field-temperature survey. Stabilization, current reversal, adaptive ramps, and stop rules address offsets and unstable transport without extrapolating beyond operating limits. **Source:** B017, B018.

### Experimental Task 5: Analyze four-arm comparisons and archive controlled dispositions

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Analysis, records and controlled feedback
Compute Jc = Ic/(wt), with uncertainty from Ic, width and thickness. Calculate Fp = BJc in N m^-3 using Jc in A m^-2 and applied B in tesla; distinguish tesla-labelled field from H in A m^-1. Treat B≈µ0H as a high-field approximation; do not interpret self-field Fp through this approximation.

Compare NZ/N at 4.2 K, 18 T against the **supplied approximately 2.4-fold target**, and all four arms above 5 T below 50 K. Report block-level interaction NZ−N−Z+P on a declared response scale with uncertainty, rather than infer cooperation solely from NZ/N.

Test Fp temperature collapse using measured maxima and h = B/Birr only where Birr is established. The proposed 100 A cm^-2 threshold must be declared and sensitivity-tested. If no crossing occurs by 18 T, report Birr>18 T; if the maximum lies beyond the window, mark scaling unresolved. Non-collapse is consistent with multiple pinning channels but is not unique proof of their mechanism.

Archive raw I–V curves, R(T), dimensions, calibrations, process logs, images, masks, fits, exclusions and analysis scripts under parent IDs. Diagnose low Tc via oxygenation/composition; poor texture via thermometry, contamination and island morphology; misaligned columns via rate/temperature calibration; unstable Ic via contacts, field alignment and heating. Correct on witnesses, freeze a new recipe and rerun matched blocks; never selectively repair one arm and pool it with unchanged controls.

**Relevant risks:** interlocked Class-4 UV laser operation; powder/precursor containment and solvent ventilation; rated hot oxygen/vacuum systems; cryogenic oxygen-deficiency monitoring and pressure relief; magnet exclusion zones, ferromagnetic screening and quench procedures; high-current heating limits.

**Optional extension:** matched B∥ab or angular transport above 5 T below 50 K to test directional pinning. It supplements, not replaces, the four-arm B∥c comparison; fields above 18 T are outside the core protocol.

#### Related Knowledge

- Jc requires measured superconducting cross-section and propagated uncertainty; pinning-force calculations require consistent SI units and must not confuse applied B with H. **Source:** B019.
- The supplied NZ/N ratio is a comparison target, while cooperation is assessed through a declared block-level interaction. Scaling remains unresolved when the field window does not establish the required maximum or irreversibility field. **Source:** B020, B021.
- Traceable failures and witness-based recipe revisions protect the matched comparison. Corrections require new matched blocks rather than selective repair and pooling of one arm. **Source:** B022.
- Laser, chemical, oxygen-system, cryogenic, magnet, and high-current safeguards are prerequisites. Angular or B∥ab measurements are optional supplements, not replacements for the core comparison. **Source:** B023, B024.
