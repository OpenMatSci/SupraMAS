# Experimental Plan: EuBCO–BHO thickness-dependent pinning protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Objective and comparison design
Reorganize the frozen EuBCO–BHO protocol into three physical stages and five experimental tasks: qualification and fabrication; structural and superconducting characterization; and disposition with controlled feedback. Test thickness-dependent pinning in nominal 6 mol% BHO–EuBCO produced by reel-to-reel multi-plume multi-turn PLD, preserving a high-quality bottom template while allowing limited natural misorientation and BHO splay.

The core arms are D11, doped EuBCO at 1.1 µm; D33, doped EuBCO at 3.3 µm; and U33, undoped EuBCO at 3.3 µm. Three independent matched deposition blocks are proposed, using one qualified buffered-tape lot and balanced arm order. Neighboring coupons provide paired measurements, not independent replication. Comparisons address the combined practical effects of thickness, cycle count and thermal exposure rather than isolated thickness causality.

### Stage 1 — Qualification and fabrication
Release qualified targets, the complete Hastelloy/MgO/LaMnO3 buffer stack, deposition hardware and measurement capabilities. Confirm the dopant denominator, laser allocation and fluence, moving-tape surface temperature, deposition per cycle and permissible thermal exposure. Use the composite target for all doped increments, prevent carryover into U33, and complete qualified cooldown and oxygenation before film hand-off. All P settings remain proposed until calibrated and approved.

### Stage 2 — Characterization
Use XRD, AFM, thickness measurements and FIB-TEM/STEM-EDS to document texture, phases and depth-dependent microstructure. Use qualified bridges, PPMS resistivity and magnetometry, and high-field transport to obtain transition definitions, normalized current densities and angular behavior. Confirm apparatus capability, geometry, backgrounds, heating limits and uncertainties before measurement.

### Stage 3 — Disposition and controlled feedback
Retain failed specimens and raw records, flag deviations without selective exclusion, and initiate a new matched block for corrective process changes. Anticipated outputs are traceable fabrication records and matched structural, microstructural and superconducting datasets—not guaranteed performance. Supplied values remain reference targets. Safety prerequisites apply throughout; optional U11 or additional splay validation does not replace the core comparison.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage 1: Define matched comparisons and qualify materials, equipment and operating settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and sample matrix
This proposal tests thickness-dependent pinning in a **6 mol% BHO–EuBCO nanocomposite** made by reel-to-reel MPMT-PLD. Preserve a high-quality bottom template while allowing limited natural misorientation and BHO splay; do not intentionally seed Cu-O particles or eliminate every secondary feature.

| Arm | Composition | EuBCO thickness | Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| D11 | EuBCO + 6 mol% BHO | 1.1 µm | Thin thickness control |
| D33 | EuBCO + 6 mol% BHO | 3.3 µm | Thick core specimen |
| U33 | Undoped EuBCO | 3.3 µm | Composition control |

**Proposed replication:** three independent matched deposition blocks using one buffered-tape lot. Identify material as `block-arm-run-longitudinal-position-coupon`; suffixes `X`, `M`, `T`, and `E` denote XRD, magnetization, transport and electron microscopy. Sample beginning, middle and end of each usable meter-scale segment. Neighboring coupons are paired, not independent replicates. Balance arm order between blocks; use separate targets and prevent BHO carryover into U33.

Primary comparisons are D33 versus D11 and D33 versus U33. Thickness also changes cycle count and thermal exposure; this design tests their combined practical effect, not isolated thickness causality.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Use certified EuBCO and BHO powders, ceramic composite and undoped targets, and qualified Hastelloy/MgO/LaMnO3 tape from a common lot. Obtain the full substrate/barrier/cap-layer specification: MgO/LaMnO3 alone must not be assumed to provide adequate metal diffusion protection. Confirm what denominator defines 6 mol% before weighing.

Required equipment: enclosed powder handling; mill, presses and oxygen furnace if targets are made internally; qualified IBAD/PLD buffer line or purchased buffered tape; industrial reel-to-reel MPMT-PLD with calibrated pulse-energy meter, pressure gauges, heater/surface thermometry and tape-speed logging; profilometry/SEM, AFM, XRD; FIB and TEM/STEM-EDS; bridge patterning/contact tools; PPMS with magnetometry; 18 T transport magnet, rotator, cryogenic controller, high-current source and nanovoltmeter.

Before release, verify PPMS magnetometry range, temperature coverage, rotator availability and background sensitivity. Verify the high-field apparatus can hold **4.2 K at 14 T** and **30 K at 5 T**, with adequate current capacity and thermal anchoring. An 18 T label alone does not establish these capabilities. A 9 T PPMS cannot supply the 14 T transport condition.

##### 3. Consolidated operating parameters
Task values are envelopes/references, not operating tolerances. All entries marked **P** are proposed choices requiring calibration and an approved traveler before use.

| Operation | Setting and control |
|---|---|
| PLD envelope, task | 300 Hz; 700–1000 mJ/pulse; O2 800–1000 mTorr = 106.7–133.3 Pa; heater range 1000–1100 °C |
| Initial PLD selection, P | 850 mJ/pulse; 900 mTorr; pulse energy ±5%, pressure ±20 mTorr; stop on sustained excursions |
| Cycle schedule, P | One calibrated 1.1 µm increment per cycle; D11: first cycle only; D33/U33: three cycles; heater setpoints 1000/1050/1100 °C; ±5 °C indicated stability |
| Thickness gate, P | Nominal thickness ±5%; measure locally, never infer only from pulses |
| Oxygenation, P | Separate oxygen-compatible furnace: 450 °C, 1 h, approximately atmospheric O2; validate uniform oxygenation through 3.3 µm |
| Cooldown, P | 5 °C/min to 500 °C, then 2 °C/min; chamber pressure within hardware rating; oxygenation follows cooldown |
| Transport geometry, P | Width 50 µm; voltage-tap separation 1.00 mm; measure actual width, thickness and spacing to ≤2% uncertainty each |
| Measurement stability, P | Temperature ±0.05 K near transition/30 K and ±0.02 K at 4.2 K; angle verification ±0.1°; field uncertainty ≤0.1%; voltage noise ≤10 nV |

**Laser consistency:** at 300 Hz, 0.7–1.0 J implies 210–300 W for that pulse train. Verify whether frequency/energy apply to each plume or the total system. A 2 mm² spot would give 35–50 J/cm², inconsistent with the draft's 1.8 J/cm². At 1.8 J/cm², the required spot would be 0.389–0.556 cm². Do not combine these settings; measure target-plane spot and calibrate ablation fluence.

**Rate consistency:** 0.5 Å/pulse would require 66,000 pulses for 3.3 µm, or 220 s of stationary exposure at 300 Hz—not an established reel-to-reel growth rate. Determine deposition per pass, tape speed, plume overlap and cycle dwell experimentally.

- The three-arm matrix supports doped thickness and undoped composition comparisons. Matched deposition blocks, balanced arm order and paired neighboring coupons preserve the stated comparison design without treating coupons as independent replicates or isolating thickness from thermal history. **Source:** B000, B001, B002, B003.
- Material release depends on confirming the 6 mol% denominator and complete buffer-stack specification. Measurement equipment must demonstrate the required temperature, field and current capabilities; instrument labels alone do not establish readiness. **Source:** B004, B005, B006.
- The operating table distinguishes envelopes from proposed settings. Pulse allocation, target-plane fluence and reel-to-reel deposition per cycle require calibration because the listed pulse energy, spot assumptions and stationary exposure calculation cannot be combined into an established growth recipe. **Source:** B007, B008, B009, B010.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage 1: Release targets and buffered tape, deposit matched films and complete oxygenation

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Target preparation.** Prefer qualified ceramic targets. For internal preparation, the following draft-derived recipe is only a P starting trial: mill in ethanol with zirconia media for 12 h; dry at 80 °C for 6 h; grind/calcine at 850 °C for 6 h in oxygen twice; add 2 wt% PVA; uniaxially press at 400 MPa then CIP at 200 MPa; debind using a furnace-approved schedule; sinter at 950 °C for 10 h with 5 °C/min heating and 2 °C/min cooling. Validate these temperatures against phase stability before scale-up.
2. Measure target density, inspect cracks, and obtain XRD/SEM-EDS maps. A doped target is deliberately multiphase: accept EuBCO plus BHO, not a fictitious single phase. Proposed density gate is ≥95% of the composition-specific theoretical density. Quarantine cracked, poorly mixed or chemically degraded targets. Record contamination and phase detection limits; EDS alone does not certify 6 mol%.
3. **Target hand-off:** provide composition calculation, lot, density, phase analysis and ablation-trial approval to the deposition operator.
4. **Buffered-tape release.** Inspect roughness, texture, cracks and barrier continuity. Do not polish or ultrasonically clean the finished buffer stack. Use its qualified cleaning procedure. If preparing tape internally, polish/clean bare metal first and use a validated full-stack traveler. Draft starting values—IBAD MgO 7–10 nm at 750 eV/45°, followed by 50 nm LaMnO3 at 700 °C/200 mTorr—are P development settings, not a qualified complete stack.
5. **Buffer hand-off:** transfer protected tape with stack identity, AFM/XRD results and maximum qualified thermal exposure. Reject exposed-metal defects or delamination.
6. **Deposition calibration.** Map heater indication to the moving tape's actual surface temperature, including emissivity changes with film thickness. Verify buffered-tape compatibility with the specified heater envelope. Calibrate deposition per cycle on witness tape. Define and log intercycle thermal trajectory and dwell; hold these identical for D33/U33.
7. Condition the appropriate target behind a shutter; 100 pulses is a P starting trial, extended until plume and deposition stabilize. Grow all doped increments from the same 6 mol% composite target. Do not alternate undoped/doped targets at 50:1 or deposit separate BHO layers. Record each pass's pressure, energy, tape speed, heater and surface temperatures, interruptions and cumulative exposure.
8. Cool within chamber limits, transfer protected tape to the oxygenation furnace, execute the validated oxygenation schedule and inspect for cracks. Do not adopt a 900 °C low-pressure hold as proof of oxygenation.
9. **Film hand-off:** submit process logs and thickness maps to characterization. Perform nondestructive XRD first; allocate neighboring coupons to magnetometry, transport and destructive FIB. Preserve an unpatterned archive.

#### Related Knowledge

- Qualified ceramic targets are preferred. Internal target preparation remains a proposed starting trial, with phase-stability validation and documented composition, density, phase analysis and ablation approval required for hand-off; EuBCO plus BHO is the intended multiphase target. **Source:** B011.
- Buffered-tape release requires stack identity, integrity and qualified thermal exposure. Finished buffers must use their qualified cleaning procedure, while the listed internal buffer-development settings do not constitute a complete qualified stack. **Source:** B011.
- Fabrication follows calibrated moving-tape temperature and deposition-per-cycle measurements, matched thick-arm intercycle histories, composite-target deposition, controlled cooldown and validated oxygenation. Process logs and thickness maps accompany the film hand-off, with nondestructive XRD preceding destructive sampling. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage 2: Measure film texture, lattice parameters and depth-dependent microstructure

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural characterization and gates
Measure θ–2θ phase/orientation scans, EuBCO **(005) rocking curves** and off-axis **(103) φ scans** at matched positions. Use suitable off-axis reflections for buffer φ scans; symmetric (002) scans cannot establish in-plane texture. Record wavelength, alignment, instrumental broadening and fit windows. For c ≈11.73 Å, d005 ≈2.346 Å; with Cu Kα1, λ=1.5406 Å, 2θ005 ≈38.3°.

Determine c using several resolved 00l reflections and Nelson–Riley extrapolation with alignment correction. For Williamson–Hall, use instrument-corrected breadth β in radians: βcosθ = Kλ/D + 4εsinθ. Report the reflection set, shape factor, fit uncertainty and anisotropy limitations; use identical analysis across arms.

Supplied structural references are c ≈11.73 Å, ω FWHM ≈1.5°, φ FWHM ≈2° and microstrain ≈0.21–0.25%. They are comparison references, not automatic rejection limits. Release intact specimens for measurement even when texture differs; flag deviations rather than selectively discarding unfavorable data.

Prepare protective-capped cross-sectional FIB lamellae from center/edge and multiple longitudinal sites. Draft-derived P settings are 30 kV lift-out, final 5 kV cleaning and <100 nm lamella thickness. Start STEM at 200 kV only after a dose-damage trial. Acquire diffraction and validated EDS maps, checking spectral overlaps/background.

Measure at least 50 identifiable columns per specimen where available, separately in bottom/middle/top regions. Report projected tilt relative to local c-axis, sizes and counting uncertainty; use orthogonal sections to assess projection bias. Quantify nanoparticles per imaged area, BHO-free regions, a-axis/misoriented grain fractions and Cu-O-associated nucleation. Do not interpret column tilt as equivalent to EuBCO rocking width or require unsupported particle dimensions/densities.

#### Related Knowledge

- EuBCO rocking curves and off-axis phi scans address different texture components. Lattice-parameter and microstrain comparisons require documented alignment, instrumental broadening and consistent reflection selection and analysis across arms. **Source:** B012, B013.
- The supplied structural values are comparison references rather than automatic rejection thresholds. Intact specimens remain eligible for measurement when texture differs, preventing selective removal of unfavorable data. **Source:** B014.
- Cross-sectional microscopy samples longitudinal and lateral positions and separates bottom, middle and top regions. Dose qualification, validated EDS and projection-aware column measurements support reporting of splay and secondary features without equating column tilt with rocking width or imposing unsupported particle specifications. **Source:** B015, B016.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage 2: Qualify bridges and measure resistive transitions, transport and magnetic current density

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Electrical and magnetic measurements
Pattern full-thickness bridges without substrate shorts. Qualify contacts on sacrificial coupons; draft Ti/Au and 120 °C epoxy curing are P options requiring contact-resistance and oxygen-loss checks. Keep metal pads outside the active gauge and characterize any shunting. Record actual geometry and milling damage.

For a 50 µm ×3.3 µm cross-section, area =1.65×10⁻⁶ cm²: **5 MA/cm² requires 8.25 A**; the 1.1 µm bridge requires 2.75 A. A 100 mA limit reaches only 0.0606 MA/cm² on D33. Proposed source capacity is ≥20 A, subject to validated lead/contact heating; it is not a guarantee that every Ic can be reached. With 1 mm tap spacing, the 1 µV/cm criterion is **0.10 µV**.

1. **ρ(T):** PPMS, compensated zero field, initial 1 mA excitation with current reversal. Verify independence on halving current. Measure 80–110 K with ≤0.1 K sampling near transition, finer if necessary; acquire warming/cooling checks. Define Tc at 50% and ΔTc=T90−T10 relative to a documented extrapolated normal-state baseline. The electric-field criterion defines Ic, not Tc.
2. **Transport:** measure 77 K self-field, Jc(B) at 4.2 K including 14 T parallel c, and Jc(θ) at 30 K/5 T. Sweep 0–180° in proposed 5° steps, refine extrema, with rotation plane perpendicular to current so B remains perpendicular to current. Define θ=0° as B parallel c; verify alignment. Stabilize after every field/angle change.
3. Ramp current adaptively, use polarity reversal and offsets, and extract Ic at E=1 µV/cm. Repeat selected sweeps at reduced ramp/duty cycle. Stop for heating, unstable voltage or contact failure. Report bounds, not extrapolated Ic, when capacity is insufficient.
4. **Magnetometry:** measure background-corrected hysteresis on dimensioned rectangular coupons at 77, 30 and 4.2 K within verified PPMS capability. For perpendicular field and a<b, use Jc=20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)], with ΔM in emu/cm³ of superconducting film and a,b in cm. Subtract matched substrate background. In-plane/oblique Bean extraction requires a validated anisotropic geometry model; do not reuse this formula blindly.

Report Jc=Ic/(wt), propagated uncertainty, Jc,max/Jc,min over the measured angular range, and Jc(θ)/Jc(0) separately. Use Fp=JcB in SI. Supplied targets are approximately 5 MA/cm² at 4.2 K/14 T, angular ratio <1.2 at 30 K/5 T, Tc≈97 K and ΔTc≈0.6 K. The supplied 77 K values 1.4 versus 2.6 MA/cm² lack unambiguous arm attribution; do not assign them as acceptance values.

#### Related Knowledge

- Transport interpretation depends on actual full-thickness bridge geometry, qualified contacts and characterized shunting. Required current and criterion voltage follow the bridge cross-section and tap spacing; source capacity remains subject to contact and lead heating. **Source:** B017, B018.
- The resistive transition uses a documented normal-state baseline, whereas critical current uses the electric-field criterion. Transport measurements require verified angular and Lorentz geometry, stabilization, reversal checks and bounds when current capacity is insufficient. **Source:** B019.
- Magnetic current-density extraction requires superconducting-film volume normalization and matched substrate-background subtraction, with a validated geometry model for nonperpendicular fields. Transport current density, angular ratios and pinning force are reported with uncertainties; supplied performance values remain targets and the ambiguous 77 K values receive no arm assignment. **Source:** B019, B020.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage 3: Disposition specimens, preserve records and control corrective feedback

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Disposition, safety and controlled feedback
Cracks/delamination: quarantine and examine thermal mismatch, thickness and intercycle history. Poor texture: investigate buffer integrity and surface-temperature calibration. Broad/low Tc: check oxygenation, phases and shorts. Apparent anisotropy: verify Lorentz geometry and heating before changing growth. Missing splay: inspect depth sampling and projection before process adjustment.

Retain raw logs, diffraction fits, calibrated images, EDS spectra, geometry, background curves, I–V traces, uncertainties and all failed specimens. Freeze settings within each matched block. Any corrective process change triggers a new matched block; do not change BHO concentration to rescue the core comparison.

Use powder containment for Ba-containing materials, solvent fire controls, press/furnace safeguards, laser interlocks, oxygen-compatible hot hardware, FIB shielding, cryogenic ventilation and magnet exclusion zones. Optional additions are U11 for a full thickness–composition factorial or orthogonal TEM/tomography for splay validation; neither replaces the core arms.

#### Related Knowledge

- Failure investigation follows the recorded defect or measurement symptom: cracks, texture deviations, transition changes, apparent anisotropy and missing splay each have specified checks before process adjustment. **Source:** B021.
- Raw records and failed specimens are retained, settings remain frozen within a matched block, and corrective process changes require a new matched block. Changing BHO concentration cannot rescue the core comparison. **Source:** B022.
- The listed powder, solvent, pressure, furnace, laser, oxygen, FIB, cryogenic and magnet safeguards are prerequisites throughout execution. U11 and additional splay-validation imaging are optional extensions rather than replacements for the core arms. **Source:** B023.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage 1 — Qualification and matched fabrication
**Tasks 1–2.** Establish D11: 1.1 µm EuBCO with nominal 6 mol% BHO; D33: 3.3 µm of the same composition; and U33: 3.3 µm undoped EuBCO. Use the proposed three independent matched deposition blocks from one buffered-tape lot, balance arm order, identify coupons by block, arm, run, longitudinal position and measurement suffix, and sample beginning, middle and end. Neighboring coupons are paired, not independent replicates. Interpret comparisons as combined thickness, cycle-count and thermal-exposure effects.

Before release, confirm the dopant denominator, complete barrier-stack specification, target composition and integrity, buffer continuity and maximum qualified thermal exposure. Prefer qualified targets; internal target and buffer recipes remain P development trials, not established production routes. Do not polish or ultrasonically clean finished buffers. Verify PPMS capabilities and high-field operation at 4.2 K/14 T and 30 K/5 T, including current capacity and thermal anchoring.

Qualify the 300 Hz, 700–1000 mJ/pulse, 800–1000 mTorr oxygen and 1000–1100 °C heater envelope. Resolve multi-plume pulse allocation, measure target-plane spot and fluence, calibrate moving-tape surface temperature, and determine deposition per pass, tape speed, overlap and dwell. Do not infer reel-to-reel growth from stationary pulse calculations. Every P selection requires calibration and an approved traveler: initial energy/pressure, tolerances, cycle schedule, thickness gate, cooldown, oxygenation, bridge geometry and measurement stability.

The proposed cycle schedule uses one calibrated 1.1 µm increment for D11 and three for D33/U33, with increasing heater setpoints of 1000/1050/1100 °C. Match thick-arm intercycle trajectories. Grow all doped increments from the same composite target; neither alternating targets nor separate BHO layers are permitted. Prevent BHO carryover into U33. Log pass conditions, interruptions and cumulative exposure, measure local thickness, stop sustained excursions, and complete hardware-compatible cooldown followed by validated oxygenation. The proposed 450 °C, 1 h oxygenation must establish uniformity through the thick film. Transfer logs and thickness maps with protected films; preserve an unpatterned archive.

### Stage 2 — Structural and superconducting characterization
**Tasks 3–4.** Perform nondestructive XRD before allocating neighboring coupons to magnetometry, transport and FIB. Measure phase/orientation scans, EuBCO (005) rocking curves and (103) phi scans at matched positions. Determine lattice parameter and instrument-corrected microstrain using consistent analyses and documented uncertainties. Structural references are not rejection limits: retain intact specimens and flag deviations.

Use protective-capped cross-sectional microscopy across lateral and longitudinal sites. Keep FIB settings proposed and qualify STEM dose before acquisition. Validate EDS overlaps and background. Where available, measure at least 50 identifiable columns per specimen, separately by depth, reporting local-axis tilt, size, counting uncertainty and projection limitations. Quantify nanoparticles, BHO-free regions, misoriented grains and Cu-O-associated nucleation without intentionally seeding particles or requiring all secondary features to disappear.

Qualify full-thickness bridges and contacts on sacrificial coupons; Ti/Au and epoxy curing remain P options. Record actual width, thickness, spacing, damage and shunting. Measure compensated-zero-field resistivity with current reversal, reduced-current checks and warming/cooling checks; define Tc at 50% and transition width from the documented baseline.

Measure 77 K self-field transport, 4.2 K field dependence including 14 T parallel c, and 30 K/5 T angular dependence. Verify alignment and maintain the rotation plane perpendicular to current. Use stabilization, adaptive current ramps, polarity reversal and reduced-duty checks. Extract Ic at 1 µV/cm; stop heating or unstable contacts and report capacity bounds rather than extrapolation. Normalize Jc by measured cross-section and propagate uncertainty. Correct magnetization for matched substrate background and superconducting-film volume; restrict the stated Bean expression to its specified geometry. Report angular ratios separately and calculate Fp = JcB in SI. Performance values remain reference targets, and ambiguous 77 K values are not assigned to arms.
