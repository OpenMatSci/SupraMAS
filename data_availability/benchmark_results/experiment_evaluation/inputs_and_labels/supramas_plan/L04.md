# Experimental Plan: Zr-dependent pinning in Gd0.5Yb0.5BCO coated conductors

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Release and qualify the comparison
The objective is to compare Zr-dependent phase evolution, texture, strain, superconducting transition temperature and field/angular critical current density in co-deposited Gd0.5Yb0.5BCO nanocomposites. Test whether improved low-to-medium-field pinning accompanies BaZrO3 formation and BaCuO2 suppression without prespecifying a dominant mechanism.

The proposed comparison comprises undoped, 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 mol% Zr arms in three independent randomized deposition blocks, totaling 18 films from one qualified tape lot. Before fabrication, resolve the Zr denominator, addition/substitution convention, four-entry formulation and Ba allocation; maintain Gd:Yb = 1:1. Confirm the complete five-layer buffer architecture, liquid-delivery chemistry, operating settings and instrument capabilities. Unresolved qualifications remain holds.

### Stage II — Fabricate and characterize matched films
Use qualified buffered Hastelloy, compatible soluble metal-organic precursors and calibrated liquid-delivery MOCVD. Nominal growth conditions are 775 °C, 0.70 Torr oxygen partial pressure, 90 nm min−1 and 0.55 µm thickness. Additional settings and tolerances remain proposed until qualified; total pressure is established separately from oxygen partial pressure. Apply a common qualified oxygenation recipe.

Complete nondestructive XRD before sectioning. Use adjacent, traceable coupons for EPMA, cross-sectional/plan-view TEM, transport and magnetization, retaining an archive. Quantify chemistry, phases, texture, morphology and strain with appropriate calibration and uncertainty.

### Stage III — Measure and consolidate the comparison
Use isolated four-probe bridges, locally measured dimensions and the 1 µV cm−1 criterion. Perform core temperature/field and angular measurements only within verified capability, alongside background-corrected magnetization. Preserve failed measurements, flagged films and independent-film distinctions. Anticipated outputs are traceable comparisons of structure, Tc and Jc, not predetermined improvements. Supplied morphology and performance values remain reference targets; high-field transport and oxygen-sensitive analysis are conditional extensions. Apply the specified chemical, thermal, electrical and cryogenic safety prerequisites.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage I — Release the comparison design, materials and measurement resources

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and release prerequisites
Compare Zr-induced phase evolution, texture, strain, Tc and field/angular Jc in **co-deposited nanocomposites**, not separate REBCO/BaZrO3 layers. Preserve the undoped control and heavy-doping arms. Test whether enhanced low-to-medium-field pinning accompanies BaZrO3 formation and BaCuO2 suppression; do not require any mechanism to dominate beforehand.

Before fabrication, the process lead must approve:
- The Zr mol% denominator, whether Zr is added or substituted, and the meanings of all four entries in `0.65:0.65:2.00:3.20`. A possible Gd:Yb:(Ba−Zr):Cu interpretation is **unconfirmed**. Document explicit cation amounts and Ba allocation to BaZrO3 for every arm; maintain Gd:Yb = 1:1.
- The complete five-layer buffer sequence, thicknesses, cap material and supplier/process record. Use qualified buffered tapes; a lone MgO layer is not equivalent.
- Liquid precursor chemistry, solvent, concentrations, vaporization/decomposition windows, carrier flows, total chamber pressure, cleaning and oxygenation recipe.
- Actual temperature, field, rotator, voltage-resolution and current capabilities. Do not assume a PPMS reaches 30 T or precisely 4.0 K.

Unresolved items above are fabrication/measurement holds, not permission to substitute another architecture.

##### 2. Samples, controls and resources
**Proposed matrix:** Zr = 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 mol%; three independent deposition blocks, one film per arm per block (18 films). Randomize order within each block, use one qualified tape lot, and track chamber conditioning/carryover. Hold non-Zr formulation and processing fixed according to the approved stoichiometric convention.

IDs: `GY-Z00-B01` through `GY-Z25-B03`. Append `-S` for structural/compositional coupon, `-T` for transport, `-M` for magnetization, and `-E` for TEM. Record coupon coordinates along/across tape; use adjacent coupons to connect measurements. Repeat I–V curves are technical repeats, not independent films.

Materials: certified buffered Hastelloy; soluble Gd, Yb, Ba, Cu and Zr metal-organic precursors compatible with liquid-delivery MOCVD; approved solvent and carrier gas; high-purity O2; lithography materials; qualified contact metal; EPMA standards; TEM preparation consumables. β-diketonate candidates from the draft require solubility and delivery qualification—do not use ceramic PLD pellets.

Equipment: calibrated liquid-delivery MOCVD with heated transfer lines, mass-flow and pressure control, substrate thermometry and exhaust treatment; oxygenation furnace; 2D-XRD; EPMA/WDS; TEM/STEM with chemical mapping; profilometer or cross-sectional thickness metrology; lithography/ion milling and contact deposition; low-noise four-probe electronics with protected current source; cryogenic magnet/rotator; Quantum Design PPMS magnetometry option. AFM may screen template roughness.

#### Related Knowledge

- The comparison concerns co-deposited nanocomposites and retains both undoped and heavy-Zr controls; it does not assume that a particular pinning mechanism must dominate. **Source:** B000.
- The unresolved composition convention, full buffer architecture, precursor delivery conditions and instrument capabilities are explicit release prerequisites, not authorization to substitute another process. **Source:** B001, B002.
- Randomized independent deposition blocks, a common tape lot and coordinate-linked coupons provide the specified comparison structure; repeated I–V curves do not supply independent film replication. **Source:** B003, B004.
- The materials and equipment lists support liquid-delivery MOCVD and complementary structural, chemical and superconducting measurements. Candidate precursors still require delivery qualification. **Source:** B005, B006.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage I — Qualify and lock growth, oxygenation and measurement settings

#### Specific Content

##### 3. Consolidated settings
Task-specified values are fixed nominal conditions. All tolerances and additional settings below are **proposed**, requiring qualification before the comparison campaign.

| Operation | Setting and operational limit |
|---|---|
| Growth | 775 °C ±5 °C; pO2 = 0.70 ±0.02 Torr = 93.3 ±2.7 Pa; rate 90 ±5 nm min−1 |
| Film thickness | 0.55 ±0.03 µm; measured locally for Jc |
| Growth duration | Derived steady-growth time: 550/90 = 6.11 min; calibrate nucleation/transients |
| Oxygenation pilot | 450 °C ±5 °C, 2 h ±5 min, O2 near 1 atm in a rated furnace; cool from growth at 5 °C min−1, then to room temperature at 1 °C min−1 |
| Template screen | In-plane texture FWHM <5° provisionally; establish out-of-plane and roughness limits from qualified templates |
| Transport geometry | Width 40 ±2 µm; bridge length 1 mm; tap separation 0.50 ±0.02 mm, measured after fabrication |
| Transport criterion | E = 1 µV cm−1; nominal Vc = 0.050 µV = 50 nV |
| Environment | Temperature stability ±0.1 K; angular alignment ±0.5°; field tolerance from calibrated magnet specification |
| Core grid | T = 4, 10, 20, 30, 50, 77 K; B = 0, 1, 3, 5, 10, 14 T; B∥c and B∥ab |
| Angular transport | 20 and 30 K, 5 T; θ = 0–180° in 5° steps, refine to 1° near peaks; θ=0 is B∥c |

Total pressure is **not** defined by pO2 alone. Establish it with carrier flow and pumping; discard the conflicting 25 Torr/750 °C/20 nm min−1 recipe. Lock the oxygenation pilot only after reproducible superconducting transitions; do not assume a specific δ. Oxygen content is `7−δ`, not δ ≈ 6.95–7.00.

#### Related Knowledge

- Fixed nominal conditions are distinguished from proposed tolerances and additional settings, which require qualification before the comparison campaign. **Source:** B007, B008.
- Oxygen partial pressure does not specify total chamber pressure; carrier flow and pumping must establish the latter without reinstating the discarded conflicting recipe. **Source:** B009.
- The oxygenation schedule remains a pilot until reproducible superconducting transitions support locking a common recipe; a specific oxygen deficiency is not assumed. **Source:** B008, B009.
- The proposed tap separation determines the nominal voltage criterion, while the temperature, field and angular grids define the intended core comparison subject to capability verification. **Source:** B008.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage II — Co-deposit, oxygenate and hand off traceable films and coupons

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Template receipt → deposition operator:** verify buffer records, inspect edges/delamination, measure representative pole figures and roughness. Reject damaged templates. Use supplier-approved cleaning; solvent rinsing/N2 drying may be qualified on sacrificial pieces. Do not ion-mill the cap or vacuum-anneal it without a compatibility trial.
2. **Precursor preparation → delivery operator:** weigh/dilute by the approved cation worksheet; log purity, lots, molarities and masses. Confirm dissolution, absence of precipitate, delivery stability and solvent compatibility. Calibrate liquid flow and vaporizer/line temperatures; verify delivered composition on pilot films by EPMA. Purge and test Zr carryover before undoped runs.
3. Load tapes consistently, leak-test and purge. Calibrate actual substrate temperature, not only heater setpoint. Stabilize pO2, total pressure and delivery before growth. Co-deliver matrix and Zr precursors for each arm; log all flows and transients. Use calibrated thickness monitoring or timed growth verified by witness sections; RHEED is not mandatory.
4. Oxygenate using the locked common recipe, cool and unload without bending. Measure thickness at center and edge. Record any excursions before assigning coupons.
5. **Deposition → characterization:** release intact labeled films with full run log. Complete nondestructive XRD before sectioning; allocate adjacent coupons to EPMA, transport, magnetization and destructive TEM. Keep an archive segment.
6. **Characterization → transport:** supply geometry/thickness maps and structural flags. Pattern the bridge without breaching buffers into conductive Hastelloy. Qualify ion-milling energy/dose and contact stack on sacrificial films; compare Tc before/after processing. Position current pads outside voltage taps and verify no substrate shunt.

#### Related Knowledge

- Template acceptance and precursor delivery qualification precede growth, including checks of buffer records, delivered composition and Zr carryover. **Source:** B010.
- Co-delivery, actual substrate-temperature calibration, stabilized pressures and documented transients preserve the specified nanocomposite fabrication workflow. **Source:** B010.
- Nondestructive XRD precedes sectioning, and adjacent labeled coupons link characterization to transport. Bridge processing requires qualified milling and contacts without a conductive substrate shunt. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage II — Quantify phases, composition, defect morphology and strain

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structure, chemistry and strain
**2D-XRD:** calibrate wavelength, detector distance/tilt and angular zero with a standard. Acquire identical exposure/geometry across arms; collect phase scans, REBCO (00l) peaks including (005), rocking curves, and off-axis REBCO (103) texture/reciprocal-space maps. Determine c-axis spacing from multiple indexed peaks and report uncertainty. Monitor BaZrO3 (200) and identified BaCuO2/RE2O3 peaks; use background, overlap, footprint and thickness corrections. A symmetric BaZrO3 (200) scan alone does not determine in-plane texture: add accessible off-axis pole figures. Calculate peak positions from wavelength and phase spacings, not an unsupported fixed 43°. Report detection limits for a-axis grains and phase disappearance. XRD intensity is not an absolute rod count.

**EPMA:** map at least five center/edge regions per film. Calibrate WDS standards, line overlaps and counting statistics. Select beam energy/current and thin-film corrections through an interaction-volume/dose pilot; 15 kV cannot be assumed to exclude substrate/buffer contributions in 0.55 µm films. Report Gd:Yb, Ba, Cu, Zr and spatial variability with uncertainty. EPMA measures chemistry, not electrical or thermal uniformity.

**TEM:** prepare cross-sectional and plan-view specimens from every arm, using protective coatings and low-energy final milling. Acquire at least ten systematically selected fields per specimen; log analyzed area, specimen thickness, dose and exclusions. Confirm BaZrO3, BaCuO2 and RE2O3 using diffraction plus chemistry. Measure rod length, width, orientation, continuity and plan-view areal density; quantify precipitate size and interface integrity. Use dose trials to limit oxygen loss/beam damage.

For strain mapping, use a cross-sectional zone axis displaying in-plane and c-axis vectors; use the same-film remote matrix as reference and undoped GdYbBCO as a control. Document GPA filtering and uncertainty. Strain maps alone cannot establish oxygen-vacancy concentration.

Supplied morphology references—BaZrO3 length >100 nm, width approximately 6 nm, density up to 2.2×10³ µm−2, and BaCuO2 intensity suppression—are hypotheses/comparison references, not compulsory acceptance thresholds. At that density, Bφ = nΦ0 ≈ 4.55 T; >100/6 implies aspect ratio >16.7. Bφ is a geometric estimate, not a demonstrated pinning optimum.

#### Related Knowledge

- Calibrated XRD with consistent geometry, corrections and off-axis measurements distinguishes phase and texture information; symmetric BaZrO3 scans alone cannot establish in-plane texture. **Source:** B011.
- EPMA requires standards, overlap treatment and thin-film interaction-volume qualification to report composition and spatial variability without assuming exclusion of buffer or substrate contributions. **Source:** B012.
- Cross-sectional and plan-view TEM combine diffraction and chemistry for phase identification and systematic morphology measurements. Strain mapping uses declared references and uncertainty, not an inferred vacancy concentration. **Source:** B013, B014.
- Supplied rod dimensions, density and phase-suppression references are comparison hypotheses rather than acceptance thresholds; the matching-field estimate is geometric, not proof of a pinning optimum. **Source:** B015.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage III — Measure superconducting transitions, transport and magnetization

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Superconducting measurements
Measure zero-field R(T) across the transition with a calibrated, nonheating sensing current. Report onset, midpoint and zero-resistance Tc using predefined criteria and noise limits. The supplied 88.4→86.1 K trend is not a processing rejection limit.

For angular scans, rotate B in a plane perpendicular to bridge current, keeping J⊥B. Verify thermometer settling and angle zero after cooldown. At each core condition acquire at least 100-point I–V sweeps, refine around Vc and repeat three times. Begin at low current; qualify ramp/averaging time by heating tests rather than imposing 10 s dwell at high current. Use offset correction/current reversal where thermally safe; interpolate the monotonic E(I) crossing. Record n-values only over a declared fit interval. Do not apply the draft's dimensionally inconsistent self-field correction; label B=0 as self-field.

Nominal superconducting area is `(0.004 cm)(5.5×10−5 cm)=2.2×10−7 cm²`. Thus the supplied Jc targets correspond to Ic = **7.70, 1.50 and 1.21 A**, respectively. A 10 A ceiling spans 45.5 MA cm−2 nominally, but leads, contacts and cooling must qualify that current. Contact power is I²Rc per contact; Rc <1 Ω is not sufficient. Establish thermal/compliance trips, verify 50 nV resolution, and use actual width/thickness for Jc = Ic/(wt). If the criterion is not reached safely, report a lower bound, not an extrapolated Ic.

**Magnetization:** measure matched unpatterned coupons at core temperatures/fields within verified capability; fix field sweep rate after a rate-dependence pilot. Subtract holder and matched buffered-tape backgrounds; assess reversible RE magnetic contribution. For a rectangular film in perpendicular field, use `Jc = 20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)]`, with a<b in cm, full loop width ΔM in emu cm−3 of superconducting film, giving A cm−2. Record film volume and demagnetization/model limitations. Do not use this formula for in-plane geometry without validation. Compare trends with transport; agreement within 20% is not mandatory.

#### Related Knowledge

- Transition measurements use nonheating current and predefined onset, midpoint and zero-resistance criteria; the supplied Tc trend is not a rejection rule. **Source:** B016.
- Angular transport maintains J perpendicular to B, while repeated I–V sweeps, offset treatment and a monotonic criterion crossing support the stated transport analysis without the discarded self-field correction. **Source:** B017.
- Jc normalization uses actual superconducting width and thickness. Current delivery, contact heating and voltage resolution require qualification, and an unreached criterion is reported as a lower bound. **Source:** B018.
- Magnetization analysis requires matched backgrounds, superconducting film volume and geometry-appropriate interpretation; the supplied perpendicular-field Bean expression is not automatically valid in-plane. **Source:** B019.

### Experimental Task 6: Stage III — Apply quality gates, archive comparisons and control optional extensions

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Quality, safety and controlled interpretation
Quarantine films with wrong formulation, delamination, failed isolation, or unqualified growth excursions; preserve their records. Retain structurally imperfect but safely measurable films as flagged data, avoiding selection by Jc. Heating, hysteretic I–V behavior, >10% contact drift or voltage/compliance instability triggers current shutdown and requalification. Low Tc prompts oxygenation/composition checks; weak texture prompts template/temperature checks; short or irregular rods prompt delivery/rate checks. Any corrective recipe change starts a new matched block including undoped and heavy-Zr controls—never selectively reprocess one arm within the primary comparison.

Use interlocked ventilation for solvents/metal-organics, oxygen-compatible hardware, rated pressure equipment, hot-zone shielding, and trained cryogen/magnet operation with oxygen-deficiency monitoring. Control lithography chemicals, ion-milling exposure, conductive debris and high-current/quench hazards.

Archive raw scans, maps, images, R(T), I–V and magnetic loops with IDs, coupon coordinates, calibrations, geometry uncertainties, actual T/B/θ, run logs, exclusions and analysis scripts. Compare independent-film distributions of phase fraction proxies, rod density, c-axis spacing, Tc, Jc(B) and Jc,min(θ); report uncertainty and failed measurements. Compute Fp = JcB in SI only where J⊥B; pinning-model fits are exploratory, not unique mechanism assignments.

**Conditional extension:** transport at 4 K and 20, 20.5, 25 and 30 T on all arms using a verified high-field facility, preserving geometry, criterion and overlap measurements at 5–14 T. Without that access, the >20 T crossover and 30 T target remain untested. Optional oxygen-sensitive analysis may investigate strain/vacancy association on matched coupons; it must not replace the core controls.

#### Related Knowledge

- Quarantine and shutdown rules separate unsafe or unqualified specimens from safely measurable flagged films. Corrective recipe changes require new matched blocks rather than selective reprocessing. **Source:** B020.
- The specified ventilation, oxygen-compatible and pressure-rated equipment, hot-zone protection, and trained cryogenic/magnet operation are safety prerequisites for execution. **Source:** B021.
- Archiving raw data, calibrations, coordinates, dimensions, exclusions and failed measurements preserves independent-film comparisons and uncertainty. Pinning-model fits remain exploratory. **Source:** B022.
- High-field transport requires verified external capability and overlap measurements; without access, the high-field crossover and target remain untested. Oxygen-sensitive analysis is optional and cannot replace core controls. **Source:** B023.
