# Experimental Plan: YBCO–BZO thin-film pinning and quench protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Stage 1 — Qualify resources and fabricate matched films
Compare APC-free YBCO with nominal 4 wt % BZO–YBCO embedded nanocomposites grown directly on STO (100), without buffers, separate BZO layers or blanket stabilizers. The objective is to assess morphology, zero-field and in-field critical current density, accommodation field, minimum quench energy and normal-zone propagation velocity. Reduced zero-field Jc, improved in-field pinning and altered quench behavior remain hypotheses.

The proposed design uses three independent deposition runs per arm, allocating intact magnetic specimens and device specimens, with tracked material reserved for microscopy after electrical testing. Resources include certified targets or qualified target manufacture, substrate preparation equipment, PLD, lithography and wet etching, structural and magnetic characterization, and PPMS-based synchronized electrical acquisition. Release requires a signed calibration sheet and verified instrument capability. All P settings require qualification, including loading convention, substrate treatment, growth, oxygenation, device layout and acquisition.

### Stage 2 — Fabricate devices and establish structural and electrical baselines
Grow approximately 200 nm films on 10 × 10 mm substrates and pattern 50 µm bridges by maskless photolithography and H3PO4 etching. Preserve intact magnetic specimens. Use XRD, microscopy, 10 K magnetic hysteresis and four-probe transport to characterize films and establish condition-specific Ic. Characterize the approximately 6 nm diameter, c-axis-oriented BZO nanorod objective rather than assuming its formation.

### Stage 3 — Measure quench behavior and consolidate comparisons
Use matched thermal mounting, a nominal 220 Ω heater and 0.7 s pulses over the specified 20–70 K and 0–8 T range. Determine MQE brackets and propagation from local 10 mV crossings at stations separated by 1 mm, subject to validated protection. Anticipated outputs are traceable morphology distributions, normalized transport and magnetic comparisons, quench brackets, uncertainties and failure records. Supplied performance values are reference targets, not measured outcomes or acceptance requirements.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Qualify the comparison matrix, equipment and PLD targets

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and experimental controls
Compare APC-free YBCO with nominal 4 wt % BZO–YBCO nanocomposites on STO (100), measuring morphology, zero-field and in-field Jc, accommodation field B*, MQE and NZPV. Reduced Jc,0, enhanced in-field pinning and altered quench behavior are hypotheses to test, not guaranteed outcomes. Patterning defines current paths; it does not remove pre-existing crystallographic grain boundaries.

**Setting status:** Task-specified conditions are marked **T** below. All other numerical recipes, tolerances, layouts and decision thresholds are **P: proposed, requiring qualification**. Release a signed calibration sheet before processing experimental samples. Do not substitute a buffer, different substrate, layered BZO architecture or stabilizer in the core comparison.

###### Sample matrix and identifiers
| Arm | Target | Proposed independent replication | Allocation per deposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | APC-free YBCO | Three independent runs | One intact magnetic specimen M; one device specimen Q |
| Z | YBCO + 4 wt % BZO | Three independent runs | Same allocation |

Use identifiers `R-or-Z/run/substrate/M-or-Q/bridge`. Co-deposit M and Q where possible; verify holder-position equivalence and rotate positions between matched runs. After electrical testing, use reserved Q material for destructive TEM. Prepare qualification coupons separately. Record substrate thickness, lot and mounting face; match thickness and thermal mounting between arms. Alternate arm order and clean/pre-ablate to prevent Zr carryover. Analyze deposition runs, not repeated pulses, as independent replicates.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release checks
Use certified YBCO and BZO powders or certified dense PLD targets; STO (100), 10 × 10 mm; oxygen; acetone, ethanol, IPA and DI water; compatible positive resist/developer; assayed H3PO4; contact metal/wires; insulating heater attachment materials; nominal 220 Ω on-chip heater.

Equipment: enclosed powder mixing/pressing and oxygen furnace if manufacturing targets; PLD with fluence meter, calibrated substrate thermometry and oxygen control; maskless lithography, wet bench, profilometer, optical microscope and AFM; XRD, SEM/EDS; SQUID-VSM; specimen preparation/FIB and Cs-corrected 200 kV HAADF-STEM; PPMS, current source, low-noise differential voltage instrumentation, heater driver and synchronized digitizer installed through the NI PXIe-1073 interface.

**Before release:** verify actual magnet limits. A SQUID-VSM limited below 8 T cannot supply the 10 K, 8 T magnetic endpoint; arrange a suitable magnetic instrument and cross-calibration. Verify PPMS 20–70 K/8 T operation, wiring current capacity, heater pulse rating, acquisition modules, input ranges and hardware shutdown. PXIe-1073 alone does not specify digitizer capability.

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
###### A. Targets → substrate/growth team
Confirm loading convention first. For total-mixture loading, weigh 48 g YBCO plus 2 g BZO per 50 g batch; 50 g plus 2 g is only 3.85 wt %. Do not substitute mol %.

If manufacturing targets, qualify the draft-derived starting route: ethanol-assisted mixing for 6 h at 300 rpm, drying at 80 °C for 12 h, pressing to the PLD holder's geometry, then sintering at 920 °C for 12 h in oxygen, 5 °C/min heating and 2 °C/min cooling. Specify solvent/powder ratio, media, pressing stress, oxygen flow and target dimensions in the calibration sheet; reject media that introduce Zr into references. A press load is not a pressure without die area.

Inspect density, cracks and ablation behavior; use powder XRD and SEM/EDS to identify unintended phases and Zr segregation. BZO is an intended phase. Do not apply epitaxial rocking-curve criteria to powder targets. Hand off target mass records, composition maps and release signature.

- Task-specified conditions and proposed settings have different release status. Instrument limits, acquisition capability and the signed calibration sheet must be resolved before experimental processing. **Source:** B001, B006, B007.
- The proposed total-mixture mass convention must be confirmed before target preparation. Target manufacture remains a qualification route with unresolved processing details, and release uses target-appropriate phase, composition and ablation checks. **Source:** B008, B009, B010.

### Experimental Task 2: Prepare and release STO substrates for deposition

#### Specific Content

##### B. Substrates → PLD operator
Clean bare STO using the table recipe; dry with nitrogen. Anneal and inspect AFM terraces and particulate contamination. Proposed AFM target: RMS <0.2 nm over 5 × 5 µm. AFM alone does not establish TiO2 termination; document supplier preparation or verify termination separately. Reclean contaminated substrates; quarantine damaged ones. Transfer covered, with substrate lot and AFM files.

#### Related Knowledge

- The cleaning and oxygen-annealing settings are proposed starting conditions, not an established substrate preparation recipe. **Source:** B007, B011.
- AFM supports assessment of terraces and contamination but does not establish TiO2 termination. Substrate release retains preparation documentation, contamination handling and covered transfer. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Deposit matched films and release their thickness and epitaxy

#### Specific Content

##### C. Growth → characterization
Deposit directly on STO using qualified PLD settings. Measure laser spot area and fluence, pre-ablate with shutter closed, rotate targets and log pulses. Calibrate thickness per pulse separately for each target; do not assume identical rates. Apply the frozen common oxygenation/cooling recipe and record its complete trajectory.

Measure thickness and uniformity at multiple positions. Use XRD θ–2θ for YBCO (00l), a suitable rocking curve such as YBCO (005), and an off-axis scan such as (103) to assess epitaxy. For conventional STO (100) indexing, symmetric substrate reflections are (h00), e.g. (200), not (002) unless axes are explicitly relabeled. Record radiation wavelength, angles and instrument broadening. Quarantine non-epitaxial, cracked or out-of-tolerance films; do not rescue one arm with an unmatched treatment.

#### Related Knowledge

- Direct PLD growth requires qualified settings, measured fluence and separate thickness-per-pulse calibration for each target. The common oxygenation and cooling trajectory must be frozen and recorded rather than inferred. **Source:** B001, B007, B012.
- Thickness mapping and complementary XRD scans support film release. Conventional STO (100) indexing and instrument broadening must be recorded correctly, and an unmatched treatment cannot rescue only one comparison arm. **Source:** B013.

### Experimental Task 4: Pattern bridges and install qualified contacts and heaters

#### Specific Content

##### D. Device fabrication → cryogenic team
Reserve intact M specimens. On Q, define a proposed 4 mm-long bridge with wide current pads, upstream heater, a 1 mm transport gauge and two local voltage-sensing stations separated by measured 1 mm. Each station uses equal proposed 0.2 mm differential gauges; extra reference contacts prevent interpreting cumulative voltage as a moving front. Approve layout, heater footprint and thermal coupling before lithography.

Avoid ultrasound on grown films. Coat, expose and develop qualified resist; inspect openings. Prepare acid by adding stock to water. Determine clearing time on sacrificial coupons for both compositions; the draft's 20 nm/min and 10 min are unverified. Etch to clearing with minimal calibrated overetch, promptly rinse and strip without ultrasound. Inspect width, undercut, isolation and cracks; reject narrowed or delaminated bridges.

Deposit qualified localized contacts outside the active gauges; no blanket metal stabilizer. Avoid unqualified ion milling, plasma or Ar annealing that may damage or deoxygenate YBCO. Attach the electrically isolated 220 Ω heater with identical geometry/coupling in both arms. Measure contact resistance and heater leakage; establish power-based contact limits. Hand off measured geometry, heater/contact maps and continuity records.

#### Related Knowledge

- Intact magnetic specimens are reserved while device specimens receive an approved layout with separate transport and local propagation gauges. Reference contacts guard against mistaking cumulative voltage for a moving front. **Source:** B014.
- H3PO4 clearing must be calibrated on sacrificial coupons for both compositions; inherited etch-rate and duration estimates are unverified. Dimensional, isolation and damage checks govern bridge release. **Source:** B007, B015.
- Localized contacts and electrically isolated heaters preserve the core unstabilized comparison. Contact heating, leakage and matched heater coupling require qualification without introducing unqualified damaging treatments. **Source:** B016.

### Experimental Task 5: Characterize inclusions and establish magnetic and transport baselines

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Characterization and transport
Measure intact M hysteresis at 10 K with identical sweep history/rate and B∥c. Subtract holder and matched bare-STO backgrounds. For rectangular in-plane dimensions a≤b, use the Bean expression `Jc = 20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)]` with a,b in cm, ΔM in emu/cm³ of **film volume**, yielding A/cm²; multiply by 10⁴ for A/m². ΔM is the full loop width. Report geometric-model and background uncertainty; patterned devices are not equivalent intact Bean specimens.

Prepare plan-view and cross-sectional lamellae from tracked material. At 200 kV HAADF-STEM, measure diameter, spacing, length, orientation, interface continuity and unwanted phases across multiple fields; confirm Zr-rich inclusions by EDS. Report distributions and preparation/beam artifacts. Approximately 20 nm square spacing implies `Bφ≈Φ0/s²≈5.2 T`; this estimate is neither measured B* nor proof of rod continuity.

Measure low-current R(T) through the transition, defining onset, midpoint and width consistently. At every quench T,B, obtain reversible four-probe I–V using low-duty ramps, current reversal and heating checks. With L=1 mm, `Vc=(1 µV/cm)L=0.1 µV=100 nV`; a 1 cm gauge would require 1 µV, not 10 µV. Calculate `Jc=Ic/(wt)` using measured dimensions.

Nominal cross-section is 10⁻¹¹ m². Supplied 10 K/8 T Jc targets imply Ic≈0.20 A for Z and 0.05 A for R; these do not predict Ic at quench temperatures. Qualify source, wiring and pads for actual Ic. Fit B* using one preregistered plateau-to-power-law crossover model and common fit range; report undefined crossovers rather than force a value.

#### Related Knowledge

- Bean-model analysis uses intact specimens, background subtraction and magnetization normalized to film volume. Patterned bridges are not equivalent magnetic specimens. **Source:** B017.
- Tracked plan-view and cross-sectional STEM measurements establish inclusion distributions and continuity, with EDS confirmation and artifact reporting. A spacing-derived matching-field estimate is not measured accommodation field or proof of continuous rods. **Source:** B003, B018.
- Transport Ic is determined separately at every quench condition using reversible measurements and measured geometry. The voltage criterion scales with gauge length, while supplied current estimates do not predict Ic at quench temperatures. **Source:** B019, B020.
- Accommodation-field comparison requires one preregistered crossover model and a common fit range; an undefined crossover must remain undefined. **Source:** B020.

### Experimental Task 6: Measure heater energy, MQE brackets and local propagation timing

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Quench sequence and analysis
Mount STO backs with identical calibrated thermal interface, clamp and thermometer placement. Measure heater R(T,B), pulse current/voltage and leakage. Electrical energy is `Eh=∫VhIh dt≈Ih²Rhτ`; absorbed bridge energy requires separate coupling calibration.

At nominal 220 Ω and 0.7 s, 0.1–1 J requires 25.5–80.6 mA, 5.6–17.7 V and 0.143–1.43 W during the pulse. Verify driver and heater ratings; do not use the draft's 100 mJ/100 ms-limited generator unchanged.

At each stable T,B, set 0.9 Ic. Record baseline, then apply a 0.7 s pulse while acquiring heater and local voltages synchronously. Bracket recoverable versus self-sustaining post-pulse events by stepping energy conservatively, then bisect. P: resolve the bracket to ≤10%, repeat the boundary three times and require voltage growth/persistence for 100 ms after heater-off, subject to validated safe shutdown. Report MQE as a bracket and operational criterion, including early-cutoff censoring.

For NZPV, use baseline-corrected 10 mV crossings at the two local stations: `v=Δx/Δt`. At 200 mm/s, 1 mm gives 5 ms; 100 ksample/s provides 500 samples. Verify channel skew, bandwidth, crossing interpolation and equivalent local voltage response. Reject heater pickup, simultaneous global transitions or missing crossings; report apparent threshold velocity if front equivalence cannot be demonstrated. Repeat three valid events per condition, cooling fully between events.

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched thermal mounting and measured heater resistance, voltage and current underpin electrical pulse-energy calculation. Electrical heater energy is distinct from absorbed bridge energy unless coupling is separately calibrated. **Source:** B021, B022.
- MQE is an operational bracket between recovery and self-sustaining post-pulse behavior. Proposed resolution, repetition and persistence criteria remain subject to qualified safe shutdown, with early cutoffs reported as censoring. **Source:** B023, B025.
- Propagation analysis uses baseline-corrected local threshold crossings, measured station separation and validated timing response. Pickup, global transitions and missing crossings are rejected; uncertain front equivalence limits reporting to apparent threshold velocity. **Source:** B024.

### Experimental Task 7: Enforce protection gates and consolidate qualified comparison records

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Gates, protection, records and feedback
Install hardware current/heater cutoff with validated latency and specimen-specific safe voltage/energy limits before quenches. Interrupt on contact overheating, leakage, abnormal baseline, cryogenic instability or runaway. Use laser interlocks, powder containment, acid/solvent hoods and compatible PPE; segregate acid and solvent waste. Apply oxygen-furnace, magnetic exclusion and cryogen ventilation controls.

Low/broad Tc: investigate oxygenation, contamination and etch damage. Misaligned rods: inspect target transfer and growth calibration. Excess contact heating: remake contacts, not increase bias. Timing artifacts: repair synchronization/grounding. Recheck Ic after quench series; P: >5% irreversible change quarantines subsequent data.

Archive raw moments, I–V and synchronized traces; geometry, mounting, pulse integrals, environmental logs, calibration files, microscopy images and analysis scripts. Preserve exclusions and uncertainties. Compare arms at matched normalized current, temperature, field and coupling; retain actual currents. Supplied performance numbers are targets only.

Change one qualification factor at a time, repeat matched R/Z controls and freeze settings before confirmatory runs. Optional angular Jc measurements or matched absolute-current quenches may clarify directional pinning or bias effects; neither replaces the core matrix. Morphology/transport correlations alone do not establish mean-free-path shortening, TAFF localization or strain causality.

#### Related Knowledge

- Validated hardware cutoff and specimen-specific limits precede quenches. Facility controls address laser, powder, acid, solvent, oxygen-furnace, magnetic and cryogenic hazards. **Source:** B025.
- Fault responses retain the prescribed distinction between troubleshooting and increased bias. Post-series Ic checks and the proposed irreversible-change quarantine threshold protect interpretation of subsequent data. **Source:** B026.
- Archived raw data, calibrations, geometry, mounting and exclusions support comparisons at matched normalized current, temperature, field and coupling while retaining actual currents and uncertainties. **Source:** B027.
- Qualification changes require matched controls and frozen settings before confirmatory runs. Optional angular transport or absolute-current quenches do not replace the core matrix, and correlations do not establish the listed causal mechanisms. **Source:** B028.
