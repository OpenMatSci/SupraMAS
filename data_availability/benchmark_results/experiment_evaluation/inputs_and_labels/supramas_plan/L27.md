# Experimental Plan: Multi-RE RE123 bulk synthesis and pinning characterization

## Experimental Draft

### Objective and comparison design
Prepare multi-RE REBa2Cu3O7−y powders and bulk pellets by conventional solid-state processing, targeting homogeneous RE-site mixtures without substrates, buffers or added pinning phases. Compare configurational entropy and average ionic-size associations with stacking faults, Tc, superconducting fraction estimates and magnetic Jc. The proposed incomplete-diffusion/peritectic stacking-fault mechanism remains a hypothesis, not an established explanation.

Retain all five equiatomic arms: C1 Eu/Gd/Y/Er/Yb, C2 Eu/Gd/Y/Er, C3 Eu/Gd/Y, C4 Nd/Eu/Gd/Y and C5 La/Nd/Eu/Gd/Y. C1/C5 and C2/C4 provide same-entropy comparisons, while element identity remains a confounder. Propose three independent batches per composition, randomized within batch blocks, with matched processing except for composition-specific measured Tp-dependent peak temperatures.

### Resources and workflow
Use the specified RE oxides, BaCO3, CuO, ethanol, zirconia milling media and qualified refractory supports. Required equipment includes powder containment, milling and pressing equipment, air and oxygen furnaces, calibrated DSC-TG, density metrology, XRD, FE-SEM-EDS, FIB/HRTEM and PPMS magnetometry.

Qualify materials, equipment and proposed settings before fabrication. Mix and calcine twice at 940 °C; determine Tp from representative precursor aliquots before pressing and flowing-air sintering at Tp +5 °C. Complete oxygen annealing at 400 °C for 48 h, measure density, and allocate matched specimens for structural, microscopy and superconducting measurements. Magnetic Jc is the core endpoint; direct transport is conditional and optional.

### Constraints and anticipated outputs
Retain proposed/P status, calibration tolerances and sacrificial-pilot prerequisites. Verify RE123 reformation before releasing the shared thermal schedule. Record failed preparations and uncertainties without selecting only high-performing specimens. Outputs are traceable phase, composition, fault, density and magnetic datasets with batch-level comparisons. Supplied performance and density values are reference targets, never measured outcomes or sample-selection gates.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Establish the comparison matrix and qualify materials, equipment and processing settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and comparison matrix
Prepare homogeneous RE-site mixtures by solid-state processing. Compare entropy, ionic size, stacking faults (SFs), superconducting response and magnetic Jc. Test the proposed incomplete-diffusion/peritectic SF mechanism without assuming it is established by correlation.

| Composition code | RE-site fractions | N | ΔSmix/R = ln N |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| C1 | Eu/Gd/Y/Er/Yb, each 0.20 | 5 | 1.609 |
| C2 | Eu/Gd/Y/Er, each 0.25 | 4 | 1.386 |
| C3 | Eu/Gd/Y, each 1/3 | 3 | 1.099 |
| C4 | Nd/Eu/Gd/Y, each 0.25 | 4 | 1.386 |
| C5 | La/Nd/Eu/Gd/Y, each 0.20 | 5 | 1.609 |

C2 corresponds to the supplied EuGdYEr reference. Keep C5 as the La-containing comparison; do not exclude it for low Tc. C1/C5 and C2/C4 provide same-entropy comparisons with different ionic sizes, but element identity remains a confounder.

Propose three independent synthesis batches per composition, B01–B03. IDs: `C#-B##-P` precursor, `-TG` thermal aliquot, `-L##` pellet, `-M` magnetometry coupon, `-X` diffraction aliquot, `-S` microscopy section and `-F##` FIB lamella. Randomize composition order within batch blocks. Use identical consumables, batch loading, ramps, holds and oxygenation; only the measured Tp-dependent peak temperature differs.

Supplied references—not new results—are C2 Jc = 373 kA cm−2 at 4.2 K, zero applied field; Jc >10 kA cm−2 at 5 T; approximately 98% superconducting fraction; La-free Tc >90 K; density 84–92% theoretical. They are evaluation targets, not sample-selection gates.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and parameter release
Use Eu2O3, Gd2O3, Y2O3, Er2O3, Yb2O3, Nd2O3 and La2O3, BaCO3 and CuO. Adopt draft purchasing specifications provisionally: RE oxides 99.99%, BaCO3 99.95%, CuO 99.99%; verify certificates, moisture and oxide identities. Use ethanol, zirconia milling media, a validated nonreactive refractory support, oxygen-rated tubing and high-purity oxygen. Supports are processing fixtures, not substrates. Do not introduce BN coatings or additives without compatibility testing.

Equipment: calibrated balance, ventilated powder enclosure, planetary mill, 10 mm die/press, flowing-air furnace, oxygen tube furnace, dimensional metrology, density apparatus, XRD, DSC-TG capable of temperatures above Tp, FE-SEM-EDS, FIB and HRTEM, and PPMS magnetometry.

|---|---|---|

### Experimental Task 2: Fabricate precursor powders and oxygenated bulk pellets with thermal hand-offs

#### Specific Content

##### 3. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Weigh and mix.** Correct oxide masses using certificates and validated moisture conditioning, especially for La-containing powder. For one mole of product, weigh xi/2 mole of each RE2O3, 2 mole BaCO3 and 3 mole CuO; scale to the batch mass. Record individual masses and calculations. Mill wet; inspect media wear. Dry at proposed 80 °C until constant mass and no solvent remains. XRD cannot establish mixing homogeneity by itself.
2. **Calcine twice.** Transfer labelled powder to validated refractory vessels. Run the first 940 °C treatment, cool, weigh, grind and take an XRD aliquot. Repeat at 940 °C—not 900 °C—then regrind and sample again. Preserve precursor archive. Residual carbonate, major unreacted oxides or contamination trigger a hold for investigation; any additional processing is separately identified.
3. **Thermal hand-off before sintering.** Transfer representative `-TG` aliquots to DSC-TG. Provisionally use 15 ±0.5 mg, flowing air and 10 °C/min to 1150 °C, within verified equipment/crucible limits. Run baseline and replicates; identify peritectic onset using a predeclared tangent method and TG context. Check rate dependence at 5 °C/min on pilots. Release composition-specific Tp only after uncertainty assessment. Do not reuse thermally decomposed aliquots.
4. **Press and sinter.** Load proposed 2 g portions in the 10 mm die; record actual pellet thickness. Apply 50 MPa, then 150 MPa for 60 s, then 250 MPa for 120 s; eject slowly. At 250 MPa, the required load is 19.6 kN, approximately 2.00 tonne-force; “2–3 tons” is not interchangeable with pressure. Record green mass, dimensions and cracks. Sinter at the released Tp +5 °C schedule in flowing air; log air flow, furnace position and actual temperature. Retain all fragments if failure occurs.
5. **Oxygenate.** Transfer cooled pellets to the mapped oxygen-furnace zone with approximately 5 mm spacing. Purge by a validated number of tube-volume exchanges, not an assumed fixed time. Apply the released 400 °C/48 h treatment and cooling. Weigh before/after, then store dry. Mass uptake alone does not establish absolute oxygen content or full oxygenation.
6. **Allocate final samples.** Measure envelope volume, mass and density before cutting. Validate Archimedes wetting/infiltration correction or use a qualified alternative. Calculate theoretical density from refined cell volume and composition, documenting oxygen-content assumptions. Cut matched coupons for magnetometry, powder XRD and microscopy; retain an untouched archive. Photograph cut faces and remeasure magnetic dimensions. Transfer magnetometry coupons before destructive FIB work. Do not select microscopy specimens only from high-Jc pellets.

#### Related Knowledge

- Certificate- and moisture-corrected stoichiometric weighing, wet milling and two 940 °C calcinations establish the specified precursor sequence. Intermediate weighing, grinding and diffraction sampling provide checkpoints; XRD alone does not establish mixing homogeneity. **Source:** B010.
- Representative DSC-TG aliquots must establish a released composition-specific peritectic onset before sintering. Thermally decomposed aliquots are not reused, and pressing pressure must be distinguished from the corresponding die load. **Source:** B010.
- Validated oxygen purging, logged annealing and cooling, and pre-cut density measurements preserve the processing and normalization chain. Oxygen mass uptake does not establish absolute oxygen content, and microscopy allocation must not favor only high-Jc pellets. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize phase structure, RE distribution and stacking faults

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Structural and microstructural measurements
- **XRD-Rietveld:** proposed Cu Kα, 2θ = 10–70°, 0.02° steps; calibrate peak position/instrument broadening and increase counts or range if needed. Refine a, b, c in Å, cell volume, phase fractions and uncertainties with documented structural models. Distinguish orthorhombic (200)/(020): d200 = a/2 and d020 = b/2; use 2d sin θ = λ with consistent units. Report orthorhombicity as (b−a)/(b+a), without an invented acceptance threshold. State validated detection limits rather than “zero secondary phase.”
- **FE-SEM-EDS:** survey pellet centre and edge, porosity, platelet sizes, cracks and boundary phases. Use standards/overlap corrections for RE lines; report measured RE fractions with uncertainty and maps from multiple grains. Proposed minimum: five fields per region. SEM morphology alone cannot prove low-angle boundaries; use optional EBSD if boundary-angle attribution is required.
- **FIB/HRTEM-FFT:** take proposed ≥3 lamellae per composition from predefined, distributed grains, including centre/edge sites. Deposit protection, lift out, thin and finish at low ion energy; record ion species, currents, energies and thickness. Validate beam-damage limits rather than assuming a universal dose. Acquire ≥20 interpretable images across lamellae where feasible; use [100] or [010] to view basal planar faults edge-on, verifying actual orientation. Calibrate image/FFT scales; reciprocal spacing is cycles/nm, with d = 1/g. Identify SFs from real-space stacking discontinuities supported by diffraction/FFT, not streaking alone. Report spacing distributions, fault counts per imaged area or line length, sampled grain count and censored observations. Do not impose ≤10 nm spacing or an unsupported optimum.

#### Related Knowledge

- Calibrated XRD-Rietveld analysis provides lattice parameters, phase fractions and uncertainties. Orthorhombic peak assignment requires consistent diffraction units, and phase reporting must use validated detection limits rather than an assertion of zero secondary phase. **Source:** B011.
- Centre and edge SEM-EDS surveys address porosity, cracks, boundary phases and measured RE distributions. RE-line standards and overlap corrections support composition reporting; morphology alone cannot establish boundary angles. **Source:** B011.
- Distributed FIB sampling, beam-damage qualification and verified HRTEM orientation support representative fault observations. Stacking-fault identification requires real-space discontinuities supported by diffraction or FFT, not streaking alone or an imposed optimum spacing. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 4: Measure magnetic transitions and estimate shielding and Meissner fractions

#### Specific Content

##### 5. PPMS superconducting measurements and calculations
Verify actual temperature range, bipolar field capability to at least ±7 T, moment range, residual field and calibration standards. Draft specifications of 1.9–400 K and 9 T are unverified. Mount proposed 2 ×2 ×1 mm coupons with field normal to the 2 ×2 mm face, fixed across samples; random polycrystalline pellets have no assumed global c-axis. Measure holder background using identical mounting material.

**ZFC/FC:** reset magnetic history above Tc, null field, cool to 5 K, apply proposed 20 Oe (μ0H ≈2 mT), warm to 120 K, then field-cool to 5 K. Use stabilized points: proposed 0.5 K spacing near transition, 2 K elsewhere, ≥30 s equilibration and 3 s averaging. Define onset by a predeclared baseline-intersection procedure; report midpoint and 10–90% width. Magnetometry cannot produce a zero-resistance Tc.

For fraction estimates use SI: χapp = m/(VHapplied); after background correction, fshield ≈−(1−N)χapp, with N calculated for measured geometry. Report ZFC shielding and FC Meissner estimates separately, envelope versus solid-volume normalization and porosity/demagnetization uncertainty. Screening and connectivity prevent equating shielding unconditionally with thermodynamic superconducting volume.

#### Related Knowledge

- Verified PPMS capabilities, measured coupon geometry, consistent field orientation and matched holder-background measurements are prerequisites for comparable magnetic data. Random polycrystalline pellets have no assumed global c-axis. **Source:** B012.
- The specified ZFC/FC sequence controls magnetic history and defines magnetometric transition reporting through predeclared onset, midpoint and width procedures. Magnetometry does not provide a zero-resistance Tc. **Source:** B013.
- Shielding and Meissner estimates require SI susceptibility, geometry-dependent demagnetization treatment and explicit volume normalization. Porosity, screening and connectivity prevent unconditional identification with thermodynamic superconducting volume. **Source:** B014.

### Experimental Task 5: Acquire bipolar magnetization loops and calculate magnetic critical current density

#### Specific Content

**M-H:** independently zero-field cool to 4.2 K and 77 K. After proposed 5 min stabilization, acquire complete ±7 T loops, including exact zero and ±5 T points. Proposed steps: 0.02 T within ±0.2 T, 0.1 T to ±1 T, 0.5 T beyond; ramp 0.01 T/s and average ≥3 s at stable points. Repeat three loops without rotating; verify major-loop closure and rate dependence on pilots. Record flux jumps and temperature excursions; unstable sections are not silently interpolated.

For a rectangular coupon, let a ≤ b be **full** transverse dimensions in cm, not half-widths. With ΔM = (mdown−mup)/V in emu/cm³:

**Jc = 20ΔM/[a(1−a/(3b))] A/cm².**

Use envelope volume consistently; do not multiply Bean Jc by (1−N). Report applied-field Jc, geometry and model limitations in porous granular material. Zero-applied-field loop width is the operational magnetic “self-field” endpoint, not a virgin zero-field transport result. Propagate dimensional, moment, repeatability and model uncertainties.

For the proposed coupon, V =0.004 cm³ and a = b =0.2 cm: 373 kA/cm² requires Δm ≈9.95 emu; 10 kA/cm² requires ≈0.267 emu. Verify sufficient moment range before testing; downsize all comparison coupons consistently if necessary.

#### Related Knowledge

- Independent zero-field cooling at each measurement temperature, complete bipolar loops and repeated acquisition support magnetic Jc comparisons. Loop closure, rate dependence, flux jumps and temperature excursions must remain visible in the record. **Source:** B015.
- The rectangular-coupon Bean calculation uses full transverse dimensions and envelope-volume-normalized loop width. Demagnetization correction must not be multiplied into Bean Jc, and porous granular model limitations and uncertainties must be reported. **Source:** B016, B017, B018.
- The source's target-derived moment requirements establish an instrument-range qualification check, not measured sample moments. Any necessary coupon downsizing must be applied consistently across comparison arms. **Source:** B019.

### Experimental Task 6: Disposition failed specimens and qualify any optional direct-transport extension

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Disposition, feedback and optional transport
Hold specimens with unknown identity, severe cracks, substantial secondary phases or unresolved compositional segregation. Record them as fabrication outcomes; do not erase failed arms. Low density, broad Tc or low Jc trigger checks of carbonate removal, Tp calibration, reformation cooling, oxygen flow, cracks, holder motion and magnetic history. Repeat the measurement first; reprocessing creates a distinct child ID. Any schedule change requires pilots and a complete matched matrix, retaining original data.

Optional direct transport uses separate matched coupons and cannot replace magnetic Jc. Proposed electric-field criterion: 1 μV/cm, four-terminal measurement with measured tap separation L and cross-section A. For L =2 mm, Vcriterion =0.2 μV. A 1 ×0.2 mm cross-section gives A =0.002 cm² and would require 746 A at the supplied self-field target, and >20 A at 5 T. These are derived requirements, not achievable instrument specifications. Do not energize until contacts, cooling, voltage resolution, current limits and quench protection are verified; any smaller geometry needs machining validation. Record actual geometry, field orientation, temperature and heating checks.

#### Related Knowledge

- Unknown identity, severe cracks, substantial secondary phases or unresolved segregation require a hold while remaining recorded as fabrication outcomes. Repeat measurement precedes reprocessing, and changed schedules require pilots and a complete matched matrix. **Source:** B020.
- Optional direct transport uses separate matched coupons and cannot replace magnetic Jc. The stated current and voltage requirements are derived from geometry and reference targets, not verified instrument capabilities; energization remains conditional on the listed protections and qualifications. **Source:** B021.
