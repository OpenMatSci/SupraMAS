# Experimental Plan: YBCO–BHO coated-tape fabrication and pinning protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Experimental overview

The objective is to fabricate epitaxial YxBa2Cu3Oy films containing nominal 3 vol.% self-assembled BHO nanorods on qualified IBAD-MgO buffered metallic tape, compare x = 0.90 with 1.00, and investigate composition–temperature conditions targeting Jc(20 K, 9 T, B∥c) > 0.4 MA cm−2. Supplied enhancement and morphology values are reference targets to investigate, not measured outcomes or guaranteed performance.

#### Stage 1 — Preparation and film fabrication
Use one qualified tape-stack lot and composite targets, retaining the matrix Ba:Cu ratio and accounting separately for BHO. Establish target assays, complete buffer/cap architecture, thermal compatibility, heater calibration and equipment readiness before release. The core comparison retains 870 °C; proposed 850 and 900 °C conditions require prior qualification. Plan three independent depositions per condition, randomized within tape-lot blocks. Keep BHO loading, thickness, oxygenation and other qualified deposition settings fixed. Grow films by KrF PLD and complete qualified oxygenation and cooldown.

#### Stage 2 — Structural and transport characterization
Use XRD, thickness measurement and surface inspection before UV-laser microbridge fabrication. Allocate at least three bridges per film and preserve neighboring regions for cross-sectional and plan-view FIB/STEM. Quantify BHO morphology without forcing agreement with supplied reference values. Measure four-probe DC Jc–B and Jc–θ in a verified PPMS setup, using measured film geometry and the unchanged 1 μV cm−1 criterion. Bridges remain technical replicates.

#### Stage 3 — Consolidation and qualified feedback
Evaluate independent-film results, uncertainty, exclusions and failures. Apply BOXVIA Gaussian-process/Expected Improvement optimization only to composition and temperature within an approved domain. Confirm a selected condition with independent films and fresh controls. Anticipated outputs are traceable fabrication records, structural and transport datasets, and a qualified comparison—not certification of an optimum or mechanism. Laser, powder, solvent, oxygen, furnace, press, magnet, cryogenic and electrical safety prerequisites apply throughout.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Qualify resources, define the comparison matrix, and release composite targets

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Objective and experimental design
Produce epitaxial YxBa2Cu3Oy containing nominal 3 vol.% self-assembled BHO nanorods on IBAD-MgO buffered metallic tapes. Compare x = 0.90 with 1.00 and optimize composition and growth temperature for **Jc(20 K, 9 T, B∥c) > 0.4 MA cm−2**. The supplied ~40% enhancement and morphology values are observations to investigate, not guaranteed outcomes.

Use one qualified tape-stack lot, fixed BHO loading, thickness, oxygenation and non-BO deposition settings. Do not introduce separate BHO layers or substitute substrates.

| Core matrix | Independent depositions | Transport allocation |
|---|---:|---|
| x = 0.90 and 1.00, each at 870 °C | 3 per arm | ≥3 bridges per film |
| Both compositions, each at 850 and 900 °C | 3 per condition | ≥3 bridges per film |
| Subsequent BO candidates | Sequential; repeat selected candidate in 3 independent runs | Same allocation |

The off-reference temperatures, replication and BO limits are proposed choices requiring qualification. If the stack cannot tolerate them, retain the 870 °C comparison and approve a narrower temperature domain before optimization. Randomize deposition order within tape-lot blocks. Bridges are technical replicates, not independent films.

Identifiers: `L{lot}_X{0.90/1.00}_T{C}_R{run}`; append `-B01` for bridges, `-XS01`/`-PV01` for STEM specimens. Reserve neighboring film regions for transport, thickness measurement and destructive microscopy; record their coordinates.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Materials: qualified IBAD-MgO buffered metallic tape, composite PLD targets for each composition, high-purity O2, compatible cleaning solvents, electrical contact materials and FIB protection materials. For target manufacture: Y2O3, BaCO3, CuO and HfO2, ethanol, zirconia milling media and optional PVA binder. Do not use arbitrary bulk precursor quantities.

Equipment: interlocked 248 nm KrF PLD with calibrated heater, oxygen-pressure control and target rotation; UV laser marker; XRD; profilometer/SEM; FIB and STEM with HAADF/EDS; PPMS with verified 20 K and 9 T capability, DC current source, nanovoltmeter and angular holder; BOXVIA or its verified implementation. RHEED is useful only if operable at the selected oxygen pressure.

Before release, verify the complete tape buffer/cap architecture and thermal compatibility in oxygen at the highest proposed temperature. Do not assume a bare 7–10 nm MgO layer provides a complete diffusion barrier. Use supplied qualified buffered tape rather than the draft's unverified IBAD recipe. Confirm heater-to-film temperature calibration, oxygen hardware pressure ratings, current-path limits and nanovolt resolution.

##### 3. Consolidated operating settings
Except for composition, BHO loading, 870 °C reference and test objective, numerical fabrication settings below are **provisional draft-derived choices requiring calibration**. Listed tolerances are proposed acceptance limits, not established process performance.

| Parameter | Starting setting / acceptance |
|---|---|
| Matrix composition | x = 0.90 or 1.00; proposed analytical uncertainty ≤0.01 |
| BHO | Nominal 3 vol.%; assay uncertainty to be established |
| Film temperature | Assigned value; calibrated uncertainty/stability within ±5 °C |
| PLD base pressure | <5 × 10−6 Torr |
| O2 during deposition | 200 ±5 mTorr = 26.7 ±0.7 Pa |
| Fluence, repetition, distance | 2.0 J cm−2, 5 Hz, 50 mm; qualify uniformity and log actual values |
| Target conditioning | 500 pulses with shutter closed |
| Film thickness | 150 nm starting target; proposed ±5%; determine actual rate |
| Oxygenation | 500 °C, 30 min, 1 atm O2, only in pressure-rated equipment |
| Cooling | ≤5 °C min−1; maintain oxygen through cooldown |
| Bridge / tap spacing | 50 μm nominal width / 500 μm nominal spacing; measure actual values |
| Transport | 20.0 ±0.1 K; 0–9 T; B∥c alignment within ±1° |
| Criterion | Ec = 1 μV cm−1; Vc = EcL |

Hold qualified settings fixed throughout the core matrix. Pressure or oxygenation trials belong to preliminary calibration, not to the two-factor inference dataset.

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
###### A. Composite targets
1. Obtain qualified targets or prepare both compositions using one common route. Write matrix composition as **YxBa2Cu3Oy**, not Y1−x. Account separately for Ba consumed by BHO.
2. Calculate the BHO mass fraction from phase densities: wBHO = 0.03ρBHO/[0.03ρBHO + 0.97ρmatrix]. Verify densities before weighing; 3 vol.% is not 5 mol.%.
3. A provisional manufacture route is ethanol milling for 12 h at 200 rpm with 10:1 media:powder ratio; dry safely, calcine at 850 °C for 6 h in O2, then regrind and repeat until carbonate decomposition is verified. If binder is used, validate burnout before sintering. Press within tooling ratings; 400 MPa uniaxial plus 200 MPa isostatic pressing and 950 °C/12 h O2 sintering are draft-derived starting trials, not released recipes.
4. Check density, cracks, XRD phases and bulk Y/Ba/Cu/Hf ratios. A proposed ≥95% theoretical target density is a spallation-control gate. Map Hf homogeneity. Do not require nanorods in the ceramic target: rods must self-assemble during film growth.

**Hand-off:** target ID, formulation, assays, density and qualified firing record to the PLD operator. Quarantine cracked, segregated or off-composition targets.

#### Related Knowledge

- The comparison isolates matrix Y content and growth temperature while holding the tape lot, BHO loading and other qualified fabrication settings fixed. Independent depositions, rather than bridges, provide film-level replication. **Source:** B000, B001, B002, B003.
- Release depends on verification of the complete buffer/cap architecture, oxygen thermal compatibility, temperature calibration and measurement hardware. Draft-derived operating values and tolerances remain provisional until qualified. **Source:** B005, B006, B007, B008, B009, B010.
- Composite-target formulation distinguishes nominal BHO volume fraction from mass or mole fraction and accounts separately for Ba consumed by BHO. Target assays and integrity checks support release; nanorods are not required in the ceramic target. **Source:** B011, B012.

### Experimental Task 2: Qualify tape surfaces, deposit assigned films, and complete oxygenation

#### Specific Content

##### B. Tape qualification and growth
1. Document substrate alloy and every buffer/cap layer. Inspect for delamination and contamination; obtain template XRD texture and surface roughness baselines. Draft Δω <5° and Δφ <10° are provisional screening limits.
2. Clean only by a supplier-approved process. Avoid automatic ultrasonication or UV-ozone exposure that may damage the cap. Dry and mount using a vacuum/high-temperature-compatible fixture; do not assume silver paste is suitable at 870 °C.
3. Pump down; heat in the qualified oxygen atmosphere, stabilize and record actual film-temperature calibration. Condition the rotating target with the shutter closed.
4. Deposit the assigned composition at the assigned temperature. Determine pulse count from measured rate on representative coupons. The draft's 15,000 pulses equals 150 nm only if the rate is verified as 0.010 nm/pulse; at 5 Hz this takes 50 min.
5. Record plume, pressure, laser energy and any RHEED signal. A spotty RHEED pattern is a diagnostic flag, not an automatic rejection of a nanocomposite.
6. Cool at ≤5 °C/min to 500 °C; perform qualified oxygenation, then cool in oxygen. If the PLD chamber cannot accept 1 atm O2, transfer using a validated furnace route with documented atmosphere and delay. Do not return automatically to 200 mTorr during cooldown.

**Hand-off:** intact film, growth log and region map to structural inspection. Reject delaminated films; retain failed samples for diagnosis.

#### Related Knowledge

- Supplier-approved cleaning and compatible mounting preserve the qualified cap and tape stack. Template texture and roughness baselines document the starting substrate condition. **Source:** B013.
- Deposition pulse count follows the measured growth rate, not an assumed pulse-to-thickness conversion. Temperature, pressure, laser energy and plume records preserve traceability of each assigned growth condition. **Source:** B013.
- Oxygenation and cooldown require qualified atmosphere handling and pressure-rated equipment or a validated furnace transfer. Delaminated films are rejected from hand-off but retained for diagnosis. **Source:** B013, B014.

### Experimental Task 3: Screen film structure and fabricate isolated transport microbridges

#### Specific Content

##### C. Structural screening and bridge fabrication
1. Measure film thickness at ≥5 representative positions and inspect cracks, droplets and porosity. XRD: θ–2θ for YBCO (00l) orientation, rocking curve of a resolved reflection such as (005), and φ scan of an accessible off-axis reflection such as (103). Verify indexing and peak overlap. MgO (002) can assess out-of-plane texture; in-plane texture requires an off-axis reflection, not an azimuthal scan of (002).
2. Compare Δω and Δφ across matched arms with repeatability uncertainties. Do not infer residual stress solely from lattice contraction; oxygen content and composition also shift peaks.
3. Pattern ≥3 microbridges by UV laser marking on each film. Calibrate laser dose on sacrificial regions to remove the surrounding superconducting film without conductive redeposition or damage extending into the active width.
4. Place current contacts on enlarged end pads and voltage contacts inside them. Measure width, thickness and voltage-tap spacing; target <5% width variation. Verify bridge isolation and absence of a significant metallic shunt through buffers or contacts. Cure contacts only by a qualified treatment.

**Hand-off:** geometry, microscopy, isolation/contact checks and wiring diagram to transport; protected neighboring regions to FIB/STEM.

#### Related Knowledge

- Representative thickness measurements and correctly indexed XRD scans support matched structural comparisons. In-plane texture requires an accessible off-axis reflection, and lattice contraction alone does not establish residual stress. **Source:** B015.
- UV-laser dose qualification, measured bridge geometry and isolation checks address patterning damage, conductive redeposition and metallic shunts before transport interpretation. **Source:** B015.
- Geometry, contact checks and wiring accompany the transport hand-off, while protected neighboring regions preserve the film-level link to destructive microscopy. **Source:** B016.

### Experimental Task 4: Quantify BHO nanorod morphology by cross-sectional and plan-view STEM

#### Specific Content

##### 5. STEM quantification and consistency checks
Prepare cross-sectional and plan-view specimens with protective coating, progressive FIB thinning and low-energy final cleaning. Qualify specimen thickness and electron dose rather than adopting an unsupported universal dose ceiling. Start near 200 kV if suitable for the instrument and sample.

Acquire calibrated, randomly selected fields from ≥3 separated locations per film; aim for ≥10 usable fields and ≥100 resolved rods. Record sampled area and foil thickness. Use HAADF plus Hf mapping to distinguish BHO from pores. Quantify diameter distributions, plan-view number density, continuity, interfaces and grain-boundary porosity. Determine tilt relative to the crystallographic c-axis, using orthogonal sections or tilt-series verification to avoid treating projected tilt as true tilt.

Investigate supplied 5–10 nm diameters, densities 2.1 ×10³ versus 3.5 ×10³ μm−2 and optimized 9–11° tilt without forcing agreement. Derived Bφ = nΦ0 gives approximately **4.34 and 7.24 T**, respectively, not 0.8 T. Continuous circular 5 nm rods at 3.5 ×10³ μm−2 occupy at least 6.9% area; 3% implies about 3.3 nm diameter. Resolve actual phase fraction, segmentation and rod continuity before accepting all supplied values together.

#### Related Knowledge

- Protective preparation, progressive thinning and qualified specimen thickness and electron dose support interpretable STEM specimens without imposing an unsupported universal dose limit. **Source:** B017.
- Randomly selected fields, documented sampled area and foil thickness, and HAADF with Hf mapping support rod identification and morphology quantification. Tilt requires crystallographic reference and verification beyond a single projection. **Source:** B018.
- Supplied diameter, density and tilt values are comparison references. The stated matching-field and occupied-area checks require resolving actual phase fraction, segmentation and continuity before accepting those values together. **Source:** B019.

### Experimental Task 5: Measure four-probe field and angular transport and normalize critical current

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Four-probe transport
1. Mount and thermally anchor the bridge; verify current contacts under load. A room-temperature resistance <1 Ω alone does not guarantee acceptable cryogenic heating. Establish safe current, compliance voltage and dissipated-power limits experimentally.
2. At low sensing current, record R(T) through the superconducting transition as an oxygenation diagnostic. Stabilize at 20 K for ≥10 min within ±0.1 K.
3. Define θ = 0° as B∥c. For angular measurements rotate in a plane perpendicular to bridge current so B remains transverse to current. Verify zero angle against film orientation.
4. Acquire bipolar DC I–V sweeps at B = 0–9 T in proposed 0.5 T increments. Use instrument-approved field ramp rates; wait ≥2 min and verify stability. Begin with small current steps, adapt near the criterion, and compare slower sweeps for heating/hysteresis. Remove thermoelectric offsets using current reversal. Stop on thermal drift or unsafe dissipation.
5. Measure Jc–θ at 20 K, 9 T over 0–90° in proposed 5° increments, with 1° increments around angular features and selected negative angles near c-axis to test asymmetry.
6. For w = 50 μm and d = 150 nm, area = 7.5 ×10−8 cm²; the objective requires Ic >0.030 A. At 75 μm width it requires >0.045 A. Higher self-field currents require separately verified headroom; never interpret source compliance as Ic.
7. At L = 500 μm, Vc = **50 nV**; the draft's 300–600 μm spacing corresponds to 30–60 nV. Demonstrate approximately ≤5 nV effective uncertainty for the proposed geometry. If unavailable, qualify longer tap spacing without changing Ec; report unresolved measurements rather than changing the criterion silently.

Calculate Jc = Ic/(wd) using measured nanocomposite film cross-section, excluding metal tape, buffers and contact layers. Report geometry uncertainty, individual bridges and independent-film statistics. A target claim requires uncertainty-supported Jc above 0.4 MA cm−2. Report current-limited results as censored. Engineering current density requires the entire tape cross-section and is a separate quantity.

#### Related Knowledge

- Thermal anchoring, contact checks under load, safe dissipation limits, bipolar sweeps and stability checks distinguish transport behavior from heating, offsets and current-source limitations. **Source:** B020.
- The electric-field criterion fixes the voltage threshold through measured tap spacing. Nanovolt uncertainty must be demonstrated; unresolved resolution cannot be remedied by silently changing the criterion. **Source:** B020.
- Jc uses the measured nanocomposite film cross-section, excluding tape, buffers and contacts. Individual bridges, independent-film statistics, geometry uncertainty and censored current-limited results remain separately reported. **Source:** B021.

### Experimental Task 6: Disposition failures, run qualified Bayesian feedback, and archive evidence

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Quality disposition, feedback and records
Cracks/delamination: review stack compatibility and thermal gradients. Poor texture/secondary phases: verify calibration, target chemistry and cap condition. Low Jc with broad transition: review oxygenation; normal transition with low Jc: inspect rod morphology, porosity, bridge damage and shunts. Sweep-rate-dependent Ic: correct heating/offsets before retesting. Any corrective process change requires a new matched x = 0.90/1.00 control block.

Fit BOXVIA GP/EI to independently measured Jc(20 K,9 T,B∥c), using x and temperature only. Proposed domain: x = 0.90–1.00, 850–900 °C after qualification; use only manufacturable, assayed compositions. Log kernel, noise model, acquisition settings, random seed and candidate approval. Exclude invalid transport from objective fitting but retain failure records. Proposed stopping rule: budget of 10 sequential candidates or <1% improvement across five valid iterations relative to measurement uncertainty. Confirm the selected condition in three independent films alongside fresh reference controls; do not claim a global optimum.

Archive raw I–V, R(T), temperature/field/angle histories, geometry, calibration certificates, growth/oxygenation logs, target and tape lots, XRD/STEM originals, segmentation settings, exclusions and analysis code. Relate morphology to transport at film level without claiming correlation proves mechanism.

Use laser interlocks, enclosed UV marking and particulate extraction; control toxic Ba-containing powders, flammable solvents, oxygen enrichment and furnace/press hazards. Apply magnet exclusion zones, cryogenic ventilation and electrically protected current wiring.

**Optional extensions:** higher-field irreversibility measurements require verified field capability and a predefined criterion; direct residual-stress measurements may test the stress hypothesis. Neither replaces the core composition controls, STEM or 20 K/9 T transport.

#### Related Knowledge

- Diagnostic review follows the observed failure category, and a corrective fabrication change requires a new matched composition-control block rather than retrospective pooling with unchanged conditions. **Source:** B022.
- BOXVIA uses independently measured transport objectives and only composition and temperature as inputs. Domain approval, candidate traceability, retained failure records and confirmation against fresh controls limit interpretation of the selected condition. **Source:** B023.
- Archiving raw measurements, geometry, calibration, fabrication records and analysis preserves traceability. Film-level morphology–transport association does not establish a mechanism. **Source:** B024.
- The listed laboratory safety controls apply throughout execution. Higher-field and direct residual-stress studies are optional, require their stated prerequisites, and cannot replace the core comparisons or measurements. **Source:** B025, B026.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage 1 — Release materials and fabricate matched films

Use one qualified IBAD-MgO buffered metallic-tape lot and composite PLD targets for YxBa2Cu3Oy, x = 0.90 and 1.00, with nominal 3 vol.% BHO. Preserve matrix Ba:Cu = 2:3, account separately for Ba in BHO, and verify formulation, densities, assays and target integrity. Obtain qualified targets or qualify the common provisional manufacture route; quarantine cracked, segregated or off-composition targets. Rods must self-assemble during film growth, not be required in the target. Do not substitute substrates or introduce separate BHO layers.

Before release, document every buffer/cap layer and verify oxygen thermal compatibility, heater-to-film calibration, pressure ratings and transport capability. Apply laser interlocks, enclosed UV marking and particulate extraction; control Ba-containing powders, solvents, oxygen enrichment, furnace/press hazards, magnetic exclusion, cryogenic ventilation and electrical wiring.

Retain the 870 °C comparison. The proposed core matrix adds 850 and 900 °C, with three independent depositions per condition; qualify these temperatures and the replication plan before execution. If the stack cannot tolerate the expanded domain, retain 870 °C and approve a narrower domain. Randomize runs within tape-lot blocks, use prescribed identifiers and reserve mapped neighboring regions for transport, thickness and microscopy.

Except for composition, nominal loading and the required reference temperature, fabrication settings remain provisional until calibrated. Starting settings include 200 ±5 mTorr oxygen, 2.0 J cm−2, 5 Hz, 50 mm distance, 500 conditioning pulses and a 150 nm thickness target. Hold released settings fixed; pressure and oxygenation calibration trials are outside the two-factor dataset. Use supplier-approved cleaning and compatible mounting. Determine pulse count from measured rate, log actual growth conditions, and complete qualified 500 °C/30 min oxygenation at 1 atm only in rated equipment, or through validated furnace transfer. Maintain oxygen during cooldown at ≤5 °C min−1. Reject delaminated films while retaining diagnostic records.

### Stage 2 — Establish structure and measure transport

Inspect thickness at at least five positions, surface defects and correctly indexed XRD orientation/texture. Compare matched arms with repeatability uncertainties; neither lattice contraction nor a spotty RHEED pattern alone establishes rejection or a stress conclusion. Qualify UV-laser marking on sacrificial regions, then fabricate at least three bridges per film. Measure width, thickness and tap spacing, verify isolation and shunt absence, and use qualified contacts. Bridges are technical replicates.

Prepare protected cross-sectional and plan-view STEM specimens with qualified thickness and dose. Sample at least three separated locations per film, aiming for at least ten usable fields and 100 resolved rods. Record area and foil thickness; use HAADF/Hf mapping to quantify diameter, density, continuity, interfaces, porosity and crystallographically referenced tilt. Treat supplied morphology values as reference targets. Resolve phase fraction and segmentation inconsistencies before accepting them together; the supplied densities correspond to approximately 4.34 and 7.24 T matching fields, not 0.8 T.

Verify current-path headroom, contact behavior, safe power and nanovolt resolution. Record low-current R(T), stabilize at 20 K, and acquire bipolar DC Jc–B through 9 T with B∥c and transverse-current angular measurements. Proposed field/angular increments remain explicit choices; check heating, hysteresis, offsets and stability, stopping on unsafe dissipation or thermal drift.

Use Ec = 1 μV cm−1 and Vc = EcL. A 500 μm spacing gives 50 nV and requires approximately ≤5 nV effective uncertainty; otherwise qualify longer spacing without changing Ec or report unresolved measurements. Normalize Ic by measured nanocomposite film width and thickness only. Report geometry uncertainty, individual bridges, independent-film statistics and censored current-limited results. The >0.4 MA cm−2 objective and supplied enhancement are reference targets, not outcomes.

### Stage 3 — Disposition, optimize and retain records

Investigate failures using the prescribed diagnostic categories. Any corrective process change requires fresh matched composition controls. Fit BOXVIA GP/Expected Improvement only to valid independently measured Jc(20 K, 9 T, B∥c), varying assayed, manufacturable x and temperature within the qualified proposed x = 0.90–1.00, 850–900 °C domain. Retain excluded failures and log model, acquisition and approval settings. Apply the proposed ten-candidate or uncertainty-relative stopping rule; confirm the selected condition in three independent films alongside fresh controls without claiming a global optimum.

Archive raw transport, structural data, geometry, calibrations, growth/oxygenation histories, lots, exclusions and analysis code. Relate morphology and transport at film level without claiming mechanism. Optional higher-field or direct stress measurements require their stated prerequisites and do not replace core characterization.
