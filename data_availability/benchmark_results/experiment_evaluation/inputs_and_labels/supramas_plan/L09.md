# Experimental Plan: Oxygen-conditioning of LMO-buffered YBCO tape

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Qualify and execute the controlled fabrication
The objective is to test whether oxygen conditioning of the distinct LMO buffer contracts its lattice while retaining texture, improves subsequent YBCO orientation, and increases current capacity. Preserve Hastelloy/Al₂O₃/Y₂O₃/IBAD-MgO/Epi-MgO/LMO/YBCO throughout. All processing remains proposed until the required qualifications are approved.

Compare an operational unannealed control with proposed oxygen treatments at 500, 560 and 620 °C, each for 1 or 2 h, across three independent fabrication blocks. Randomize matched segments from each qualified parent tape; retain adjacent witnesses without counting them as independent replicates. Prepare nominal 1 m, 12 mm-wide tapes per arm.

Use traceable materials, qualified roll-to-roll buffer tooling, DC-compatible reactive LMO sputtering, an oxygen furnace, and tape-compatible low-fluorine MOD equipment. Release instrument-specific recipes before study processing. Proposed settings are calibration starting points; unresolved thicknesses, MOD humidity, total pressure and flow are not operator defaults. Exclude the conflicting 800 °C LMO treatment.

### Stage II — Characterize at fabrication hand-offs
Measure exposed LMO before and after treatment using XRD, Raman and AFM, then assess YBCO orientation and thickness after common, locked MOD processing. These checks occur at the relevant fabrication hand-offs, not only after completing all tapes. Retain physically usable nonoptimal arms; document predefined fabrication failures. Common MOD processing may alter the LMO oxygen state and must be considered through witnesses.

### Stage III — Measure, compare and confirm
Map intact tapes by calibrated Mcorder induction at 77 K self-field before preparing companion four-terminal transport coupons. Measure field-dependent Jc and Fp over 30–77 K using separately verified equipment and measured dimensions. Anticipated outputs are traceable structural comparisons, current distributions, uncertainty-qualified block-paired changes, and documented failures. Approximately 20% improvement and 518/576 A are reference targets, not outcomes. Confirm the selected treatment independently before attempting 60 m scale confirmation.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage I — Establish comparison blocks and qualify materials, equipment and release prerequisites

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and experimental design
Test whether oxygen post-annealing of LMO contracts its lattice while retaining texture, improves subsequent YBCO orientation, and increases current capacity. Preserve the architecture: **Hastelloy/Al₂O₃/Y₂O₃/IBAD-MgO/Epi-MgO/LMO/YBCO**. LMO is a distinct buffer, not a superconducting layer or nanoparticle addition. All operations described here are proposed experiments.

The following matrix and tolerances are proposed choices requiring pilot calibration. The task supplies the annealing envelope, architecture, characterization and performance targets—not a complete manufacturing recipe.

| Arm | LMO treatment before YBCO |
|---|---|
| U | No intentional post-anneal; operational 0 h control |
| A500-1, A500-2 | Oxygen, 500 °C, 1 or 2 h |
| A560-1, A560-2 | Oxygen, 560 °C, 1 or 2 h |
| A620-1, A620-2 | Oxygen, 620 °C, 1 or 2 h |

Use three independent fabrication blocks B01–B03. Within each block, divide one qualified LMO-coated parent tape into randomized positions assigned to all seven arms. Produce a nominal 1 m, 12 mm-wide YBCO test tape per arm, plus adjacent witnesses. Identifier: `Bxx-arm-position-stage`, with stages L0 (as-deposited LMO), L1 (treated LMO), Y (YBCO), M (induction map), and T (transport coupon). Record parent-to-child relationships and consumed specimens. Witness locations are proposed at beginning, middle and end of each segment. Do not count witnesses as independent replicates.

After screening, repeat the selected treatment and U control in an independent confirmation block. Then attempt a 60 m selected-condition tape using the same locked process, retaining a contemporaneous 1 m U control. This is scale confirmation, not a replacement for the controlled comparison.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Use traceable Hastelloy tape, qualified buffer sources, a DC-compatible La–Mn sputtering source, high-purity Ar/O₂, low-fluorine YBCO precursor, compatible cleaning solvents and contact materials. Use the same material lots within blocks where practical. Verify La:Mn and Y:Ba:Cu composition; do not introduce unvalidated Mn or Cu excess. Document actual precursor fluorine content: a Ba-TFA formulation cannot simply be declared F/Ba = 1.8 without chemical verification.

Required equipment: roll-to-roll buffer-deposition line including IBAD and epitaxial MgO capability; DC reactive magnetron system; oxygen-compatible furnace with gas control; tape-compatible MOD coater and humidified-atmosphere furnace/exhaust; calibrated XRD, Raman and AFM; thickness metrology; Mcorder induction system at 77 K; and a **separate verified** variable-temperature, high-field four-terminal transport facility. Do not assume Mcorder contains a 14 T magnet or transport electronics.

Before experimental release, approve instrument-specific SOPs covering substrate cleaning, all buffer thicknesses, LMO thickness and DC-source conductivity, coating loading, MOD humidity/flow, current-contact fabrication and safe electrical limits. Missing values are prerequisites, not operator-selected defaults.

#### Related Knowledge

- The comparison preserves LMO as a distinct buffer and uses randomized arms within independent fabrication blocks; adjacent witnesses support characterization but do not provide independent replication. **Source:** B000, B002, B003.
- Material composition, precursor fluorine content and instrument-specific recipes require qualification before experimental release; missing settings cannot become operator-selected defaults. **Source:** B005, B007.
- Induction mapping and high-field four-terminal transport require distinct verified capabilities. Independent confirmation and scale confirmation supplement rather than replace the controlled comparison. **Source:** B004, B006.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage I — Calibrate processing and fabricate matched annealed-LMO/YBCO tapes with sequential hand-offs

#### Specific Content

##### 3. Consolidated settings
All entries marked P are proposed starting choices requiring calibration. Calibrated recipes must be signed off before processing study samples.

| Operation | Conditions and controls |
|---|---|
| Tape handling | 12 mm nominal width; measure actual width; maintain qualified tension and bend radius (prerequisite) |
| LMO sputtering, P | 600 °C ±5 °C; 20 mTorr ±5%; Ar:O₂ = 3:1; 200 W starting power; nominal 0.5 Å/s, verified by thickness witnesses |
| LMO oxygen anneal | Matrix above; P: 1 atm absolute O₂, ±5%; sample temperature ±5 °C; dwell ±2 min; ramp/cool 5/2 °C/min; gas flow calibrated for furnace exchange and uniformity |
| MOD starting profile, P | Dry 150 °C/2 min; pyrolyze 400 °C/30 min at 5 °C/min; crystallize 780 °C/1 h at 10 °C/min; oxygenate 450 °C/2 h in 1 atm O₂; temperature ±5 °C, dwell ±2 min |
| MOD atmosphere | P starting O₂ partial pressures: 100 mTorr pyrolysis, 150 mTorr crystallization, ±5%; total pressure, humidity and flow unresolved until validated |
| XRD, P | Cu Kα; 10–80° 2θ, 0.01° steps; exposure calibrated for film/substrate discrimination |
| Raman, P | 532 nm; 100–800 cm⁻¹ acquisition, emphasize 500–650 cm⁻¹; 10 × 10 s accumulations; establish nondamaging power by power series |
| AFM, P | Tapping mode, 5×5 µm², 512×512 pixels, 0.5 Hz; three fields per witness; calibrate tip, XY and Z |
| Transport, P | 30, 40, 50, 60, 77 K; stability ±0.1 K; B normal to tape, alignment within 1°; field steps 0.5 T through 5 T, then 1 T to verified maximum, preferably 14 T |

Do not apply the draft's 800 °C LMO post-anneal. MOD crystallization is a later, common processing step and may itself change LMO oxygen state; investigate this limitation using witnesses.

##### 4. Sequential operations and hand-offs
1. **Qualify the parent stack.** Clean Hastelloy under the approved SOP; omit acid etching unless compatibility is established. Deposit every buffer in the stated order using qualified continuous recipes. Confirm thickness, coverage, texture and absence of barrier failure. Insulating oxide targets require compatible RF/reactive tooling, not unverified conventional DC sputtering. Quarantine incomplete or delaminated stacks.
2. **Deposit LMO.** Stabilize pressure, temperature and gas ratio; precondition the source behind a shutter until deposition is stable. Verify that DC operation is non-arcing and yields the required composition and epitaxy. Calibrate line speed against actual thickness; deposition rate alone does not define thickness. Log tape position and tension. Hand L0 witnesses and the parent tape to characterization with chain-of-custody records.
3. **Assign and anneal.** Randomize matched segments. Archive L0 witnesses; expose each treated segment and L1 witness to its assigned cycle. Start dwell only when the sample-temperature surrogate reaches tolerance. Record actual temperature/pressure/flow traces and oxygen cooling history. Handle U identically except for intentional furnace treatment. Protect surfaces from contamination. Hand L1 witnesses to characterization; keep main tapes intact.
4. **Release to MOD.** Assess LMO phase, contraction, texture and roughness. Retain physically usable nonoptimal arms—they are needed for inference. Exclude only predefined fabrication failures, documenting reason and repeating the complete affected block where necessary.
5. **Fabricate YBCO.** Filter qualified precursor through a compatible 0.2 µm filter. Use calibrated tape coating, not the unrelated single-crystal spin-coating procedure. Freeze precursor concentration, wet loading, coating count, final thickness, humidity and thermal history across arms. Dry, pyrolyze, crystallize and oxygenate using the validated profile. Do not infer continuous layer-by-layer growth from the recipe. Inspect between coats for cracking and residual material. Hand Y tapes to structural inspection and then intact-tape induction mapping; reserve adjacent Y witnesses for thickness and transport.
6. **Map and section.** Perform Mcorder mapping before destructive processing. Subsequently prepare matched transport coupons using validated patterning that does not attack buffers or deoxygenate YBCO. Add contacts under a qualified oxygen-preserving process; do not use the draft's unvalidated 400 °C Ar contact anneal. Measure dimensions after processing.

#### Related Knowledge

- All P settings are proposed calibration starting points. MOD oxygen partial pressure does not resolve the separately pending total pressure, humidity or flow qualifications. **Source:** B008, B009.
- LMO oxygen conditioning precedes YBCO fabrication; the conflicting 800 °C treatment is excluded. Later common MOD crystallization may change the LMO oxygen state, a limitation addressed through witnesses. **Source:** B010, B011.
- Qualified stack integrity, stable non-arcing DC LMO deposition and actual thickness calibration precede randomized annealing. Witness assessment provides the release hand-off while preserving usable nonoptimal arms. **Source:** B011.
- Common calibrated tape MOD processing preserves comparability across arms. Intact-tape induction mapping precedes destructive coupon preparation, and contact fabrication must preserve oxygenation. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage II — Characterize exposed LMO and inspect YBCO structure at the required hand-offs

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Characterization and scientific checks
Measure L0 and L1 before they are buried. Fit multiple isolated LMO reflections, including symmetric and off-axis measurements where possible; distinguish strain from lattice stoichiometry effects. For cubic indexing, use dₕₖₗ = a/√(h²+k²+l²). With Cu Kα λ ≈ 1.5406 Å, LMO (110) moves approximately **32.27° → 32.87° 2θ** for a = 3.92 → 3.85 Å: contraction shifts peaks upward, not downward. Verify peak identity against buffer overlaps.

Measure LMO ω width on an accessible symmetric reflection and φ width on an accessible off-axis reflection; document indices, tilt and instrumental correction. Supplied Δφ ≈7° and Δω ≈2.5° are retained-texture targets, not <1° requirements. Establish allowable change from repeatability before screening.

For YBCO, fit (00l) and identifiable a-axis peaks with background/overlap corrections. YBCO (005) is approximately 38.5° for c = 11.68 Å. Calculate the (103) tilt from measured lattice parameters rather than automatically setting ψ =45°. Report texture and an orientation proxy; do not equate raw peak ratios with volume fractions without calibration.

Compute mismatch as 100(aLMO,in-plane − aYBCO,in-plane)/aYBCO,in-plane, identifying epitaxial directions. Rounded 3.92 and 3.85 Å imply 1.82%, not exactly 1.85%; equal rounded 3.85 Å values cannot establish 0.06%. That target corresponds to approximately 0.00231 Å difference, requiring adequate uncertainty and in-plane measurement.

For Raman, calibrate wavenumber and compare fitted Mn–O features under identical geometry/power. Neither Raman intensity ratios nor XRD broadening independently quantify δ or Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺. For AFM, report raw and consistently plane-leveled RMS; retain scratches and particles in primary statistics rather than masking failures.

#### Related Knowledge

- Multiple identified LMO reflections and suitable symmetric and off-axis measurements support lattice and texture comparisons. Contraction shifts diffraction peaks upward, while overlap and strain require consideration. **Source:** B012, B013.
- YBCO orientation assessment requires corrected peak fits and measured lattice parameters for tilt selection. Raw peak ratios are orientation proxies rather than calibrated volume fractions. **Source:** B014.
- Mismatch must use identified in-plane epitaxial directions and sufficient measurement precision; rounded lattice parameters cannot establish the supplied small-mismatch target. **Source:** B015.
- Consistent Raman geometry and nondamaging power support comparisons without independently quantifying oxygen deficiency or manganese valence. AFM primary statistics retain scratches and particles. **Source:** B016.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage III — Map intact-tape current and measure companion transport and pinning-force density

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Current measurements and calculations
**Induction:** calibrate Mcorder response using traceable/reference tapes and companion transport coupons. Document conversion method, spatial resolution, bath temperature, background and residual applied field. Map the full 1 m and 60 m lengths at 77 K self-field; report distributions, local minima and excluded end regions, not only the highest value.

**Transport:** measure bridge width, superconducting thickness and voltage-tap separation individually. The proposed 50 µm bridge with 1 mm tap separation has Vcriterion = (1 µV/cm)(0.1 cm) = **0.1 µV**. Verify offset/noise below this threshold; otherwise redesign tap geometry before testing. Jc = Ic/(wt), excluding substrate and stabilizer thickness.

At illustrative—not prescribed—t =120 nm, a 50 µm bridge carries 0.12 A at 2 MA/cm². A 12 mm tape at the same thickness carries only 28.8 A. Achieving 518–576 A would require approximately 36–40 MA/cm² at that thickness. Therefore discard the draft's thin-film thickness/performance pairing; calibrate actual tape thickness. Direct full-width transport requires capacity above 576 A, suitable contacts/leads and thermal protection; proposed headroom is 700 A, subject to facility verification. Induction itself does not require a 700 A source.

Thermalize for at least 15 min after temperature stabilization; after each field change wait at least 2 min and verify settling. Begin bridge ramps at proposed 10 mA/s, then validate heating independence. Acquire three sweeps with offset checks/current reversal, compliance and quench aborts. Repeatability within 5% is a proposed gate, not assigned uncertainty. Do not subtract an assumed lead resistance from four-terminal data.

Calculate Fp = JcB in N/m³ using Jc in A/m² and B in T, with current perpendicular to applied field. Report Bmax only if bracketed. Treat self-field Fp and unobserved maxima cautiously. Propagate dimensional, electrical, temperature, field and induction-calibration uncertainties separately.

#### Related Knowledge

- Calibrated induction mapping reports full-length distributions and local minima, with companion transport supporting the conversion rather than substituting a highest-value report. **Source:** B017.
- Transport normalization uses individually measured superconducting dimensions and voltage-tap separation. The proposed short tap geometry requires a verified noise floor, and thin-film thickness cannot be paired uncritically with full-tape current targets. **Source:** B018, B019.
- Thermal and field settling, heating checks, repeated sweeps and electrical aborts qualify transport measurements. Proposed repeatability is a gate, not an assigned uncertainty. **Source:** B020.
- Pinning-force density uses consistent Jc and field units; a peak field is reportable only when bracketed. Dimensional, electrical, environmental and induction-calibration uncertainty contributions remain separate. **Source:** B021.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage III — Resolve failures, compare paired results and control confirmation and optional follow-up

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Quality, feedback, safety and records
Cracks, delamination, contamination or barrier failure: quarantine; diagnose cleaning, tension and thermal stress. Missing contraction: verify temperature, oxygen delivery, starting state and diffraction assignment. Texture loss: inspect anneal uniformity and reduce treatment severity within the task envelope in a separately identified follow-up. Low Ic: check calibration, thickness, cracks, a-axis grains and common MOD processing before attributing it to LMO. Unstable transport: check contacts, offsets and heating; abort rather than increasing current blindly.

Freeze core processing before comparisons. Record all recipe deviations, raw traces/maps, calibration files, fitted peaks, dimensions, excluded specimens and dispositions. Compare block-paired Ic and Jc changes against U with uncertainty; seek the supplied approximately 20% improvement and 518/576 A targets without treating them as pass-guaranteed measurements.

Control oxygen fire/pressure hazards, sputter high voltage, furnace burns, solvent toxicity, fluoride-bearing MOD exhaust, cryogenic asphyxiation, magnetic projectile/quench risks and electrical heating under facility SOPs.

Optional task-linked extensions: matched inert-atmosphere annealing to separate thermal and oxygen effects; TEM of selected interfaces; calibrated valence spectroscopy; empirical pinning-force scaling only with justified Birr and observed field coverage. These do not replace U or prove a unique vacancy, stress or pinning mechanism.

#### Related Knowledge

- Failure diagnosis distinguishes fabrication defects, diffraction or oxygen-delivery problems, common MOD effects and unstable transport before attributing current changes to LMO treatment. **Source:** B022.
- Frozen core processing, complete deviation records and block-paired comparisons against the control support interpretation without treating supplied performance targets as measured or guaranteed results. **Source:** B023.
- Facility SOPs govern oxygen, high-voltage, thermal, chemical, cryogenic, magnetic and electrical hazards throughout the workflow. **Source:** B024.
- Inert-atmosphere comparisons, selected interface microscopy, calibrated valence spectroscopy and qualified empirical scaling are optional extensions; they neither replace the control nor establish a unique mechanism. **Source:** B025.
