# Experimental Plan: Bilayer REBCO coated-conductor protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Reconstructed experimental overview

#### Objective and comparison design
Prepare a direct-interface EuBCO(BHO-NR)/YBCO(YO-NP) bilayer on qualified 4 mm-wide IBAD-MgO/LaMnO3-buffered Hastelloy C-276 and assess complementary angular pinning and parallel layer-current addition. Compare the core bilayer with Eu-NR and Y-NP single-layer controls. Three independent fabrication blocks, alternating arm order, and undoped constituent references are proposed. Match cap/anneal histories and use sham thermal holds for omitted layers, recording unavoidable differences.

The supplied targets—Jc >10⁴ A/cm² at 77 K, 3 T, B∥c; A <0.55 at 65–77 K and nonzero fields ≤3 T; and irreversibility field near 9 T at 77 K—are reference objectives, not measured outcomes. The definition of A remains unresolved.

#### Materials and workflow
Use qualified targets and buffered tape, a LEAP-300C multi-target PLD system operating at 300 Hz, Ag sputtering, an oxygen furnace, thickness/XRD/morphology tools, STEM/HAADF/EDS, bridge patterning, and four-probe DC transport with automated rotation, Cernox thermometry and Hall tracking. Provide separate above-Tc capability for R(T).

Approve composition denominators and qualify deposition before growing the lower Eu-NR layer and upper Y-NP layer without a spacer or vacuum break. Apply the required 2 µm Ag cap and qualified ex-situ oxygen anneal. Perform structural, inclusion and superconducting checks before bridge transport. Analyze actual-geometry-normalized currents, angular response and high-field detection limits against the controls.

#### Constraints and anticipated outputs
Proposed compositions, thicknesses, temperatures, pressures, fluences and annealing settings remain calibration candidates. Retain layer/total thickness limits, in-plane Δφ <5°, sensitivity qualification, safety prerequisites and diagnostic holds. Outputs are traceable process records, structural observations, raw electrical data, uncertainty-qualified comparisons and documented failures; coupon results do not establish long-length or mechanical qualification.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Approve prerequisites, define comparison arms and prepare resources

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Objective and release prerequisites
Prepare EuBCO(BHO-NR)/YBCO(YO-NP) tapes and test complementary angular pinning and parallel layer-current addition. Assess the supplied objectives: **Jc >10⁴ A/cm² at 77 K, 3 T, B∥c; A <0.55 at 65–77 K, ≤3 T; irreversibility field near 9 T at 77 K**. These are not assumed results.

Before execution, approve composition denominators, buffered-tape qualification, bridge layout and calibrated 300 Hz PLD operation. Verify magnet/rotation capability, voltage sensitivity and current protection. Obtain the definition of A before claiming its threshold. All settings labeled proposed require calibration; release prerequisites are not assumed satisfied.

##### 2. Sample matrix and identifiers
Use adjacent sections of one qualified 4 mm-wide IBAD-MgO/LaMnO3-buffered Hastelloy C-276 lot. Propose three independent fabrication blocks and alternate arm order to expose drift.

| Arm | REBCO stack, bottom → top | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| B | Eu-NR 200 nm / Y-NP 150 nm | Core bilayer |
| E | Eu-NR 200 nm | Core correlated-pin control |
| Y | Y-NP 150 nm | Core particle-pin control |
| E0 | Undoped EuBCO 200 nm | Proposed composition reference |
| Y0 | Stoichiometric YBCO 150 nm | Proposed excess-Y reference |

Apply identical cap/anneal histories. Give single layers sham thermal holds corresponding to the omitted deposition; record unavoidable differences. Thicknesses are proposed starting choices.

ID format: `lot-block-arm-position-destination`; destinations `W` witness, `X` XRD/morphology, `M` microscopy, `T01/T02` bridges. Record longitudinal coordinates, travel direction and film normal. Retain adjacent intact material for damage diagnosis; choose coupon lengths from the approved contact layout.

##### 3. Materials, equipment and parameters
Materials: qualified buffered tape; EuBCO/BHO, excess-Y YBCO and undoped targets; Ag; high-purity O2/N2; oxygen-compatible carriers; lithography, milling and contact materials. Custom targets additionally require oxide/carbonate precursors, ethanol, milling media, presses and furnaces.

Provide separate above-Tc temperature capability for R(T).

### Experimental Task 2: Qualify targets and tape, deposit the bilayer, cap and oxygen-anneal

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Targets:** Prefer qualified commercial targets. For custom preparation, approve signed mass balances: Eu:Ba:Cu=1:2:3 plus BHO; Y:Ba:Cu=(1+x):2:3. Do not equate excess-Y percentage with the same percentage of added Y2O3. A possible BHO denominator is moles BHO per mole EuBCO, but approval is mandatory before weighing.
2. **Proposed custom-target calibration route:** ethanol milling 12 h; dry 80 °C/6 h; oxygen calcination 850 °C/6 h; regrind and repeat 850 °C/4 h; press; oxygen sinter 950 °C/12 h. Proposed heating/cooling rates: 5/2 °C/min. Qualify binder burnout, furnace chemistry and pressures below tooling ratings. These are candidates, not validated synthesis conditions. Inspect density, cracks, XRD phases and multi-location EDS. Intended BHO/YO phases are not impurities; target microstructure does not establish film nanostructure.
3. **Tape acceptance:** inspect surface/edges and archive certificates and texture measurements. Never polish an existing buffer; use supplier-qualified cleaning only. Quarantine contamination, cracks or Δφ≥5°. In-house buffer production needs a separately qualified complete stack, including necessary barriers/planarization. Hand accepted tape, targets and records to PLD.
4. **PLD qualification:** calibrate pressure, tape temperature, laser energy/spot area and thickness versus pulse exposure/tape speed at 300 Hz. Pre-ablate with shutter closed; proposed initial 500 pulses, extending until stable. No lower-frequency dwell time is transferable without calibration. Verify target-switch transients and witness epitaxy before matrix release.
5. **Growth:** deposit Eu-NR then Y-NP without breaking vacuum or adding a spacer. Shutter during target changes; stabilize temperature/pressure before reopening. Rotate/raster targets, inspect plume and particulate generation, and log interruptions. RHEED, if compatible with pressure/geometry, is diagnostic, not a fixed intensity gate.
6. **Cap hand-off:** cool under qualified oxygen conditions and transfer cleanly to sputtering with IDs intact. Calibrate rate and sputter 2 µm Ag. At a proposed starting rate of 0.5 nm/s, deposition requires **4000 s, approximately 67 min**; qualify sample heating, adhesion, gas pressure and power.
7. **Anneal hand-off:** load capped samples/witnesses on clean oxygen-compatible carriers. Purge until gas exchange is verified. Propose heating ≤5 °C/min to 300 °C, then 2 °C/min to 350 °C; hold 3 h at 200 sccm; cool ≤3 °C/min under O2 to room temperature. Log actual temperature/flow. Do not liquid-nitrogen quench. Inspect cracking, delamination and cap continuity; store dry and transfer to characterization.

#### Related Knowledge

- Qualified commercial targets are preferred. Custom targets require approved mass balances and qualification of the candidate preparation route; excess-Y percentage is not interchangeable with added Y2O3 percentage, and target microstructure does not establish film nanostructure. **Source:** B009.
- Tape acceptance preserves the existing qualified buffer. PLD release depends on calibrated pressure, actual tape temperature, laser fluence and exposure/thickness relationships at 300 Hz, including target-switch transients and witness epitaxy. **Source:** B009.
- The specified sequence deposits Eu-NR before Y-NP without a spacer or vacuum break, followed by clean transfer for Ag capping and a qualified flowing-oxygen anneal. Proposed sputtering and annealing settings remain candidates, with actual conditions and post-process damage recorded. **Source:** B008, B009.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize texture, inclusion morphology and superconducting transition

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural and superconducting gates
Before destructive sampling, inspect optically/SEM and measure thickness and XRD. Proposed acquisition: Cu Kα, λ=1.5406 Å; 10–90° 2θ, 0.02° steps; calibrate counting time. Use REBCO (00l) for orientation, (005) rocking curves for Δω, and off-axis (103) φ scans for Δφ. The **Δφ<5° requirement is in-plane**, not a Δω limit. For (005), d=c/5 and c=5λ/(2 sinθ); identify Ag/buffer peaks and Eu/Y overlap. Do not impose an unsupported c parameter. MgO (002) alone cannot determine in-plane texture; use a verified off-axis reflection, such as {111}. Verify LaMnO3 indexing separately.

Prepare adjacent cross-sectional lamellae with preserved orientation. Proposed final FIB polish: 2–5 kV, calibrated for damage. Acquire whole-stack HAADF, Eu/Y/Ba/Cu/Hf/O EDS and lattice FFTs in at least three separated regions per arm/block. Measure BHO rod length/inclination and YO size/aspect ratio/distribution; assess cracks and interface registry. Expected task morphology is approximately 40 nm rods and 10–30 nm tablet-like particles. Use plan-view/orthogonal sections to test randomness where projections are ambiguous. EDS alone does not establish crystal phase or oxygen stoichiometry.

Determine imaging/EDS dose limits on sacrificial regions. Log voltage, current, dwell, area and repeat exposure; blank between acquisitions and compare before/after images. Do not assume a fixed dose compatible with long-dwell mapping.

Measure low-current R(T) through Tc with verified non-heating excitation; proposed sweep 300→77 K, 0.5 K/min near transition. Report midpoint and 10–90% width, accounting for Ag shunting. Supplied Tc≈94 K is an objective; ΔTc<1 K is a proposed diagnostic goal, not an acceptance claim. Missing inclusions, poor epitaxy, cracks or depressed Tc trigger hold and diagnosis.

#### Related Knowledge

- XRD distinguishes orientation, rocking-curve width and in-plane texture: Δφ <5° is the in-plane requirement, not a Δω limit. Peak overlap and buffer indexing must be addressed without imposing an unsupported lattice parameter. **Source:** B010.
- Oriented cross-sectional HAADF, EDS and lattice FFT observations support assessment of the whole stack, inclusion dimensions and interface registry. Additional section orientations address ambiguous projections; EDS alone cannot establish crystal phase or oxygen stoichiometry. **Source:** B011.
- Sacrificial-region dose qualification limits microscopy-induced damage. Non-heating R(T), with Ag shunting accounted for, supplies the transition assessment; missing inclusions, poor epitaxy, cracks or depressed Tc trigger a hold and diagnosis rather than an assumed pass. **Source:** B012, B013.

### Experimental Task 4: Prepare isolated microbridges and measure angular DC transport

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Bridge preparation and transport
Transfer accepted T coupons and thickness maps to patterning. Define two 50 µm bridges where feasible. Isolate the path, including surrounding Ag; remove Ag from the voltage-gauge region while retaining contact pads. Verify width, tap spacing, substrate leakage, isolation and milling damage. Proposed width gate: 50±2 µm. Record actual dimensions and residual cap; validate shunt correction if Ag remains.

Nominal bilayer area=(0.005 cm)(3.5×10⁻⁵ cm)=**1.75×10⁻⁷ cm²**. Thus Jc=10⁴ A/cm² gives **Ic=1.75 mA**; E/Y give 1.00/0.75 mA. At the allowed 3.1 µm total thickness, the threshold is 15.5 mA. Nominal 4 mm tape gives 0.14 A at the same Jc, but bridge data cannot certify tape current.

At 1 mm tap spacing, Vc=EcL=**0.10 µV**. Qualify noise/baseline uncertainty below approximately 10 nV and current resolution well below 0.1 mA. A 1 mA-resolution source is inadequate near the target. Set current, voltage and power compliance using sacrificial ramps and thermal tests, not equipment maximum current.

Mount current along tape; rotate B perpendicular to current, maintaining B⊥J. Define θ=0° as B∥c and 90° as B∥ab. Calibrate Hall tracking against holder geometry and sample-mounted Cernox thermometry; diffraction alignment cannot replace magnetic-angle calibration.

Acquire bipolar DC I–V with offset cancellation, stepped ramps and return-to-zero checks. Stop on heating, unstable voltage or compliance; report censored Ic, not extrapolated values. Repeat selected curves for hysteresis.

Proposed grid: T=65,70,77,81 K; B=0,0.5,1,2,3,5,7,9,12,15 T; θ=0–180° every 5°, refining peaks to 1°. Complete all core arms at 65–77 K, ≤3 T first, then acquire the high-field surface. At zero field, measure once; angular anisotropy is undefined. Stabilize each setpoint and reverse selected angular scans.

#### Related Knowledge

- Bridge preparation must isolate the surrounding Ag and verify actual geometry, leakage and milling damage. Residual Ag requires validated shunt correction; nominal current calculations do not certify full-width tape current. **Source:** B014, B015.
- The selected electric-field criterion and tap separation determine the voltage criterion, requiring qualified nanovolt sensitivity and sufficiently fine current resolution. Compliance is established through sacrificial ramps and thermal tests, not equipment maximum current. **Source:** B016.
- Transport maintains B perpendicular to current with Hall-calibrated angle tracking and sample thermometry. Bipolar measurements, return-to-zero checks and stop rules address offsets and instability; core low-field comparisons precede the proposed high-field grid, and zero-field anisotropy is undefined. **Source:** B017, B018, B019.

### Experimental Task 5: Normalize results, diagnose failures and bound follow-up work

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Analysis, dispositions and feedback
Calculate Jc=Ic/(wtREBCO), excluding Ag/buffers; propagate geometry, electrical and alignment uncertainties. Predict bilayer sheet current K=tEJc,E+tYJc,Y and Ic=wK without angle-specific fitted weights. The supplied 0.535/1.05 coefficients require original reference geometry and must not be transferred automatically.

Report extrema, Jmax/Jmin and explicitly provisional spread (Jmax−Jmin)/Jmean. Do not call this A without its source definition. Predefine a high-field detection criterion; report irreversibility estimates with threshold, orientation and detection limit.

Archive IDs, mass balances, calibration records, process traces, dimensions, microscopy, raw I–V/R(T), uncertainties, exclusions and failed specimens. Preserve independent-block results.

Diagnose low Tc through oxygen history/contacts; abnormal angular response through Hall calibration, texture and inclusions; non-additivity through thickness, interface, current transfer and shunts; droplets through targets/plume; cracks through thermal gradients/handling. Quarantine fabrication failures, remake damaged bridges, and retain valid underperforming samples in analysis. Change one factor per follow-up block within task limits, retaining B/E/Y controls; do not automatically reanneal above 400 °C.

##### 8. Safety and bounded extensions
Enforce Class 4 laser interlocks, oxygen-compatible clean hardware, hot-surface guards, pressure-rated tooling, powder/solvent extraction, magnet exclusion/quench procedures, electrical compliance and cryogenic/milling PPE.

Optional extensions vary layer fraction/composition or map longitudinal uniformity without replacing core controls. Coupon performance does not establish long-length scalability. The supplied >50 GN/m³ load statement is not mechanical qualification: Fp=JB; at 3 T it requires J≈1.67×10⁶ A/cm², far above the minimum target. Dedicated endurance testing requires separate fixture and acceptance qualification.

#### Related Knowledge

- Jc uses measured bridge width and REBCO thickness, excluding Ag and buffers. Parallel-current prediction uses constituent thicknesses and Jc without angle-specific fitted weights; supplied coefficients cannot be transferred without their reference geometry. **Source:** B020.
- Angular extrema and provisional spread can be reported, but the spread is not A without its source definition. Irreversibility estimates require a predefined detection criterion, orientation and detection limit. **Source:** B021.
- Traceable archives preserve independent-block results, failures and exclusions. Diagnosis distinguishes fabrication failures and damaged bridges from valid underperforming samples; bounded follow-up retains B/E/Y controls and does not automatically reanneal above 400 °C. **Source:** B022, B023.
- Safety controls apply throughout execution. Optional composition, layer-fraction and uniformity work cannot replace core controls; coupon performance and the supplied load statement do not establish scalability or mechanical qualification. **Source:** B024, B025.

Acquire bipolar I–V with offset cancellation, return-to-zero checks and selected repeats. Stop for heating, instability or compliance and report censored Ic. Complete core arms at 65–77 K and ≤3 T before the proposed 65–81 K, through-15 T grid. Stabilize setpoints, reverse selected scans and measure zero field once.
