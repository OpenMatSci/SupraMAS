# Experimental Plan: EuBCO–BHO tape transverse-uniformity protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Stage 1 — Qualify resources and fabricate comparison tapes

The objective is to determine whether transverse thermal homogenization and mechanical mitigation reduce edge overheating, Cu segregation, Ni transfer, buffer decomposition and arched weak-link grains while retaining c-axis texture and aligned BHO columns. Preserve the EuBCO–BHO composite, complete C276/Al2O3/Y2O3/IBAD-MgO/LaMnO3/CeO2 stack, and reel-to-reel multi-plume multi-turn PLD with helical travel.

The proposed comparison comprises qualified routine operation, thermal homogenization only, mechanical mitigation only, and both. Use three independent runs per arm, blocked by buffered-tape lot and randomized within blocks. Keep target lot, BHO loading, buffer stack, oxygenation, nominal thickness and plume exposure fixed. Mechanical changes may alter temperature; retain measured profiles rather than assume physical independence.

Required resources include qualified buffered tape and composite targets, calibrated PLD temperature/pressure/tension monitoring, oxygenation equipment, mechanical slitting and dimensional metrology, four-probe cryogenic transport, micro-XRD, SEM-EDS, FIB-TEM/EDS, ICP-OES and VSM. Release depends on qualified materials, buffer-safe growth, instrument capability and safety controls. Numerical operating proposals require calibration and freezing before comparison runs; unresolved buffer and target-manufacturing recipes remain unresolved.

### Stage 2 — Measure transverse transport and registered structure

Sample stable early, middle and late positions, retain unslit witnesses, and register adjacent role coupons. Acquire optical and micro-XRD maps before destructive preparation. Profile the complete width using nominal 1 mm slit filaments, measured widths and kerfs, then correlate transport with registered structural, compositional and magnetic measurements. Distinguish film-normalized Jc from complete-conductor Je.

### Stage 3 — Apply gates and consolidate evidence

The reference acceptance target is Je ≥2.5×10^9 A m−2 at 77 K, self-field, throughout 10 mm, with ≤5% transverse variation—not a measured outcome. Anticipated outputs are coordinate-linked maps, uncertainty-qualified acceptance decisions, run-level comparisons, and archived failures and process records. Optional field/temperature extensions follow the core comparison.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage 1: Qualify materials, comparison design and operating settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Objective and comparison design
Preserve the EuBCO/BHO nanocomposite on **C276/Al2O3/Y2O3/IBAD-MgO/LaMnO3/CeO2**, fabricated by reel-to-reel multi-plume multi-turn PLD. Determine whether thermal homogenization and mechanical mitigation reduce edge overheating, Cu segregation, Ni transfer, buffer decomposition and arched weak-link grains while retaining c-axis texture and aligned BHO columns.

The acceptance target is **Je ≥2.5×10^9 A m−2 at 77 K, self-field, throughout the 10 mm width, with ≤5% transverse variation**. Supplied edge-recovery percentages are context, not promised outcomes.

| Arm | Thermal configuration | Mechanical configuration |
|---|---|---|
| A | Qualified routine profile | Qualified routine routing/cleaning |
| B | Transverse homogenization | Same as A |
| C | Same as A | Clean inter-lane gaps; reduce edge contact/torsion |
| D | Homogenization | Same mitigation as C |

This factorial design is proposed. Never deliberately accumulate residue, overheat tape or operate outside safe limits. If mechanical mitigation changes the thermal profile, retain measured profiles and analyze that mediation; do not describe the factors as physically independent.

Use three independent runs per arm, blocked by buffered-tape lot and randomized within blocks. Sample stable early, middle and late deposition positions; filaments are spatial subsamples, not independent runs. IDs: `lot-arm-run-position-role-F01…F10`, with roles `TR` transport, `MX` microstructure, `VS` magnetometry and `IC` digestion. Mark travel direction, left/right edges, transverse coordinate x=0–10 mm and longitudinal coordinate. Archive an unslit witness from each position.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Use qualified buffered 10 mm C276 tape, homogeneous EuBCO–BHO composite PLD targets, oxygen, compatible cleaning solvents, qualified Ag contact materials, FIB protective Pt and validated ICP digestion reagents/standards. Keep target lot, BHO loading, buffer stack, oxygenation, nominal thickness and plume exposure fixed across arms.

Required equipment: interlocked reel-to-reel MPMT-PLD with pressure logging, calibrated transverse pyrometry, tension/routing monitoring and target rastering; oxygenation equipment; precision mechanical slitter; optical dimensional metrology; four-probe cryogenic station and protected current supply; micro-XRD with 50 µm footprint; SEM-EDS; FIB and TEM/STEM-EDS; ICP-OES; VSM.

Before release, verify actual tape/layer thicknesses, intact buffers, texture, target composition/density, contact compatibility, source current compliance, cryostat temperature accuracy and VSM field capability. Use the qualified buffer manufacturing route: do not strip, repolish or reconstruct accepted stacks. Buffer deposition recipes and thicknesses are unresolved, not established by this proposal.

Use qualified purchased or in-house composite targets. If synthesizing, calculate Eu2O3:BaCO3:CuO as **0.5:2:3 mol**, then add BHO using measured phase densities. Do not use the draft gram quantities. Milling, calcination and sintering require a separate qualified recipe; target particles need not be aligned. Columns must form in the deposited composite.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage 1: Fabricate, oxygenate and register comparison coupons

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Receiving → fabrication:** inspect buffer witnesses by microscopy/XRD; record stack certification, thicknesses and transverse texture. Reject damaged or mismatched lots. Clean only by a buffer-compatible qualified procedure; omit the unqualified 800 °C pre-anneal.
2. Map heater/tape temperatures across the width and along each turn, including edges. Calibrate emissivity against a reference under representative atmosphere and coating conditions. Correlate transient hot spots with routing, tension and lane-gap contact. Three-point pyrometry alone cannot exclude narrow edge hot spots.
3. Record residue and routing before each run. For C/D, clean gaps and adjust alignment/contact/torsion within qualified tension and curvature limits. For B/D, tune heater zones or equivalent validated thermal controls. Keep average growth temperature and plume exposure matched; document any unavoidable differences.
4. Load composite target and tape; condition target with shutter closed. Confirm fluence, pressure, tape travel and stable plume. Deposit all arms without alternating distinct EuBCO/BHO layers. Log temperatures, pressure, laser pulses, speed, tension and interventions continuously.
5. On excursions beyond the qualified safety/decomposition window, shutter the laser and stop safely. Mark affected longitudinal intervals and segregate them; they are not valid stable-position samples.
6. Oxygenate all arms identically. Use atmospheric oxygen only in a chamber rated for it. If PLD cannot support this, use a validated oxygenated cooldown/transfer to a separate furnace; qualify the transfer before the comparison.
7. **Fabrication → metrology:** cool, label and measure thickness/width. Cut adjacent role coupons at registered positions. Acquire micro-XRD and optical maps before destructive operations. Preserve unslit witnesses and hand off a coordinate sheet with every coupon.

#### Related Knowledge

- Receiving inspection and buffer-compatible cleaning precede fabrication. Transverse and turn-resolved temperature mapping, representative emissivity calibration, and routing/tension records support the prescribed thermal and mechanical comparisons; three-point pyrometry cannot exclude narrow edge hot spots. **Source:** B012.
- Composite deposition uses matched average growth temperature and plume exposure without alternating separate EuBCO/BHO layers. Continuous logs and segregation of unsafe excursion intervals preserve traceability and distinguish stable-position samples from affected material. **Source:** B012.
- Identical oxygenation requires appropriately rated equipment or a qualified transfer to a separate furnace. Registered adjacent coupons, pre-destructive maps, coordinate sheets and unslit witnesses provide the hand-off basis for subsequent measurements. **Source:** B012.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage 2: Prepare slit filaments and measure transverse transport

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Transport profiling and consistency checks
Mechanically slit TR coupons across the entire width into approximately ten strips; record every kerf and actual surviving x interval. Inspect for burrs, cracks and delamination. Qualify slitting on sacrificial siblings; damaged filaments are invalid measurements, not evidence of growth degradation. Never omit an edge silently.

Apply width-compatible Ag contacts; pads must fit a 1 mm strip, not the draft 2 mm-wide pads. Validate curing without changing oxygenation. Establish four-wire contact resistance and current-transfer length; place current pads outside the voltage span. Confirm electrical isolation between filaments.

For a 5 mm tap span, **Vc=(1 µV cm−1)(0.5 cm)=0.5 µV**. Record actual tap spacing. At 77 K, measure compensated I–V curves, initially ramping at 0.1 A s−1. Use offsets/current reversal where safe, calibrated filtering, thermal monitoring and automatic voltage/current trip limits. Repeat three times with recovery; verify a second ramp rate on representative edge/center strips. Heating or contact-limited curves require repair/retest, not extrapolation.

Calculate:
- `Jc=Ic/(w·tfilm)` using superconducting-composite thickness.
- `Je=Ic/(w·ttotal)` using the complete conductor cross-section at measurement, including any continuous added metal.
- Target current: **Ic,target=2.5·w(mm)·ttotal(µm) A**.

Thus a 100 A source can reach the target only if total thickness is ≤40 µm for a 1 mm filament, or ≤4 µm for the full 10 mm tape. Verify thickness and source capability before testing. Upgrade the supply or use separately validated narrower diagnostic bridges if necessary; bridges do not replace the specified slit-filament core map. Source-limited Ic is a lower bound and cannot establish target compliance.

#### Related Knowledge

- Measured surviving intervals and kerfs establish transverse coverage. Slitting damage must be qualified and identified separately from growth degradation; strip-compatible contacts, validated curing, current-transfer checks and electrical isolation support valid four-probe measurements. **Source:** B013, B014.
- The electric-field criterion must use actual tap spacing. Compensated repeated sweeps, noise control, thermal monitoring and representative ramp-rate checks address measurement validity; heating- or contact-limited curves require repair and retesting rather than extrapolation. **Source:** B015.
- Jc uses superconducting-composite cross-section, whereas Je uses the complete conductor cross-section, including continuous added metal. Thickness and source compliance determine whether the reference target is testable; source-limited values are lower bounds, and diagnostic bridges do not replace the core filament map. **Source:** B016, B017.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage 2: Correlate transverse structure, composition and magnetometry

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Correlative characterization
**Micro-XRD:** map EuBCO (005) intensity and position; measure rocking curves and suitable verified off-axis reflections for in-plane texture. For (005), d005=c/5 and 2d sinθ=λ; establish the actual 2θ window from a reference and calibrated wavelength. Do not confuse intensity uniformity with complete texture equivalence. Normalize for exposure, footprint and thickness; keep edge footprints on tape or apply documented clipping corrections.

**SEM-EDS:** inspect matched edge/center regions and registered low-Ic sites. Quantify arched morphology and precipitate area density; map Eu, Ba, Cu, Hf and Ni, plus Ce/La/Mn in exposed cross-sections. Use reference standards and matrix-aware Ba/Cu ratios. Check dose dependence; nominal beam spot is not EDS spatial resolution.

**FIB-TEM/EDS:** lift out matched edges and center, plus defect sites. Apply protective Pt, reduce milling current progressively and finish at low voltage. Image interfaces, diffraction/orientation, arched grains and BHO columns. Report grain misorientation, cap/buffer continuity, segregation, column diameter, tilt and areal density with sampled area. Do not infer decomposition from surface EDS alone.

**ICP-OES:** digest registered edge and center sibling film fractions separately using a validated recovery procedure. Include reagent blanks, buffer/substrate controls, standards and spikes. Record mass, area, dilution and recovery. Mixed-layer digestion is not film-specific composition unless contamination is resolved.

**VSM:** use registered 2×2 mm unslit sibling pieces, field normal to tape (approximately parallel to c-axis). Subtract holder and qualified matched substrate/buffer backgrounds. For a rectangular specimen, use `Jc=20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)]`, with a≤b in cm, ΔM the full loop width in emu cm−3 normalized to superconducting volume, and Jc in A cm−2; multiply by 10^4 for A m−2. Report dimensions, sweep rate and thin-film/geometry limitations. Magnetic and transport Jc need not agree at different effective electric fields.

#### Related Knowledge

- Micro-XRD intensity, peak position and orientation measurements require reference-based peak windows and exposure, footprint and thickness normalization. Intensity uniformity alone does not establish complete texture equivalence. **Source:** B018.
- Registered SEM-EDS and FIB-TEM/EDS connect morphology and elemental distributions with interfaces, grain orientation and BHO-column measurements. Standards, dose checks and protected low-damage preparation are specified safeguards; surface EDS alone cannot establish decomposition. **Source:** B019, B020.
- ICP-OES requires recovery controls and resolved substrate/buffer contributions for film-specific interpretation. VSM requires matched background subtraction, superconducting-volume normalization and the specified rectangular Bean expression, with geometry limitations and possible transport–magnetic differences reported. **Source:** B021, B022.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage 3: Apply acceptance gates, diagnose failures and archive controlled feedback

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Gates, diagnosis, records and controlled feedback
Define proposed variation as **U=max|Je,i−mean(Je)|/mean(Je)×100%** across all valid filaments; also report range and edge/center ratios. Pass only if every transverse interval meets the Je target and U≤5%, with uncertainty sufficiently small to decide. Missing/damaged intervals or insufficient current capability make acceptance indeterminate. Report Jc maps separately and propagate width, thickness, Ic and tap-spacing uncertainties; do not assign fixed error bars.

If edge temperature/Ni rises, investigate gap contact and routing. If Cu-rich precipitates and arched grains co-localize with low Ic, inspect buffers and thermal histories. If degradation is uniform, check oxygenation, thickness, target/plume stability and contacts. Weak texture/column alignment requires qualification of growth conditions, not retrospective removal of unfavorable samples.

Archive raw I–V/M–H/XRD data, images/spectra, calibrations, coordinate maps, process logs, failed samples and analysis scripts. Compare arms using run-level replication and longitudinal blocks; document interactions. Change one qualified control at a time during development, then freeze settings and confirm with fresh independent runs.

Enforce laser enclosures/interlocks, oxygen-compatible hardware, pressure protection, hot-tape guarding, powder/solvent ventilation, cryogenic oxygen-deficiency controls, magnet exclusion zones, high-current shielding and validated acid-digestion/FIB procedures.

##### 8. Optional extensions
After completing the core comparison, measure selected edge/center filaments at 77 K and 0.5, 1, 2, 3 and 5 T, field normal within proposed ±1°, and optionally down to 4.2 K with verified equipment/current capacity. Calculate Fp=JcB in N m−3; do not impose Fp∝B−1/2 or infer a unique pinning mechanism from it. Dopant sweeps or nanocolumn-spacing studies require separate experiments and cannot replace the thermal/mechanical controls.

#### Related Knowledge

- Acceptance requires every transverse interval to meet the Je reference target and the proposed variation definition, with sufficient uncertainty resolution. Missing or damaged intervals and inadequate current capability make acceptance indeterminate rather than compliant. **Source:** B023.
- Diagnosis follows registered temperature, composition, morphology and transport evidence. Run-level comparisons retain longitudinal blocking and interactions; failed samples remain archived, and qualified development changes are frozen before confirmation with fresh independent runs. **Source:** B024, B025.
- The listed laser, oxygen, pressure, thermal, chemical, cryogenic, magnetic and high-current protections apply before execution. Field/temperature measurements are optional after the core comparison, while dopant or column-spacing studies require separate experiments. **Source:** B026, B027.
