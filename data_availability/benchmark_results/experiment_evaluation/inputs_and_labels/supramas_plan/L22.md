# Experimental Plan: EuBCO–BSHO coated-conductor protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Reconstructed experimental overview

#### Stage 1 — Qualify materials and fabricate matched films
The objective is to fabricate a single EuBCO–BSHO nanocomposite layer on verified Hastelloy-backed IBAD-MgO tape by reel-to-reel multi-plume multi-turn PLD, then compare column morphology, interface defects, texture and transport with undoped EuBCO. The co-doped arm contains 4.5 mol% BSO and 3.5 mol% BHO, subject to approval of the composition denominator. Three independent paired blocks are proposed, using adjacent template sections and matched thickness, growth conditions and oxygenation. Prepare and release qualified targets, verify the existing buffer/cap stack, calibrate moving-tape exposure, and deposit nominally 1 µm films at a demonstrated rate exceeding 100 nm/s. All P settings remain provisional until pilot qualification.

#### Stage 2 — Characterize structure and preserve specimen linkage
Use nondestructive inspection and calibrated XRD before cutting linked transport, cross-sectional TEM and plan-view TEM specimens. Combine lattice and texture measurements, qualified Williamson–Hall analysis, microscopy and calibrated STEM-EDS to assess column dimensions, density, alignment, interruptions and interfaces. Structural and morphology values are reference objectives, not measured outcomes or automatic rejection thresholds.

#### Stage 3 — Measure transport and consolidate comparisons
Use qualified four-probe bridges, current protection and rotatable cryogenic environments with 18 T and 25 T instruments to measure both arms over 4.2–77 K and through 24 T. Apply the same electric-field criterion and measured geometry throughout. Normalize transport to actual superconducting cross-section, calculate transverse pinning force consistently, and compare paired blocks with uncertainty. Anticipated outputs are traceable process records, structural datasets, raw current–voltage curves and paired comparisons—not certified performance. Required resources include powder-processing equipment, interlocked PLD, thickness metrology, XRD, electron microscopy and protected transport equipment; unresolved qualifications, failed specimens and optional follow-up remain explicitly documented.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Prepare and qualify matched EuBCO and co-doped targets

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and comparison matrix
Fabricate a **single EuBCO nanocomposite layer** containing BSHO nanocolumns by reel-to-reel multi-plume multi-turn PLD (MPMT-PLD). Test the relationship between column morphology, interface defects, texture and intermediate-/high-field transport. All numbers designated **P** below are proposed starting settings requiring pilot qualification; supplied structural and transport values are objectives, not acceptance-certified results.

| Arm | Target composition | Replication and identifiers |
|---|---|---|
| U | Undoped EuBCO | Three independent paired blocks: E01-U through E03-U |
| C | EuBCO + 4.5 mol% BSO + 3.5 mol% BHO | E01-C through E03-C |

Replication is P. Within each block, use adjacent sections of the same template lot, matched film thickness, thermal history, growth rate and oxygenation. Alternate arm order between blocks; pre-ablate after target changes to limit carryover. Record unavoidable differences. Split each deposited section into linked suffixes **X** (XRD), **T** (transport), **CS** (cross-sectional TEM) and **PV** (plan-view TEM), retaining a reserve. Perform nondestructive inspection/XRD before cutting. Destructive microscopy must never consume the sole transport specimen.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and prerequisites
Use Eu2O3, BaCO3, CuO, BSO and BHO powders with certificates; ethanol, compatible cleaning solvents, validated optional binder, high-purity oxygen, Hastelloy-backed IBAD-MgO template tape, Au contact metallization/wires and electrically insulating thermal mounting material.

Required equipment: ventilated powder handling, balance, mill, furnace with oxygen control, rated press, target-density measurement, reel-to-reel MPMT-PLD with interlocks and calibrated pressure/temperature/laser monitors; thickness metrology; XRD with instrumental-broadening standard; SEM, TEM/STEM-EDS with cross-section and plan-view preparation; lithography/qualified etching; nanovoltmeter, current source and protection; 18 T and 25 T magnets with qualified rotatable 4.2–77 K sample environment.

Verify the supplied template's complete buffer/cap sequence and EuBCO compatibility. Preserve the IBAD-MgO/Hastelloy architecture; use one verified stack throughout. Do not execute the draft's unverified CeO2-then-LaMnO3 sequence or assume <1% mismatch. Additional buffer fabrication requires a separately qualified stack before any core runs.

##### 3. Consolidated operating parameters

| Operation | Setting, tolerance or prerequisite |
|---|---|
| Composition basis | P: 0.045 mol BSO and 0.035 mol BHO per mol EuBCO; approve this denominator before weighing |
| Target processing | P: ethanol milling 300 rpm, 6 h, 10:1 media/powder mass ratio; drying 80°C, 12 h |
| Calcination/sintering | P: 850°C, 6 h; regrind, 850°C, 4 h; sinter 950 ± 5°C, 12 h in flowing O2; ramps 5°C/min heating, 2°C/min cooling |
| Pressing/density | P: uniaxial 400 MPa followed by isostatic 200 MPa, 10 min, only within tooling ratings; proposed density >95% of verified composite theoretical density |
| PLD starting point | P: base pressure <5×10^-6 Torr; 780 ± 5°C; 300 ± 10 mTorr O2 = 40.0 ± 1.3 Pa; target distance 5–6 cm |
| Laser/exposure | P: 248 nm, 2.5–3.5 J/cm²; frequency, plume number, overlap and reel speed determined by rate qualification, not assumed |
| Growth | Task: >100 nm/s, 1 µm; P: aim 120 ± 5 nm/s and 1.0 ± 0.1 µm |
| Oxygenation | P: cool at 5°C/min to 450 ± 5°C; 60 ± 5 min in approximately 1 atm O2; cool at 2°C/min; use only pressure-rated equipment |
| Transport geometry | P: width 50 µm, taps 5 mm apart; measure actual width, superconducting thickness and tap separation; geometry uncertainty goal ≤5% |
| Transport stability | P: ±0.1 K, field uniformity/calibration ≤0.5%, angular alignment ±0.5°; demonstrate before use |
| Criterion | Ecrit = 1 µV/cm; actual Vcrit = Ecrit × tap separation |

Freeze calibrated settings before paired blocks. Furnace flow depends on tube geometry; establish and record flow sufficient to maintain the intended atmosphere. The draft's 0.70–1.00 mbar is not equivalent to 300 mTorr and is not used.

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
**A. Targets.** Calculate all masses from the approved molar denominator and assay corrections, not the draft's disconnected gram quantities. For one mole of EuBCO precursor, use 0.5 mol Eu2O3, 2 mol BaCO3 and 3 mol CuO, plus the specified additives for C. Mill, dry, calcine and regrind using the qualified pilot schedule. Inspect XRD for residual carbonate and unintended phases before pressing. If binder is needed, qualify its compatibility and complete burnout separately. Sinter, measure density, inspect cracks and map Eu/Ba/Cu/Sn/Hf homogeneity. Nanocolumn formation is a film-growth objective, not a sintered-target quality criterion.

**Gate A → deposition:** release only intact targets with documented composition and phase inventory. Carbonate, segregation or low density requires reprocessing and reassessment; do not claim an uncalibrated XRD detection limit.

#### Related Knowledge

- The paired undoped/co-doped design uses adjacent template sections, matched processing and linked specimen identifiers to support comparison while preserving transport material. **Source:** B000, B001, B002.
- The verified Hastelloy-backed IBAD-MgO architecture is retained; the actual buffer/cap stack must be checked rather than replacing it with an unqualified sequence. **Source:** B003, B004, B005.
- Target preparation depends on an approved molar denominator and pilot-qualified processing. Release is based on documented composition, phase inventory and target integrity, not on nanocolumn formation in the target. **Source:** B007, B008, B009, B010.

### Experimental Task 2: Qualify moving-tape deposition and grow oxygenated paired films

#### Specific Content

**B. Templates and rate qualification.** Inspect tape/cap integrity and texture. Use a supplier-compatible solvent cleaning procedure verified on sacrificial tape; avoid unqualified water exposure, ultrasonic cleaning or high-temperature vacuum treatment. Mount in the reel path, not with stationary-stage silver paste. Verify reel tension, heater calibration, target raster and plume coverage. Pre-ablate behind a shutter, P: 1000 pulses, stopping only after stable ablation is confirmed.

Use witness films and ex-situ thickness to calibrate local growth rate and integrated moving-tape exposure. QCM is auxiliary unless its tooling factor has been verified. At 120 nm/s, 1 µm requires approximately **8.3 s cumulative growth exposure**; sum all plume/turn residence times. The draft's 10–20 Hz cannot establish this rate without measured deposition per pulse and plume multiplicity. Log temperature/pressure fast enough to resolve each exposure, P: ≥10 Hz where available.

**C. Core growth.** Stabilize at the qualified temperature/pressure, P: 30 min. Deposit each arm with identical qualified reel trajectory and process settings. Record laser energy, frequency, spot area, plume count, exposure, tape speed/tension and deviations. Oxygenate both arms identically. If the PLD chamber is not rated for approximately atmospheric oxygen, transfer through a validated procedure to a rated furnace; record transfer time and atmosphere.

**Gate B → characterization:** label positions, measure thickness at leading/middle/trailing locations, inspect cracks, particulates and delamination. Out-of-tolerance thickness or failed rate qualification disqualifies a specimen from the matched primary comparison; retain it as diagnostic material. Hand off the position map, process log and target/template IDs.

#### Related Knowledge

- Template cleaning, reel mounting, tension, heater calibration and stable pre-ablation require qualification compatible with the supplied tape. **Source:** B011.
- Witness-film thickness and cumulative plume/turn residence times establish moving-tape growth rate; laser frequency alone does not demonstrate the required rate. **Source:** B012.
- Matched growth and oxygenation require recorded process settings and pressure-rated handling. Thickness and integrity checks determine eligibility for the primary paired comparison while preserving excluded material for diagnosis. **Source:** B013, B014.

### Experimental Task 3: Measure film texture, strain and nanocolumn microstructure

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural characterization
**XRD:** calibrate wavelength, zero offset and line broadening. P: Cu Kα1, λ = 1.5406 Å; θ–2θ range 5–80°, 0.02° steps. Index EuBCO (005), (006), (007), expected near 38.3°, 46.4°, 54.7° for c = 11.744 Å. Fit multiple reflections using c = lλ/(2 sinθ), accounting for substrate overlap and specimen displacement. Measure (005) ω FWHM and an accessible asymmetric EuBCO reflection's φ FWHM at three positions. Determine the correct tilt from lattice geometry/reciprocal-space alignment; **do not assume χ = 45° for (103)**. Use P: 0.005° ω and ≤0.1° φ increments near peaks.

For Williamson–Hall analysis, correct instrumental broadening and fit βcosθ = Kλ/D + 4εsinθ with β in radians. Report reflections, fit uncertainty and whether anisotropic broadening invalidates a single ε. Compare with objectives c = 11.744 Å, Δφ = 2.6°, Δω = 1.5°, ε = 0.29% without imposing fabricated tolerances.

**TEM/STEM-EDS:** prepare CS and PV specimens from mapped positions using a protective cap, coarse milling followed by low-energy polishing; verify preparation damage. P: begin at 300 kV and 50 pA, then qualify the lowest useful dose using repeated-image damage tests. Do not combine the draft's long EDS acquisition with an unsupported 80 e^-/Å² limit. Log current, area, dwell, frames and accumulated dose.

Acquire CS images along suitable a/b zone axes for column continuity, splay, stripe-like EuBCO interruptions and interface dislocations. Count PV columns in ≥10 randomly selected fields spanning ≥1 µm² total per specimen; measure ≥50 diameters and report sampling uncertainty. Calibrate EDS with BSO/BHO standards, fit spectral overlaps and demonstrate Sn/Hf co-localization inside individual columns. Nominal x = 4.5/8 = 0.5625 is a feed ratio, not a required measured column composition. Examine the proposed two-edge-dislocation interface motif; EDS alone cannot establish oxygen vacancies.

**Gate C → transport/analysis:** document morphology and texture deviations rather than rejecting unfavorable outcomes. Preparation damage or unresolved spectral overlap requires new lamellae/analysis, not a composition claim.

#### Related Knowledge

- Calibrated multi-reflection XRD separates lattice and texture measurements from instrumental and alignment effects; Williamson–Hall interpretation must retain uncertainty and possible anisotropic broadening limitations. **Source:** B015, B016.
- Cross-sectional and plan-view microscopy provide complementary observations of column continuity, dimensions and density, with preparation damage, sampling uncertainty and electron dose documented. **Source:** B017, B018.
- Calibrated spectral fitting and individual-column Sn/Hf co-localization support composition assessment. Feed ratio is not measured column composition, EDS does not establish oxygen vacancies, and unresolved damage or overlap requires reassessment. **Source:** B018, B019.

### Experimental Task 4: Prepare four-probe bridges and measure field-temperature-angle transport

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Transport preparation and measurements
Pattern T specimens using a sacrificial-tape-qualified lithography/etch process; do not assume the draft's nitric-acid etch is selective. Verify isolation and actual bridge dimensions. Apply four distinct Au pads, outer current/inner voltage, with no metal bypass across the gauge region. Check contact linearity, resistance, lead isolation and cooldown integrity; establish contact-heating limits experimentally.

For 50 µm × 1 µm, area = **5×10^-7 cm²**. The supplied Jc targets require **2.00 A** at 50 K/3 T and **2.10 A** at 4.2 K/24 T; 77 K self-field target requires 1.30 A. The draft's 1 A source is insufficient. Qualify a source/lead/contact system of P: at least 5 A, with compliance and thermal protection; low-field Ic may require more. If current capacity remains inadequate, qualify narrower bridges consistently across both arms. At 5 mm taps, Vcrit = **0.5 µV**. Verify noise, offsets and inductive settling below this threshold.

Define θ = 0° as B∥c, perpendicular to tape. Rotate B in a plane perpendicular to current so J remains perpendicular to B. Use only qualified field ramp rates; P: 1 T/min maximum if manufacturer-approved. Stabilize field/temperature, P: ≥15 min initially, then confirm drift before every I–V sweep. Use both current polarities, adaptive increments near criterion, zero-current offsets and a repeated sweep at a slower ramp to test heating/rate artifacts. Never extrapolate Ic above the source limit.

Core matrix for both arms: T = 4.2, 20, 50, 77 K; θ = 0°; B = 0, 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 18 T plus 20, 22, 24 T on the 25 T instrument. Include every supplied target point and application conditions 50 K/≥5 T and 20 K/≥20 T. Measure angular curves at 50 K/5 T and 20 K/20 T, P: 0–90° in 5° steps with finer sampling near peaks. Cross-check one overlapping field between instruments. Unreachable criterion gives a lower bound; instability gives an invalid measurement, not zero Jc.

#### Related Knowledge

- Qualified patterning, measured bridge geometry and four separate contact pads prevent unsupported etch assumptions and metallic bypasses from compromising transport interpretation. **Source:** B020.
- Current capacity and voltage sensitivity must accommodate the supplied targets and actual tap spacing; source-limited measurements must not be extrapolated. **Source:** B021.
- Defined transverse geometry, field and temperature stabilization, polarity reversal and slower repeated sweeps address measurement consistency and heating or ramp artifacts across the core matrix. **Source:** B022, B023.

### Experimental Task 5: Normalize results, compare paired arms and control diagnostic follow-up

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Analysis, disposition and controlled feedback
Calculate Jc = Ic/(wt); for transverse geometry **Fp = Jc B**, with Jc in A/m² and B in tesla, without an additional μ0. The supplied 4.2 MA/cm² at 24 T implies **1008 GN/m³**, approximately 1.8% above 990; retain both supplied values as approximate targets and report internally consistent calculations. For n = 2200 µm^-2, nΦ0 = **4.55 T**. Treat the supplied 7.2 T theoretical value as unresolved, not derivable from this density.

Assess whether Fp continues increasing toward 24 T using high-field slopes and uncertainty; an unobserved maximum must not be declared saturated or assigned an invented maximum. Compare paired arms and replicate variation. Record raw I–V, timestamps, geometry, uncertainties, instrument calibrations, microscopy field selection and all failed specimens.

Cracks/agglomerates: inspect target transfer, thermal mismatch and plume uniformity. Poor texture: inspect template and temperature calibration. Low Jc with acceptable texture: inspect oxygenation, contacts and pattern damage. Heating: reduce duty cycle/improve contacts and repeat. Change one process factor at a time in pilot specimens; after qualification repeat complete paired blocks. Keep diagnostic runs separate.

Optional single-dopant controls can test synergy, and vacancy-sensitive spectroscopy can test the vacancy hypothesis; neither replaces U/C controls. The core comparison alone cannot prove differential Sn/Hf diffusion or uniquely identify random pinning.

Use powder/solvent ventilation, laser interlocks, oxygen-compatible pressure-rated hardware, press guards, FIB/beam controls, cryogenic oxygen-deficiency precautions, magnet exclusion zones and current/quench protection. Stop on interlock failure, pressure excursion, thermal runaway or unstable transport.

#### Related Knowledge

- Transport normalization uses actual superconducting cross-section and transverse Fp = Jc B. The supplied pinning-force values remain approximate references, and the stated theoretical matching-field value remains unresolved. **Source:** B024.
- High-field slopes, uncertainty and replicate variation support comparison without inventing an unobserved maximum. Raw data, calibrations, specimen failures and diagnostic changes remain traceable. **Source:** B025, B026.
- Optional single-dopant controls and vacancy-sensitive spectroscopy do not replace the core comparison or establish mechanisms from it. Execution remains subject to the listed safety protections and stop conditions. **Source:** B027, B028.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage 1 — Qualification and matched fabrication

**Tasks 1–2.** Establish undoped U and co-doped C arms, with C containing 4.5 mol% BSO and 3.5 mol% BHO on the approved EuBCO denominator. Three independent paired blocks, E01–E03, are P. Use adjacent sections from one verified Hastelloy-backed IBAD-MgO template architecture; verify its complete buffer/cap stack and do not impose the unqualified alternative sequence. Match thickness, thermal history, growth rate and oxygenation, alternate arm order, and document unavoidable differences.

Prepare targets from assay-corrected masses using the source's P milling, calcination, pressing and oxygen-sintering schedule only after pilot qualification and tooling-rating checks. Qualify any optional binder separately. **Gate A:** release intact targets with documented composition, phase inventory, density and homogeneity; reprocess carbonate-containing, segregated or low-density targets. Nanocolumn formation is a film-growth objective.

Qualify supplier-compatible cleaning on sacrificial tape, reel handling, heater and pressure calibration, target raster and plume coverage. Freeze calibrated settings before paired runs. PLD starting settings remain P: 780 ± 5°C, 300 ± 10 mTorr O2, 248 nm and 2.5–3.5 J/cm². Determine frequency, plume configuration and reel speed from witness-film thickness and cumulative moving-tape exposure—not assumed frequency. Demonstrate growth exceeding 100 nm/s; P aims are 120 ± 5 nm/s and 1.0 ± 0.1 µm. Apply identical qualified oxygenation; the P schedule includes 450 ± 5°C for 60 ± 5 min near atmospheric oxygen. Use a validated transfer to a rated furnace if necessary.

**Gate B:** map positions, inspect integrity and measure leading/middle/trailing thickness. Failed rate qualification or out-of-tolerance thickness excludes material from the matched primary comparison, not diagnostic retention. Preserve process logs and target/template identifiers.

### Stage 2 — Structural characterization

**Task 3.** Complete nondestructive inspection/XRD before cutting linked X, T, CS and PV specimens; retain a reserve and never consume the sole transport specimen for microscopy. Calibrate XRD wavelength, offsets and broadening. Fit multiple reflections for lattice parameters, measure ω and φ widths at three positions, and determine asymmetric-reflection alignment rather than assuming a tilt. Apply instrument-corrected Williamson–Hall analysis with fit uncertainty and anisotropy limitations.

Use mapped cross-sectional and plan-view TEM/STEM-EDS specimens with verified preparation quality and qualified dose. Examine continuity, splay, interruptions and interface dislocations. Count columns in at least 10 random fields spanning at least 1 µm² per specimen and measure at least 50 diameters. Calibrate EDS, resolve overlaps and assess individual-column Sn/Hf co-localization. Feed ratio is not measured composition; EDS cannot establish oxygen vacancies. Compare with supplied morphology and structural objectives only as references. **Gate C:** record unfavorable morphology rather than reject it; preparation damage or unresolved overlap requires new preparation/analysis.

### Stage 3 — Transport, analysis and disposition

**Tasks 4–5.** Qualify patterning on sacrificial tape, isolation, four distinct Au pads, contacts and cooldown integrity. P geometry is 50 µm width and 5 mm tap separation; measure actual width, superconducting thickness and separation. Qualify current capacity, protection and noise: P capacity is at least 5 A, while 5 mm taps require a 0.5 µV threshold at the fixed 1 µV/cm criterion. Any narrower bridge alternative requires consistent qualification across arms.

Measure both arms at 4.2, 20, 50 and 77 K, B∥c, across the specified 0–24 T matrix, using the 25 T instrument above the qualified 18 T range. Include angular curves at 50 K/5 T and 20 K/20 T and an overlapping-field cross-check. Maintain transverse geometry; qualify stability, ramping and alignment. Use both current polarities, offset correction and slower repeat sweeps. Report unreachable criteria as lower bounds and instability as invalid—not zero Jc.

Calculate Jc = Ic/(wt) and Fp = Jc B without additional μ0. Retain supplied performance values as reference targets; report consistent calculations, the approximate 990 versus 1008 GN/m³ distinction, and the unresolved 7.2 T value. Compare paired variation and high-field slopes without inventing a maximum. Archive raw data, uncertainties, sampling and failures. Keep one-factor pilot feedback and optional controls separate from core blocks.

Throughout, require ventilation, interlocks, rated oxygen hardware, press/beam controls, cryogenic precautions, magnet exclusion and current/quench protection. Stop for interlock failure, pressure excursion, thermal runaway or unstable transport.
