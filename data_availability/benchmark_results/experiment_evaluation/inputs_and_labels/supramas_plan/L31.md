# Experimental Plan: Cu-rich YBCO epitaxial-film protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Qualify resources and grow paired films
The objective is to test whether directly grown Cu-rich YBCO contains epitaxially embedded cubic domains within an orthorhombic matrix, without granular particles, and whether this architecture correlates with improved pinning while retaining smooth surfaces. No separate pinning layers or buffers are introduced.

Compare nominal 1:2:4-target films with pure-YBCO 1:2:3 controls on STO, LAO and LSAT, prioritizing LSAT for device-relevant interpretation. All core films are 40 nm thick. Proposed choices include polished (001) substrates and three independent paired growth blocks per substrate, totaling 18 growth units. Match substrate lots and processing, randomize composition order within pairs, and maintain mapped transport, magnetometry and microscopy specimens.

Use certified targets, qualified substrates, oxygen-compatible KrF PLD and annealing hardware, and calibrated growth monitoring. Deposit at 248 nm, 1.00 J cm−2, 780 °C and 100 mTorr oxygen; subsequently anneal at 400 °C for 60 minutes under 700 Torr absolute oxygen. Proposed auxiliary settings and tolerances require qualification before freezing core conditions.

### Stage II — Establish composition, morphology and architecture
Complete nondestructive screening before patterning or destructive preparation. Use AFM, SEM-EDS, XRD and HR-TEM to assess thickness, composition, surface quality, texture, relaxation and embedded-domain relationships. Distinguish nominal target Cu/Y=4 from the film objective Cu/Y≈3.6. Neither annealing nor thickness establishes oxygen deficiency or strain relaxation.

### Stage III — Measure performance and consolidate evidence
Use qualified bridge processing, direct four-terminal transport and background-corrected VSM measurements at 77 and 80 K. Verify current, voltage-noise and magnetic-field capabilities before execution. Anticipated outputs are paired structural and performance comparisons with uncertainty, documented exclusions and archived failures. Supplied performance values remain reference targets, not outcomes; unresolved qualifications and optional follow-up remain separate from the core comparison.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Qualify targets, substrates, equipment and paired specimen allocation

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Objective, matrix and custody
Test whether directly grown Cu-rich YBCO contains embedded epitaxial cubic domains without granular particles, and whether these correlate with increased pinning while retaining smooth surfaces. Preserve the nanocomposite architecture; use no separate pinning layers or buffers.

| Factor | Core matrix |
|---|---|
| Substrate | STO, LAO, LSAT; polished (001) proposed |
| Composition | C: nominal 1:2:4 target; P: pure-YBCO 1:2:3 control |
| Thickness | 40 nm in every arm |
| Replication | Proposed three independent paired growth blocks/substrate: 18 growth units |
| Testing | Structure, composition, morphology; transport and VSM at 77 and 80 K |

Use identifiers `S-C/P-Bnn`. Match substrate lots and processing; randomize C/P growth order within blocks. Each unit supplies T (transport), M (unpatterned magnetometry) and E (destructive microscopy) specimens through mapped film sections or co-deposited witnesses. Qualify witness-position equivalence. Record specimen maps, operator, time and condition at every hand-off: preparation → growth → nondestructive screening → T to patterning/transport, M to VSM, E to TEM. Archive failures and replacements separately.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and conditions
Materials: certified 1:2:4 and 1:2:3 ceramic targets; STO/LAO/LSAT; high-purity oxygen; electronic-grade acetone/ethanol, DI water and nitrogen; qualified resist/developer/contact metal; non-Cu TEM supports for compositional analysis.

Equipment: interlocked KrF PLD with shutter/target rotation and calibrated fluence, pressure and substrate-temperature monitoring; oxygen-rated annealing hardware; AFM; XRD with asymmetric diffraction capability; SEM-EDS; FIB/HR-TEM; lithography, ion milling and contact deposition; PPMS AC transport, VSM, and auxiliary nonlinear-current/voltage instrumentation if required.

**P marks proposed settings/tolerances requiring qualification. Task setpoints stay fixed in core growth.**

| Parameter | Setting |
|---|---|
| Laser | 248 nm; 1.00 J cm−2 at target; ±5% P |
| Growth temperature | 780 °C; ±5 °C P, actual substrate temperature calibrated |
| Growth oxygen | 100 mTorr =13.33 Pa =0.1333 mbar; ±5 mTorr P |
| Pulse rate/distance/rotation | 5 Hz/50 mm/10 rpm P, selected from draft |
| Thickness | 40 ±2 nm; tolerance P |
| Oxygen anneal | 400 °C/60 min/700 Torr absolute (93.3 kPa); ±5 °C/±2 min/±10 Torr P |
| Cooling | 10 °C/min to anneal; 2 °C/min thereafter in anneal oxygen, P |
| Measurement temperature | 77 and 80 K; ±0.05 K P |
| Field alignment | B parallel to c; error ≤0.5° P |
| Transport field grid | 0, 0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 1.2, 2, 3, 5 T P, within verified capability |
| Transport criterion | E=1 μV/cm, draft convention |

Verify installed current limits, waveform support, magnet ratings, temperature range and VSM thin-film sensitivity. Draft specifications of 14 T PPMS and ±3 T VSM are not equipment certification.

##### 3. Target and substrate release
1. Prefer certified targets. Verify nominal cation inventory, spatial homogeneity and phases using calibrated composition analysis, SEM-EDS and XRD. Target Cu/Y is nominally 4, not 3.6. Identify off-stoichiometric target phases; do not require unsupported Y124-majority or zero-CuO criteria. Reject cracked, loose or compositionally inconsistent targets.
2. If synthesizing targets, qualify this separately before core growth. Correct precursor mole ratios Y₂O₃:BaCO₃:CuO are 0.5:2:4 for C and 0.5:2:3 for P; add no unvalidated extra CuO. Proposed draft-derived starting route: ethanol milling, 10:1 media:powder mass ratio, 200 rpm/12 h; 850 °C/6 h in air; regrinding; 880 °C/4 h in oxygen; pressing; 930 °C/12 h oxygen sintering. Qualify ramps, binder burnout, pressing geometry/pressure, densification and stable ablation before release.
3. Inspect and label substrates. Proposed cleaning: acetone ultrasonication 10 min, ethanol 5 min, DI rinse 5 min, nitrogen dry. Use clean separate containers; inspect edge damage.
4. AFM-map three sites. Substrate RMS <0.2 nm is a proposed draft-derived acceptance target, distinct from film RMS <1 nm. If needed, qualify 800 °C/2 h oxygen pretreatment separately on each substrate type. Reject pitted/roughened specimens; avoid unqualified acid etching or repolishing.
5. Transfer released substrates in covered carriers; record storage and loading delay.

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched substrate lots, randomized composition order and mapped specimen allocation preserve the paired comparison and traceability through nondestructive, transport, magnetometry and destructive branches. Witness-position equivalence remains a qualification prerequisite. **Source:** B001, B002.
- Nominal target composition and the film-composition objective are distinct. Target release depends on verified inventory, homogeneity and phases; any target synthesis route requires separate qualification rather than automatic adoption. **Source:** B008.
- Fixed core setpoints do not establish the proposed tolerances or auxiliary settings. Installed transport, magnetic-field and thin-film sensitivity capabilities must be verified independently of draft equipment specifications. **Source:** B005, B006, B007.

### Experimental Task 2: Deposit paired films, oxygenate and release verified thicknesses

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Growth and oxygenation
1. Mount without touching growth faces. Follow chamber cleaning procedures; do not heat the entire chamber to 800 °C unless rated for it.
2. Pump to the qualified base pressure, establish oxygen, heat to 780 °C and stabilize. Shutter-pre-ablate until plume and pulse energy stabilize; determine stabilization time and pulse count during calibration.
3. Calibrate growth-per-pulse for both targets and substrate positions using X-ray reflectivity, genuine step-height measurements or cross-sectional TEM. A quartz monitor is secondary and requires correlation under actual growth conditions. AFM roughness is not thickness.
4. Deposit the calibrated pulse count for 40 nm. Log pulse energy, spot area, temperature, pressure, rotation, pulse count and interruptions.
5. Cool in oxygen to 400 °C using a qualified pressure-ramp sequence; hold 60 min at 700 Torr absolute, then cool in oxygen. Verify pressure rating and oxygen compatibility before pressurization. Do not infer δ from annealing alone.
6. Unload cold into clean carriers. Verify thickness/uniformity before downstream release. Retain out-of-tolerance films as process failures; regrow under new identifiers.

#### Related Knowledge

- Growth-per-pulse calibration must cover both targets and substrate positions using a genuine thickness measurement. AFM roughness is not thickness, and a quartz monitor remains secondary unless correlated under actual growth conditions. **Source:** B009.
- The oxygenation sequence requires qualified pressure transitions and rated, oxygen-compatible hardware. The anneal does not independently establish oxygen deficiency. **Source:** B006, B009.
- Thickness and uniformity verification precede downstream release. Out-of-tolerance films remain recorded as process failures, with regrowth assigned new identifiers. **Source:** B009.

### Experimental Task 3: Screen film surfaces and establish composition, texture and embedded-domain architecture

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural and morphology gates
Complete nondestructive measurements before patterning/destructive preparation.

- **AFM/SEM:** map three 5×5 μm² sites plus 1×1 and 10×10 μm² checks. Record flattening, RMS, steps and particles. Film objective: RMS <1 nm and no granular particles in surveyed regions. SEM complements surface surveying, not embedded-domain identification. Limit beam dose on transport specimens.
- **SEM-EDS:** map multiple locations with standards and calibrated beam conditions. At 40 nm, substrate excitation limits composition accuracy. Validate thin-film correction or an independent method if necessary to test Cu/Y≈3.6; report uncertainty and detection limits.
- **XRD:** proposed 10–80° 2θ, 0.02° steps, initially 2 s/step; increase counting for weak films. For Cu Kα, λ≈0.15406 nm and c=1.169 nm, Bragg's law places (003) near 22.8° and (005) near 38.5°, not (005) at 23.5°. Correct zero error and fit multiple (00l) peaks. Measure (005) rocking width; <0.5° is a proposed screening goal. Select an accessible asymmetric YBCO reflection after checking geometry/indexing, then measure φ texture. Use reciprocal-space maps for in-plane relaxation. Assess supplied Δc<0.001 nm against a composition/oxygen-matched relaxed reference with uncertainty; c alone does not prove relaxation.
- **HR-TEM:** prepare protected cross-sections at multiple lateral positions spanning substrate interface to surface; add plan-view specimens where feasible. Proposed FIB rough thinning at 30 kV and final polish at 2 kV; qualify damage, thickness and imaging voltage. Use low-dose diffraction/FFT from domains and matrix to establish cubic symmetry and epitaxial relationships. Do not preassign CuO. Quantify domain size, fraction, clustering, depth distribution and interface defects; record sampled volume and artifacts. Contrast alone is insufficient.

Films lacking the intended architecture remain recorded but cannot support the embedded-domain inference. Weak domain XRD signals alone do not justify rejection. Reprepare damaged lamellae.

#### Related Knowledge

- Nondestructive screening precedes patterning and destructive preparation. AFM quantifies surveyed roughness, while SEM complements surface inspection without establishing embedded-domain identity. **Source:** B010, B011.
- Thin-film SEM-EDS requires attention to substrate excitation, correction validity and uncertainty. XRD texture and relaxation assessments require appropriate geometry, multiple reflections and a suitable reference rather than inference from the c parameter alone. **Source:** B011.
- Domain identification requires diffraction or FFT evidence of cubic symmetry and epitaxial relationships, not contrast alone or a preassigned CuO identity. Films without the intended architecture cannot support the embedded-domain inference, but weak domain XRD signals alone are not a rejection basis. **Source:** B011, B012.

### Experimental Task 4: Qualify transport bridges and measure criterion-based critical current

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Transport preparation and measurement
1. Pattern T specimens into nominal 50 μm-wide, 3 mm-long bridges, with proposed 1 mm voltage-tap spacing and wider current pads. Qualify resist processing, milling endpoint and contacts on sacrificial films; fixed etch duration or contact annealing is not automatically safe. Inspect isolation, shorts and edge damage; measure actual width, thickness and tap separation.
2. Verify stable contacts and low-current R(T). Use PPMS AC transport for low-excitation R(T), proposing 17 Hz initially; test amplitude independence. Record transition onset, midpoint and zero-resistance definitions without assuming Tc.
3. **Current/voltage prerequisite:** 40 nm=4×10−6 cm and 50 μm=5×10−3 cm, so wt=2×10−8 cm². The supplied Jc=3.7×10⁶ A/cm² implies Ic=0.074 A=74 mA. A 10 mA source reaches only 0.5 MA/cm². Verify nonlinear transport capability beyond 74 mA with calibrated headroom, wiring and thermal control; the target does not establish an ampere-class requirement. At L=1 mm=0.1 cm, Vc=0.1 μV=100 nV. Verify noise substantially below this criterion. Recalculate using measured geometry.
4. Acquire direct four-terminal V(I) with validated current reversal or short pulses. Record pulse timing; do not substitute a lock-in harmonic for the electric-field criterion. Start low, bracket Ic and repeat with altered pulse duration/sweep rate to check heating. Stop on instability or compliance. Freeze the qualified waveform for all arms.
5. At each temperature, zero-field cool and record field history. Measure the grid including exactly 1.2 T, stabilizing after changes; proposed ramp ceiling 0.1 T/min. Extract Ic at |V|/L=1 μV/cm and Jc=Ic/(wt), propagating uncertainty. Applied 0 T is self-field, not zero local induction. Report current-limited observations as bounds.

#### Related Knowledge

- Bridge processing and contacts require sacrificial-film qualification. Actual width, thickness and voltage-tap separation determine current-density normalization and the voltage corresponding to the electric-field criterion. **Source:** B013.
- The supplied current-density target implies approximately 74 mA for the nominal bridge, while the nominal voltage criterion is 100 nV. These calculations motivate capability and noise qualification without establishing an ampere-class requirement. **Source:** B013.
- Direct four-terminal V(I), heating checks and a frozen qualified waveform support consistent criterion extraction across arms. Matched field history, inclusion of exactly 1.2 T and reporting current-limited results as bounds preserve the specified comparison. **Source:** B013.

### Experimental Task 5: Measure magnetic irreversibility, consolidate paired results and document failures

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Magnetometry and irreversibility
Measure unpatterned M specimens and matched bare substrates/holders at 77 and 80 K. Zero-field cool, then acquire symmetric loops over verified range; proposed sweep 10 mT/s with refined steps near closure. Match history/rate across pairs, subtract backgrounds and inspect drift.

Proposed primary Birr criterion: branch separation is indistinguishable from zero within three times measured background uncertainty for three consecutive points. Report criterion and field-resolution interval. A ±3 T VSM cannot establish closure above 3 T; report Birr>3 T and require higher-field magnetometry to quantify the 20% comparison.

Optional magnetic Jc cross-check: use Jc=20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)] only after validating the rectangular thin-film model, with a≤b in cm and ΔM in emu/cm³ normalized by film volume; output is A/cm². Measure dimensions and subtract substrate moment. Do not impose 15% agreement. A secondary 100 A/cm² transport threshold is not 1% of 3.7 MA/cm²; label it separately from VSM Birr.

##### 8. Diagnosis, records, feedback and safety
Low Tc: check temperature calibration, oxygen history, contacts and damage. Low Jc: first check geometry, noise, heating and alignment, then oxygenation/texture/domains. Roughness: inspect target erosion, particles and fluence calibration. Poor texture: check cleanliness, actual substrate temperature and plume alignment. Diagnose before changing conditions; rerun complete affected pairs, not selectively reannealing one arm.

Archive raw images/spectra, V(I), R(T), loops/backgrounds, calibration files, geometry, uncertainty and exclusions. Report substrate-specific paired Jc ratios at 80 K/1.2 T, Birr ratios, self-field Jc and 77 K results. Treat 3.7 MA/cm², 1.6× and 20% as supplied targets, not measured outcomes. The supplied 150% increase lacks an unambiguous comparison condition. Correlation alone does not prove Ba-site Cu occupation or a unique pinning mechanism.

Freeze qualified settings before core growth. Investigate one changed factor at a time in separately identified pilots; document approvals and complete paired reruns. Optional LSAT sub-100 nm patterning/microwave tests follow core characterization and never replace controls.

Use Class-4 laser interlocks, oxygen-compatible regulators/rated pressure hardware, hot-surface protection, ventilated solvent/powder handling, press guards, FIB/X-ray safeguards and magnet/cryogen exclusion/quench procedures. Stop for leaks, interlock faults or thermal/electrical compliance failures.

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched bare-substrate and holder measurements support background subtraction for thin-film loops. The proposed irreversibility criterion depends on measured background uncertainty, and field-limited nonclosure must be reported as a bound. **Source:** B014, B015.
- Magnetic current-density extraction is an optional cross-check requiring validation of the rectangular thin-film model, measured dimensions and film-volume normalization. It does not impose agreement with transport or replace the primary VSM irreversibility definition. **Source:** B016.
- Diagnosis precedes condition changes, and affected comparisons require complete paired reruns. Archived raw data, uncertainty and exclusions distinguish supplied targets from outcomes and prevent correlation from being presented as proof of a unique mechanism. **Source:** B017, B018, B019.
- Laser, oxygen-pressure, thermal, solvent, powder, press, FIB, X-ray and magnet/cryogen safeguards are execution prerequisites. Leaks, interlock faults and thermal or electrical compliance failures require stopping. **Source:** B020.
