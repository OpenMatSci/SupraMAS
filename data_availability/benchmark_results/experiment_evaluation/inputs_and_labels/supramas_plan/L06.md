# Experimental Plan: GdYbBCO hierarchical-pinning coated-conductor protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Experimental overview

Reconstruct a controlled comparison of undoped Gd0.5Yb0.5Ba2Cu3O7−δ nanocomposite films on identical IBAD-MgO buffered Hastelloy tapes. The objective is to test whether composition-controlled, self-assembled BaCuO2 nanorods and (Gd,Yb)2O3 nanodot layers correlate with directional pinning, interface coherence and slow field decay. No separate pinning layers or dopants are introduced.

The matrix comprises Cu/Ba = 1.15, 1.17, 1.19 and 1.21 at RE/Ba = 0.65, and RE/Ba = 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70 and 0.75 at Cu/Ba = 1.19. The shared center gives eight unique compositions; RE increments and three independent runs per composition remain proposed. Maintain equal Gd and Yb, randomize run order within blocks and use common qualified substrate, growth, oxygenation and contact procedures. Resolve delivered versus incorporated ratios before assigning composition labels.

Use reel-to-reel MOCVD, calibrated delivery and temperature controls, thickness metrology, SEM/EDS, calibrated 2D-XRD, FIB/TEM with ex-situ GPA, four-probe transport with rotation, and magnetization equipment verified for 14 T and actual 4 K operation. Candidate growth and oxygenation settings require qualification before freezing.

The workflow proceeds through preparation and qualification, fabrication and coupon hand-offs, structural/interface characterization, transport, and magnetization with consolidated analysis and archiving. Target 540 nm films and specified texture; assess architecture and interface references without assuming their realization. Preserve adjacent transport, magnetic, microscopy and archive coupons. Anticipated outputs are traceable composition–structure–property comparisons with uncertainties, raw records, failures and unresolved interpretations retained. Supplied performance values are reference targets, not measured outcomes; the >20 T comparator study is optional and requires separately verified access.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Establish the comparison matrix and qualify materials, equipment and operating conditions

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and comparison matrix
Test whether composition-controlled, self-assembled BaCuO2 nanorods and (Gd,Yb)2O3 nanodot layers correlate with directional pinning, interface coherence and slow field decay. Grow one nanocomposite film on each identical IBAD-MgO buffered Hastelloy substrate; do not deposit separate pinning layers or add dopants.

Use identifiers `Cxxx-Rxxx-Bnn-position-use`, where composition codes represent ratios ×100, `Bnn` identifies an independent growth run, and use is `T` transport, `M` magnetization, `X` microscopy or `A` archive.

| Arm | Cu/Ba | RE/Ba | Composition IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cu sweep | 1.15, 1.17, 1.19, 1.21 | 0.65 | C115/117/119/121-R065 |
| RE sweep | 1.19 | 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75 | C119-R055/060/065/070/075 |

The shared C119-R065 center gives eight unique compositions. Cu levels follow the draft; RE increments and three independent runs per composition are proposed. Hold Gd:Yb=1:1. Randomize run order within blocks; use one qualified substrate lot and common growth/oxygenation/contact recipes. Retain off-center films even when their secondary-phase morphology differs from the center.

**Prerequisite:** establish whether the brief's atomic ratios describe delivered precursors or incorporated film composition. Calibrate delivery versus incorporation before assigning composition labels. If provenance remains unavailable, label both values separately and identify the sweep as delivery-ratio controlled.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and qualification
Use buffered tapes with documented buffer stack, dimensions and supplier lot; compatible Gd, Yb, Ba and Cu metal-organic precursors, O2 and carrier gas, qualified Ag contacts, and TEM preparation consumables. The draft's thd complexes are candidate precursors, subject to vapor-pressure, purity and delivery-compatibility checks. No ceramic targets, powder pressing or pellet sintering are required.

Required equipment: reel-to-reel MOCVD with calibrated tape-temperature measurement, independent precursor metering, pressure/oxygen control and abated exhaust; thickness metrology; SEM/EDS; calibrated 2D-XRD; FIB/low-energy finishing and HRTEM/STEM with GPA; four-probe cryogenic transport with rotator; magnetometer verified to reach 14 T and 4 K.

Before production, verify buffer compatibility, precursor transfer functions, gas interlocks, field calibration, actual 4.0 K capability, sample-space dimensions, magnetic-moment sensitivity, current delivery and voltage noise. A nominal 4.2 K-only system cannot claim a 4 K result.

##### 3. Consolidated operating conditions
Task targets are distinguished below from **candidate settings**, which require pilot qualification. Freeze qualified values and tolerances before comparison runs.

| Quantity | Requirement or candidate setting |
|---|---|
| Film thickness | Task: 540 nm; proposed acceptance ±20 nm, measured locally |
| Matrix texture | Task: Δω<3°, Δφ<5°; c=11.63–11.67 Å |
| Growth temperature | Candidate 800°C; proposed stability ±5°C |
| Growth rate | Candidate 10 nm/min; qualification range 8–12 nm/min |
| Growth pressure | Proposed 25 Torr total, 20 Torr O2; proposed stability ±5% |
| Oxygenation | Candidate 500°C for 2 h; proposed ±5°C, ±5 min |
| Cooling | Candidate 5°C/min under qualified oxygen atmosphere |
| Transport temperatures | Proposed grid 4, 10, 20, 40, 60, 77 K; stability ±0.1 K |
| Transport fields | 0–5 T; proposed 0.5 T increments plus low-field grid |
| Rotation | 0–180°, proposed 10° increments; verified accuracy ±1° |
| Magnetization | ±14 T, B parallel to c; candidate sweep 0.1 T/min |

Never use 20 Torr oxygen partial pressure at 5 Torr total pressure. Candidate precursor-source temperatures, carrier flows and oxygenation pressure must be determined from the installed delivery system; gas-flow ratios are not automatically atomic ratios.

#### Related Knowledge

- The two composition sweeps share a center composition, giving eight unique comparisons. Equal Gd and Yb, randomized order and common qualified procedures support the intended comparison; RE increments and replication remain proposed. **Source:** B000, B002, B003.
- Composition labels depend on resolving delivered precursor ratios versus incorporated film ratios. Gas-flow ratios cannot substitute automatically for atomic composition, and oxygen partial pressure must remain compatible with total pressure. **Source:** B004, B010.
- The specified route uses buffered tape and independently metered metal-organic precursors, not ceramic targets or pellet processing. Candidate precursors, operating settings and instrument capabilities require qualification before comparison runs. **Source:** B005, B006, B007, B008, B009.

### Experimental Task 2: Fabricate the nanocomposite matrix and transfer mapped coupons

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Receive and map substrate.** Photograph surfaces, record buffer certificates and map texture at representative reel positions. Use compatible cleaning validated on sacrificial buffered tape; do not polish or apply unqualified ion etching to the finished buffer. Quarantine damaged or unsuitable material.
2. **Qualify delivery on sacrificial tape.** Measure delivered metal fluxes and film EDS ratios using standards and substrate/background corrections. If EDS cannot distinguish adjacent ratio levels, qualify a complementary compositional assay. Set each recipe from this calibration, not nominal carrier flow alone. Proposed ratio tolerance is ±0.005 absolute, contingent on adequate analytical uncertainty.
3. **Qualify growth and oxygenation at the center composition.** Establish temperature, pressure, rate, oxygenation and buffer survival. At 10 nm/min, 540 nm requires 54 min cumulative hot-zone growth exposure; determine reel speed from measured effective hot-zone length, including any repeated passes. Verify thickness rather than assuming residence time guarantees it.
4. **Run the comparison matrix.** Load identified tape; stabilize temperature and gas delivery; initiate simultaneous metal delivery and oxidation. Log all transients and reel positions. Apply the frozen oxygenation and cooling schedule identically. GPA is not an in-reactor control measurement.
5. **Fabrication-to-characterization hand-off.** Supply the run log, recipe, position map and uncut-film inspection. After optical/SEM inspection and thickness/XRD mapping, cut adjacent T, M, X and archive coupons. Preserve orientation and adjacency records. TEM preparation must not consume transport coupons.
6. **Characterization-to-measurement hand-off.** Release mechanically intact samples with measured thickness, composition and texture records. Flag failures rather than silently deleting them. Prepare transport bridges only after documenting the as-grown film.

#### Related Knowledge

- Substrate mapping and sacrificial-tape cleaning qualification preserve the finished buffer; damaged material is quarantined rather than subjected to unqualified polishing or ion etching. **Source:** B011.
- Measured delivery and incorporated composition establish recipes. Adjacent ratio levels require adequate analytical discrimination, and measured hot-zone exposure and thickness determine the qualified reel-to-reel implementation. **Source:** B011.
- Sequential hand-offs retain run logs, position maps and as-grown inspection records. Adjacent transport, magnetization, microscopy and archive coupons preserve traceability while preventing microscopy preparation from consuming transport material. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize texture, secondary-phase morphology and interfaces

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structure and interface characterization
Perform SEM/EDS surveys at proposed three longitudinal positions, including surface precipitates and cracks. Measure matrix rocking curves, off-axis φ scans and c-spacing with calibrated XRD. Acquire 2D-XRD at χ=45° to test BaCuO2 orientation; identify (011) using reference crystallography and TEM diffraction, not a guessed peak angle. For Cu Kα radiation, MgO (002)/(200) is near 42.9°; use an off-axis reflection such as {220} for in-plane texture, not a specular (002) φ scan.

Prepare cross-sectional lamellae from at least three preselected representative regions per sample; include separate defect-targeted regions. Use protective deposition, coarse FIB milling and qualified low-energy finishing. Document beam energies, lamella thickness and damage checks. Start TEM near 200 kV only after beam-damage testing.

Measure ≥100 rods **per sample**, distributed over regions: length, width, aspect ratio, orientation and continuity. The supplied 52/7 nm dimensions imply aspect ratio approximately 7.4, not 10–30. Measure nanodot size, layer spacing and layer tilt. Density 3700 μm⁻² per layer requires plan-view counting or a validated stereological conversion using lamella thickness; raw cross-sectional counts are not directly equivalent.

Image representative interfaces at sufficient calibrated resolution to resolve lattice planes and dislocations. Report sampled interface length and detection limits; do not infer universal absence from FFT alone. Apply ex-situ GPA with declared reference lattices, coordinate axes, masks, spatial resolution and uncertainty. Distinguish phase lattice differences from elastic strain; test the supplied approximately +3% BCO and −12% matrix estimates without imposing the draft's ±0.5% limit. Report mismatch using explicitly identified interface directions and spacings. The definitions underlying |f1/f3|=1.42 remain unresolved.

#### Related Knowledge

- Calibrated diffraction and TEM diffraction support phase orientation assignments. The protocol distinguishes appropriate off-axis texture measurements from an inappropriate specular MgO (002) azimuthal scan. **Source:** B012.
- Representative and defect-targeted lamellae, documented preparation damage checks and at least 100 rods per sample provide the prescribed morphology sampling. Per-layer density requires plan-view counting or a validated stereological conversion. **Source:** B013, B014.
- Interface conclusions depend on calibrated resolution, sampled length and detection limits. Ex-situ GPA must distinguish phase lattice differences from elastic strain; supplied strain estimates and unresolved mismatch-ratio definitions are not established process limits. **Source:** B015.

### Experimental Task 4: Qualify transport bridges and measure field, temperature and angular critical current

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Transport preparation and measurement
Use proposed 50 μm-wide, 3 mm-long bridges with **2 mm voltage-tap spacing**, subject to layout verification. Qualify lithography/etching on sacrificial material; do not assume the draft's nitric-acid exposure is selective. Measure width, thickness and tap spacing after processing; proposed dimensional uncertainty is ≤5%. Validate contact processing against an adjacent untreated coupon.

At nominal dimensions, area is 2.70×10⁻⁷ cm²; 13.2 MA cm⁻² requires **3.56 A**. A 1 A ceiling limits Jc to 3.70 MA cm⁻² and cannot test the target. Qualify a source, leads and contacts for at least 5 A; determine a safe sample-specific current ceiling because self-field Ic may be higher. Otherwise use a separately qualified narrower bridge and disclose geometry effects.

For 2 mm taps, the 1 μV cm⁻¹ criterion is **0.20 μV**. Proposed rms voltage noise must be ≤0.02 μV. Use shielded low-thermal-emf wiring, current reversal/offset correction and contact-resistance monitoring.

Stabilize each temperature/field until drift meets the table limits. Acquire I–V curves at B parallel to c and at 45° and 90°. Use a pilot-qualified stepped or pulsed current schedule, recording dwell/pulse duration and duty cycle; verify ramp-rate independence and stop on heating, unstable voltage or contact degradation. The draft's 0.1 mA/s would require approximately 9.9 h to reach 3.56 A and is not adopted.

Measure angular Jc at proposed 1 and 3 T, 4 and 77 K, with θ=0° parallel to c and 90° parallel to ab. Choose rotation geometry keeping B perpendicular to current throughout and verify it. Seek directional pinning peaks and angular minimum Jc, not prescribed isotropy. Report Ic and Jc=Ic/(wt) with uncertainties and complete I–V curves.

#### Related Knowledge

- Bridge and contact processing require sacrificial qualification and comparison with an adjacent untreated coupon. Measured post-processing width, thickness and voltage-tap spacing determine current-density normalization. **Source:** B016, B020.
- The nominal bridge requires 3.56 A to test the supplied current-density target, while the voltage criterion corresponds to 0.20 μV. Current capacity, safe compliance and low-noise voltage measurement therefore require explicit qualification. **Source:** B017, B018.
- A qualified stepped or pulsed schedule replaces the rejected slow ramp. Verified angular geometry keeps field perpendicular to current, and measurements seek directional peaks and angular minima rather than assuming isotropy. **Source:** B019, B020.

### Experimental Task 5: Measure magnetic response, consolidate comparisons and archive controlled outcomes

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Magnetization and analysis
Measure dimensioned adjacent rectangular coupons and matched substrate blanks at proposed 4, 10, 20 and 77 K. Record full ascending/descending loops, sweep rate and relaxation checks. Normalize corrected moment to superconducting-film volume, not substrate volume.

For B parallel to c, use Jc=20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)] with ΔM in emu cm⁻³, in-plane a<b in cm, giving A cm⁻². Verify thin-film geometry, demagnetization and current-loop assumptions. Calibrate against transport over overlapping 0–5 T conditions; a proposed >15% discrepancy triggers investigation, not automatic rescaling. State effective electric-field/time-scale differences and reject an unsupported Bean conversion.

Fit Jc∝B⁻α only over a declared, preselected power-law interval; report confidence intervals and transport/magnetization fits separately. Add proposed low-field points 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.40, 0.55, 0.75 and 1 T. Define B* before analysis; absent provenance, report the curve without labeling B* an irreversibility field.

Calculate Fp=JcB for the appropriate perpendicular-current geometry. At 13.2 MA cm⁻² and 5 T, Fp=660 GN m⁻³, approximately 1.5% below supplied 670; treat these as rounded targets, not identical exact results. Evaluate the supplied 551 mT target at 10 K and also retain 4 K data because the brief conflicts.

##### 8. Gates, safety, records and controlled feedback
Release only traceable, intact films with qualified geometry and instrumentation. Quarantine recipe/texture/thickness failures; retain their diagnostic data. Morphology or performance below supplied targets is an experimental outcome, not grounds for exclusion.

Diagnose surface CuO/RECuO2 by SEM/EDS/diffraction; check delivery calibration before attributing it to composition. For poor texture, inspect substrate mapping and thermal history; for shortened rods, compare RE content and interface structure. For low Jc, examine oxygenation, bridge damage, contacts and heating before changing growth. For magnetic disagreement, check blank subtraction, volume and loop geometry.

Use precursor containment and exhaust abatement, oxygen-compatible gas hardware, leak/pressure interlocks, hot-reel guarding, cryogenic ventilation/oxygen-deficiency monitoring, magnet exclusion zones, current-compliance shutdown and approved FIB/chemical procedures.

Archive raw reactor logs, geometry, composition, diffraction/images, segmentation, GPA settings, I–V curves, magnetic loops and analysis scripts. Record uncertainty, exclusions and all hand-offs. Change one process variable only during qualification; freeze it for the full matrix. Any corrective recipe change requires a new block with repeated center and relevant controls.

##### 9. Optional extension
Only after the core matrix, obtain verified >20 T, 4 K access and a documented 15% Zr-doped GdYBCO comparator with matched geometry and measurement conditions. This is additional testing: 14 T magnetization or extrapolation cannot establish the supplied >20 T comparison or coil suitability.

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched substrate blanks and superconducting-film volume normalization are required for magnetic interpretation. Bean conversion remains conditional on geometry and current-loop assumptions and is checked against overlapping transport measurements. **Source:** B021, B022.
- Power-law fits require a declared interval and separate transport and magnetic results. The meaning of B* remains unresolved without provenance, and supplied pinning-force values are rounded reference targets rather than identical exact results. **Source:** B023, B024.
- Release gates distinguish qualification failures from legitimate morphology or performance outcomes. Diagnostic checks, safety prerequisites, archived raw records and new comparison blocks for corrective recipe changes preserve controlled interpretation. **Source:** B025, B026, B027, B028.
- The >20 T comparison is optional after the core matrix and requires verified access plus a documented matched comparator. Measurements to 14 T or extrapolation cannot establish that comparison or coil suitability. **Source:** B029.
