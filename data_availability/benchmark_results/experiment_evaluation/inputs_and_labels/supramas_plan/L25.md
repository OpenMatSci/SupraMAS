# Experimental Plan: Matched granular Y-123/Dy-123 transition protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Reconstructed experimental overview

Compare bulk, randomly oriented granular YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋δ and DyBa₂Cu₃O₇₋δ ceramics using matched processing, oxygenation, specimen geometry, contacts and field-cooled measurement histories. The objective is to quantify the grain-transition onset, operational Josephson-network zero-resistance boundary and intervening dissipative width. Fixed-current resistivity does not directly establish internal magnetic fields, critical current or pinning efficiency.

The proposed design uses three independent matched batch pairs, each providing a transport bar and structural, microscopy and oxygen-assay witnesses sharing the final thermal history. The proposed field grid is 0, 250, 500, 1000, 1500 and 1900 Oe, with independently reset repeats at 0, 250 and 1900 Oe. Lock this design before acquisition and alternate composition order across pairs.

Required resources include documented oxide/carbonate precursors, clean milling tools, a rated uniaxial press, oxygen-controlled furnace, low-damage saw, dimensional metrology, DRON UM-1 Cu Kα diffraction, SEM/EDS, validated oxygen analysis and calibrated four-probe cryogenic transport equipment with permanent-magnet field control.

Prepare stoichiometric ceramics, perform the required 950 °C/24 h treatment and 500 °C final oxidation, characterize matched witnesses, and release qualified bars before applying Ag contacts. Milling, preparatory calcination, oxidation dwell, oxygen delivery and other proposed settings require qualification rather than automatic adoption. Pressing is blocked until the reported pressure unit and equipment ratings are authorized.

Acquire transverse-field resistivity at 2 A cm⁻² using actual geometry and a shared nominal cooling rate of 0.003 K s⁻¹. Anticipated outputs are qualified specimen records, resistivity curves, explicit 77 K values and paired transition comparisons with uncertainty and batch scatter. Supplied performance values remain reference targets, never measured outcomes. Optional history, orientation or magnetometry work follows the core comparison and cannot establish internal-field causality by itself.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Define matched batches, qualify resources and authorize shared conditions

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and sample matrix
Prepare bulk ReBa₂Cu₃O₇₋δ ceramics (Re = Y, Dy), retaining random granular texture and approximately 1 µm grains. Compare field-cooled resistive transitions to test whether Dy-associated magnetism depresses the Josephson-network transition and enlarges dissipation below the grain transition. The experiment does not directly measure internal field or establish critical-current/pinning efficiency from fixed-current resistivity alone.

Use three independent matched batch pairs, identified `Y-B01`/`Dy-B01` through `B03` **(proposed replication)**. Give each batch a transport bar `-T`, powder witness `-X`, microscopy witness `-S`, and oxygen-assay witness `-O`. All witnesses must share final thermal history. Record parent–child links and masses. No substrate, CIP, Pt additives, excess Ba or substitution intermediates enter the core comparison.

| Samples | FC fields, Oe | Measurements |
|---|---|---|
| Every Y and Dy transport bar | 0, 250, 500, 1000, 1500, 1900 | Cooling ρ(T); explicit 77 K value |
| Every bar | Repeat 0, 250, 1900 | Independent thermal reset and repeatability |
| Every batch's witnesses | Not applicable | Powder XRD, SEM/EDS, oxygen assay |

Use the same field sequence within each pair, alternating which composition is measured first across pairs. Proposed field grid and repeat count must be fixed before acquisition.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and authorization
Use documented high-purity Y₂O₃, Dy₂O₃, BaCO₃, CuO, ethanol, Ag paste with a verified low-temperature cure, and oxygen. Equipment: analytical balance; clean milling/grinding tools; compatible crucibles; oxygen-controlled 950 °C furnace; rated guarded uniaxial press/die; low-damage diamond saw; dimensional metrology; DRON UM-1 with Cu Kα and diffraction standard; SEM/EDS; validated iodometric titration or equivalent oxygen assay; cryostat with Nd₂Fe₁₄B magnet pairs; calibrated Hall probe and sample-adjacent thermometer; DC source and low-noise voltmeter, such as the draft's Keithley 2400/2182A after capability checks.

**Pressing hold point:** 650 kgf mm⁻² = 6374 MPa. A proposed 10 mm diameter pellet requires F = PA ≈ 501 kN (about 51 tonne-force). This is not interchangeable with a 2–3 tonne load or 650 kgf cm⁻². Obtain confirmation of the stated unit and certified die/press ratings before pressing. If corrected by the task owner, record authorization and apply one common pressure to both arms; otherwise do not proceed.

Verify cryostat operation over 50–100 K, stable ultra-slow cooling, and fields through 1900 Oe at the sample. Permanent-magnet gaps, not assumed electrical field ramps, set the field. In air/vacuum, 250 Oe corresponds approximately to 0.025 T and 1900 Oe to 0.190 T; do not call these internal sample fields.

##### 3. Consolidated conditions
Only entries marked **task** are supplied requirements. Other entries are proposed settings or draft-derived starting choices requiring calibration on sacrificial paired material.

| Operation | Shared setting | Tolerance/verification |
|---|---|---|
| Main reaction/sinter | **Task:** 950 °C, 24 h, cooling 5 °C min⁻¹ | Proposed sample-zone ±2 °C, dwell ±10 min, cooling ±0.5 °C min⁻¹ |

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched batch pairs, linked witnesses and alternating measurement order provide the prescribed comparison framework; replication, field grid and repeat choices remain proposed until fixed before acquisition. **Source:** B000, B001, B002, B003.
- The reported pressing pressure entails a specific equipment-rating and unit hold point; a smaller nominal load or different pressure unit is not an interchangeable implementation. **Source:** B005.
- The consolidated conditions distinguish supplied requirements from proposed settings. Permanent-magnet gaps require sample-position field qualification, and external field values are not internal sample fields. **Source:** B004, B006, B007, B008.

### Experimental Task 2: Fabricate paired ceramics and complete shared final oxygenation

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Ceramic fabrication and hand-off
1. Calculate precursor amounts from n(Re₂O₃):n(BaCO₃):n(CuO) = 0.5:2:3. State whether batch mass denotes precursor or finished ceramic mass; calculate each composition independently using assay-corrected molar masses. Do not use the draft's inconsistent fixed gram quantities or Ba excess.
2. Mix with clean zirconia tools. Draft-derived starting choice: ethanol milling, 12 h, 300 rpm, media:powder mass ratio 10:1; dry at 80 °C for 6 h. Validate contamination and particle-size effects before locking this recipe; use identical conditions for both compositions.
3. Proposed carbonate-removal preparation: calcine at 850 °C for 6 h, regrind, then 900 °C for 6 h under the shared oxygen program; cool at 5 °C min⁻¹. Inspect powder XRD and mass evolution on pilot batches. These preparatory holds are not supplied task conditions.
4. Press binder-free powder into pellets only after pressure authorization. Record mass, die area, force trace, dwell and release procedure. Pressing alone does not determine final grain size. Reject cracked green pellets; omit additional densification steps.
5. Fire at 950 °C for 24 h using the consolidated program, cool at 5 °C min⁻¹, and inspect for cracks/warping. Cut transport bars and reserve witnesses before final oxygenation; support fragile ceramics and avoid water exposure. Measure preliminary dimensions.
6. Oxidize both compositions and witnesses at 500 °C using the calibrated shared dwell/oxygen program, then cool at approximately 5 °C min⁻¹ without quenching. Store dry. Confirm oxygenation is stable on sacrificial material before production.
7. **Fabrication → characterization:** transfer labeled specimens with weighing, pressing, furnace and dimensional records. No specimen proceeds to contacts until characterization release.

#### Related Knowledge

- Composition-specific precursor calculations use the stated 0.5:2:3 molar ratio and assay corrections, avoiding inconsistent fixed masses or barium excess. **Source:** B009.
- Milling and preparatory calcination are starting choices requiring qualification, whereas the principal reaction and final oxidation conditions retain their supplied status. Cutting before final oxygenation allows bars and witnesses to share that final treatment. **Source:** B008, B009.
- Press authorization, crack inspection, specimen lineage and the fabrication-to-characterization hand-off prevent unresolved fabrication conditions from becoming an accepted production recipe. **Source:** B005, B009.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize structure, grain matching and oxygen content for release

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural, microstructural and oxygen release
Collect powder XRD from representative annealed witnesses over 15–75° 2θ at 0.01° increments. Measure a standard for zero offset and instrumental response; refine Pmmm lattice parameters, secondary phases, uncertainty and fit residuals. Cu Kα₁ λ = 1.5406 Å is applicable only if the optical configuration warrants it; otherwise model the Kα doublet.

Use supplied lattice values as comparison references, not acceptance windows: Y (a,b,c) = (3.828,3.8947,11.7286) Å; Dy = (3.8341,3.898,11.721) Å. The convention Δ = 2(b−a)/(b+a) gives approximately 0.0173 and 0.0165, consistent with the supplied distortion values. Equal structure does not require identical peak positions. For orthorhombic indexing, d⁻² = h²/a²+k²/b²+l²/c²; (005) has d = c/5 ≈2.345 Å and 2θ ≈38.4°. Its shift alone cannot certify δ or 10⁻⁴ Å accuracy.

SEM: examine polished/fractured witnesses, not coated transport bars. Draft starting settings are 15 kV and 10 mm working distance, adjusted for imaging quality. Quantify at least 100 grains from several fields; record distribution, porosity, cracks and EDS cation maps. EDS oxygen is not a quantitative δ assay; nanometre junction thickness is not established by routine SEM.

Assay oxygen on annealed witnesses using a validated method with blanks, standards and propagated uncertainty; verify applicability to Dy material. Do not impose δ = 0.10 from the draft. Require paired δ agreement within combined uncertainty, unless a stricter validated bound was preregistered.

Release only specimens with Pmmm-compatible patterns, no significant unmatched secondary phase, comparable approximately 1 µm grain distributions and no current-path cracks. Predeclare quantitative matching bounds from pilot repeatability. Unmatched batches are quarantined, not selectively discarded according to Tc. Retain all records and repeat complete paired preparation when necessary.

#### Related Knowledge

- Diffraction standards and an appropriate Cu Kα optical model support lattice refinement; supplied lattice parameters are comparison references, and a single peak shift cannot certify oxygen content or the targeted accuracy. **Source:** B010, B011.
- SEM/EDS supports grain-distribution and cation-homogeneity assessment but does not quantify oxygen deficiency or establish nanometre boundary thickness. Oxygen matching instead requires a validated assay and propagated uncertainty. **Source:** B012, B013.
- Release depends on phase, grain, crack and oxygen matching with predeclared bounds; unmatched material is quarantined rather than selected according to its transition temperature. **Source:** B013, B014.

### Experimental Task 4: Apply qualified Ag contacts and establish geometry-normalized transport

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Contacts and electrical hand-off
After final annealing, clean contact areas gently with ethanol and dry; avoid aggressive polishing or ultrasonic treatment of fragile bars. Apply four narrow Ag bands, proposed width ≤0.3 mm, centered at 0.5, 2.5, 5.5 and 7.5 mm along an 8 mm bar. Outer bands inject current across the ends; inner bands sense voltage. Measure effective voltage spacing and photograph the geometry. Four 5 mm-spaced contacts cannot fit a 10 mm pellet.

Use a manufacturer-verified cure compatible with oxygen preservation; 150 °C for 30 min is a draft-derived starting choice, not a guaranteed cure. Check adhesion, absence of bridging and lead strain relief. Adjacent two-terminal resistance includes ceramic resistance and is not an isolated contact-resistance measurement. Check source compliance, contact stability and local heating instead.

**Characterization → transport:** transfer released bar, geometry/contact map, structural/oxygen results and calibration status. For A = 0.5 mm² = 0.005 cm², I = jA =10 mA. For actual A, set I accordingly; never force both 10 mA and 2 A cm⁻² on incompatible geometry. This setup does not test the draft's 10⁴ A cm⁻² claim, which would require 50 A at this area.

#### Related Knowledge

- Ag contacts follow final annealing and characterization release. Their effective voltage spacing and geometry must be measured rather than inferred from an incompatible contact layout. **Source:** B009, B015, B017.
- The contact cure requires manufacturer verification and oxygen compatibility; adjacent two-terminal resistance includes the ceramic and is not an isolated contact-resistance measurement. **Source:** B016.
- Current is derived from the measured cross-sectional area at the required current density. The example 10 mA value is geometry-dependent and does not establish high-current-density performance. **Source:** B017.

### Experimental Task 5: Acquire field-cooled resistivity and extract qualified transition boundaries

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Field-cooled acquisition and transition analysis
1. Mount with field perpendicular to current and, proposed, normal to the broad bar face. Random ceramics have no single pellet ab-plane or c-axis. Use twisted shielded leads, electrical isolation and a nearby thermometer. Map field over the active volume at each gap; record residual field for nominal zero.
2. Verify temperature lag and voltage noise with sacrificial runs. Compute ρ = VA/(IL), using consistent units. Proposed operational zero criterion: ρcrit =1 µΩ cm. At j =2 A cm⁻² and L =0.3 cm, Ecrit =2 µV cm⁻¹ and Vcrit =0.6 µV. Require offset-corrected voltage noise ≤0.2 µV; otherwise improve measurement sensitivity or declare Tc2J unresolved rather than changing the criterion retrospectively.
3. Warm to 100 K and confirm a normal-state baseline above grain onset. Set the magnet gap while normal; proposed stabilization is 10 min after thermal/field stability. Reset above the transition before every field, including repeats. Do not insert minor field loops into core FC runs.
4. Cool 100→50 K at 0.003 K s⁻¹, recording every 0.1 K with timestamp, T, field, actual I, voltage and compliance. A full sweep takes approximately 4.63 h; each 0.1 K interval allows about 33 s for averaging. Use paired positive/negative DC readings to remove thermoelectric offsets and log both raw values. At 77 K, retain the same FC history and record a stabilized value.
5. Proposed Tc1g definition: departure below an extrapolated normal-state baseline exceeding three times baseline residual noise for three consecutive points. Tc2J is the highest cooling temperature below which ρ remains below ρcrit for three points, with noise qualification. Report both criteria, interpolation, uncertainties, derivative curves and W = Tc1g−Tc2J. Unresolved two-stage separation must be stated, not manufactured from inflections.

#### Related Knowledge

- Field mapping, residual-field recording and resetting above the transition preserve a defined external-field and thermal history; random ceramics do not supply a single crystallographic field orientation. **Source:** B006, B018.
- Actual geometry, paired current polarities and qualified voltage noise support resistivity normalization and the proposed operational zero-resistance criterion. **Source:** B017, B018.
- Explicit onset and zero-resistance definitions support reporting of transition width and uncertainty. Insufficient sensitivity or unresolved two-stage separation must be reported rather than repaired by retrospective criterion changes. **Source:** B018.

### Experimental Task 6: Review paired results, control failures and preserve safety and records

#### Specific Content

##### 8. Dispositions, records and controlled feedback
Archive raw files, calibration certificates, recipes, specimen lineage, dimensions, contacts, oxygen assays, fits, extraction code and every failed/repeated run. Compare paired field-dependent Tc2J, W and ρ(77 K), with batch scatter and uncertainty. The supplied 250 Oe temperatures, approximately 8 K broadening and 77 K Y behavior are comparison observations, not results of this proposal.

Contact drift/compliance or polarity asymmetry: stop, inspect wiring/adhesion, repair under the locked cure and repeat the full FC run. Temperature lag or rate dependence: repeat selected paired fields at 0.002 K s⁻¹. Unexpected broadening: check cracks, oxygen and phase/microstructure matching; do not reject merely for disagreeing with supplied observations. Any processing correction must be tested on both compositions, documented and locked before a new paired series.

Use contained powder handling for barium/copper compounds; flammable-solvent controls; guarded press operation; oxygen-compatible regulators, ventilation and hot-furnace protection; cryogenic face/hand protection; magnet pinch/projectile exclusion and implant precautions. Do not seal flowing-oxygen furnaces or operate unrated dies.

#### Related Knowledge

- Paired field-dependent transition boundaries, widths and 77 K resistivity are compared with batch scatter and uncertainty; supplied comparison observations are not outcomes of this work. **Source:** B019.
- Electrical instability, temperature lag and unexpected broadening have specified dispositions. Processing corrections apply to both compositions and require documentation and locking before a new paired series. **Source:** B020.
- Powder, solvent, press, oxygen-furnace, cryogenic and magnet controls are prerequisites throughout execution, including the prohibition on unrated dies and sealed flowing-oxygen furnaces. **Source:** B005, B021.

### Experimental Task 7: Conduct optional paired history, orientation or magnetic extensions

#### Specific Content

##### 9. Optional extensions
After completing core controls, paired ZFC or warming runs may assess history dependence; paired rotations relative to bar axes may test macroscopic isotropy. These are not single-crystal H∥c/H∥ab tests. Independently calibrated magnetometry may examine Dy-associated susceptibility, but superconducting screening complicates low-temperature interpretation. Optional work neither replaces FC comparisons nor proves internal-field causality by itself.

#### Related Knowledge

- Optional paired zero-field-cooled or warming runs address history dependence only after core controls are complete; they do not replace the field-cooled comparison. **Source:** B022.
- Rotations relative to bar axes concern macroscopic isotropy rather than single-crystal orientations. Independently calibrated magnetometry has screening-related interpretation limits and cannot alone prove internal-field causality. **Source:** B022.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Consolidated execution plan

#### 1. Lock the comparison and qualification prerequisites
Prepare matched bulk, randomly oriented Y-123/Dy-123 ceramics targeting comparable approximately 1 µm grains. Retain the proposed three independent batch pairs, linked transport and XRD/SEM/oxygen witnesses, field grid of 0, 250, 500, 1000, 1500 and 1900 Oe, and independently reset repeats at 0, 250 and 1900 Oe. Fix proposed choices before acquisition; use the same field sequence within pairs and alternate composition order across pairs. Exclude substrates, additives, excess Ba, substitution intermediates and additional densification.

Before execution, establish powder containment, solvent controls, guarded press operation, oxygen-compatible furnace services, ventilation, thermal and cryogenic protection, and magnet exclusion/implant precautions. Do not seal flowing-oxygen furnaces. Pressing remains blocked until the reported 650 kg mm⁻² unit and certified press/die ratings are confirmed. Any owner-authorized correction must be recorded and shared by both arms; otherwise stop.

#### 2. Fabricate and release matched specimens
Calculate assay-corrected precursor quantities independently at Re₂O₃:BaCO₃:CuO = 0.5:2:3, stating the batch-mass basis. Qualify the proposed milling, drying and preparatory calcination choices on sacrificial paired material. Likewise qualify shared ramps, oxygen delivery, oxidation dwell and proposed tolerances; do not treat them as supplied requirements.

After pressing authorization, retain force and specimen records and reject cracked green pellets. Apply 950 °C for 24 h followed by 5 °C min⁻¹ cooling. Inspect, cut supported transport bars and reserve witnesses before final oxidation. Oxidize at 500 °C using the qualified shared program, then cool at approximately 5 °C min⁻¹ without quenching. Confirm oxygenation stability on sacrificial material and preserve common final thermal history and lineage.

Collect DRON UM-1 Cu Kα XRD over 15–75° 2θ in 0.01° increments with a standard and appropriate spectral model. Report Pmmm refinement, secondary phases, residuals and uncertainty; 10⁻⁴ Å remains an accuracy target. Quantify at least 100 grains across several SEM fields, recording distributions, porosity, cracks and EDS cation maps. Use validated oxygen analysis applicable to Dy material, not EDS oxygen or an imposed δ. Release only phase-compatible, crack-free, comparably granular pairs with oxygen agreement within combined uncertainty and predeclared matching bounds. Quarantine mismatches without selecting by transition temperature.

#### 3. Contact, acquire and analyze
Apply identical narrow Ag contacts only after final annealing and characterization release. Verify the oxygen-compatible cure rather than assuming the proposed cure is adequate. Photograph contacts, measure actual cross-section and voltage spacing, and check adhesion, bridging, strain relief, compliance and heating. Set current from actual area at 2 A cm⁻²; 10 mA applies only to 0.005 cm².

Qualify cryostat operation, temperature lag, field mapping, residual field and voltage sensitivity. Use transverse external field set by magnet gaps; the proposed broad-face orientation is not a crystallographic orientation. Warm to 100 K, establish the normal baseline, set the gap and stabilize before each independently reset field-cooled run. Cool to 50 K at nominal 0.003 K s⁻¹ within the required 0.002–0.004 range. Proposed sampling is 0.1 K. Log timestamps, temperature, field, actual current, compliance and both current-polarity voltages; retain a stabilized 77 K value with unchanged history. Calculate ρ = VA/(IL).

Retain explicit proposed onset and operational-zero definitions: three-point onset above baseline noise and three-point persistence below 1 µΩ cm, respectively. Qualify offset-corrected voltage noise at ≤0.2 µV; otherwise improve sensitivity or report Tc2J unresolved. Report criteria, interpolation, uncertainties, derivatives and W = Tc1g − Tc2J without manufacturing two-stage separation.

#### 4. Disposition and reporting
Compare paired Tc2J, W and ρ(77 K), including batch scatter. Supplied performance values are reference targets only. Stop for contact instability, compliance or polarity asymmetry; repair under the locked cure and repeat the complete run. Address lag with selected paired repeats at 0.002 K s⁻¹; investigate broadening through matching checks, not outcome-based rejection. Archive all raw, failed and repeated runs and supporting records. Lock any shared processing correction before a new series. Optional history, rotation or calibrated magnetometry follows core controls and cannot independently establish internal-field causality, critical current or pinning efficiency.
