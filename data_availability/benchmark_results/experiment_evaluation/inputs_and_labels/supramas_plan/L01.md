# Experimental Plan: YBCO–BHO coated-conductor protocol proposal

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Establish the comparison and qualify readiness

Determine how pristine BHO size and loading relate to coarsening, embedded particle density, defects, texture, self-field connectivity and field-dependent pinning in YBCO coated conductors. Preserve preformed spherical BHO embedded above a pure YBCO seed, without nanorods or separate BHO layers. The proposed comparison comprises 3, 5.5 and 10 nm particles at 5, 10, 15 and 20 mol%, plus matched undoped controls, across three independent fabrication blocks. Confirm the mol% denominator before dosing.

Use certified LaMnO3/MgO/Y2O3/Al2O3/Hastelloy templates, qualified low-fluorine YBCO MOD precursor, BHO synthesis reagents, controlled-atmosphere reel-to-reel equipment, microscopy, diffraction and calibrated magnetic/transport instrumentation. Approve synthesis, atmosphere, residence-time and cooling procedures before execution. All P settings remain proposed until pilot qualification and freezing.

### Stage II — Fabricate and allocate matched tapes

Qualify polyol-solvothermal BHO colloids, prepare assayed formulations, release templates and pure YBCO seeds, then build composite films through repeated MOD coating and multistep epitaxial growth at 785 °C with intermediate cooling. Confirm the proposed interpretation of 2.4 µm total superconducting thickness including the seed. Preserve matched cycle counts, thermal histories and longitudinal provenance; allocate adjacent electrical, magnetic, microscopy and archive coupons.

### Stage III — Characterize, measure and consolidate

Use XRD, FIB/HRTEM/STEM and GPA to assess retained texture, particle populations, strain and defects. Measure magnetic loops, self-field transport and angular transport only within verified field, current and voltage capabilities. Apply substrate-background subtraction, actual film dimensions, the 1 µV/cm transport criterion and explicit uncertainty. Outputs are traceable datasets, matched comparisons and documented failures—not presumed reproduction of supplied performance targets. Complete core analysis before any optional 100 m extension; enforce the source safety prerequisites throughout.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage I — Define the size/loading comparison and dosing convention

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and comparison matrix
Determine how pristine BHO size and loading affect coarsening, embedded particle density, defects, texture, self-field connectivity and field-dependent pinning. Preserve spherical, embedded BHO rather than introducing nanorods or separate BHO layers. The supplied performance and microstructural values are comparison targets, not acceptance specifications or newly measured results.

| Arm | Pristine BHO size | Loading | Replication |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| U | None | 0 | One matched control per fabrication block |
| S03-Cxx | 3 nm | 5, 10, 15, 20 mol% | Three independent blocks |
| S055-Cxx | 5.5 nm | 5, 10, 15, 20 mol% | Three independent blocks |
| S10-Cxx | 10 nm | 5, 10, 15, 20 mol% | Three independent blocks |

This 13-arm, 39-run matrix and replication are proposed. Identify tapes as `B01-S055-C10`, etc.; append longitudinal position and `TR` (transport), `MAG`, `TEM`, or `ARC` (archive). Record synthesis lot separately. Independently prepare size lots in each block where feasible; otherwise identify synthesis-lot confounding. Randomize arm order and bracket coating runs with undoped witnesses. Use one qualified template lot and identical seed, thickness, coating-cycle count and thermal history throughout.

Before dosing, confirm the source mol% convention. If unavailable, obtain scientific approval for a declared proposed convention, such as 100 n(BHO)/n(YBCO formula units); do not imply equivalence to the supplied loading values.

#### Related Knowledge

- The comparison isolates pristine particle size and loading while retaining spherical embedded BHO; supplied performance and microstructural values are reference targets rather than acceptance specifications or measured outcomes. **Source:** B000, B001.
- Independent fabrication blocks, matched undoped witnesses, randomized arm order and synthesis-lot records support interpretable comparisons without treating coupon repeats as independent fabrication. **Source:** B002.
- Loading cannot be reproduced faithfully until the mol% convention is confirmed; an approved proposed convention must remain explicitly distinguished from the supplied loading values. **Source:** B003.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage I — Release materials, templates and equipment readiness

#### Specific Content

##### 2. Materials, equipment and readiness
Use certified biaxially textured LaMnO3/MgO/Y2O3/Al2O3/Hastelloy templates, preferably 12 mm wide for direct full-width comparison. Do not reconstruct the buffer stack using the draft's unqualified recipes. Verify layer order and template certificates.

Materials: Ba acetate, HfCl4, ethylene glycol, oleic acid, PVP, ethanol/acetone; qualified low-fluorine YBCO MOD precursor with matrix Y:Ba:Cu = 1:2:3; compatible dispersion solvent; high-purity O2/N2; contact metal, lithography consumables and FIB protective coating. Record actual purity, water content, fluorine content and metal concentration. Added BHO Ba must not inadvertently alter matrix stoichiometry.

Equipment: rated solvothermal reactor, centrifuge, sonicator, viscometer, TEM/DLS, ICP-OES and powder XRD; reel-to-reel coater and controlled-atmosphere furnaces; profilometry/SEM, AFM, XRD with rocking/azimuthal scans; FIB, HRTEM/HAADF-STEM/EDS and GPA; SQUID-VSM, M-Corder-compatible high-current transport electronics, nanovoltmeter and transverse-field rotator.

**Release prerequisite:** approve qualified synthesis and low-fluorine MOD recipes, including gas composition, pressure, humidity, furnace residence time and intermediate-cooling endpoints. The draft does not establish these. Furnace temperature capability alone is insufficient. Calibrate temperature at tape position, flowmeters, thickness, current, voltage, field and angular zero; document uncertainty and expiry.

#### Related Knowledge

- Certified templates preserve the complete specified buffer stack without substituting unqualified buffer-fabrication recipes. **Source:** B004.
- Assayed precursor composition and material records protect the YBCO matrix stoichiometry when BHO contributes additional Ba. **Source:** B005.
- Instrument availability does not establish process readiness: qualified recipes, tape-position calibration and documented measurement uncertainty are release prerequisites. **Source:** B006, B007.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage I — Qualify and freeze operating conditions

#### Specific Content

##### 3. Consolidated operating parameters
All values marked P are proposed starting settings requiring pilot qualification; they are not task-supplied conditions. Freeze approved settings before the comparison matrix.

| Stage | Setting and tolerance/disposition |
|---|---|
| BHO synthesis, P | 0.5 mmol each Ba acetate/HfCl4; 20 mL glycol, 1 mL oleic acid, 0.1 g PVP; stir 60 °C, 300 rpm, 30 min; screen 180–220 °C and 4–12 h |
| Particle release, P | TEM mean within ±10% of size label; CV ≤20%; no dominant aggregate population; phase/stoichiometry verified |
| Thickness | Task: 2.4 µm; P: total including 250 nm seed, seed allowed 200–300 nm; total 2.4 ±0.2 µm |
| Dispersion/coating, P | 40 kHz sonication up to 30 min with temperature monitoring; mix 300 rpm, 2 h; viscosity 30–50 mPa·s at 25 °C; 50 µm blade gap, initially 1 m/min |
| Dry/pyrolyze, P | 150 °C/5 min; initial screen 250 °C/10 min then 400 °C/15 min; ramp ≤5 °C/min; atmosphere and humidity qualified separately |
| Epitaxy | Task: 785 °C, multistep growth with intermediate cooling; P: ±5 °C at tape, initially 60 min per qualified growth stage |
| Oxygenation/cooling, P | Final 500 °C/2 h in flowing O2 at recorded pressure; 5 °C/min to oxygenation, 1 ±0.2 °C/min thereafter |
| Magnetic testing, P | 4.2, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 77 K; stability ±0.1 K; H parallel c; initially 0.1 T/min |
| Angular transport, P | 77 K; 1, 3, 5 T; 0–360° in 10° steps; alignment ±0.5°; θ=0 means field parallel c |

Do not substitute 200 sccm O2 for a specified oxygen partial pressure. Approve total pressure, pO2, water activity and exhaust flow independently. Determine coating shrinkage experimentally: neither a 50 µm gap nor a 2.4 µm wet coating establishes 2.4 µm crystalline thickness. At 1 m/min, a 60 min continuous dwell requires 60 m of effective hot path; use qualified multipass/stop-and-go processing if that path is unavailable.

#### Related Knowledge

- The consolidated parameter table distinguishes required conditions from P starting settings; proposed synthesis, coating, thermal and measurement settings require pilot qualification before the comparison matrix. **Source:** B008, B009.
- Oxygen flow is not oxygen partial pressure, and blade gap or wet thickness does not establish crystalline film thickness; these quantities require separate qualification or measurement. **Source:** B010.
- Line speed and residence time must be physically compatible with the available heated path; qualified multipass or stop-and-go processing is the stated alternative when necessary. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage II — Synthesize BHO, formulate MOD precursors and fabricate matched tapes

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Synthesize and qualify BHO.** Handle HfCl4 under dry inert gas. Prepare the pilot solution above, load within reactor fill/pressure limits, react and cool fully before opening. Purify by three ethanol/acetone washes, initially 3:1 by volume; convert the draft's 8000 rpm to rotor-specific relative centrifugal force and validate particle recovery. Prefer a stable redispersion over drying if drying causes aggregation. Determine dry-equivalent solids by an approved assay. TEM defines core diameter; DLS checks aggregation, not a interchangeable core-size measurement. Use XRD/electron diffraction and ICP-OES to confirm BHO and Ba:Hf. This chemistry is only a candidate: incomplete perovskite formation requires synthesis redevelopment, not release. **Hand-off:** released colloid, size histogram, solids assay and synthesis history to precursor preparation.
2. **Prepare formulations.** Split one matrix-precursor lot into all loadings and an undoped control. Dose by the approved molar convention and assayed BHO content. Perform compatibility/aging checks, including microscopy or scattering before and after mixing. Validate solvent exchange and any dispersant removal. Do not filter away BHO without measuring composition loss. **Hand-off:** labeled formulation, mass balance, viscosity and dispersion release sheet to coating.
3. **Release templates and seed.** Inspect for scratches/delamination; measure baseline XRD texture and representative AFM roughness. Clean only by a template-compatible procedure; avoid unqualified plasma/vacuum anneals. Deposit pure YBCO seed using low-fluorine reel-to-reel MOD, dry, pyrolyze and crystallize with the qualified 785 °C schedule. Use sacrificial seed witnesses for thickness and texture. **Hand-off:** released seeded tape and furnace log to composite coating; quarantine failed seed runs.
4. **Build composite thickness.** Calibrate crystalline thickness per pass on witnesses. Repeat composite coating, drying, pyrolysis and qualified epitaxial growth stages, with documented intermediate cooling and identical cycle counts across arms. Record cumulative thermal exposure because it controls BHO coarsening. Match undoped overlayer processing. Complete final oxygenation/cooling; inspect thickness, cracks, secondary phases and texture. **Hand-off:** released finished tape and position map to sample allocation.
5. **Allocate adjacent coupons.** Reserve full-width transport pieces, rectangular magnetic coupons, center/edge microscopy coupons and untouched archives. Preserve longitudinal provenance. Measure electrical properties before destructive FIB preparation; never reuse a damaged coupon as an independent replicate.

#### Related Knowledge

- BHO release combines core-size microscopy, aggregation assessment, solids assay and phase/stoichiometry checks; incomplete perovskite formation requires redevelopment rather than particle release. **Source:** B011.
- Formulation hand-offs depend on the approved dosing convention, measured BHO content and compatibility checks, while seed release precedes composite coating. **Source:** B011.
- Measured crystalline thickness per pass, matched coating cycles and documented cumulative thermal exposure preserve the fabrication comparison; adjacent coupon allocation retains provenance and protects electrical measurements from destructive preparation. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage III — Characterize film quality, embedded particles and strain

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Characterization and quality gates
Acquire Cu Kα XRD initially over 2θ=20–80°, step 0.02°; use YBCO (005) rocking curves and accessible off-axis (103) φ scans with correct tilt. MgO (202) is symmetry-equivalent to (220); verify instrument geometry. BHO (110) may be weak/overlapped, so its absence alone does not establish decomposition. Proposed texture gate: Δω≤4°, Δφ≤7°, and no degradation beyond measurement uncertainty relative to matched undoped films. These replace conflicting draft thresholds and require pilot approval. Quarantine cracked, delaminated or out-of-thickness material; retain and report failures rather than silently removing arms.

Prepare FIB cross-sections with protective Pt, initial 30 kV milling and decreasing-energy polishing, ending near 1 kV where supported. Measure lamella thickness and damage; use beam-dose pilots before atomic imaging at 200–300 kV. Sample seed/interface, midlayer and surface, at center and edge. Proposed minimum: ten distributed fields and ≥200 particles per arm, increasing sampling if confidence intervals remain broad. Report size distributions, aspect ratio, aggregation, coherence, number density, Y248 intergrowth and fault statistics, including stereological/projection uncertainty.

For GPA define x/y axes and reference defect-free YBCO in the same image. Use two non-collinear, indexed reflections present in the chosen zone: for [100], verify accessible (020)/(002), rather than incompatible (103). Calibrate mask width and spatial resolution, test reference/mask sensitivity and report εxx/εyy uncertainty. Lamella bending, scan drift and ion damage must not be interpreted as strain.

#### Related Knowledge

- Texture assessment requires appropriate diffraction geometry and matched undoped comparisons; weak or overlapping BHO diffraction alone cannot establish decomposition, and the proposed texture gates require pilot approval. **Source:** B012.
- Distributed cross-sectional sampling and explicit lamella, dose and projection uncertainties support particle and defect statistics without assuming representative results from isolated images. **Source:** B013.
- GPA requires indexed accessible reflections, a same-image defect-free reference and sensitivity checks; bending, drift and ion damage must not be interpreted as strain. **Source:** B014.

### Experimental Task 6: Stage III — Measure magnetic, self-field and angular electrical response

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Magnetic and transport measurements
**Capability gate:** the listed ±7 T SQUID-VSM cannot measure 9 T. Obtain a verified ≥9 T magnetometer covering 4.2–77 K for complete loops, or explicitly label the 7–9 T dataset missing. Do not splice transport Jc into magnetic Jc without method distinction.

Measure matched uncoated-template blanks and holders at each temperature; Hastelloy/buffers can contribute magnetic hysteresis. Establish a valid subtraction and film-volume normalization. Record full ascending/descending loops, field history, sweep rate and flux jumps. For rectangular film dimensions a<b in cm and full branch difference ΔM in emu/cm³ of superconducting film, use Jc=20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)] in A/cm². Verify model applicability and substrate-background uncertainty; reject unsupported conversion. Retain actual measured field, distinguishing applied μ0H from internal B near self-field.

Measure M-Corder transport at 77 K self-field on full-width tape using four terminals and calibrated contact/current sharing. Proposed tap spacing 10 mm gives Vc=1 µV. Verify ≥1 kA current capability, leads, protection and cooling before pursuing >800 A. Ramp or pulse with validated settling/duty cycle, subtract offset, acquire both polarities and stop on heating, compliance or quench. Record Ic, criterion, n-value, contact resistance and uncertainty.

For angular tests use calibrated bridges or full-width tape within current capability; rotate field perpendicular to current to maintain maximum Lorentz force. Proposed bridges: 75±5 µm wide, actual tap spacing 3 mm; Vc=0.3 µV requires effective voltage uncertainty ≤0.03 µV. The draft's 0.1 µV sensitivity needs improvement or longer taps.

**Current checks:** 12 mm×2.4 µm gives 2.88×10⁻⁴ cm²; 827 A corresponds to 2.87 MA/cm². A 75 µm bridge gives 1.80×10⁻⁶ cm²: the supplied 20.8 and 8.7 MA/cm² imply 37.4 and 15.7 A respectively; the self-field equivalent is 5.17 A. Thus 100 mA scans, a 1 A source and a 10⁵ A/cm² ceiling cannot establish these currents. Qualify adequate electronics or report lower bounds. Patterning damage must be checked against adjacent unpatterned material.

#### Related Knowledge

- Magnetic measurements require verified field capability, matched template/holder backgrounds and superconducting-film-volume normalization before applying the stated Bean conversion. **Source:** B015, B016.
- Transport capability must be established using actual current demand, contact behavior and tap spacing; voltage criteria and uncertainty must support the specified electric-field criterion. **Source:** B017, B018, B019.
- Angular measurements preserve field perpendicular to current, and patterned bridges require comparison with adjacent unpatterned material to check patterning damage. **Source:** B018, B019.

### Experimental Task 7: Stage III — Analyze matched comparisons, document failures and gate optional scale-up

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Analysis, diagnosis and controlled feedback
Calculate lifting factor Jc,doped/Jc,undoped at identical temperature, field, orientation and method; calculate Fp=JcB in SI where B is justified. Do not multiply volumetric particle density by Φ0 to obtain a matching field. Report density directly; any effective field scale requires an explicit geometrical model. Define η before testing the supplied peak-near-3-T expectation.

Compare self-field loss, in-field gain and angular response against measured final particle/defect statistics. Evaluate the supplied 3→approximately 20 nm and 5.5→7±3 nm coarsening, density 20.5×10²¹ m⁻³ and electrical targets without assuming reproduction. Angular isotropy of particles does not imply isotropic total Jc. Strain/defect correlations alone cannot partition pinning energy or prove grain-boundary transparency.

Aggregation: inspect colloid compatibility and reformulate before coating. Cracks/carbon: qualify thinner passes, pyrolysis and gas/humidity control on new witnesses; do not blindly re-pyrolyze finished films. Low Ic: first check geometry, contacts, oxygenation, texture and secondary phases. Excess coarsening: audit cumulative dwell and dispersion. Noisy loops: check background, mounting and flux jumps; document any changed sweep/history.

Record raw images/loops/I–V curves, failed runs, calibration files, recipes, sample dimensions, software settings and uncertainty. Analyze independent fabrication blocks separately from coupon repeats. Freeze core conditions after pilots; changes require a new block with all matched controls.

##### 8. Risks and optional extension
Use pressure-rated reactor procedures, cooled depressurization, dry handling of HfCl4, solvent ventilation and hazardous-metal waste segregation. Furnace exhaust must accommodate fluorinated decomposition products; segregate oxygen from solvent-rich zones and use interlocks. Enforce cryogenic oxygen-deficiency, magnet/projectile, high-current/quench and FIB/TEM controls.

Only after core analysis, optionally run 100 m tapes of selected composition plus matched undoped tape. Sample every 10 m and start/middle/end texture; declare any proposed ≤5% uniformity goal and statistic beforehand. This extension must not replace the size/loading controls.

#### Related Knowledge

- Lifting factors require identical temperature, field, orientation and method; pinning-force and effective-field interpretations must respect the stated dimensional and geometrical limitations. **Source:** B020, B021.
- Diagnosis follows recorded material and measurement checks, while condition changes require a new fabrication block with matched controls; raw data, failures and calibration records remain part of the analysis. **Source:** B022, B023.
- The specified reactor, chemical, furnace, cryogenic, magnet, current and microscopy controls apply throughout execution. Long-length testing is optional, follows core analysis and cannot replace the size/loading controls. **Source:** B024, B025.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage I — Define the comparison and release qualified conditions

Execute the proposed 13-arm, 39-run comparison: pristine BHO sizes of 3, 5.5 and 10 nm, each at 5, 10, 15 and 20 mol%, with a matched undoped control in each of three independent fabrication blocks. Confirm the source mol% denominator before dosing; if unavailable, obtain approval for a declared proposed convention without claiming equivalence. Randomize arm order, bracket coating runs with undoped witnesses, retain synthesis-lot identities and identify any lot confounding.

Use one qualified lot of certified LaMnO3/MgO/Y2O3/Al2O3/Hastelloy templates and qualified low-fluorine YBCO MOD precursor. Verify certificates, composition and calibrations. Before fabrication, confirm the proposed thickness interpretation: 2.4 µm total superconducting thickness including a nominal 250 nm pure YBCO seed and approximately 2.15 µm composite overlayer.

Approve synthesis and MOD recipes, including atmosphere, total pressure, oxygen partial pressure, humidity, residence time and intermediate-cooling endpoints. Every P setting remains provisional until pilot qualification and freezing; the source parameter table retains the detailed starting settings. Do not equate oxygen flow with partial pressure, wet coating with crystalline thickness, or furnace temperature capability with process qualification. Reconcile reel speed and heated-path length with dwell requirements.

Apply safety prerequisites before work: rated-reactor procedures and cooled depressurization, dry HfCl4 handling, solvent ventilation, hazardous-metal waste segregation, suitable fluorinated-product exhaust, oxygen/solvent segregation and interlocks, plus cryogenic, magnet, high-current/quench and FIB/TEM controls.

### Stage II — Fabricate and release matched specimens

Synthesize BHO by the candidate polyol-solvothermal route only under approved conditions. Purify, establish dry-equivalent solids and verify core size, aggregation, phase and Ba:Hf composition using the specified complementary methods. TEM defines core diameter; DLS assesses aggregation. Incomplete perovskite formation requires redevelopment, not release.

Split one matrix-precursor lot among loadings and control; dose from assayed BHO using the approved convention without altering matrix Y:Ba:Cu. Release formulations after compatibility, aging, solvent-exchange and composition-loss checks. Inspect templates, establish baseline texture/roughness and use only compatible cleaning. Deposit and qualify the pure YBCO seed before composite coating; quarantine failed seed runs.

Calibrate crystalline thickness per pass on witnesses. Build the composite through matched coating, drying, pyrolysis and multistep epitaxial growth at 785 °C with documented intermediate cooling. Keep cycle count, seed, thickness and thermal history consistent across arms; record cumulative dwell. Complete qualified oxygenation/cooling and inspect finished films. Allocate adjacent full-width transport, magnetic, center/edge microscopy and archive coupons with longitudinal provenance. Measure electrical properties before destructive FIB preparation.

### Stage III — Characterize, measure and consolidate evidence

Use XRD texture/phase checks and distributed FIB/HRTEM/STEM sampling to quantify particle distributions, aggregation, density, coherence, Y248 intergrowths and faults. Keep proposed texture and sampling gates subject to pilot approval. Quarantine cracked, delaminated or out-of-thickness material while retaining failures. GPA must use suitable indexed reflections, same-image references and uncertainty checks; exclude imaging artifacts from strain interpretation.

Acquire magnetic loops with matched template/holder subtraction and superconducting-film-volume normalization; verify Bean-model applicability. Obtain verified equipment reaching at least 9 T or label the 7–9 T data missing. Keep magnetic and transport methods distinct. For transport, use 1 µV/cm calculated from actual tap spacing, calibrated geometry and adequate current/voltage capability. Check contact/current sharing and patterning damage; stop on heating, compliance or quench. Angular measurements retain maximum Lorentz-force geometry. Report unsupported current capability as lower bounds rather than established target performance.

Calculate matched-method lifting factors and justified SI pinning force; define η before testing its supplied expectation. Compare electrical response with measured final microstructure without assuming isotropic total Jc or causal partitioning of pinning. Supplied electrical and coarsening values remain reference targets only. Archive raw data, uncertainties, failed runs and settings; separate fabrication-block replication from coupon repeats. Diagnose failures using the source checks, and place changed conditions in new matched blocks. Only after core analysis, optionally study selected and undoped 100 m tapes with the stated sampling and any proposed uniformity statistic declared beforehand.
