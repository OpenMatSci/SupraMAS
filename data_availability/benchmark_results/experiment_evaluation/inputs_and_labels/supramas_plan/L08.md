# Experimental Plan: BHO size–loading optimization in LF-MOD YBCO coated conductors

## Experimental Draft

### Reconstructed experimental overview

#### Objective and comparison design
Compare starting BHO particle size and loading in embedded BHO/YBCO nanocomposites prepared by continuous reel-to-reel LF-MOD on Hastelloy/Al₂O₃/Y₂O₃/MgO/LMO. Retain the two-step growth architecture and 1.5 µm matrix target. The matrix comprises pristine P0 and both 5.5 and 6.5 nm starting populations at proposed 5, 10 and 15 mol% levels. Three independent fabrication blocks are proposed, with randomized coating order and matched substrate lot, matrix chemistry, thickness and thermal history. Adjacent coupons are technical replicates.

#### Resources and workflow
Use qualified BHO dispersions, matrix precursor, compatible vehicle and buffered tape. Required resources include calibrated reel-to-reel coating and thermal equipment, atmosphere monitoring and treated exhaust, thickness metrology, XRD, AFM/SEM, DLS, qualified FIB/HRTEM/EDS instruments and PPMS magnetometry.

First release materials, equipment and proposed settings; then formulate, coat, dry, pyrolyze, complete both growth stages and oxygenate. Partition material for structural characterization, magnetic measurements and optional transport. Assess retained cubic BHO, particle distribution, aggregation, coarsening, film texture and localized strain. Obtain matched-background hysteresis loops and calculate volume-normalized magnetic Jc and Fp.

#### Constraints and anticipated outputs
Confirm the proposed loading definition before formulation. Proposed/P settings require calibration and written release; unresolved atmosphere, coating and residence configurations are not established recipes. Use the specified safety controls and retain failed material and deviations in the records.

Expected outputs are traceable process records, structural datasets, Jc(B,T), measured-range pinning-force peaks and blockwise comparisons with uncertainty. Supplied performance values remain separately evaluated reference targets, not measured outcomes or automatically compatible acceptance criteria. Optional transport and growth-route or angular comparisons do not replace the core matrix.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Define the comparison matrix and qualify materials, equipment and process settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and comparison matrix
Prepare embedded BHO/YBCO nanocomposites by continuous reel-to-reel LF-MOD on Hastelloy/Al₂O₃/Y₂O₃/MgO/LMO. Preserve the two-step growth architecture and 1.5 µm matrix target. Compare starting particle size and loading against pristine YBCO using microstructure, texture, strain and magnetic Jc(B,T).

Supplied targets—not measurements—are 2.94× pristine Jc at 30 K/3 T, Fp,max = 4.9 GN/m³ at 4.2 K, and self-field Jc >2 MA/cm² at 77 K. Also evaluate >2 MA/cm² at 20–30 K/3–7 T. Their compatibility requires clarification below.

| Arm | Starting BHO diameter | Loading versus Y |
|---|---:|---:|
| P0 | None | 0 mol% |
| S55-C05/C10/C15 | 5.5 nm | 5/10/15 mol% |
| S65-C05/C10/C15 | 6.5 nm | 5/10/15 mol% |

Propose three independent fabrication blocks, each containing all seven arms. Randomize coating order; match substrate lot, matrix chemistry, thickness and thermal history. ID: `block-arm-G2-webposition`, with suffix M for magnetometry, X for microscopy and T for optional transport. Adjacent coupons are technical replicates, not independent films. Keep both sizes regardless of preliminary performance.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and prerequisites
Use qualified BHO dispersions, a qualified LF-MOD Y:Ba:Cu = 1:2:3 matrix precursor, compatible solvent/ligand vehicle, buffered Hastelloy, O₂/N₂, approved cleaning solvents, protective Pt/C and TEM grids. Confirm the proposed loading definition: 100 n(BHO formula units)/n(matrix Y). Assay solids; do not subtract BHO-bound Ba from matrix Ba. Match added solvent/ligand exposure in P0.

Required equipment: reel-to-reel coater; calibrated drying, pyrolysis and growth furnaces; flow controllers, O₂/dew-point monitors and treated exhaust; thickness metrology; XRD, AFM/SEM, DLS; FEI Helios Nanolab 600i FIB; JEOL JEM-2010F HRTEM with EDS; Quantum Design PPMS with a verified magnetometry option. Equivalent microscopy instruments require documented qualification.

Before production, verify PPMS ±7 T, 4.2–77 K operation, holder clearance, moment range and sensitivity. Qualify sample-temperature calibration, gas pressure/flow, precursor decomposition and reel residence time. The draft nanoparticle synthesis does not establish either size or cubic phase: procure qualified stocks or qualify synthesis separately. Use qualified buffered tape; internal fabrication must preserve the complete stack and establish biaxial MgO texture, not assume ordinary sputtering produces it.

##### 3. Consolidated settings
**F:** task-fixed. **P:** proposed pilot setting from the draft or laboratory design, requiring calibration and written release. Freeze qualified settings across the core matrix.

| Operation | Setting; tolerance/status |
|---|---|
| Final matrix thickness | F: 1.5 µm; P gate ±0.15 µm; measure actual value |
| Particle lot size | F: 5.5 or 6.5 nm; P mean tolerance ±0.5 nm; distributions must be distinguishable |
| Loading | F range 5–15 mol%; P levels 5/10/15; formulation ±5% relative |
| Dispersion | P: 40 kHz bath, 30 min; monitor temperature; test stability over 24 h |
| Drying | P: 100 ±2 °C, 5 min, N₂ |
| Pyrolysis | P: 3 °C/min to 400 ±2 °C, 30 min; O₂ with 2 vol% H₂O |
| Pre-growth | F: 785 °C; P: ±1 °C, 60 min, 100 ppm O₂ in N₂ |
| Final growth | F: 785–795 °C; P: 790 ±1 °C, 120 min, 200 ppm O₂ in N₂ |
| Ramps | P: 10 °C/min into growth; 5 °C/min to oxygenation |
| Oxygenation | F: 450 °C; P: ±2 °C, 4 h dry O₂, then 5 °C/min cooling in O₂ |
| Magnetic coupon | F: nominal 4 × 4 mm; P dimensional uncertainty ≤0.01 mm |
| Measurement temperatures | P: 4.2, 20, 30, 77 K; ±0.1 K; ≥10 min equilibration |
| Field program | P: full ±7 T loops, B ∥ c within 1°, nominal 0.1 T/min |

Gas flow, pressure, growth humidity, coating gap, concentration, pass count, tension and residence configuration remain release prerequisites. ppm O₂ is mole fraction: pO₂ = xO₂P, not a supplied partial pressure. Log both. A 1 µm wet film cannot be assumed to yield 1.5 µm oxide. At the draft speed of 1 m/min, 60/120 min holds require 60/120 m equivalent zones; qualify multi-pass or stop-and-hold reel processing if these lengths are unavailable.

- The comparison preserves embedded BHO, both starting sizes and a matched pristine control; independent fabrication blocks must be distinguished from adjacent technical replicates. **Source:** B000, B002, B003.
- Formulation depends on qualified particle stocks, assayed solids and confirmation of the proposed BHO-formula-unit-to-matrix-Y loading definition. BHO-bound Ba is not subtracted from matrix Ba. **Source:** B004, B006.
- Task-fixed conditions and proposed pilot settings have different release status. Oxygen mole fraction must be distinguished from partial pressure, and reel residence time and oxide yield require qualification rather than inference from nominal speed or wet thickness. **Source:** B007, B008, B009.

### Experimental Task 2: Release incoming materials and fabricate, oxygenate and partition composite films

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Incoming release:** inspect buffer continuity, cleanliness, XRD texture and AFM roughness. Proposed gates: RMS <2 nm over 5 × 5 µm and in-plane FWHM <5°. Quarantine failures; do not substitute LaAlO₃ or apply unqualified buffer anneals.
2. **Particle release:** collect powder XRD and TEM distributions, proposed ≥100 particles per lot. DLS monitors hydrodynamic aggregation, not crystalline core diameter. Record phase, ligands, solids assay and size uncertainty. Release only identifiable cubic BHO stocks with distinguishable size populations.
3. **Formulate:** calculate additions from Y moles and assayed BHO solids. Mix all loadings and vehicle-matched P0; sonicate using the released procedure. Proposed stability gate: no sediment and <10% DLS diameter change over 24 h. Avoid unqualified ligand exchange or pH adjustment. Retain aliquots. Hand recipe, lot records and release sheet to coating operator.
4. **Coat/dry:** clean without damaging LMO. Calibrate deposited mass and oxide yield on witnesses. Coat the embedded composite; repeat qualified coating/drying/pyrolysis cycles as needed for thickness. Record web position, passes, tension and mass. Validate cleaning against carryover.
5. **Pyrolyze:** apply the released schedule with fluorinated-exhaust treatment. Inspect witnesses for blistering, cracks and incomplete decomposition. Hold defective sections before crystallization.
6. **Grow/oxygenate:** execute both growth stages and the 450 °C treatment; log actual temperature, atmosphere, humidity and time. Flag excursions above 795 °C as process deviations, not automatic proof of coarsening. Qualify oxygenation using transition behavior and phase/lattice checks; routine EDS cannot establish δ.
7. **Partition:** inspect thickness, cracks and film texture at multiple positions. Cut M coupons without edge damage; retain adjacent X and optional T material. Prepare processed buffer-only blanks. Hand coupons, dimensions, thickness maps and process logs to characterization/measurement operators.

#### Related Knowledge

- Incoming substrate and particle release precede formulation. Powder XRD and TEM establish the particle phase and core-size populations, whereas DLS monitors hydrodynamic aggregation. **Source:** B010.
- Coating and thermal processing are sequential hand-offs, with deposited-mass and oxide-yield calibration, defect holds before crystallization and recorded actual growth conditions. **Source:** B010.
- Oxygenation qualification uses transition behavior and phase/lattice checks rather than routine EDS determination of oxygen deficiency. Partitioning preserves adjacent microscopy and optional transport material and provides processed-buffer blanks. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize film texture, embedded BHO distributions and localized strain

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural characterization
Measure film θ–2θ scans, YBCO (005) rocking curves and off-axis (103) φ scans. Distinguish radial peak width from rocking width; verify buffer reflection indices and symmetry. Compare doped texture against matched pristine films. Do not require unsupported <1% lattice mismatch.

Prepare protected FIB cross-sections spanning the full film and multiple web positions. Use electron-deposited protection before ion deposition; proposed milling starts at 30 kV with progressively reduced current, thinning below 100 nm and final cleaning near 5 kV. Qualify currents/doses and inspect implantation artifacts.

At a dose-qualified HRTEM setting near 200 kV, acquire proposed ≥10 nonoverlapping fields per arm/block and ≥100 particles where feasible; report inadequate counts. Measure particle size, number density, aggregation and depth distribution. Identify embedded BHO using diffraction/FFT plus Hf-localized EDS; confirm retained cubic phase rather than assuming it.

For cubic BHO, d(hkl) = a/√(h²+k²+l²); the draft's approximately 0.29 nm assignment to (111) requires correction by calibrated indexing. For YBCO, d(001) = c and d(003) = c/3: approximately 0.39 nm is not d(001). Determine lattice parameters from data.

Use calibrated Hf/Y mapping and formulation mass balance for loading checks; Ba/Hf alone is confounded by matrix Ba. GPA requires matrix references away from particles, archived masks and uncertainty estimates. Assess coherence, localized strain, coarsening and macroscopic cracking without imposing invented strain magnitudes or defect densities.

#### Related Knowledge

- Film diffraction separates radial peak width from rocking width and compares doped texture with matched pristine films, without imposing an unsupported lattice-mismatch threshold. **Source:** B011.
- Protected, artifact-qualified FIB cross-sections and dose-qualified HRTEM provide the basis for particle-size, aggregation and depth-distribution measurements. Diffraction/FFT together with Hf-localized EDS supports embedded BHO identification. **Source:** B012, B013.
- Lattice assignments require calibrated indexing. Loading checks use calibrated Hf/Y mapping and formulation mass balance, while GPA requires matrix references, archived masks and uncertainty estimates. **Source:** B014, B015.

### Experimental Task 4: Measure magnetic hysteresis and calculate comparative Jc and pinning force

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Magnetic measurement and analysis
Measure holder and matched processed-buffer backgrounds using identical loops. Mount film normal along field; verify contact and alignment. At each temperature standardize field history, obtain ascending/descending branches and test branch closure. Keep sweep/dwell timing identical; collect proposed ≥3 readings per point and repeat selected loops. For flux jumps, qualify slower sweeps and retest matched controls identically.

Subtract backgrounds branchwise. Normalize loop-width moment Δm by superconducting-film volume, not tape thickness. With ΔM in emu/cm³ and a ≤ b in cm:

**Jc [A/cm²] = 20 ΔM / [a(1 − a/3b)].**

For a = b = 0.4 cm, Jc = 75 ΔM. At 1.5 µm thickness, volume = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ cm³; Jc = 2 MA/cm² requires Δm = 0.64 emu. Verify instrument range accordingly. Propagate thickness, geometry, moment and background uncertainty. Perpendicular thin-film demagnetization is large, not approximately zero; identify low-field limitations and distinguish magnetic applied-zero-field Jc from transport self-field Jc.

Convert A/cm² to A/m² using ×10⁴; calculate Fp ≈ JcB using applied field where internal-field corrections are negligible. At 2 MA/cm² and 3 T, Fp = 60 GN/m³. Thus the application objective cannot be reconciled automatically with a universal 4.9 GN/m³ maximum: the supplied targets lack matching sample/field definitions. Preserve both as separately evaluated supplied targets, not simultaneous acceptance criteria. Report the measured-range peak; a rising curve at 7 T leaves the true maximum unresolved.

Report blockwise 15 mol%/P0 ratios at 30 K/3 T, size/loading effects, uncertainty, and microstructural correlations. Correlation alone does not establish strain-mediated causality.

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched holder and processed-buffer loops, controlled field history, alignment and identical timing support branchwise background subtraction and comparable magnetic measurements. **Source:** B016, B017.
- Extended Bean analysis uses superconducting-film volume and measured rectangular geometry. Moment range, dimensional uncertainty and low-field demagnetization limitations must be retained in interpreting magnetic Jc. **Source:** B017, B018, B019.
- Pinning-force calculations require consistent units and appropriate applied-field interpretation. Supplied targets are evaluated separately, measured-range peaks may not establish the true maximum, and structural correlations do not establish strain-mediated causality. **Source:** B020, B021.

### Experimental Task 5: Manage optional cross-checks, quality dispositions, archival hand-offs and safety

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Optional extensions
**Transport cross-check:** use adjacent T material, not M coupons. Proposed bridge: width 100 ±5 µm, voltage-tap spacing 1.00 ±0.05 mm, measured thickness and verified insulation from substrate. Proposed criterion E = 1 µV/cm gives Vc = 0.10 µV. At 1.5 µm and 2 MA/cm², Ic = 3 A; an unpatterned 4 mm width requires 120 A. Verify source/compliance, noise below Vc, contact heating and thermal trips before testing 77 K self-field and, if capable, 30 K/3 T. Archive I–V curves and offsets; abort heating/nonrepeatability. Magnetic and transport criteria are not interchangeable.

Matched single-step specimens may test growth-route causality; angular measurements may test isotropy. Neither replaces core controls. Pinning scaling fits require a constrained irreversibility field.

##### 8. Quality dispositions, feedback and safety
Quarantine cracked, off-thickness, aggregated or texture-degraded material; retain failures in the dataset. Diagnose dispersion via solids/ligands, cracks via pyrolysis and thickness, coarsening via thermal history, and poor transitions via gas calibration/oxygenation. Change one pilot factor at a time with written authorization; freeze qualified conditions and remake the affected complete comparison block, not only a favored arm.

Archive recipes, calibration certificates, logs, web maps, raw diffraction/images, segmentation/GPA settings, moments/backgrounds, I–V data, exclusions and analysis scripts. Record every hand-off and deviation.

Use solvent ventilation, nanoparticle containment, fluorinated-exhaust controls, oxygen-compatible hardware, hot-web guarding, cryogen ventilation, magnet exclusion zones and interlocked FIB/TEM procedures.

#### Related Knowledge

- Optional transport uses adjacent material and requires qualified geometry, substrate insulation, electrical capability and thermal safeguards. Its criterion is not interchangeable with magnetic Jc. **Source:** B022.
- Single-step and angular specimens are optional extensions rather than replacements for core controls; pinning-scaling fits require a constrained irreversibility field. **Source:** B023.
- Failures remain in the dataset. Authorized pilot changes require frozen qualified conditions and remaking the affected complete comparison block, with archived raw data, exclusions and hand-off records. **Source:** B024, B025.
- Execution requires the specified solvent, nanoparticle, exhaust, oxygen, hot-web, cryogen, magnet and microscopy safety controls. **Source:** B026.
