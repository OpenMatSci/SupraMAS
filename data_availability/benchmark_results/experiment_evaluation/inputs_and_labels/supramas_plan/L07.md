# Experimental Plan: SEG-123 bulk composition–pinning protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Release the comparison design and preparation resources
The objective is to compare BaO₂ suppression of RE/Ba substitution, Ag₂O-assisted liquid-phase modification and their interaction in single-domain SEG-123 bulk grown by top-seeded infiltration growth (TSIG) in air, with Pt fixed. The design uses six BaO₂ levels, 0–5 wt%, and two Ag₂O levels, 0 and 10 wt%, with 0.5 wt% Pt. Three independent growths per cell are proposed (P), giving 36 bulks. Optimize Ba-only compositions within 0–4 wt%; retain 5 wt% as an excess-addition diagnostic. Randomization and matched controls preserve the comparisons.

Use verified SEG-211, additive powders, separate Y-123/Ba₃Cu₅O₈ liquid-source pellets and c-axis-oriented Nd-123 seeds. Required resources include enclosed powder handling, rated pressing equipment, mapped growth and oxygenation furnaces, cutting/polishing equipment, Cu-Kα XRD, FE-SEM/EDS and qualified SQUID capability. A signed process sheet must resolve proposed settings and qualification prerequisites before the comparison batch begins.

### Stage II — Fabricate and oxygenate traceable bulks
Verify incoming phases, weigh and mix additives under the approved mass basis, prepare porous precursor and liquid-source pellets, assemble the seed above the precursor, and execute calibrated TSIG in air with a 0.2 °C h⁻¹ growth window. Oxygenate within 450–350 °C using a qualified schedule, then map and cut matched central/edge specimens.

### Stage III — Characterize, compare and archive
Assess phase assemblage, orthorhombicity, texture, domain consistency and microstructure before interpreting SQUID transition and hysteresis data. Report geometry-normalized magnetic Jc, uncertainty, spatial variation and replicate dispersion. Apply diagnostic gates, preserve failed-parent records and compare against supplied reference targets without claiming attainment. Optional transport remains separately qualified and reported; anticipated outputs are traceable structural, microstructural and magnetic datasets with explicit limitations.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage I — Qualify materials, equipment and the randomized composition matrix

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and release prerequisites
Grow single-domain (Sm,Eu,Gd)Ba₂Cu₃O₇−δ by top-seeded infiltration growth (TSIG) in air. Compare BaO₂ suppression of RE/Ba substitution, Ag₂O-assisted liquid-phase modification and their interaction, with Pt fixed. Supplied property values are targets, not measurements generated by this proposal.

**P denotes a proposed setting requiring calibration and approval before production.** Release a signed process sheet resolving precursor composition, additive mass basis, liquid-source quantity, thermal window, seed survival, oxygenation and equipment limits. Do not begin the comparison batch with these unresolved.

SEG-211 means the mixed-RE 211 phase, nominally (Sm,Eu,Gd)₂BaCuO₅, not SEG-123 or Y₂BaCuO₅. The RE ratio is unspecified; use one verified lot throughout. Equimolar Sm:Eu:Gd is a P fallback only after approval.

##### 2. Sample matrix and identifiers

| Factor | Levels | Role |
|---|---|---|
| BaO₂ | 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 wt% | Optimize through 4%; 5% diagnoses excess addition |
| Ag₂O | 0, 10 wt% | Matched no-Ag and Ag arms |
| Pt | 0.5 wt%, all cells | Fixed refiner |
| Replication | P: three independent growths/cell | 36 bulks; not three pieces of one bulk |

Use `SEG-Bxx-Ayy-Rz`, e.g. `SEG-B04-A10-R2`. Append `-M-C`, `-M-E`, `-X`, `-S`, or `-T` for central/edge magnetic, XRD, microscopy or transport specimens. Record parent coordinates and orientation.

Each growth block contains all 12 compositions; randomize preparation order and furnace position. Hold precursor lot, seed geometry, liquid supply, pressing, atmosphere and thermal program constant. B00-A00 is the reference; B00-A10 is Ag-only; Bxx-A00 is Ba-only. Select the optimized Ba level from 0–4 using the Ba-only results before evaluating its matched co-doped arm. The full factorial also estimates interaction without replacing these controls.

##### 3. Materials, equipment and consolidated settings
Use verified SEG-211, BaO₂, Ag₂O, Pt, Y-123, Ba₃Cu₅O₈, c-axis-oriented Nd-123 seeds, compatible crucibles/supports, polishing consumables and nonmagnetic sample holders. Record certificates, actual assays and powder storage history; no unsupported purity requirement is imposed.

Provide a balance, enclosed powder mixer, guarded hydraulic press, mapped air-growth furnace, oxygen-rated annealing furnace with flow monitoring, calibrated thermocouples, diamond saw, polishing equipment, Cu-Kα XRD with texture capability, FE-SEM with BSE/EDS, and SQUID capable of 1 mT, ±5 T and 65–100 K. Verify holder clearance, maximum magnetic moment and field accuracy before cutting coupons.

| Operation | Setting and control |
|---|---|
| Additive weighing | P: mass per dry SEG-211 mass; ±0.1 percentage point for each additive; document balance uncertainty |
| Mixing | P: enclosed dry, low-energy mixing for 4 h; qualify homogeneity and contamination before use |
| Green pellet | P: 10 mm diameter, 200 MPa; thickness/density fixed after infiltration pilot |
| Precursor conditioning | P candidate: 900 °C, 4 h in air; accept only if phase retention and open porosity are demonstrated |
| Liquid source | Y-123 + Ba₃Cu₅O₈; P starting ratio 1:1 molar; source mass and dimensions require pilot calibration |
| TSIG peak | Draft candidate 1050 °C/10 h, not a released setting; calibrate melt formation and seed survival |
| Growth window | 0.2 °C h⁻¹; P rate tolerance ±0.02 °C h⁻¹; calibrated start/end temperatures; P spatial temperature agreement within ±1 °C |
| Oxygenation | 450→350 °C in flowing O₂; P initial duration 72 h, linear descent, then controlled cooling; calibrate flow and oxygen uptake |
| XRD | Cu-Kα; P 2θ = 10–80°, step ≤0.02°; calibrate zero and wavelength |
| FE-SEM | P BSE at 10 kV, 500–10,000×; adjust for phase contrast and charging |
| SQUID | Tc: 1 mT; loops: ±5 T at 65, 77 and P 90 K; H ∥ c within P ±1° |
| Temperature control | P stability ±0.1 K for loops; near Tc use ≤0.2 K steps and equilibrium checks |

The 200 MPa load on a 10 mm die is approximately 15.7 kN; verify press and die ratings. A growth window of ΔT °C takes ΔT/0.2 hours: 20 °C requires 100 h, excluding ramps and holds. Do not confuse cooling rate with a linear crystal-growth velocity.

#### Related Knowledge

- The factorial matrix retains no-additive, Ag-only and Ba-only controls with fixed Pt. Selecting the Ba-only optimum within 0–4 wt% before assessing its matched co-doped arm preserves the specified comparison, while 5 wt% remains diagnostic. **Source:** B004, B006.
- The equipment inventory and consolidated settings define qualification needs, not released recipes. Press-load and growth-window calculations support equipment and scheduling checks without interpreting cooling rate as crystal-growth velocity. **Source:** B007, B008, B009, B010.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage II — Prepare pellets, perform TSIG, oxygenate and transfer mapped specimens

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Incoming-material gate.** Obtain powder XRD for SEG-211 and liquid-source constituents; confirm identity and inspect contamination. Hold mislabeled lots. Retain aliquots from every lot.
2. **Weigh and mix.** For precursor mass m, weigh BaO₂ = xm/100, Ag₂O = ym/100 and Pt = 0.005m under the approved basis. Add all refiners before TSIG; do not add Ag after growth. For P m = 10 g, B04-A10 requires 0.40 g BaO₂, 1.00 g Ag₂O and 0.050 g Pt. Independently check calculations and masses. Inspect representative mixed-powder aliquots for segregation.
3. **Condition and press.** Use the qualified conditioning route; gently deagglomerate without unqualified high-energy milling. Press porous precursor pellets and separate liquid-source pellets. Record green mass, dimensions, density and cracks. Do not apply a dense 950 °C/48 h oxygen sinter unless separately shown compatible with infiltration.
4. **Hand-off to growth operator.** Transfer labeled pellets, seed orientation photograph, mass sheet, pilot-approved liquid quantity and thermal recipe. Place liquid source below the SEG-211 body and seed on its upper surface. Qualify crucible compatibility, support geometry and overflow containment; the separate pellets are reagents, not a final layered architecture.
5. **TSIG in air.** Run the approved heat-up, melt/infiltration hold, approach to growth start and 0.2 °C h⁻¹ window. Log actual temperatures continuously. Peak temperature minus growth temperature is not peritectic undercooling; calculate undercooling only from a verified composition-dependent peritectic temperature. Cool after growth at a qualified rate. Photograph growth sectors, seed condition, cracks and leaked liquid.
6. **Hand-off to oxygenation operator.** Transfer parent ID, dimensions, growth log and defect map. Anneal within 450–350 °C using the approved O₂ schedule. Verify uptake/uniformity on pilot coupons; 72 h is not assumed sufficient for every bulk thickness.
7. **Hand-off to characterization.** Map the grown bulk before cutting. Take matched central and edge coupons, excluding the seed and Nd-contaminated region; retain the remainder. P magnetic coupon size is approximately 2 × 1 × 0.5 mm, subject to SQUID qualification. Measure every actual dimension and orientation. Polish adjacent sections to a mirror finish, with P final 0.05 µm alumina; clean gently and avoid unqualified ultrasonication of cracked material.

#### Related Knowledge

- Incoming powder identification, independently checked additive weighing and segregation inspection provide traceable preparation controls. All refiners are introduced before growth, using the approved additive basis. **Source:** B011.
- Separate porous SEG-211 and liquid-source pellets, with the seed above the precursor, define the infiltration assembly. Conditioning, liquid inventory, crucible compatibility and thermal settings remain qualification-dependent; the pellets are reagents rather than a final layered architecture. **Source:** B011.
- Growth, oxygenation and characterization hand-offs carry parent identity, geometry, logs and defect maps. Pilot verification of oxygen uptake and measured coupon geometry prevents assuming that the proposed annealing duration or specimen dimensions are universally suitable. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage III — Characterize phase assemblage, texture and inclusion distributions

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural and microstructural characterization
Collect powder XRD from a representative fragment and oriented surface scans. Determine phases and lattice parameters with uncertainty; resolve orthorhombic a/b splitting rather than using c alone. Oriented (00l) dominance supports c-axis texture but does not establish phase purity or a single domain. Map several positions with rocking curves and an asymmetric reflection/pole measurement; correlate with sector microscopy and, if ambiguous, orientation mapping.

For c = 11.74 Å, d₀₀₅ = 2.348 Å; Cu-Kα₁ λ ≈1.5406 Å predicts 2θ₀₀₅ ≈38.3°. Confirm indexing against the full pattern. Report c near 11.74 Å without imposing an unsupported acceptance tolerance.

Image equivalent center, intermediate and edge locations. P minimum: five fields/location and at least 100 segmented 211 particles/specimen. Record equivalent-circle diameter distribution, area fraction, clustering, cracks, pores, liquid pools and Ag-rich regions; verify phase assignments by EDS where feasible. Report 3.2–3.3 µm as a supplied comparison, not “sub-micron.” Do not equate area fraction with a stipulated volume fraction or infer atomic RE/Ba occupancy from EDS.

#### Related Knowledge

- Powder and oriented XRD serve different purposes: orthorhombic a/b splitting informs structural assessment, while oriented (00l) dominance alone establishes neither phase purity nor a single domain. Spatial orientation measurements support the domain assessment. **Source:** B012.
- The supplied lattice and reflection calculation supports indexing against the full diffraction pattern. A c parameter near 11.74 Å is reported without inventing an acceptance tolerance. **Source:** B013.
- Equivalent spatial sampling and particle-distribution reporting support microstructural comparisons. The supplied 3.2–3.3 µm comparison is not sub-micron, area fraction is not a stipulated volume fraction, and EDS does not establish atomic RE/Ba occupancy. **Source:** B014.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage III — Measure SQUID transitions and loops and calculate magnetic current density

#### Specific Content

##### 6. SQUID measurements and analysis
1. Measure empty-holder background and confirm residual-field correction. For Tc, zero-field cool from above Tc to 50 K, apply **1 mT**, and measure on warming to 100 K. Optional field-cooled data use the same field. In vacuum, 1 mT corresponds approximately to 10 Oe, not 5 Oe.
2. P onset definition: intersection of normal-state baseline and tangent to the steep transition. P ΔTc definition: 10–90% normalized diamagnetic transition using predeclared baselines. Archive curves and definitions; supplied widths may have used a different convention.
3. Reset magnetic history above Tc before each temperature. Stabilize at 65, 77 and P 90 K, then acquire complete loops −5→+5→−5 T, including dense points near zero and exactly 3 T. Qualify and fix field sweep rate, dwell and averaging across all samples; repeat a representative loop at a slower rate to assess relaxation artifacts.
4. For rectangular coupons with field along c, let a ≤ b be full dimensions perpendicular to field in cm. Use volume-normalized branch separation ΔM in emu cm⁻³:
   **Jc = 20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)]**, in A cm⁻².
   Record thickness, volume, background subtraction and propagated uncertainty. Reject inappropriate geometry or unresolved domains rather than applying this formula blindly. At zero applied field, label the result magnetic self-field Jc; internal field is not zero.
5. Report Jc(B), central/edge differences and replicate dispersion. Calculate Fp = JcB using Jc in A m⁻², giving N m⁻³. Fit normalized Fp only if Birr is determined under a declared noise-based criterion. If irreversibility persists at 5 T, report Birr >5 T and do not fabricate h = B/Birr.

For a Dew-Hughes fit, hmax = p/(p+q): (0.5,2) gives 0.20 and (1,2) gives 0.333, not 0.286 and 0.40. Fit parameters without assigning a unique mechanism from peak position. δl denotes mean-free-path fluctuations; distinguishing δl from δTc requires supporting temperature scaling, not SEM alone.

#### Related Knowledge

- Holder-background measurement, residual-field correction, declared transition definitions and magnetic-history resets provide consistent magnetic comparisons. The transition field is 1 mT, and loop acquisition includes exactly 3 T. **Source:** B015.
- The extended Bean calculation requires volume-normalized branch separation and measured full transverse dimensions for an appropriate rectangular coupon. Geometry, background subtraction and uncertainty must accompany magnetic self-field Jc and spatial comparisons. **Source:** B015.
- Normalized pinning-force fitting depends on a declared, noise-based determination of Birr. Dew-Hughes peak position does not uniquely establish a mechanism, and distinguishing mean-free-path from transition-temperature fluctuations requires supporting temperature scaling. **Source:** B015, B016.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage III — Apply diagnostic gates, consolidate comparisons and qualify optional transport

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Gates, feedback and records
Hold incomplete infiltration, gross cracks, seed failure, parasitic grains or uncertain phase identity for diagnosis; retain all failed-parent records. Secondary phases and liquid pools in B05 are diagnostic outcomes, not grounds to remove that composition retrospectively. For blocked infiltration, inspect porosity and liquid inventory; for parasitic growth, inspect seed survival and temperature mapping; for Ag segregation, inspect mixing and melt distribution. Correct pilot settings, then rerun matched controls—not selected successful arms only.

Broad Tc or edge/center disagreement triggers checks of temperature calibration, oxygenation and cracks. If reannealing is justified, retain pre-anneal data and apply the same treatment to matched controls. Do not silently lower BaO₂, increase Ag₂O or alter growth atmosphere.

Select Ba-only optimum by retained Tc, narrowed transition and Jc(77 K, self-field), reporting trade-offs. Compare matched co-doping at 65 K/3 T; estimate interaction as [Jc(B,10)−Jc(B,0)]−[Jc(0,10)−Jc(0,0)]. Targets include 33.55 and 50.54 kA cm⁻², Tc 94.57 K/ΔTc 0.88 K, and co-doped Tc >93 K/ΔTc <1.5 K. Missing these is an experimental outcome, not an automatic invalidation.

Archive IDs, lots, masses, geometry, randomization, furnace/gas logs, hand-offs, images with scales, raw XRD/SQUID data, calibration files, analysis scripts, uncertainties, exclusions and approved deviations. Freeze analysis definitions before unblinding composition labels.

##### 8. Optional bulk transport and safety
Transport validates selected bulk coupons; it does not replace magnetic comparisons. P miniature geometry: 0.5 mm ×0.1 mm cross-section, ≥8 mm length, 5 mm voltage-tap separation, current along ab. Verify machinability, measured section and four separated contacts before use. P criterion 1 µV cm⁻¹ gives **0.5 µV** across 5 mm. At 50.54 kA cm⁻², area 0.0005 cm² requires **25.27 A**; qualify a ≥40 A source, sub-µV metrology and thermally anchored contacts. A 2 ×1 mm section would require 1010.8 A and is unsuitable for an ordinary station. Qualify pulsed ramps, reversal, heating limits and contact resistance; <1 Ω is unacceptable at these currents. Log I–V, actual temperature, field and geometry; report transport Jc separately.

Use enclosed dust handling for toxic Ba/RE powders; segregate BaO₂/Ag₂O oxidizers from organics and combustible milling media. Require a compatibility review before any solvent route. Use rated dies, hot-zone guarding, oxygen-clean gas equipment, ventilation, cryogen oxygen monitoring and magnet exclusion controls. Liquid-oxygen operation is not required for the 90 K SQUID assessment; no LOX or trapped-field demonstration is implied. Thin-film deposition is outside this bulk protocol.

#### Related Knowledge

- Failed growths and diagnostic excess-addition outcomes remain recorded. Pilot corrections and justified reannealing require matched controls and retained prior data rather than selective reruns or silent composition changes. **Source:** B017, B018.
- Ba-only selection combines retained Tc, transition width and self-field Jc at 77 K; matched co-doping is compared at 65 K and 3 T. The interaction contrast and supplied targets guide reporting without turning target attainment into a validity gate. **Source:** B019.
- Frozen analysis definitions and complete archives preserve traceability. Optional transport requires separate geometry, current, voltage and heating qualifications and does not replace magnetic comparisons; powder, press, oxygen, cryogen and magnet safeguards remain prerequisites. **Source:** B020, B021, B022.
