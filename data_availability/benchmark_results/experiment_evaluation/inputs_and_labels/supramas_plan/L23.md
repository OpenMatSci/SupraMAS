# Experimental Plan: Protocol: mixed-RE YREBCO nanocomposite films on STO

## Experimental Draft

### Reconstructed experimental overview

Compare rare-earth mixing, liquid assistance and deposition relaxation in YREBCO films grown directly on STO(001), keeping all core depositions at **790 °C**. Preserve the superconducting matrix with incidental mixed-RE sesquioxide inclusions; exclude buffers, discrete oxide layers, preformed nanoparticle loading and excess CuO.

The core comparison comprises YBCO and (Y₀.₈Gd₀.₁Yb₀.₁)Ba₂Cu₃O₇₋y under SSD, +L and +L+LLD conditions. An approved additional mixed-RE series, including a higher-variance Nd-containing composition, is required at SSD; liquid-assisted variants are follow-up only. Use randomized growth blocks and **P: three independent films per arm**, with bridges treated as within-film repeats.

Required resources include certified ceramic targets, phase-checked BaCu₂O₂, STO substrates, calibrated KrF PLD with simultaneous addition delivery and programmable bursts, growth monitoring, AFM/thickness metrology, XRD, Raman, HAADF-STEM with chemical analysis, patterning equipment and qualified four-probe transport at 20 K and up to 10 T.

First approve recipes, radius definitions, addition denominator and transfer calibration, burst dose and instrument capability. Then prepare substrates, qualify delivery on sacrificial witnesses, deposit matched-thickness films and oxygenate identically. Complete nondestructive characterization before patterning; obtain destructive microscopy after transport or from mapped edge material. Measure transition behavior and field-dependent critical current using verified geometry and the prescribed electric-field criterion.

All **P** settings remain proposed until qualified and frozen across comparisons. Anticipated outputs are traceable structural, inclusion, transition and transport datasets with uncertainties and failure dispositions. Inclusion size ≤14 nm, rocking-curve FWHM as low as 0.2° and Jc(20 K, 8 T) of 3.75 MA cm⁻² are supplied reference targets, not measured outcomes or release requirements.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Approve comparison arms and qualify materials, equipment and common settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and release prerequisites
Compare RE mixing, liquid assistance and deposition relaxation at fixed **790 °C**. Preserve direct epitaxial YREBCO/STO growth, with incidental (Y,RE′,RE″)₂O₃ inclusions inside the matrix. Do not add CeO₂, preformed nanoparticle loading, excess CuO or discrete oxide layers.

Before releasing experimental growth, approve: additional RE recipes; ionic-radius convention; the denominator of **26 mol% BaCu₂O₂**; calibrated addition delivery; LLD burst dose; and instrument capability. Unresolved items below are stop conditions, not authorization to guess.

All settings marked **P** are proposed starting choices requiring calibration. Freeze them across comparison arms after pilot qualification. Supplied performance values are comparison targets, not guaranteed outcomes.

##### 2. Sample matrix and identifiers
Use `composition–process–growthID–replicate–piece/bridgeID`. Record substrate and target lot separately. Randomize growth order within blocks; use three independent films per arm (**P**), not three bridges as independent replicates.

| Composition | SSD | +L | +L+LLD | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Y: YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋y | Required | Required | Required | Matched process controls |
| GY: (Y₀.₈Gd₀.₁Yb₀.₁)Ba₂Cu₃O₇₋y | Required | Required | Required | Primary mixed-RE contrasts |
| V01…: approved mixed-RE series | Required | Follow-up only | Follow-up only | Variance comparison against Y and GY at SSD |

The variance panel must include a higher-variance Nd-containing composition. Select additional recipes from the task’s Y/Gd/Yb/Dy/Ho/Sm/Nd family before growth; record RE′, RE″ and x. Do not assume that arbitrary 10%/10% combinations have matched mean radius. Calculate mean radius and variance using one documented RE³⁺ coordination convention. Verify whether 2.31×10⁻⁴ denotes normalized variance or a dimensional quantity. If mean radii cannot be matched, report mean-radius change as a confounder rather than claiming clean variance isolation.

For each film, reserve an intact transport area and identified sacrificial microscopy region. Complete nondestructive characterization before patterning; prepare destructive STEM specimens after transport or from mapped edge material. Track every hand-off and specimen relationship.

##### 3. Materials, equipment and consolidated settings
Use composition-certified ceramic YBCO/YREBCO targets, phase-checked BaCu₂O₂ addition material, STO(001) single crystals, oxygen, solvent-cleaning supplies, resist, Ag contact material and cryogenic wiring. **P:** use 5 × 5 mm² STO from a common lot. If making targets internally, qualify synthesis independently by chemical assay, XRD, density and stable ablation; neither conflicting draft firing schedule is an approved recipe. Target particles need not reproduce film nanoparticle sizes.

Required equipment: KrF PLD with calibrated heater, pressure gauge and programmable bursts; simultaneous addition delivery; compatible growth monitor, preferably RHEED; AFM/profilometer; calibrated XRD; Raman microscope; true HAADF-STEM with chemical analysis and FIB access; lithography/ion mill; four-probe transport with low-noise voltage acquisition, current reversal and a verified 20 K/10 T sample environment.

| Parameter | Setting/control |
|---|---|
| Growth temperature | 790 °C; **P:** actual substrate stability ±2 °C, verified independently of heater readout |
| Film thickness | **P:** 300 ±15 nm; use measured local thickness in Jc |
| Growth O₂ | **P:** 0.40 ±0.02 mbar for all arms; qualify liquid compatibility |
| Laser | **P:** 248 nm, 2.0 ±0.1 J cm⁻² at target, 50 ±1 mm target distance |

| Liquid addition | 26 mol%, denominator unresolved; **P:** qualified delivery within ±1 mol-percentage point |
| Oxygenation | **P:** 500 ±5 °C, 60 ±5 min, 1 bar O₂ only in rated apparatus; 5 °C min⁻¹ cooling |
| Transport | 20.0 ±0.1 K (**P** tolerance); 0–10 T, **P:** 0.5 T steps; include 8 T |
| Field alignment | B ∥ c; **P:** ±0.5°, verified from mounting geometry/XRD |
| Bridge/taps | 25 µm width; **P:** width within ±1 µm, 1.00 ±0.01 mm tap spacing |

If the PLD chamber is not rated for oxygenation conditions, use a qualified external furnace with identical transfer and thermal histories for every arm.

#### Related Knowledge

- The comparison preserves direct YREBCO/STO growth and requires unresolved recipes, addition definitions, delivery, burst dose and instrument capability to be approved before experimental growth. Proposed settings require qualification rather than assumption. **Source:** B000, B001, B002.
- The matrix separates composition and process contrasts. Independent films provide replication, while a documented ionic-radius convention and explicit mean-radius confounding prevent unsupported interpretation of the variance panel. **Source:** B003, B004, B005.
- Certified materials, calibrated equipment and common settings support matched comparisons. Internal target synthesis is independently qualified, and oxygenation requires rated apparatus or a qualified external furnace. **Source:** B007, B008, B009, B010.

### Experimental Task 2: Prepare STO, qualify addition delivery, deposit films and oxygenate

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Receive and verify.** Assign IDs; inspect STO and targets. Confirm RE fractions and RE:Ba:Cu stoichiometry by quantitative assay. Confirm addition identity by diffraction. Release only approved recipes and stable targets; reject cracked or compositionally incorrect targets.
2. **Clean STO.** **P:** ultrasonicate in acetone 10 min, then IPA 10 min; DI rinse and nitrogen dry. **P:** anneal at 1000 °C for 2 h in controlled oxygen, ramp 10 °C min⁻¹ and cool 5 °C min⁻¹. Document pressure/flow before use. AFM central and edge regions; proposed preparation target RMS <0.2 nm with reproducible terraces. Quarantine contaminated, scratched or nonuniform substrates. No buffer deposition.
3. **Hand-off to PLD.** Supply substrate/target IDs, AFM results and approved recipe. Calibrate heater offset at the actual mounting geometry, fluence, pressure and thickness per pulse for each target. **P:** base pressure <10⁻⁶ mbar; pre-ablate behind a closed shutter until plume and yield stabilize. Use common mounting, target raster and substrate rotation.
4. **Qualify addition on sacrificial witnesses.** Proposed simultaneous co-ablation adds BaCu₂O₂-derived material throughout +L growth; it does not deposit a separate layer. Determine dose from assayed transferred material, not nominal source pressure. If 26 mol% means fraction of total formula-unit feed, the addition:host ratio is 0.26/0.74 = 0.351; if relative to host, it is 0.26. Obtain the definition before choosing either. Verify that delivery at 790 °C and selected O₂ produces liquid-assisted growth rather than persistent segregated deposits. Do not label a nominal feed as a demonstrated liquid.
5. **Deposit experimental films.** SSD uses host only; +L uses continuous host/addition delivery; +L+LLD uses synchronized host/addition bursts at 50 Hz and relaxation. Calibrate pulses per layer using growth-monitor response and thickness witnesses; pauses every 50 nm alone do not establish LLD. Reach matched thickness by calibrated pulse count, not assumed deposition time. Record pulses, pauses, growth signals and all deviations. Stop for uncontrolled dose, temperature drift or gross particulates; quarantine affected films.
6. **Oxygenate identically.** Apply the qualified common thermal/O₂ schedule. Inspect for cracking, delamination and residue; measure thickness at mapped locations. Hand off with full thermal and dose history.

#### Related Knowledge

- Receipt checks, substrate inspection and mounting-specific calibration establish traceable inputs before direct deposition without a buffer. **Source:** B011.
- The addition dose depends on the approved molar denominator and assayed transferred material. Witness qualification must distinguish liquid-assisted growth from persistent segregated deposits. **Source:** B011.
- Layer-by-layer deposition requires calibrated burst dose and growth-monitor response, not merely periodic pauses. Matched pulse-count thickness and identical oxygenation preserve process comparisons. **Source:** B009, B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize film structure, Raman response and inclusion microstructure

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural and microstructural characterization
**XRD:** obtain θ–2θ scans for orientation, secondary phases and lattice parameters; measure the same YREBCO (005) rocking curve and radial peak profile on every film. **P:** angular sampling ≤0.01° and instrumental width sufficiently below 0.2°. Use an appropriate standard and record wavelength, optics, background and deconvolution method. With Cu Kα₁ = 1.5406 Å and illustrative c = 11.68 Å, d₀₀₅ = 2.336 Å and 2θ ≈38.5°; use the observed peak, not this approximate position. Si(111) ≈28.44° applies only for this wavelength. Do not identify impurities from a single assumed peak. Verify in-plane epitaxy with suitable asymmetric reflections/φ scans. Assess orthorhombicity using resolvable asymmetric lattice information; 00ℓ texture alone is insufficient.

**Raman:** map matched areas before destructive preparation. **P:** 532 nm, initially ≤1 mW, 100–800 cm⁻¹, 10 × 30 s accumulations and a 10 × 10 grid. Calibrate spectral position with a standard; verify power independence and no damage. Report shifts, linewidths and polarization relative to matched Y controls. Do not convert shifts to strain using an unvalidated coefficient. A ~1 µm spot averages many ≤14 nm inclusions; it cannot resolve their individual strain fields.

**STEM:** sample center/edge and through-thickness regions. **P:** FIB coarse milling at 30 kV, final polish at 2 kV; qualify protection and beam damage. Start HAADF at 200 kV only after dose testing. Identify inclusions using composition plus lattice/diffraction evidence. Aim for ≥20 distributed fields and ≥200 particles per condition (**P**); report smaller counts honestly. Include large particles and agglomerates. Record diameter distributions, interface morphology and spatial heterogeneity.

Calculate number density as N/Σ(Aᵢtᵢ), using measured **lamella thickness**, not the 300 nm film thickness for cross-sectional projected images. Document overlap and truncation corrections. GPA can assess nanoscale strain with reference-region and scan-distortion controls. ≤14 nm is the supplied size target; no unsupported density or dispersion threshold is imposed.

#### Related Knowledge

- Comparable XRD profiles require instrumental calibration and consistent reflections. In-plane epitaxy and orthorhombicity need suitable asymmetric information rather than 00ℓ texture alone. **Source:** B012.
- Matched Raman maps require spectral calibration and checks for power dependence and damage. Their spatial averaging does not resolve individual inclusion strain fields, and unvalidated shift-to-strain conversion is excluded. **Source:** B013.
- Inclusion identification combines chemical and lattice or diffraction evidence. Number density uses measured lamella volume with overlap and truncation documentation; destructive preparation follows transport or uses mapped edge material. **Source:** B006, B014, B015.

### Experimental Task 4: Pattern transport bridges and qualify geometry, contacts and voltage sensitivity

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Patterning and transport hand-off
Pattern 25 µm bridges with four-terminal taps and wider current leads. **P:** Ag pads outside the active span; initially qualify 500 eV Ar milling on witnesses, using measured endpoint rather than assumed etch rate. Verify no conductive residual film, pad shunting or edge damage. Measure width at five positions, local thickness and tap separation with uncertainties. Any contact anneal requires separate qualification and identical exposure across arms; do not automatically re-anneal completed films.

At 25 µm ×300 nm, area = 7.50×10⁻⁸ cm². The supplied 3.75 MA cm⁻² target corresponds to **Ic = 0.281 A**. A 10 A source is not a safe sweep endpoint. Establish current limits from lead/contact heating and sample thermal anchoring; the source must deliver at least 0.281 A, with qualified headroom. At this current, a 1 Ω current contact dissipates 79 mW, so a generic <1 Ω check is inadequate.

The criterion is **0.2 µV cm⁻¹**, giving **20 nV across 1 mm**. Demonstrate **P:** ≤2 nV effective uncertainty after averaging and offset rejection; nominal 10 nV resolution alone is insufficient. If unattainable, improve acquisition or approve and measure longer tap spacing while retaining the electric-field criterion. Do not substitute another criterion.

Hand off bridge geometry, continuity/insulation checks, contact resistance versus temperature and safe current limit to the transport operator.

#### Related Knowledge

- Bridge patterning requires qualified milling and checks for residual conduction, shunting and edge damage. Measured width, local thickness and tap spacing determine transport normalization. **Source:** B016, K04_442.
- The supplied current target is a planning reference, not a safe sweep endpoint. Current limits must reflect contact and lead heating and thermal anchoring. **Source:** B017.
- The prescribed electric-field criterion corresponds to 20 nV across 1 mm, so effective uncertainty must be demonstrated. The transport hand-off includes geometry, electrical checks, contact behavior and a safe current limit. **Source:** B018, B019.

### Experimental Task 5: Measure Tc and field-dependent Jc, disposition films and archive comparisons

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Tc and Jc acquisition
Measure low-current R(T) through the transition in zero applied field. **P:** initially 100 µA, verifying current independence; report onset, midpoint, zero-resistance temperature and transition width with explicit definitions, not a mandatory 90 K cutoff.

Mount with B ∥ c; verify wiring and thermal anchoring. At 20 K, **P:** stabilize for 10 min. Set each field from 0 to 10 T, wait for field/temperature stability, then acquire bipolar I–V curves with offset rejection. Begin well below expected Ic; use adaptive fine steps near criterion, never an automatic 0–10 A ramp. Repeat three sweeps (**P**), and compare dwell or pulse duration to detect heating. Abort on thermal drift, contact voltage growth or irreversible I–V change.

Interpolate Ic at E = V/L = 0.2 µV cm⁻¹; report Jc = Ic/(wt). Save complete I–V data. If the safe current ceiling precedes criterion, report a lower bound; if voltage sensitivity is inadequate, report unresolved Ic. Use independent-film statistics and separate bridge repeatability. Record field history; optional descending-field data must be labeled separately.

##### 8. Quality dispositions, safety and controlled feedback
Exclude only documented fabrication/measurement failures, never merely low Jc or broad FWHM. Retain and label scientifically valid off-target films. For poor texture inspect substrate, calibration, target transfer and oxygenation; for segregation inspect addition calibration; for suppressed Tc inspect oxygen history and composition; for unstable transport inspect contacts, heating and geometry. Quarantine failures and regrow rather than repairing selectively within comparisons.

Use enclosed powder handling, solvent ventilation, laser interlocks, oxygen-compatible rated hardware, hot-surface controls, FIB/ion-mill interlocks, cryogen ventilation and magnet exclusion procedures. Zero current before changing connections; obey magnet ramp/quench and cryostat limits.

Archive raw images/spectra/I–V curves, calibration files, actual settings, IDs, dimensions, uncertainties, processing scripts and exclusions. Compare GY versus Y at SSD, +L versus SSD, and +L+LLD versus +L. These contrasts do not alone prove a unique pinning mechanism. Any parameter change requires a documented hypothesis, pilot qualification and matched-control regrowth while retaining 790 °C and core compositions.

**Optional extensions:** add LLD-only arms to separate liquid/relaxation interaction, or angular transport after completing core measurements. Neither replaces the variance panel or matched controls. Do not assign δTc versus δℓ pinning from a single-temperature field exponent.

#### Related Knowledge

- Transition measurements use verified low-current behavior and explicit definitions. Field-dependent transport requires stable temperature and field, bipolar acquisition, offset rejection and heating checks. **Source:** B020, B021.
- Critical current is interpolated at the fixed electric-field criterion and normalized by measured bridge cross-section. Current-limited and sensitivity-limited measurements are reported as lower bounds or unresolved values rather than substituted results. **Source:** B022.
- Only documented fabrication or measurement failures justify exclusion. Safety controls, raw-data archiving and matched-control qualification govern execution and changes; optional extensions do not replace core comparisons or establish a unique pinning mechanism. **Source:** B023, B024, B025, B026.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Consolidated execution plan

#### 1. Release and comparison design
Keep direct YREBCO/STO growth at **790 °C**, without buffers, discrete oxide layers, nanoparticle loading or excess CuO. Run Y and GY under SSD, +L and +L+LLD; include the approved SSD variance series with a higher-variance Nd-containing composition. Additional liquid-assisted variants are follow-up only. Approve recipes, the RE³⁺ radius convention, the definition and units of 2.31×10⁻⁴, the 26 mol% denominator, delivery calibration, LLD dose and instrument capability before growth. Report unmatched mean radius as a confounder.

Randomize growth order; use **P: three independent films per arm**. Track film, bridge, specimen and lot identities. Reserve intact transport and mapped microscopy regions. Qualify all **P** settings before freezing them across arms. Supplied size, FWHM and Jc values remain reference targets, not outcomes or release gates.

#### 2. Prepare and fabricate
Verify target composition, addition identity and substrate condition. Independently qualify internally synthesized targets; inherited firing schedules are not approved recipes. Apply the proposed STO cleaning/annealing sequence only after qualification, document oxygen pressure/flow, and assess central/edge AFM. Quarantine unsuitable substrates; deposit no buffer.

Calibrate substrate temperature, fluence, pressure and target-specific thickness per pulse at the actual mounting geometry. Proposed common settings include **300 ±15 nm**, **0.40 ±0.02 mbar O₂**, **2.0 ±0.1 J cm⁻²**, **50 ±1 mm** target distance and **10 Hz** continuous SSD/+L delivery. Retain the source's proposed tolerances and preparation details.

Qualify simultaneous BaCu₂O₂-derived addition on sacrificial witnesses using assayed transfer and the approved denominator. Demonstrate liquid-assisted growth rather than labeling nominal feed as liquid. SSD uses host only; +L uses continuous host/addition delivery; +L+LLD uses synchronized **50 Hz bursts**, calibrated layer dose and **P: initial 5 s relaxation**. Match thickness by calibrated pulse count. Record signals, pulses, pauses and deviations; stop and quarantine uncontrolled dose, temperature drift or gross particulates.

Apply identical qualified oxygenation: **P: 500 ±5 °C, 60 ±5 min, 1 bar O₂ and 5 °C min⁻¹ cooling**, only in rated apparatus. Otherwise use a qualified external furnace with matched transfer histories. Inspect damage/residue and map thickness.

#### 3. Characterize and prepare transport
Complete nondestructive XRD and Raman before patterning. Use consistent calibrated XRD profiles, observed peak positions and suitable asymmetric measurements for epitaxy and orthorhombicity. Raman requires calibrated spectra, damage/power checks and matched controls; do not infer strain with an unvalidated coefficient.

Prepare destructive STEM specimens after transport or from mapped edge material. Qualify milling and beam exposure; identify inclusions chemically and structurally, include agglomerates, and report sampling honestly. Normalize density by measured lamella volume, documenting overlap/truncation corrections. GPA requires reference and distortion controls.

Pattern **25 µm** bridges with qualified milling and Ag pads outside the active span. Check residual conduction, shunting and edge damage; measure width, local thickness and tap spacing with uncertainties. Contact annealing needs separate qualification and matched exposure. Establish thermally safe current limits, not an automatic source-range sweep. Demonstrate **P: ≤2 nV effective uncertainty** for the **20 nV** criterion across **1 mm**; otherwise qualify improved acquisition or approved longer spacing without changing the electric-field criterion.

#### 4. Acquire, disposition and archive
Measure low-current zero-field R(T), verify current independence and define transition temperatures/width. Acquire bipolar I–V curves at **20 K**, **0–10 T including 8 T**, **B ∥ c**, with stability, alignment, offset-rejection and heating checks. Retain proposed stepping, stabilization and repeat-sweep settings after qualification. Abort thermal drift, contact-voltage growth or irreversible changes.

Interpolate Ic at **0.2 µV cm⁻¹** and calculate **Jc = Ic/(wt)** from measured geometry. Report current-ceiling lower bounds or sensitivity-limited unresolved Ic. Separate independent-film statistics from bridge repeatability. Retain valid off-target films; exclude only documented failures and quarantine/regrow rather than selectively repair.

Before execution, apply source safety controls for powders, solvents, lasers, oxygen, heat, milling, cryogens and magnets; zero current before connection changes. Archive raw data, calibrations, settings, uncertainties, scripts and exclusions. Compare GY/Y at SSD, +L/SSD and +L+LLD/+L without claiming a unique mechanism. Changes require documented qualification and matched-control regrowth. Optional LLD-only or angular measurements follow core work and do not replace it.
