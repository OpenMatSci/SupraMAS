# Experimental Plan: BaCeO3-modified single-grain GdBCO–Ag bulk protocol

## Experimental Draft

### Experimental overview

Prepare single-grain Gd123–Gd211–Ag-derived bulks with homogeneously dispersed sol-gel nanosize BaCeO3, and compare microstructure, superconducting transition, magnetic Jc(B), and whole-bulk trapped field. Ce segregation and Ba/Gd substitution remain hypotheses, not established mechanisms.

The proposed comparison comprises an undoped control and BaCeO3 levels of 0.189, 0.473, 0.945, 1.891, and 2.836 wt%, with three independent growth replicates per level. Approve the percentage denominator before weighing: the proposed mass convention is Gd123:Gd211:Ag2O = 100:30:10, with BaCeO3 referenced to their combined mass. CeO2 equivalence is only a reporting conversion. Randomize preparation and balance compositions across furnace runs and positions; coupons are subsamples.

Use traceable powders, qualified sol-gel BaCeO3, NdBCO seeds, verified MgO supports, zirconia/ethanol milling, binder-free pressing, and qualified TSMG and oxygenation equipment. Characterization requires polarized-light microscopy, SEM-EDS, calibrated XRD, VSM, and a cryogenic Hall scanner with an intact-bulk field-cooling arrangement.

First approve materials, equipment, geometry, and proposed process settings through pilot qualification. Then prepare powders and pellets, perform seeded melt growth, and oxygenate at 400 °C for 10 days. Establish a common thickness and consistent finishing route for the 17.5 mm final-diameter target. Inspect and Hall-map intact bulks at 77 K after 1.4 T field-cooling before sectioning for structural, microchemical, and VSM measurements.

Anticipated outputs are traceable fabrication records, failure rates, spatial Gd211 statistics, Tc and transition width, magnetic Jc(B), and trapped-field maps with uncertainties and replicate scatter. The supplied 6×10⁴ A/cm² and 0.42 T values are reference targets only. Qualification failures, safety prerequisites, and unresolved conditions remain explicit.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Approve the comparison design and qualify materials, equipment, and operating settings

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and controlled comparison
Prepare Gd123 single-grain bulks containing Gd211 and Ag-derived material. Test whether increasing nanosize BaCeO3 changes Gd211 refinement/distribution, superconducting transition, magnetic Jc(B), and whole-bulk trapped field. Ce segregation and Ba/Gd substitution are mechanistic hypotheses, not assumed findings.

**Composition prerequisite:** approve the percentage denominator before weighing. Proposed convention: for Gd123 mass *m*, use Gd211 = 0.30*m*, Ag2O = 0.10*m*, and BaCeO3 = (*x*/100)(1.40*m*). Do not substitute a 60:30:10 normalized mixture without authorization. Record both additive-basis and final-mixture mass fractions.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Use traceable Gd123, Gd211, Ag2O, sol-gel BaCeO3, NdBCO seeds, sufficiently large MgO supports, zirconia milling media, ethanol, oxygen and liquid nitrogen. No Pt, graded dopant, binder or thin-film processing is included.

Required equipment: analytical balance; enclosed wet mill; solvent-rated drying system; sieve; rated press/die; programmable TSMG furnace; oxygen-compatible annealing furnace; diamond saw/polisher; polarized-light microscope; SEM-EDS; calibrated XRD; VSM with 77 K control; 1.4 T field-cooling magnet accommodating the intact bulk and cryostat; calibrated cryogenic Hall scanner.

Release prerequisites: approve composition convention; verify furnace temperature/atmosphere and seed survival; establish peritectic/growth window; demonstrate final geometry and support compatibility; confirm press load capacity; verify VSM field, moment range, sensitivity and holder background; calibrate Hall response at 77 K, sensor active-plane height and scanner travel. Instrument models in the draft are not proof of available capability.

Confirm that these temperatures permit decomposition and seeded growth without destroying the seed; reject the schedule if pilot evidence fails.

### Experimental Task 2: Qualify the dopant and fabricate, grow, and oxygenate labeled bulks

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Sol-gel dopant preparation.** Use a qualified BaCeO3 sol-gel SOP. If unavailable, propose Ba/Ce nitrate precursors at Ba:Ce = 1:1, citrate:total-metal molar ratio 1.5, pH approximately 7, evaporation to gel, drying at 120 °C and initial calcination at 900 °C for 4 h. Calibrate dissolution, gel formation, burnout and phase formation before use; temperatures/times are not established synthesis conditions. Inspect XRD and particle-size distribution. Adjust only in qualification trials; do not accept coarse or multiphase material as nanosize BaCeO3. **Hand-off:** released dopant lot, synthesis record and retained aliquot to powder preparation.
2. Weigh each independent batch using the approved formula; use a balance capable of resolving the smallest dopant charge. Premix BaCeO3 with a small base-powder fraction, then combine and wet-mill. Include an undoped milling blank. Remove ethanol using solvent-rated equipment, dry, sieve and retain an aliquot for phase/dispersion checks. Do not claim a specific powder D50 from milling time alone.
3. Press binder-free pellets; record mass, dimensions, pressure and cracks. Pre-react using the table. Ag2O may decompose during heating: document the precursor addition, not presumed retained Ag2O. **Hand-off:** sound pellets and powder records to the growth operator. If pellets lack handling strength, calibrate compaction separately rather than adding binder mid-series.
4. Place each pellet on its qualified MgO support; center the NdBCO seed with its c-axis nominally normal to the top face, misalignment <5°. Execute the qualified TSMG schedule; log actual temperature continuously. Photograph growth sectors and inspect cracks, seed loss, melt leakage and parasitic grains. **Hand-off:** labeled grown bulks and furnace traces to oxygenation.
5. Oxygenate at 400 °C for 10 days in flowing O2. Count dwell after thermal equilibration. Record oxygen flow, pressure, temperature and cooling. Establish any diameter finishing needed in the pilot; perform it consistently before final oxygenation. Measure final diameter and thickness. Proposed diameter acceptance: 17.5 ±0.2 mm. **Hand-off:** intact oxygenated bulks to nondestructive characterization and Hall mapping; do not cut first.

#### Related Knowledge

- A qualified sol-gel SOP is preferred. The fallback precursor and thermal settings are qualification proposals, not an established synthesis recipe; phase and particle-size checks govern release of the dopant lot. **Source:** B011.
- Approved weighing, dopant premixing, wet milling, retained aliquots, and binder-free compaction preserve batch traceability. Milling duration alone does not establish powder size, and Ag2O addition does not establish its retention after heating. **Source:** B011, B001.
- Seeded growth must use qualified supports and thermal conditions, followed by documented oxygenation. Consistent finishing precedes final oxygenation, and intact oxygenated bulks are handed off for whole-bulk measurements before cutting. **Source:** B011, B009, B010.

### Experimental Task 3: Inspect intact bulks and map trapped field after field-cooling

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Whole-bulk inspection and trapped-field mapping
Photograph every face and map visible growth sectors/cracks. Obtain top-face θ–2θ scans at center and accessible peripheral positions before magnetization.

For each trapped-field cycle, warm above its measured/anticipated Tc, establish a uniform **1.4 T** field parallel to c, and cool to **77 K**. Proposed equilibration: ≥20 min after temperature stabilizes. Ramp the applied field to zero initially at 0.01 T/s, subject to magnet and mechanical qualification. Verify residual applied field independently. Start the scan at a fixed proposed delay of 5 min; log every point's time. Maintain 77 K and constant sensor active-plane height.

Scan the entire bulk and surrounding background, including edges. Three readings per point are proposed. Record Bz(x,y), peak location/value, asymmetry and center-field relaxation before/after scanning; render a three-dimensional surface plot. This is not a full vector-field measurement. Repeat one cycle per bulk to assess reproducibility and inspect cracks between cycles. Compare the supplied **0.42 T** target only with stated height, thickness and delay; the task does not supply these reference geometries.

#### Related Knowledge

- Face photographs, crack and sector mapping, and top-face XRD precede magnetization, preserving nondestructive observations of the intact bulk. **Source:** B012, B004.
- The trapped-field cycle requires 1.4 T field-cooling to 77 K with the field parallel to c. Equilibration, field-removal rate, scan delay, and sensor height remain proposed controls subject to qualification and documentation. **Source:** B013, B009.
- Scanning the bulk and surrounding background records Bz rather than a full vector field. Repeat cycles, point timestamps, relaxation records, and crack inspection support reproducibility assessment; the 0.42 T target is interpretable only with stated geometry, height, and delay. **Source:** B014, B013.

### Experimental Task 4: Section mapped bulks and characterize phases, orientation, and Gd211 distribution

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Destructive characterization and VSM hand-off
After Hall testing, section using low-force diamond cutting. Use normalized positions within radius 8.75 mm; never sample 10 mm from the center. Preserve center/mid-radius/edge and axial coordinates. Polish `-X` with 9→3→1 µm diamond; avoid etching and aggressive ultrasonic cleaning. Transfer measured, dry coupons to VSM with an orientation sketch.

**XRD:** proposed Cu Kα, λ=1.5406 Å; 2θ=10–80°, 0.02° steps, 2 s/step. Index actual Gd123 **(00l)** peaks. If accessible and sufficiently intense, acquire a (005) rocking curve over ±5° at 0.01° increments; proposed FWHM <2° is an orientation screen, not proof of a single grain. Use powdered representative material to inspect Gd123, Gd211, Ag and residual dopant phases. Embedded Gd211 precludes a “single-phase Gd123” gate. Peak shifts alone cannot establish Ba/Gd substitution or oxygen content.

**Microscopy:** map polarized-light sectors and cracks. Acquire BSE fields at each radial location and at top/mid/bottom depths. Proposed minimum: five randomized fields per region and ≥100 resolved Gd211 particles per bulk. Calibrate pixel size and segmentation against EDS; do not assume bright contrast uniquely identifies Gd211. Report equivalent-circle diameter, median, mean, SD, resolved-size cutoff, distributions and fieldwise area fraction. Estimate V211 stereologically only for representative sections; report V211/d211 with d in µm and uncertainty, excluding pores/Ag appropriately.

Start EDS at 15 kV, 1–5 nA; calibrate acquisition and overlap corrections with standards. Compare spectra/maps from ≥20 Gd211 interfaces, particle interiors and matrix locations. Report detection limits. A nominal nanometre beam spot is not nanometre EDS resolution; absent detectable enrichment does not disprove Ce segregation.

#### Related Knowledge

- Sectioning follows Hall testing and preserves radial, axial, and seed-orientation coordinates. Sampling must remain within the bulk radius, and measured dry coupons carry an orientation sketch into VSM testing. **Source:** B015, B004.
- Actual Gd123 (00l) peaks and representative powdered phases guide XRD interpretation. An accessible rocking curve is an orientation screen, not single-grain proof; embedded Gd211 prevents a single-phase Gd123 gate, and peak shifts alone cannot establish substitution or oxygen content. **Source:** B016.
- Spatially distributed microscopy, calibrated segmentation, and EDS-supported phase identification underpin Gd211 size and area-fraction statistics. Stereological estimates require representative sections and explicit exclusions and uncertainties. **Source:** B017.
- EDS acquisition requires calibration, overlap correction, and detection-limit reporting. Beam-spot size does not establish nanoscale chemical resolution, and undetected enrichment does not disprove Ce segregation. **Source:** B018.

### Experimental Task 5: Measure superconducting properties and consolidate quality-controlled comparison records

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Superconducting measurements and consistency checks
**ZFC Tc:** proposed cool from 110 to 70 K in verified near-zero field; apply 10 Oe (approximately 1 mT in vacuum), then warm to 110 K at 1 K/min with 0.1 K sampling. Define Tc onset by a documented background/transition fit and ΔTc by 10–90% normalized diamagnetic change. ΔTc <2 K is a proposed diagnostic, not a task-mandated rejection threshold.

**Loops:** measure rectangular coupons initially 2×2×1 mm³, with measured in-plane dimensions a≤b and field parallel to c. Use a common proposed ±5 T range: 0→+5→−5→+5 T, comparing complete opposing branches. Verify capability before use. Initial spacing: 0.1 T below 1 T, 0.5 T above; refine around any observed secondary peak. Start with 10 s/point; test dwell and sweep-rate dependence rather than assuming equilibrium.

Subtract holder background; normalize moment by measured volume. Extended Bean estimate:

Jc = 20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)],

with a,b in cm, full branch difference ΔM in emu/cm³, yielding A/cm². Label this **magnetic Jc**, including geometry/model uncertainty. For a=b=0.2 cm and volume 0.004 cm³, Jc=6×10⁴ A/cm² requires ΔM=400 emu/cm³ and Δm=1.6 emu. The draft's 0.1 emu sensitivity is inadequate for precise low-width loops: require demonstrated branch-width uncertainty ≤5% where quantitative comparison is claimed. At lower signals, report limits.

No transport current, voltage criterion or voltage taps are specified or required. Do not convert magnetic Jc into measured transport Ic. Report applied µ0H in tesla; self-field effects remain near zero applied field. Calculate nominal pinning force JcB using Jc in A/m², clearly identifying the field approximation.

##### 8. Quality gates, diagnosis, records and feedback
Quarantine unidentified powders, unqualified geometry/equipment, lost seeds, parasitic grains, major cracks and incomplete records. Retain failed samples and report failure rates by composition; exclude them from the successful-single-grain subset only using predefined criteria. Low performance alone is not exclusion grounds.

Agglomeration: inspect dopant/milling dispersion; adjust qualification, not production doses. Parasitic grains: inspect seed survival, alignment and thermal window. Cracking: inspect gradients, support reaction and cooldown. Broad/low Tc: investigate oxygen delivery, thickness and composition before any re-annealing; Tc alone does not determine δ. Irreproducible loops/maps: check mounting, background, drift, relaxation and sensor height. Tag all reprocessed samples separately.

Control respirable oxide powders, nitrate/citrate burnout gases, flammable ethanol, press stored energy, hot furnaces, oxygen enrichment, cryogen asphyxiation and magnetic projectile forces using approved laboratory procedures. Never evaporate ethanol in an ordinary hot oven or introduce solvent into oxygen equipment.

Archive masses, lots, approvals, thermal traces, geometry, specimen coordinates, calibrations, raw images/spectra/loops/maps, segmentation settings, scripts, uncertainties and exclusions. Plot composition against Tc, ΔTc, Gd211 statistics, Jc(B), observed peak position and trapped field with replicate scatter. Do not force monotonic/linear fits or a 3–4 T peak. Evaluate the supplied 6×10⁴ A/cm² target without treating it as observed.

Freeze the core series after pilot qualification. Any corrective process change requires a fresh matched control and balanced composition block. Optional follow-up may refine the dopant grid or use higher-resolution chemical/site-occupancy analysis to test segregation/substitution; it must not replace the original controls or retrospectively establish a mechanism from correlations alone.
