# Experimental Plan: EuBCO coated-conductor wet-etch optimization

## Experimental Draft

### Stage I — Qualification, comparison design and baseline preparation
The objective is to remove the 25 µm Cu stabilizer over a 10 mm window on qualified EuBCO/Hastelloy coated conductor while initially retaining the 2.8 µm Ag cap and preserving superconducting connectivity. Cu-stage reference targets are Ic retention ≥95% and n ≥25 at 77 K, self-field; these are not measured outcomes.

Use one qualified lot with its existing IBAD/PLD buffers. The proposed factorial compares FeCl₃ concentrations of 50, 200 and 300 g L⁻¹ at 0, 25 and 60 °C for 10, 17 and 30 min, with proposed independent replication, matched witnesses, water controls and untreated controls. A separate endpoint-confirmation arm examines 200 g L⁻¹ at 0 °C near 17 min, contingent on complete removal. Required resources include compatible masking, temperature-controlled baths, optical and mass measurements, cross-section tools, SEM/EDS, XRD, 488 nm Raman and qualified LN₂ four-probe equipment.

### Stage II — Controlled metal removal
Establish baseline geometry and transport before masking and assigned Cu exposure. Keep fixed-time and endpoint arms distinct. Validate rinsing, document residual Cu and damage, and measure Cu-stage transport before any designated Ag removal. Ag processing remains gated by clarification of the supplied ammonia/peroxide stocks, 1:4 ratio basis, safe preparation and endpoint; pair it with equal-duration 5 °C water shams.

### Stage III — Measurement, qualification and reporting
Apply consistent transport measurements at baseline, after Cu removal and, where contacts permit, after Ag or sham processing. Compare stage-matched structural and oxygen indicators without attributing Ag-induced changes solely to FeCl₃. All P settings require pilot calibration. Anticipated outputs are documented removal classifications, transport retention, structural comparisons, uncertainties, failures and a condition requiring confirmation—not a guaranteed recipe or joint-performance demonstration.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Stage I — Qualify resources and establish the specimen comparison matrix

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and release prerequisites
Optimize removal of 25 µm Cu over a 10 mm joint-preparation window on EuBa₂Cu₃O₇₋ₓ/Hastelloy conductors with their existing IBAD/PLD buffers and 2.8 µm Ag cap. Preserve superconducting connectivity, not deliberately create pinning defects. Acceptance targets after **Cu removal** are Ic retention ≥95% and n ≥25 at 77 K, self-field.

Use one qualified conductor lot. Obtain layer sequence, native width, EuBCO thickness, Cu distribution on front/back/edges and initial texture records. Verify thicknesses on sacrificial sections. Do not substitute SrTiO₃, add BaHfO₃/CuO, or redeposit layers. New conductor fabrication requires a separately qualified manufacturing recipe.

Before release, verify: masking compatibility and leakage; bath capacity at 0–60 °C; current-source/lead/interlock capability; voltage-contact current-transfer geometry; and the stock concentrations, volume/mass basis and safe preparation procedure for the supplied NH₃·H₂O/H₂O₂ 1:4 mixture. Silver etching must not proceed until these are resolved.

All settings identified below as **P** are proposed choices requiring pilot calibration, not supplied experimental conditions.

##### 2. Specimen matrix and identifiers
Use native-width, approximately 60 mm-long coupons (**P**), with sufficient intact Cu-covered ends for contacts. Randomize adjacent tape segments within longitudinal blocks.

| Arm | Conditions | Independent replicates |
|---|---|---:|
| Factorial F | FeCl₃: 50, 200, 300 g L⁻¹; bath: 0, 25, 60 °C; immersion: 10, 17, 30 min | 3/cell (**P**); 81 coupons |
| Water W | 0 g L⁻¹; same temperatures and times | 3/cell (**P**) |
| Untreated U | Masking, rinsing, storage and repeat transport without immersion | 3/block (**P**) |
| Endpoint confirmation E | 200 g L⁻¹, 0 °C; stop upon calibrated complete-Cu endpoint near 17 min | 3 new coupons (**P**) |

Do not replace fixed-time arms with endpoint-stopped samples. Incomplete removal is a result, not permission to prolong that coupon's assigned exposure.

ID: `lot-block-C###-T##-t##-r#-stage`, with stages B (baseline), C (Cu-stage), A (Ag-stage) and S (sham Ag-stage). Record actual conditions separately. Assign matched sacrificial witnesses to every cell for cross-sections and exposed-EuBCO characterization. Following Cu-stage measurements, randomly assign additional matched coupons to Ag etching or a 5 °C DI-water sham of equal duration; measure both before and after this second stage. Retain Cu-stage-only specimens.

Equipment: ventilated chemical workstation, chilled/heated nonmetallic baths, calibrated temperature probe, timer, optical camera, 0.1 mg-resolution balance, profilometer/cross-section tools, SEM/EDS, calibrated XRD, 488 nm Raman, LN₂ four-probe fixture, adequate current supply, nanovoltmeter and fast current shutdown.

- Randomized adjacent segments, independent coupons, water and untreated controls, matched witnesses and a separate endpoint arm support comparisons without replacing assigned fixed-time exposures. **Source:** B004, B005, B006, B007.
- Release qualifications and P labels distinguish unresolved preparation and operating choices from supplied conditions, particularly for the ammonia/peroxide mixture and equipment capability. **Source:** B002, B003, B008, B009.

### Experimental Task 2: Stage I — Prepare coupons, qualify contacts and establish baselines

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Preparation and baseline hand-off
1. Cut without bending or overheating the tape; inspect edges and quarantine mechanically damaged coupons. Measure width, thickness, exposed area and mass. Photograph fiducials and native defects.
2. Establish current contacts on intact Cu ends and voltage contacts outside the future etched window. Qualify low-heat contact attachment on witnesses; avoid heating the active window. Measure contact resistance, stability and current-transfer length. The extra 2 mm inside the voltage span must remain unchanged; verify that it does not dominate the measured transition.
3. Measure baseline Ic and n using Section 7. Keep contact geometry unchanged through Cu processing. Obtain baseline XRD and matched-witness SEM/Raman where accessible; intact metal may obstruct EuBCO signals.
4. Apply validated masking to protect contacts and non-window surfaces. Keep exposed sidewall geometry identical across arms. Weigh the masked coupon separately; mask mass changes must not be interpreted as Cu loss.
5. Baseline operator releases the coupon, geometry sheet and raw transport files to the etch operator. Reject leaking masks before immersion.

#### Related Knowledge

- Geometry records, fiducial photographs and mechanical-damage screening establish the pre-exposure condition and support subsequent matched comparisons. **Source:** B010.
- Qualified contacts, unchanged voltage geometry and validated masking protect the transport comparison; mask mass changes must not be interpreted as Cu loss. **Source:** B010, B009.

### Experimental Task 3: Stage II — Perform assigned Cu etching and verify removal

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Cu etching and hand-off
1. Dissolve FeCl₃ in DI water under cooling, then bring to final volume after thermal equilibration. Document reagent form, concentration, pH, bath volume, temperature and preparation time. Equilibrate before loading.
2. Immerse one coupon in its assigned fresh bath; start timing at complete wetting. Log temperature continuously and optical progression every 30 s (**P**). Do not repeatedly remove transport coupons for weighing; use separate interrupted witnesses for rate calibration.
3. At assigned time, transfer immediately to sequential DI-water rinses. A proposed three-rinse sequence must be validated by rinse conductivity and chloride/iron checks. Do not use isopropanol as the primary salt-removal rinse. Dry gently with nitrogen; avoid rubbing, ultrasonics and rapid flexing.
4. Inspect, photograph and weigh after drying to a stable mass. Determine residual stabilizer using optical coverage plus profilometry/cross-sections and surface-sensitive EDS relative to Ag and layer references. **Cu detected by EDS is not uniquely residual stabilizer: EuBCO itself contains Cu.**
5. Record Cu-stage damage before any silver removal. Etch operator hands intact coupons and exposure/rinse logs to transport; sacrificial witnesses go to characterization. Store dry in clean containers and match elapsed time-to-measurement across arms.

Mass is supporting evidence only. For one 25 µm Cu face, using ρCu ≈8.96 g cm⁻³, expected loss is approximately **22.4 mg cm⁻² of exposed face**. Sum actual exposed faces/edges. Never stop at 90% of total coupon mass loss. A 25 µm layer removed in 17 min implies an average thickness rate of **1.47 µm min⁻¹**, a calibration check rather than an assumed rate.

#### Related Knowledge

- Assigned bath exposure, prompt validated rinsing and gentle drying preserve the distinction between exposure conditions and subsequent handling; interrupted rate measurements use separate witnesses. **Source:** B011, B006.
- Removal assessment combines optical coverage, dimensional or cross-sectional evidence and referenced EDS because Cu signals can also originate from EuBCO. **Source:** B011.
- Mass loss and the supplied thickness-rate calculation are supporting calibration checks, not standalone endpoints or assumed removal rates. **Source:** B012.

### Experimental Task 4: Stage II — Conduct qualified Ag removal and matched sham processing

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Ag removal and characterization hand-off
After Cu-stage transport, process designated A specimens and matched witnesses using the clarified 1:4 ammonia/peroxide mixture at 5 °C. Calibrate the minimum complete-Ag endpoint on sacrificial specimens; log exposure and rinse promptly with DI water. Run matched S controls in 5 °C water for the same time. Retain protected contact pads. Re-measure transport where contact integrity permits.

Characterization staff receive C, A and S specimens with stage-specific records. Do not attribute damage introduced during Ag removal solely to FeCl₃. Characterize C surfaces/interfaces first; use Ag-stripped witnesses to reveal EuBCO, comparing specimens subjected to identical Ag preparation and sham controls. If the Ag step measurably changes oxygen indicators, report the attribution limitation.

#### Related Knowledge

- Ag removal follows Cu-stage transport and requires clarified mixture preparation plus sacrificial endpoint calibration; equal-duration water shams distinguish second-stage handling effects. **Source:** B002, B013.
- Stage-specific characterization and identical Ag preparation prevent damage or oxygen-indicator changes introduced during Ag removal from being assigned solely to FeCl₃. **Source:** B014, B007.

### Experimental Task 5: Stage III — Measure stage-resolved transport and normalize Ic and n

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Transport measurement and consistency checks
Use four probes in LN₂ at measured approximately 77 K, without applied magnetic field. Record residual ambient field, lead arrangement and temperature. Stabilize until voltage offset and temperature are steady; use twisted voltage leads and isolated grounding.

Size equipment from **measured baseline Ic**, not the draft's ±10 A source. With verified EuBCO thickness t, Jc = Ic/(wt). Illustratively, 1 µm = 10⁻⁴ cm and Jc = 3 MA cm⁻² imply **300 A per cm width**, not 100 or 500 A/cm. A 500 A/cm value would imply 5 MA cm⁻² at that thickness; neither is an acceptance requirement here.

Validate contact heating through P = I²R: 300 A through 1 µΩ produces 0.09 W per contact. Qualify cooling and shutdown accordingly. Voltage noise should be below 0.1 µV (**P**); the lower fit limit at 12 mm is only 0.12 µV, so improve averaging or raise that limit if necessary.

Ramp in calibrated coarse-to-fine steps, recording current, voltage, temperature and dwell time. Correct offsets using zero-current readings and qualified polarity reversal. Stop on unstable voltage, heating or the validated protection limit; never force the full fit range. Three repeat ramps (**P**) check reproducibility, not independent replication.

Compute E = V/L and interpolate Ic at Ec. Fit positive, noise-resolved data to E = Ec(I/Ic)ⁿ; report fit range, residuals and uncertainty. A universal 110% Ic ceiling is inappropriate: reaching 10Ec requires I/Ic = 10^(1/n), approximately 1.096 for n =25 but 1.122 for n =20. Record truncated fits or instrument-limited Ic as such.

#### Related Knowledge

- Four-probe measurements require recorded thermal and electrical conditions, equipment sized from measured baseline Ic and qualified contact heating and shutdown. **Source:** B015, B016, B017.
- Electric-field normalization uses the actual voltage span, and Ic is interpolated at the specified electric-field criterion rather than an unrelated fixed voltage. **Source:** B009, B019.
- Noise-resolved fitting and safe ramp limits determine the usable n-fit range; repeat ramps assess reproducibility rather than independent replication, and truncated or instrument-limited results remain explicit. **Source:** B017, B018, B019.

### Experimental Task 6: Stage III — Characterize morphology, texture and oxygen indicators

#### Specific Content

##### 8. Multi-scale characterization
- **SEM/EDS:** Survey the entire window optically, then registered center, edge and native-defect sites; minimum three sites (**P**). Start SEM near 5 kV with low dose; calibrate EDS energy for required lines. Avoid coatings on reusable coupons, plasma cleaning and unvalidated beam exposure. Quantify delamination area, crack density and ring-crack diameter in µm, and inspect Ag/EuBCO cross-sections on witnesses. EDS supports elemental distribution, not Fe oxidation state or definitive BaCuO₂ identification.
- **XRD:** Proposed Cu Kα wavelength 1.5406 Å, 20–60° 2θ survey, 0.02° steps; extend coverage if needed for phase identification. Align a reference standard and reproduce specimen height/footprint. Use integrated, background-subtracted I(005)/I(006). For (00l), c = lλ/[2sinθ]; fit multiple reflections with displacement correction. At illustrative c =11.68 Å, (005)/(006) occur near 38.5°/46.6° 2θ, within the survey. Report Δc with uncertainty, texture changes and verified secondary-phase peaks. Do not claim bulk weight fractions from textured thin-film intensities without a validated model.
- **Raman:** Use **488 nm**, calibrated wavenumber reference, fixed geometry/polarization and matched sites. Begin at low power; calibrate power-independence to exclude heating. Proposed acquisition: three 30 s accumulations/site. Fit O(4) position and width; report shifts relative to preparation-matched controls. Absolute δ requires independently calibrated EuBCO standards; neither color nor a universal 5 cm⁻¹ shift establishes δ.

#### Related Knowledge

- Registered optical and SEM/EDS observations connect window-scale coverage with local defects and witness cross-sections, while retaining limits on elemental and phase interpretation. **Source:** B020.
- XRD comparisons require alignment, matched geometry, integrated background-subtracted intensities and displacement-corrected lattice analysis; textured-film intensities do not independently establish bulk phase fractions. **Source:** B020.
- Raman comparisons use 488 nm excitation, power-independence checks and preparation-matched controls; absolute oxygen deficiency requires independently calibrated EuBCO standards. **Source:** B020, B014.

### Experimental Task 7: Stage III — Apply acceptance gates, diagnose failures and confirm conditions

#### Specific Content

##### 9. Gates, diagnosis, safety and controlled feedback
A Cu-stage specimen passes only with verified complete Cu removal, retention 100Ic,C/Ic,B ≥95%, n ≥25 and valid transport. Structural targets are preserved (00l), no observable peeling/buckling, ring-crack diameter ≤10 µm and c-axis increase <0.02 Å within measurement uncertainty. Report detection limits; absence of visible damage does not prove adhesion or low residual stress.

Incomplete Cu: classify failure of removal, inspect bath capacity/masking and calibrate on new witnesses. Peeling/cracks: quarantine, examine edge ingress, pinholes and flow; do not mechanically flatten. Reduced Ic/n: first check temperature, contacts, geometry and residual Cu, then correlate morphology and oxygen indicators. Raman shifts: repeat at lower power and review Ag controls. Calibration failures invalidate measurements, not necessarily specimens. Do not anneal damaged core samples to conceal degradation.

Handle FeCl₃ as corrosive and ammonia/peroxide as reactive oxidizing chemistry in a hood with compatible PPE, cooling and splash protection. Follow approved mixing order; keep waste streams separate and collect metal-containing waste through institutional disposal. Do not improvise neutralization. LN₂ requires ventilation, face protection and oxygen-deficiency controls; guard high-current connections and interlock shutdown.

Archive raw files, IDs, positions, dimensions, recipes, bath histories, endpoint evidence, rinse checks, images, fit settings, uncertainties and every deviation. Retain failures and outliers unless a documented technical cause justifies exclusion. Analyze independent replicates using concentration–temperature–time interactions and block effects. Confirm the selected complete-removal condition on new specimens. Optional finer FeCl₃ spacing or exposure timing follows, without deleting controls. Joint bonding, high-field magnet qualification and oxygen-restoration trials require separate proposals; this experiment does not establish persistent-current joint resistance.

#### Related Knowledge

- Cu-stage acceptance combines verified complete removal, valid transport retention and n with structural targets and reported uncertainty; lack of visible damage does not establish adhesion or residual stress. **Source:** B021.
- Failure diagnosis first separates processing damage from measurement or calibration faults, retains damaged specimens without concealment and preserves documented failures and outliers. **Source:** B022, B024.
- Approved chemical handling, separate waste streams, LN₂ precautions and high-current interlocks are safety prerequisites, while confirmation and optional refinement remain distinct from separately proposed joint or restoration studies. **Source:** B023, B024.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage I — Qualification, design and baseline preparation
**Tasks 1–2.** Use one qualified EuBCO/Hastelloy conductor lot with its existing IBAD/PLD buffers, 25 µm Cu stabilizer and 2.8 µm Ag cap. Obtain architecture, dimensions, Cu-distribution and texture records; verify thicknesses on sacrificial sections. Do not substitute substrates, add pinning materials or redeposit layers.

Before release, qualify masking and leakage, bath capacity across the selected temperatures, contacts and current-transfer geometry, current-source capability and shutdown. Clarify ammonia/peroxide stock strengths, the supplied 1:4 ratio basis and approved preparation procedure. All **P** settings remain proposed and require pilot calibration, including coupon dimensions, replication, tolerances, bath loading/mixing and acquisition choices.

Randomize adjacent native-width coupons within longitudinal blocks. Preserve the proposed factorial of 50/200/300 g L⁻¹ FeCl₃ × 0/25/60 °C × 10/17/30 min, with three independent transport coupons per cell (**P**), matched witnesses, corresponding water controls and untreated controls. Keep the separate 200 g L⁻¹, 0 °C endpoint-confirmation arm near 17 min conditional on calibrated complete removal. Assign traceable IDs and actual-condition records; retain Cu-only specimens and allocate additional matched coupons to Ag or sham processing after Cu-stage measurements.

Inspect and document dimensions, mass, fiducials and native defects; quarantine mechanical damage. Qualify low-heat contacts on intact Cu ends and establish baseline transport before masking. Keep the proposed 12 mm voltage span bracketing the 10 mm window unchanged and verify that the unchanged extra span does not dominate the transition. Record mask mass separately and reject leaking masks.

### Stage II — Controlled Cu removal and gated Ag processing
**Tasks 3–4.** Prepare assay-documented FeCl₃ baths under cooling and thermal equilibration. Record concentration, pH, volume, temperature and preparation history. Expose each coupon according to its assigned arm, logging temperature and optical progression. Fixed-time coupons must not receive extended exposure for incomplete removal; calibrate rates with separate interrupted witnesses.

Transfer promptly to validated sequential DI-water rinses, checking conductivity and chloride/iron removal. Dry gently with nitrogen without rubbing, ultrasonics or rapid flexing. Photograph, inspect and weigh at stable mass. Verify removal using optical coverage, profilometry or cross-sections and referenced EDS. Mass is supporting evidence only, and Cu in EDS is not uniquely residual stabilizer. Record damage before Ag removal, then hand off coupons and logs for Cu-stage transport; match storage and elapsed measurement times across arms.

Only after Cu-stage transport and resolution of mixture prerequisites, process designated Ag specimens at 5 °C using a sacrificially calibrated minimum complete-Ag endpoint. Run equal-duration 5 °C DI-water shams, retain protected contact pads and remeasure transport where contact integrity permits. Characterize Cu-stage interfaces first. Compare Ag-exposed EuBCO only with preparation-matched specimens and sham controls; report any Ag-related oxygen-indicator attribution limitation.

### Stage III — Stage-resolved measurement, acceptance and reporting
**Tasks 5–7.** Apply the transport procedure at baseline, Cu-stage and eligible Ag/sham stages, despite its consolidated placement here. Measure in LN₂ near 77 K without applied field, recording temperature, residual field and lead arrangement. Size equipment from measured baseline Ic; qualify contact heating, noise and protection limits. Correct offsets, use calibrated ramps and stop for instability or heating. Repeat ramps are reproducibility checks, not independent replicates.

Compute E = V/L and interpolate Ic at 1 µV cm⁻¹; the proposed 12 mm span gives 1.2 µV. Fit n only over positive, noise-resolved, safely accessible data, reporting range, residuals and uncertainty. Record truncated fits and instrument limits. Normalize Cu-stage retention as 100Ic,C/Ic,B; use verified width and EuBCO thickness for any Jc calculation.

Combine registered optical/SEM/EDS observations with calibrated XRD and 488 nm Raman. Report morphology, integrated I(005)/I(006), displacement-corrected c-axis changes and preparation-matched O(4) shifts. Retain limits on phase identification, textured-film fractions and absolute oxygen deficiency.

Pass requires complete Cu removal, valid transport, retention ≥95% and n ≥25. Structural reference targets include preserved (00l), no observable peeling/buckling, ring cracks ≤10 µm and c-axis increase <0.02 Å within uncertainty. These are targets, not outcomes. Diagnose failures without flattening or restorative annealing. Archive raw data, deviations, uncertainties, failures and justified exclusions; analyze independent replication, interactions and block effects, then confirm the selected condition on new specimens. Optional finer spacing follows without deleting controls. Joint bonding, high-field qualification and oxygen restoration remain separate proposals.

Throughout, require approved hood/PPE/cooling practices, separate chemical waste, LN₂ ventilation and oxygen-deficiency controls, guarded high-current connections and interlocked shutdown.
