# Experimental Plan: Bulk (Y,NSG)BCO composite fabrication and testing

## Experimental Draft

### Stage 1 — Define comparisons and qualify production
The objective is to fabricate monolithic hollow bulk cylinders by sol-casting, rapid-prototyped gelcasting and top/bottom NdBCO-seeded infiltration growth, then assess inclusions, texture, connectivity, magnetic properties and liquid-nitrogen shielding. Compare unsubstituted Y-123, the specified 20 wt% mixed Nd/Sm/Gd-123 formulation without W, and the same formulation with 0.5 wt% WO3 initially 2–6 nm. Match 211 loading and size, Ba excess, geometry, seeds and thermal history. These comparisons distinguish mixed-RE and W-addition effects, not a factorial synergy interaction.

Use characterized powders, compatible 211, approved excess-Ba precursor, Ba–Cu–O infiltrant, qualified gelcasting materials and compatible seeds/supports. Required facilities include controlled mixing and casting, thermal processing, XRD, microscopy/EDAX, susceptibility, VSM, calibrated shielding equipment and mandatory nanoscale chemical/structural analysis. Release mass denominators, nominal RE-site occupancy, the unresolved 211 fraction basis and an executable verification pathway before production.

### Stage 2 — Fabricate and establish material conformance
**P:** three independent matched fabrication blocks, with randomized furnace positions and test order. Prepare and cast permeable preforms, conduct qualified air infiltration growth, oxygenate, and maintain specimen lineage. All **P** geometry, slurry, mixing, thermal and measurement settings remain proposed until calibrated. Assess phase fractions, texture, spatial oxygenation, defects and post-growth inclusion identity; W detection alone does not verify retained WO3 nanoparticles.

### Stage 3 — Measure properties and consolidate evidence
Perform intact-cylinder shielding at 77 K before cutting mapped specimens for characterization and magnetic measurements. Derive volume-normalized coupon Jc and pinning-force curves with documented alignment, geometry and limitations. Supplied performance values are reference targets, never measured outcomes. Anticipated outputs are traceable fabrication records, conformance classifications, comparison curves and uncertainty-qualified shielding data. Optional transport remains separate from core VSM testing. Preserve failures, unresolved qualifications and safety prerequisites throughout.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Define matched comparison arms and release production prerequisites

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Objective and comparison matrix
Produce monolithic hollow cylinders by sol-casting, rapid-prototyped gelcasting (RPGC), and top/bottom NdBCO-seeded infiltration growth (IG). Evaluate embedded inclusions, texture, connectivity, magnetic Jc, pinning force and liquid-nitrogen shielding. **P** denotes a proposed choice requiring calibration, not a supplied condition.

| Arm | Matrix | WO3 addition |
|---|---|---|
| Y | Unsubstituted Y-123 | None |
| NSG | Specified 20 wt% mixed Nd/Sm/Gd-123 formulation | None |
| NSGW | Same mixed-RE formulation | 0.5 wt%, initially 2–6 nm |

Match 211 loading/size, Ba excess, geometry, seeds and thermal history. Y is an unsubstituted-matrix control, not a secondary-phase-free material. These arms distinguish mixed-RE effects without W and W-addition effects within mixed RE; they do not establish a factorial synergy interaction.

**P:** Three independent blocks, each containing all arms; randomize furnace positions and test order. IDs: `B01-Y-C01`, `B01-NSG-C01`, `B01-NSGW-C01`, through B03. Append mapped top/middle/bottom location and test suffixes to fragments. Retain failed specimens and parent–child records.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Materials: characterized Y-123, equimolar Nd/Sm/Gd-123, composition-compatible (Y/RE)2BaCuO5, WO3 nanopowder, approved excess-Ba precursor, Ba–Cu–O infiltrant, compatible gelcasting solvent/dispersant/binder/gel system, NdBCO film seeds and inert supports. Record lots, moisture, composition and particle-size evidence.

Equipment: balance, contamination-controlled mixer, cooled sonicator, rheometer, annular split moulds, drying/debinding and air IG furnaces, oxygenation furnace, metrology, diamond saw, optical microscope, Cu Kα XRD with texture capability, FE-SEM/EDAX, AC susceptometer, VSM, solenoid, calibrated Hall probe and temperature acquisition. **Mandatory additional access:** TEM/STEM, specimen preparation and chemical/structural analysis capable of resolving 2–6 nm inclusions and distinguishing WO3 from alternative W-bearing phases.

Before production, release a mass-balance sheet defining all weight denominators, 211 charge, infiltrant composition/mass and Ba basis. Include calculated nominal matrix RE-site occupancy, formula weights, oxygen-content assumptions, and assumed partitioning between matrix, 211 and liquid-derived phases. Actual occupancy remains a characterization question. Resolve the supplied 37% 211 fraction basis. If BaCO3 supplies elemental-equivalent Ba, 197.34/137.33 = 1.437: 2 g Ba requires 2.87 g BaCO3, not 2 g.

Verify furnace uniformity, seed survival, VSM ±9 T/10–77 K capability, holder clearance/moment range and solenoid calibration. Qualify nanoscale resolution, phase discrimination and a spatial sampling plan on pilots. No production release without an executable recipe and verification pathway.

#### Related Knowledge

- The three arms separate mixed-RE effects without W from W-addition effects within the mixed-RE formulation. Matching 211, Ba excess, geometry, seeds and thermal history supports those comparisons; the Y control still contains secondary phases. **Source:** B000, B001, B002.
- The released mass balance must resolve weight denominators, the supplied 211 fraction basis and nominal RE-site occupancy while accounting for matrix, 211 and liquid-derived phases. Nominal occupancy does not establish actual substitution. **Source:** B006.
- Production depends on qualified furnace, seed, magnetic-measurement and nanoscale-analysis capabilities, including representative sampling and discrimination of WO3 from other W-bearing phases. **Source:** B004, B005, B007.

### Experimental Task 2: Calibrate operating settings and fabricate oxygenated hollow cylinders

#### Specific Content

##### 3. Consolidated operating parameters

| Operation | Condition/status |
|---|---|
| Cylinder | **P:** final OD 16 mm, ID 8 mm, height 8 mm, each ±0.2 mm; nominal wall 4 mm. Calibrate mould shrinkage. |
| Slurry | **P:** 50 wt% solids; screen 500–800 mPa·s at recorded temperature/shear rate. Validate actual gel chemistry. |
| Mixing | **P:** coarse constituents 300 rpm/2 h; W sonication nominal 20 kHz/150 W, up to 30 min with cooling. Calibrate delivered energy. |
| Drying/burnout | Pilot drying/TGA-derived stages and endpoints; connected preform porosity must permit infiltration. |
| IG | Air. **P:** screening maximum 1010°C/2 h, nominal 1°C/min approach to growth interval; maximum, undercooling and slow-growth schedule require composition-specific calibration. |
| Oxygenation | **P:** flowing O2, 450°C/24 h, then 0.5°C/min cooling; specimen-zone temperature ±2°C. Validate duration across wall thickness. |
| XRD | **P:** Cu Kα, λ = 1.5406 Å; powder 10–80° 2θ, 0.02° steps; exposure calibrated. |
| SEM/EDAX | **P:** 5–20 kV, 8–10 mm working distance; calibrate interaction volume and spectral sensitivity. |
| AC susceptibility | **P:** 1 kHz, 1 Oe; screen 60–100 K, transition steps ≤0.1 K, stability ±0.1 K. |
| VSM | ±9 T, 10–77 K; **P:** 10, 30, 50, 65, 77 K; equilibrium stability ±0.1 K, H–c alignment ±2°. |
| Shielding | 77 K; dc field through 41 G = 4.1 mT; current limits derived from calibrated coil field/current relation. |

Thermal schedules are pilot starting points, not validated growth or complete-oxygenation recipes. Release actual setpoints, ramps, dwell times and tolerances after calibration.

##### 4. Sequential operations and hand-offs
1. **Powder preparation:** Weigh against the released sheet; retain input aliquots. Use Y-211 for Y and compatible mixed-RE-211 for NSG/NSGW. Mill coarse constituents with contamination blanks. Disperse W separately, then incorporate without prolonged aggressive milling. Record solids, temperature, viscosity, energy and settling; re-disperse segregating slurry before casting.
2. **Casting:** Degas without solvent boiling; fill annular moulds without trapped air. Record gel endpoints; demould when self-supporting. Dry incrementally to stable mass, then debind by the qualified schedule. Record dimensions, mass, cracks and permeability. Reject collapsed or cracked preforms; do not seek impermeable green bodies.
3. **Growth hand-off:** Transfer labeled preforms with inspection records and orientation marks. Photograph the approved reservoir/support arrangement. Wet the annulus without obstructing the bore or either seed; use qualified infiltrant and compatible supports.
4. **Seeding/IG:** Verify film orientation and carrier thermal compatibility. Place films against top and bottom surfaces; **P:** mutual crystallographic alignment within 2°. Execute the released air schedule and log actual temperatures. Check infiltration, seed survival and growth-front impingement. Two seeds do not prove one domain.
5. **Oxygenation hand-off:** Photograph/weigh grown cylinders; flag cracks, residual reservoir and incomplete infiltration. Remove external residue by a documented non-damaging method. Oxygenate using the released cycle; confirm spatial transition consistency before accepting oxygenation completeness.
6. **Testing hand-off:** Inspect intact cylinders and perform shielding before cutting. Saw coolant-assisted mapped top/middle/bottom specimens. Allocate polished sections to microscopy, representative powder to Rietveld, oriented coupons to susceptibility/VSM, and distributed lamellae to mandatory nanoscale analysis. Preserve archive material and cutting coordinates.

#### Related Knowledge

- The listed geometry, slurry, mixing and thermal settings are proposed starting points. Growth and oxygenation require released composition-specific schedules rather than treating screening conditions as established recipes. **Source:** B009, B010.
- Separate W dispersion, recorded slurry behavior and qualified drying/debinding support the prescribed casting sequence. Connected preform porosity is required for infiltration; impermeable green bodies are not the intended endpoint. **Source:** B011.
- Top/bottom seed placement requires verified orientation, carrier compatibility and survival. Growth-front impingement must be checked because two seeds do not establish a single domain. Intact-cylinder shielding precedes destructive specimen allocation. **Source:** B011.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize phases, texture, inclusions and spatial oxygenation

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Characterization and material-conformance gates
**XRD:** Use representative crushed fragments for phase fractions/lattice refinement and intact mapped surfaces with rocking/pole/azimuthal scans for texture. Record standards, overlap treatment, preferred orientation and residuals. Supplied comparison values are a = 3.824(2), b = 3.888(9), c = 11.694(0) Å; the last notation does not establish zero uncertainty or uniquely determine oxygen content.

For orthorhombic (103), 1/d² = 1/a² + 9/c² gives d ≈2.730 Å and Cu Kα 2θ ≈32.78°. Its normal is approximately 45.5° from c: establish c independently rather than aligning magnetic measurements to the cylinder axis by assumption.

**Optical/FE-SEM/EDAX:** Survey multiple circumferential sectors at all three heights. Quantify cracks, porosity, boundaries, 211 size/area fraction and RE/Ba/Cu/W distributions. Include agglomerates and defective fields; report stereological assumptions and detection limits. A literal zero-size particle is not measurable; report the lower detection limit for the supplied 0–2 µm range.

**Mandatory nanoscale gate:** Examine spatially distributed post-growth NSGW lamellae using qualified TEM/STEM imaging plus chemical mapping and diffraction or another validated structural discriminator. Measure inclusion sizes and distinguish WO3 from reaction products; W detection alone is insufficient. Record sampling coverage, preparation artifacts and uncertainty. Analyze control lamellae for comparison. If identity or representative 2–6 nm retention cannot be established, classify material conformance as unresolved; if transformed/coarsened, classify as nonconforming. Retain safe property measurements, but do not attribute them to verified WO3 nanoinclusions. Requalify processing on pilots before another matched block. Atomic substitution and stacking-fault claims require direct adequate evidence, not SEM inference.

**Susceptibility:** Record χ′/χ″, holder background and orientation. Define Tc midpoint and normalized 10–90% ΔTc; supplied ΔTc ≈1.5 K is a reference target. Broad transitions trigger oxygenation/connectivity diagnosis. Quarantine mechanically unstable or through-wall-cracked specimens from shielding/current tests; preserve low-risk characterization. Final conformance requires documented inclusion evidence, texture assessment and spatial oxygenation assessment; record any unresolved criterion explicitly.

#### Related Knowledge

- Representative powder diffraction serves phase and lattice analysis, while mapped intact surfaces serve texture assessment. The c-axis must be established independently; a (103) orientation does not justify assuming magnetic alignment along the cylinder axis. **Source:** B012, B013.
- Microscopy sampling includes multiple sectors and heights, defective fields and agglomerates. Particle-size and composition reporting must retain stereological assumptions and detection limits rather than interpreting a literal zero-size lower bound. **Source:** B014.
- Qualified post-growth nanoscale imaging, chemical mapping and structural discrimination determine whether representative 2–6 nm WO3 inclusions are retained. Unresolved identity and transformed/coarsened inclusions require distinct conformance classifications. **Source:** B015.
- Susceptibility uses defined transition measures and spatial assessment to evaluate oxygenation consistency. Broad transitions require diagnosis, and mechanically unstable or through-wall-cracked specimens are excluded from shielding/current tests while low-risk characterization is preserved. **Source:** B016.

### Experimental Task 4: Measure magnetic hysteresis and intact-cylinder shielding

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Magnetic measurements and analysis
At each temperature, zero-field cool from above the measured transition; subtract holder background. Acquire 0 → +9 → −9 → +9 → 0 T with matched branch fields. **P:** dense points around zero and 0.2, 3, 7, 8 and 8.5 T; nominal 30 s dwell, validated by relaxation checks. Record sweep rate and history.

For rectangular coupons, Jc = 20ΔM/[a(1−a/3b)], with transverse a ≤ b in cm, full branch separation ΔM in emu/cm³, and Jc in A/cm². Normalize moment by measured volume. For 2 × 2 × 1 mm with field along the 1-mm dimension, a = b =0.2 cm and Jc =150ΔM. Do not apply this formula to the hollow cylinder; report geometry, demagnetization and crack limitations.

At high field use B ≈ μ0H: Fp[N/cm³] =0.01 Jc[A/cm²]B[T]. Thus 400 A/cm² at 8.5 T gives 34 N/cm³; 39.6 N/cm³ at 8 T requires 495 A/cm². Compare full curves with supplied targets and 7–8 T peak positions; qualify low-field self-field effects. **P:** Hirr criterion Jc =100 A/cm² with sensitivity analysis. If unreached at 9 T, report Hirr >9 T and omit uniquely normalized fits. Dual peaks or fit exponents alone do not establish mechanism.

**Shielding:** Map empty-coil field and cold probe offsets. Zero-field cool the intact cylinder; record axial bore-probe depth. Ramp axial dc field through 4.1 mT in both directions, recording external/bore fields, temperature, current and dwell. Report attenuation and S =Bexternal/Bbore, including detection-limited bounds. No supplied numerical S threshold exists; define the attenuation criterion before interpreting “shielding at 41 G.”

#### Related Knowledge

- Matched hysteresis branches, zero-field cooling, background subtraction and recorded field history provide the prescribed basis for magnetic comparisons. Proposed dwell times require relaxation checks. **Source:** B017.
- The rectangular-coupon Jc calculation requires full branch separation, measured-volume normalization and transverse dimensions. It must not be transferred to hollow-cylinder geometry, and geometry, demagnetization and cracks remain reporting limitations. **Source:** B018.
- Pinning-force conversion uses the stated high-field approximation. An unreached irreversibility criterion yields a lower bound and precludes uniquely normalized fits; dual peaks or fit exponents alone do not establish a mechanism. **Source:** B019.
- Shielding interpretation requires calibrated external fields, cold probe offsets, bore-probe depth and detection-limited bounds. The supplied 41 G field does not supply a numerical shielding-factor acceptance threshold. **Source:** B020.

### Experimental Task 5: Control optional transport, manage failures and archive traceable results

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Optional transport, risk controls and feedback
Optional four-probe transport does not replace VSM. **P:** 6 ×1 ×0.5 mm bar, 3-mm tap spacing, 1 µV/cm criterion: Vc =0.3 µV. Area 0.005 cm² gives Ic ≈22 A at 4.4 kA/cm² and 2 A at 0.4 kA/cm². Verify actual geometry, contacts, noise, cooling and proposed ≥33 A capability; separately verify 8.5-T transport access. Use current reversal and heating aborts.

Contain Ba/RE/W dust; ventilate solvents and exclude ignition sources. Use cooled sonication, guarded furnaces, oxygen-compatible plumbing, cryogenic PPE/ventilation and magnet-projectile controls. Never seal LN2 vessels; interlock solenoid heating. LN2 testing does not demonstrate cryogen-free operation.

Diagnose segregation from slurry records, failed growth from seed/thermal maps, broad transitions from oxygenation versus cracks, anomalous Jc from volume/background/alignment, and poor shielding from end effects versus defects. Change one calibrated process variable in a new matched block; preserve core compositions and original data. Reoxygenated specimens receive paired before/after identifiers.

Archive recipes, actual thermal histories, dimensions, calibration files, raw images/spectra/diffraction/magnetic traces, analysis code, exclusions and uncertainties as `SampleID_Date_Test`. Separate independent-cylinder statistics from coupon and technical repeats.

#### Related Knowledge

- Optional four-probe transport supplements rather than replaces VSM. Proposed geometry and current capability require verification together with contacts, noise, cooling and separate high-field access. **Source:** B021.
- The protocol retains dust containment, solvent ventilation, cooled sonication, furnace guarding, oxygen compatibility, cryogenic precautions and magnet controls as execution prerequisites. Liquid-nitrogen testing does not establish cryogen-free operation. **Source:** B022.
- Failure diagnosis uses the existing slurry, growth, oxygenation and measurement records. Any subsequent matched block changes one calibrated process variable while preserving core compositions, original data and paired reoxygenation identifiers. **Source:** B023.
- Archiving preserves recipes, actual histories, calibrations, raw measurements, exclusions and uncertainties. Independent-cylinder statistics remain separate from coupon and technical repeats. **Source:** B024.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Stage 1 — Release the comparison design and execution prerequisites
**Task 1: Comparison and production release.** Retain three matched arms: Y-123 without W; the specified 20 wt% mixed Nd/Sm/Gd-123 formulation without W; and that formulation with 0.5 wt% WO3 initially 2–6 nm. Preserve embedded compatible 211, the supplied 0–2 µm size range with an explicit lower detection limit, and 2 wt% excess Ba. Match architecture, 211 loading/size, seeds and thermal history. These arms do not establish factorial synergy. **P:** three independent blocks containing all arms, randomized furnace positions and test order, with parent–child identifiers and retained failures.

Before batching, release weight denominators, 211 charge and fraction basis, infiltrant composition/mass, Ba precursor and elemental-equivalent basis, oxygen assumptions and nominal RE-site occupancy accounting for phase partitioning. Mass fraction is not atomic substitution fraction. Qualify furnace uniformity, seed survival, instrument capabilities and representative nanoscale resolution/phase discrimination. No production release without an executable recipe and verification pathway. Apply the existing dust, solvent, sonication, furnace, oxygen, cryogenic, magnet and solenoid safety controls throughout; never seal liquid-nitrogen vessels.

Record solids, temperature, viscosity, energy and settling, and re-disperse segregating slurry. Degas, cast annular preforms, document gel endpoints, dry to stable mass and debind by the qualified schedule. Reject collapsed or cracked preforms; preserve connected porosity for infiltration.

Transfer labeled, oriented preforms with inspection records. Document the reservoir/support arrangement without obstructing the bore or seeds. Check infiltration, seed survival and growth-front impingement; two seeds do not prove one domain. Document grown-cylinder condition, remove external residue non-destructively and apply the released oxygenation cycle. The listed **P** dimensions, slurry/mixing settings, seed alignment and thermal schedules remain calibration-dependent; spatial transition consistency is required before accepting oxygenation completeness.

**Task 3: Characterization and conformance.** Inspect intact cylinders and perform eligible shielding measurements before cutting. Allocate mapped top/middle/bottom material to microscopy, representative powder diffraction, oriented susceptibility/VSM coupons and distributed nanoscale lamellae; preserve archives and cutting coordinates. Determine phases and texture using the prescribed XRD approaches, and establish c independently of the cylinder axis. ### Stage 3 — Measure properties, manage failures and preserve evidence
**Task 4: Magnetic and shielding measurements.** Conduct intact-cylinder shielding at 77 K before destructive sampling, using calibrated empty-coil fields and cold offsets, recorded bore-probe depth and bidirectional ramps through 4.1 mT. Report attenuation, field ratios and detection-limited bounds; define the attenuation criterion before interpreting shielding at 41 G.

Validate **P** temperature points, dwell and dense-field sampling. Normalize moment by measured volume; use the rectangular-coupon Jc expression only for suitable coupons. Retain geometry, demagnetization, crack and low-field limitations. Derive Fp using the supplied conversion. Keep the **P** irreversibility criterion explicit; if unreached, report Hirr >9 T and omit uniquely normalized fits. Supplied Jc, Fp, transition-width and lattice values remain reference targets, not outcomes; curve features alone do not establish mechanisms.

**Task 5: Optional testing and closure.** Optional transport requires verified geometry, contacts, noise, cooling, current capability and separate high-field access, with reversal and heating aborts; it does not replace VSM. Diagnose failures from retained process and measurement records. Preserve original data, exclusions and uncertainties, distinguish independent cylinders from repeats, and archive traceable raw data and analysis.
