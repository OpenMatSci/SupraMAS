# Experimental Plan: Substrate-induced pinning in epitaxial YBCO

## Experimental Draft

### Experimental overview

Test whether an STO+CeO2 nanocomposite buffer and YBCO pulse frequency jointly alter threading-dislocation morphology and low-temperature, high-field transport without degrading superconductivity. CeO2 remains confined to the buffer; no foreign pinning phase is intentionally added to YBCO.

The proposed core design crosses MgO(100)/STO/YBCO and MgO(100)/(STO+CeO2 NC)/YBCO with 10, 20 and 100 Hz YBCO deposition. Three independent films per cell give 18 films, randomized within matched blocks. Technical bridge replicates do not replace independent depositions. Match substrate lot, thicknesses, fluence, temperature, oxygenation, transfer and measurement histories.

Use stoichiometric targets, a composition-qualified homogeneous composite buffer target, separate 248 nm KrF PLD chambers, thickness metrology, high-resolution XRD, FIB/STEM-EDX, qualified bridge fabrication and four-probe transport in a rotatable 9 T PPMS. Current delivery, cryogenic wiring, voltage uncertainty and thermal response require qualification before electrical release.

The workflow is qualification and preparation, buffer/YBCO fabrication with controlled hand-offs, structural characterization, transport measurement, and consolidated quality review. Buffers use fixed 10 Hz, 880 °C and 400 mTorr oxygen conditions. Proposed YBCO growth, oxygenation, geometry and tolerances remain pilot-dependent; the unresolved NC composition must be qualified before releasing the matrix. Thickness is controlled using measured pulse yield, not equal deposition time.

Anticipated outputs are traceable growth records, epitaxy and morphology measurements, measured Tc, resistivity, Jc and Fp, uncertainties, and independent-film comparisons of buffer and frequency effects. Supplied performance values are reference targets, not measured outcomes. Preserve failures and compliance-limited bounds. Optional references and model fits remain separate from the core matrix and subject to their stated unresolved qualifications.

## Detailed Experimental Tasks

### Experimental Task 1: Define the comparison matrix and qualify materials, equipment and operating conditions

#### Specific Content

##### 1. Scope and comparison matrix
Test whether an STO+CeO2 nanocomposite buffer and YBCO pulse frequency jointly alter threading-dislocation morphology and improve low-temperature, high-field transport without degrading superconductivity. CeO2 is confined to the buffer; do not intentionally add foreign pinning phases to YBCO.

| Core arm | Substrate/buffer/YBCO | YBCO frequencies |
|---|---|---|
| S | MgO(100)/STO/YBCO | 10, 20, 100 Hz |
| N | MgO(100)/(STO+CeO2 NC)/YBCO | 10, 20, 100 Hz |

**Proposed design:** three independent deposition replicates per cell, 18 films total. Use IDs `B01-S-F020-R1` with block, buffer, frequency and replicate; append `-W` for witnesses, `-MB1` for bridges and `-XS1` for lamellae. Randomize deposition order within matched blocks. Multiple bridges are technical replicates, not independent films. Match substrate lot, buffer and YBCO thickness, fluence, temperature, oxygenation, transfer history and measurement history across cells.

Supplied performance values are evaluation targets, not promised measurements: Fp=665 GN m−3 at 20 K/9 T, self-field Jc=2.63 MA cm−2 at 77 K, Tc≥89 K. Also compare measured 77 K Fp maxima with supplied NC/STO values of 4.13/1.56 GN m−3. Do not discard films merely for missing a performance target.

##### 2. Materials, equipment and release prerequisites
Use MgO(100), stoichiometric YBCO and STO PLD targets, and a homogeneous STO+CeO2 composite target with a qualified composition. The NC composition is unspecified: establish it on pilot witnesses before releasing the matrix. Do not substitute sequential STO/CeO2/STO layers or a CuO-enriched YBCO target.

Required equipment: separate buffer and YBCO PLD chambers with 248 nm KrF laser, heater capable of calibrated 880 °C, oxygen pressure control, target rotation and fluence monitoring; thickness metrology; high-resolution XRD; FIB and STEM-EDX; photolithography, qualified YBCO ion milling and contact metallization; microscope; PPMS with 9 T magnet and rotator; externally integrated current source, low-noise voltmeter and current-rated cryogenic wiring. AFM and RHEED are useful diagnostic tools, not substitutes for structural verification.

Before growth, verify laser operation at 100 Hz without fluence drift, substrate-temperature calibration, pressure gauges, deposition uniformity and growth rate for each material/frequency. Verify PPMS operation at 20 K/9 T and transport above 95 K for Tc and resistivity. Approve oxygen-compatible pressure hardware and electrical feedthrough/current ratings.

##### 3. Consolidated operating conditions
**P denotes a proposed setting or tolerance requiring pilot calibration.** Fixed task conditions must not be changed during the core comparison.

| Operation | Setting |
|---|---|
| Substrate | MgO(100); 10×10 mm, RMS roughness <0.5 nm (P) |
| Buffers | 10 Hz; 880 °C; 400 mTorr O2 = 53.3 Pa = 0.533 mbar |
| Buffer tolerance/thickness | ±2 °C, ±5% pressure (P); allowed 100–150 nm, nominal 125±10 nm (P) |
| YBCO | 10, 20, 100 Hz; allowed 300–360 nm, nominal 330±15 nm (P) |
| YBCO growth | 780±2 °C, constant 200 mTorr/26.7 Pa O2 ±5% (P) |
| PLD geometry/fluence | 50 mm target distance; 2.0 J cm−2 ±5% for each target (P; qualify independently) |
| Oxygenation | Cool at 10 °C min−1 to 450 °C; hold 30 min in 1 bar O2; cool at 5 °C min−1 (P; pressure-rated apparatus required) |
| Microbridge | 50–100 μm measured width; nominal 1.00 mm voltage-tap separation (P) |
| Transport stability | Temperature ±0.1 K, angle accuracy ±0.1° (P; verify capability) |
| Criterion | Ecrit=1 μV cm−1; Vcrit=Ecrit×measured tap separation |

Determine pulse counts from measured thickness-per-pulse, not equal deposition time. Keep growth fluence and background pressure constant; record frequency-dependent growth duration. Do not assume equal pulse yield at all frequencies. Qualify the common oxygenation schedule for comparable Tc; do not infer δ from annealing settings.

### Experimental Task 2: Fabricate qualified buffers, YBCO films and transport bridges with controlled hand-offs

#### Specific Content

##### 4. Sequential fabrication and hand-offs
1. **Prepare substrates.** Log lot, orientation and surface condition. Proposed cleaning: acetone and IPA ultrasonication, 10 min each; brief DI rinse and immediate N2 drying. Avoid prolonged water exposure to MgO. Proposed conditioning: 800 °C for 1 h near 10−6 Torr in PLD, after verifying chamber capability. Inspect for contamination, roughening and cracks; reject damaged substrates before assignment.
2. **Prepare targets.** Record composition certificates, density and conditioning history. Measure spot area and pulse energy at the target plane. Pre-ablate with shutter closed until plume/energy stabilize; log pulses. Use identical conditioning rules for all arms.
3. **Grow buffers.** Deposit STO or co-grown NC directly on MgO at the fixed conditions. Use the composite target for simultaneous STO/CeO2 delivery. Deposit to calibrated thickness; no extra seed, cap, MgO interlayer or passivation on the growth surface. Include paired witnesses. RHEED, if available, is diagnostic; NC growth need not show layer-by-layer oscillations.
4. **Buffer quality gate and hand-off.** Qualify a pilot witness by XRD, thickness and STEM before production. Require correct thickness, continuous film, cube-on-cube alignment and STO φ-width <3°. NC must show rods within an STO matrix. Then transfer production buffers to the YBCO chamber using a logged, identical clean-transfer procedure. If vacuum transfer is unavailable, standardize dry storage, exposure time and non-damaging surface conditioning on pilots. Do not coat production buffers for microscopy.
5. **Grow YBCO.** Apply the assigned frequency and calibrated pulse count. Record temperature, pressure, pulse energy, growth time and interruptions. Perform the common oxygenation schedule. Label and store cooled films dry. Quarantine excursions; do not repair only one arm and treat it as matched.
6. **Structural hand-off.** Conduct nondestructive XRD/thickness checks on every film before patterning. Retain an unpatterned region. Route qualified films to microfabrication; reserve paired material for destructive microscopy, preferably after transport or outside the bridge region.
7. **Pattern bridges.** Proposed photolithography plus low-damage Ar ion milling through YBCO, stopping in the buffer, isolates 50–100 μm channels. Qualify milling damage and edge bias on pilots. KI/I2 chemistry may pattern Au, not isolate YBCO. Place Au only on contact pads, not across the voltage span. Measure width at several positions, film thickness, tap separation and edge defects. Bond current-rated leads; verify isolation, pad adhesion and contact resistance separately from bridge resistance.
8. **Electrical hand-off.** Release only with geometry record, wiring diagram, current/voltage compliance limits and thermal qualification. Record pre/post-pattern Tc to detect processing damage.

#### Related Knowledge

- Simultaneous STO/CeO2 delivery from the composite target preserves the required NC architecture. Pilot XRD, thickness and STEM checks establish the buffer gate before production and standardized transfer. **Source:** B010.
- Assigned-frequency YBCO deposition, common oxygenation and logged transfer histories preserve matched processing. Excursions are quarantined rather than repaired selectively and treated as matched films. **Source:** B010.
- Bridge isolation requires qualified YBCO milling; Au-patterning chemistry does not isolate YBCO. Measured geometry, separate contact checks, thermal qualification and pre/post-pattern Tc records support electrical release. **Source:** B010.

### Experimental Task 3: Characterize epitaxy, buffer rods and YBCO dislocation morphology

#### Specific Content

##### 5. Structural characterization
Use calibrated Cu Kα XRD, proposed 2θ coverage 5–80° and 0.02° steps, with detailed peak scans. Record YBCO (00l), rocking curves and indexed asymmetric φ scans relative to MgO azimuths. For Cu Kα, MgO(200) is near 42.9°, STO(002) near 46.5°, CeO2(002) near 33.1°; CeO2 near 47.5° is (022)/(220), **not (002)**. YBCO(005) is near 38.5°. Confirm indexing against calibrated reference structures.

Calculate d=λ/(2 sinθ), and for YBCO(00l), c=l d. Use multiple peaks and an instrumental standard; report strain relative to an explicitly chosen, oxygenation-matched reference. Peak shifts alone do not quantify strain-transfer percentage or prove unchanged oxygen content.

Prepare proposed FIB lamellae at 30 kV with final 2 kV polishing and protective cap confined to analytical regions. Establish a STEM dose budget by a damage series; verify contrast in multiple diffraction conditions. Map Y, Ba, Cu, Sr, Ti, Ce and O with calibrated EDX and appropriate spectral deconvolution. Quantify rods, interfaces, dislocation continuity, waviness and subgrain misorientation using multiple spatially separated fields per film; proposed minimum 50 rod-spacing measurements where available. Use GPA with stated reference and uncertainty.

Report line-intercept dislocation counts in μm−1, sampled length and viewing geometry. The supplied 10–20 μm−1 is not an areal density. Report rod diameter and both edge-to-edge and centre-to-centre spacing: the supplied dimensions are ambiguous. Do not derive a YBCO matching field from buffer rod spacing alone. Investigate incidental Y2O3 and Ce-bearing reaction products; report detection limits, not absolute phase absence from EDX alone.

#### Related Knowledge

- Calibrated, correctly indexed XRD supports epitaxy and lattice-parameter analysis. Multiple peaks, an instrumental standard and an oxygenation-matched reference are required for the specified strain reporting; peak shifts alone do not establish oxygen content or strain-transfer percentage. **Source:** B011, B012.
- The proposed FIB preparation, qualified STEM dose budget, multiple diffraction conditions and calibrated EDX provide the specified basis for examining rods, interfaces and dislocation trajectories while tracking analytical damage and uncertainty. **Source:** B013.
- Line-intercept counts must retain their length-based units and viewing geometry. Distinct spacing definitions and detection limits prevent ambiguous rod dimensions or EDX observations from becoming unsupported matching-field or phase-absence claims. **Source:** B014.

### Experimental Task 4: Qualify electrical capability and measure temperature-, field- and angle-dependent transport

#### Specific Content

##### 6. Transport and equipment consistency
For bridge area wt=1.5–3.6×10−11 m2:
- Supplied 77 K Jc implies Ic=0.395–0.947 A.
- Fp=665×10^9 N m−3 at 9 T implies Jc=Fp/B=7.39×10^10 A m−2=7.39 MA cm−2 and Ic=1.11–2.66 A.

A 100 mA source is inadequate. A proposed ≥5 A source must be integrated and thermally qualified; colder self-field currents may exceed the calculated target requirement. At 1 mm tap spacing, Vcrit=0.1 μV; qualify noise/offset uncertainty ≤0.01 μV (P), not merely nominal voltmeter resolution. Verify tap separation >5 bridge widths and current-pad geometry for uniform flow. Never retain a universal 10^7 A cm−2 ceiling without assessing whether it truncates measurements.

Measure low-current R(T) from at least 100 to 80 K; proposed Tc is the 50% normal-state resistance point, with 90–10% transition width and zero-resistance temperature also reported. Measure ρ(95 K)=Rwt/L. Verify negligible current heating.

Measure I–V at 20, 40, 65 and 77 K, B||c, 0–9 T in proposed 0.5 T increments. Use consistent field history, settling and polarity reversal; remove offsets and interpolate the measured criterion crossing rather than fitting the entire nonlinear curve linearly. Start low, refine steps near Ic, stop promptly above criterion. Qualify pulse duration/duty cycle or DC sweeps by heating tests and waveform recording. If compliance is reached first, report Ic/Jc lower bounds, not fitted values.

Measure angular Jc at 65 K and 40 K at proposed 1, 3 and 5 T: θ=0° is B||c, θ=90° is B||ab; use 5° steps, refined near peaks. Rotate about the current axis to maintain B⊥J; verify alignment and both rotation directions.

Calculate Fp=JcB in SI using applied μ0H≈B away from self-field. At nominal zero field report self-field Jc, not an inferred Fp. Report maxima within the measured window, normalized Jc with explicit denominator, and uncertainty from Ic, width and thickness.

#### Related Knowledge

- The target-derived current requirements establish why a 100 mA source is inadequate. The proposed higher-current source, wiring, thermal response and voltage uncertainty require qualification; these calculations are not measured film performance. **Source:** B015, B016.
- Low-current resistance measurements define the proposed Tc convention and resistivity calculation. Criterion-crossing interpolation, offset removal and heating-qualified waveforms support transport extraction; compliance-limited measurements remain lower bounds. **Source:** B017, B018.
- Rotation about the current axis maintains the specified transverse field geometry. Fp uses JcB away from self-field, while zero-field reporting remains self-field Jc; normalization denominators and geometry uncertainties must be explicit. **Source:** B019, B020.

### Experimental Task 5: Review failures, enforce safety and consolidate comparative analysis and records

#### Specific Content

##### 7. Quality, diagnosis, records and feedback
Cracked, misoriented or wrong-thickness films are process failures: retain records, quarantine and remake the affected matched block. Low Tc triggers checks of oxygenation, milling damage and contamination; reoxygenation is a separately labelled diagnostic, not silently substituted data. Missing NC rods triggers composition/plume checks. Weak high-frequency pinning requires morphology and texture analysis, not automatic frequency adjustment. Heating/hysteresis triggers reduced duty cycle, improved contacts or narrower bridges within the specified range; rerun all comparison arms consistently.

Use laser interlocks/UV protection, solvent ventilation, oxygen-compatible pressure hardware, hot-surface precautions, FIB/STEM training and magnet exclusion controls. Current shutdown limits must derive from tested thermal response and wiring ratings; abort on voltage runaway or temperature excursion.

Archive raw growth logs, calibration files, sample genealogy, images/spectra, geometries, full I–V/time traces, T/B/angle histories, exclusions and analysis scripts. Compare buffer and frequency main effects and their interaction using independent-film statistics; correlate transport with blinded morphology analysis. Change only one pilot parameter at a time, freeze qualified settings before the matrix, and repeat matched controls after changes.

##### 8. Optional extensions
After the core matrix, direct YBCO on STO single crystal, a clearly defined unbuffered reference, or YBCO+5 vol% BaHfO3 may contextualize the supplied comparisons. Their fabrication details remain unresolved; they do not replace the MgO/STO versus MgO/NC controls. Pinning-model fits are optional only if an independently measured irreversibility field is accessible; do not extrapolate it from a truncated 9 T sweep. Tape scalability and strain-transfer magnitude are not established by this experiment.

#### Related Knowledge

- Process failures retain their records and trigger quarantine and matched-block remakes. Reoxygenation remains separately labelled diagnostic data, and morphology or processing checks precede any interpretation of weak pinning. **Source:** B021.
- Laser, solvent, oxygen-pressure, hot-surface, microscopy and magnet controls are safety prerequisites. Current shutdown limits derive from tested thermal response and wiring ratings. **Source:** B022.
- Raw data, genealogy, exclusions and analysis scripts support independent-film comparison of buffer and frequency effects and their interaction. Blinded morphology analysis and frozen qualified settings preserve the stated comparison. **Source:** B023.
- Optional references have unresolved fabrication details and cannot replace the core controls. Pinning-model fits require an independently measured irreversibility field; the experiment does not establish tape scalability or strain-transfer magnitude. **Source:** B024.

## Refined Experimental Plan

### Consolidated execution plan

#### 1. Freeze the comparison and release prerequisites
Use MgO(100)/STO/YBCO and MgO(100)/(STO+CeO2 NC)/YBCO at 10, 20 and 100 Hz YBCO frequency. The proposed design has three independent depositions per cell, 18 films total, randomized within matched blocks. Track film, witness, bridge and lamella genealogy; bridges are technical replicates. Match substrate lot, thickness, fluence, temperature, oxygenation, transfer and measurement histories. CeO2 belongs only in the co-grown buffer; exclude layered substitutes and intentional YBCO pinning additions.

Qualify the unresolved composite-target composition, growth rates at each frequency, laser stability at 100 Hz, temperature/pressure calibration, uniformity and transport capability before matrix release. P denotes proposed settings or tolerances requiring pilot qualification. Freeze qualified settings; change one pilot parameter at a time and repeat matched controls after changes. Require laser/UV, solvent, oxygen-pressure, hot-surface, microscopy and magnet safety controls.

#### 2. Fabricate and release matched specimens
Prepare and inspect substrates and condition targets using common logged procedures. Proposed cleaning and conditioning remain capability-dependent. Grow 100–150 nm buffers in the separate buffer chamber at fixed 10 Hz, 880 °C and 400 mTorr O2. Proposed nominal thickness is 125±10 nm, with ±2 °C and ±5% pressure tolerances. Qualify witnesses for continuity, thickness, cube-on-cube alignment, STO φ-width <3° and NC rods within STO before production transfer. Standardize clean transfer; do not coat production growth surfaces for microscopy.

Grow 300–360 nm YBCO using assigned frequency and calibrated pulse counts, not equal deposition time. Proposed nominal thickness is 330±15 nm; proposed growth is 780±2 °C at constant 200 mTorr O2 ±5%. Use 50 mm target distance and proposed, independently qualified 2.0 J cm−2 ±5% fluence. Record frequency-dependent duration and excursions. Qualify the common proposed oxygenation schedule: cool at 10 °C min−1 to 450 °C, hold 30 min in 1 bar O2, then cool at 5 °C min−1 in pressure-rated apparatus. Do not infer oxygen deficiency from this schedule.

Check every film nondestructively before patterning. Retain unpatterned material and reserve destructive analysis outside bridges or preferably after transport. Qualify proposed photolithography/Ar milling through YBCO into the buffer; Au belongs only on pads. Measure 50–100 μm bridge widths, thickness, tap separation and edge defects. Release with isolation/contact checks, pre/post-pattern Tc, wiring, compliance limits and thermal qualification.

#### 3. Establish structural measurements
Use calibrated XRD with verified indexing, rocking curves and asymmetric φ scans. Derive lattice parameters from multiple peaks with an instrumental standard and explicitly chosen oxygenation-matched strain reference. Peak shifts do not prove unchanged oxygen content or quantify strain transfer.

Use proposed FIB preparation with analytical-region protection and a qualified STEM dose budget. Examine multiple separated fields and diffraction conditions; map the specified elements with calibrated EDX. Report rods, interfaces, dislocation continuity/waviness, subgrain misorientation and GPA reference/uncertainty. Keep line-intercept counts in μm−1 with sampled length and geometry; distinguish edge-to-edge from centre-to-centre spacing. Report incidental phases and detection limits, without deriving matching fields from buffer spacing.

#### 4. Measure and normalize transport
Qualify the proposed ≥5 A source, current-rated wiring and heating response; 100 mA is inadequate for target-derived currents. Verify tap separation exceeds five bridge widths. Apply Ecrit=1 μV cm−1 using measured separation; proposed 1 mm spacing gives 0.1 μV, requiring proposed uncertainty ≤0.01 μV. Verify proposed temperature and angular stability.

Measure low-current R(T) over at least 100–80 K, proposed 50% Tc, transition width, zero-resistance temperature and ρ(95 K). Measure I–V at 20, 40, 65 and 77 K, B||c, 0–9 T, with proposed 0.5 T increments. Maintain field history, settling, polarity reversal and heating-qualified waveforms; interpolate criterion crossings and report compliance-limited lower bounds. Measure angular Jc at 40/65 K and proposed 1/3/5 T, rotating about current with defined c/ab angles. Calculate Jc from measured geometry and Fp=JcB away from self-field; report measured-window maxima, explicit normalization and uncertainty.

#### 5. Consolidate decisions and outputs
Quarantine process failures, preserve records and remake affected matched blocks. Label diagnostic reoxygenation separately; apply heating-related corrections consistently across arms. Abort voltage runaway or temperature excursions using tested shutdown limits. Archive raw records and compare independent-film buffer/frequency effects and interaction against blinded morphology. Treat supplied Fp, Jc and Tc values, including 77 K NC/STO Fp references, only as targets—not exclusion criteria. Optional references remain unresolved; model fits require independently measured irreversibility fields.
